| **Bride's father** | **Thomas Garmly** |
| **Married** | **10 August 1884**, **St Luke Church, Port Pirie** |

### What it settles

**The father is named.** Our tree gives the emigrant's father as *Joannes /
Ivan Žubrinić, born 19 June 1837, Forest Ranger, Otočac*. The 1884 marriage,
written in Australia in his son's lifetime, names him **John Zubrinich** —
independent civil confirmation of a link the archive had only from the tree.

### What it adds that we did not have

- **A marriage date and place.** We held neither.
- **The bride's father: Thomas Garmly / Gormley.** Our record for
  **Rose Gormley (b. 17 March 1860, County Cavan, Ireland; d. 24 September 1949,
  Port Pirie)** carries **no parents at all**. She now has a father.
- A Croatian emigrant marrying an **Irish** woman in an **Anglican** church at
  Port Pirie in 1884 — the family is Catholic, and later children are baptised
  at St Mark's RC. That is worth noticing rather than smoothing over.

### What it does not settle

It does **not** join the Australian island to Hedviga. That break is further up,
at **Casparus Žubrinić (b. 1767)**, and it is a Croatian record that will fix it.

| **Birth year** | **1859** |

Our record says **died 15 December 1930 at Port Pirie** — the date matches
**exactly**, and Solomontown is the adjoining settlement where many of his
children were born.

### The birth year will not settle

Three sources, three answers: the **marriage (1884)** implies **1862**, the
**death (1930)** implies **1859**, and our tree says **6 March 1860**. None is a
document of birth. His actual baptism is in Croatia and has never been found —
and our tree places it at **Senj** while the whole family is **Otočac**, which
remains unresolved.

**This transcript is an index, not the certificate.** The original South
Australian certificate — orderable on registration **525/341** — would carry
**father's name, mother's name and maiden name**, and that is the document worth
paying for.
