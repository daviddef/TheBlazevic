# Senj births, 1878–1894: the register for 4 March 1879

Waypoint 9R2D-JWL (Senj births 1878–1894, 269 images), read 24 November 2026: image 29 for the first time (printed Str. 21, entries 38–43, baptised 18 to 29 May 1879). Images 25–28 (entries 15–37) are the readings of 25 February and 3 March (`senj-births-february-1879-the-25th.md`, `senj-births-march-1879-the-3rd.md`); the dates below are from those readings.

## The question

Is any child born on 4 March 1879, or baptised on 4 March 1879, in the Senj register, and is any of them a Perpić, Prpić or Tomljanović of the household at Klaričevac 82?

## What the pages are

The printed Croatian table of the earlier 1879 notes: entries numbered from 1 each year, in order of baptism. Image 29 is Str. 21 (entries 38–43): both pages read at full size, and the name and house cells of entry 41 zoomed.
Months: ožujka = March, travnja = April, svibnja = May.

## What was found

- **Baptised on 4 March: none.** The baptisms run 2 March (entries 17, 18), 3 March (entry 19), 11 March (entry 20), 16 March (entry 21) and on.
- **Born on 4 March: none in entries 15–43.** Births of entries 15–37 are in the 3 March note; entries 38–43 are born 16 April, 17 May, 22 May, 5 May, 22 May and 22 May (baptised 18, 22, 25, 25, 25 and 29 May). The nearest births to the date are entry 20 (2 March) and entry 30 (7 March).
- **Where the late baptisms stop.** The longest delay in entries 15–43 is entry 30, born 7 March and baptised 23 April (47 days). A child born on 4 March and baptised by 29 May would be 86 days late.
- **A Perpić of the Krivi Put parish is in entry 41, and it is not the Klaričevac household.** Entry 41: **Milutin Radoslav**, zakonit, born 5 May, baptised 25 May 1879; father «Jerko Perpić c. kr. satnik», mother «Ljudmila rodjena Fabiani»; house «Senj iz župe Krivoputske» (from the parish of Krivi Put); godparents Marko (surname not read), trgovac, and a Babić woman, both «rim. katolici»; baptiser Mirko Babić kapelan; Opazke empty. The surname was zoomed (Perpić, the same hand as the earlier Perpić entries).
  What it shows: a household named Perpić, with a father who is a captain and a mother of another surname, lives in or comes from the Krivi Put parish in 1879. The Klaričevac couple are a farmer (poljodjelac) and Lucia née Prpić, house kb. 82, so this is a different father. What it does not show: whether Jerko is a brother, cousin or other relation of any Perpić of this archive. The tree has no Jerko Perpić, no Milutin Radoslav and no Ljudmila Fabiani, so nothing is added to it.
- Entries 38–43 hold no other Perpić, Prpić or Tomljanović.
  Snapshots: `/snapshots/#senj-births-1879-05-entries-38-43` (image 29, both pages), `#senj-births-1879-05-entry-41-perpic` (entry 41 zoomed); the earlier ones are `#senj-births-1879-02-entries-15-21` (image 25), `#senj-births-1879-05-entries-32-37` (image 28).

## What this does not show

- A birth on 4 March baptised after 29 May would stand at entry 44 or later; those were not read. Not a walk of the register or of 1879: entries 15–43 of images 25–29 only.
- Which Perpić line Jerko belongs to: nothing here ties him to Klaričevac 82 or to any person in the tree.

## What follows

Perpić and Prpić are in the Atlas catalogue and were covered. «Jerko» is a form of a given name the Atlas catalogue does not list as such (it has Jerolim and Jeronim as leads under Girolamo); this page does not say which name it is a form of, so nothing was added. Fabiani is a surname of the mother; nothing ties it to this line, so nothing was added.
