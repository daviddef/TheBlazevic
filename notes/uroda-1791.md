# Dominus Georgius Uroda, died 10 September 1791, buried in his own crypt

15 September 2026. The 1806 marriage read the day before names a groom as
***Joannis filii defuncti Dñi Georgij Uroda*** — a Georgius Uroda already dead,
and styled *Dominus*. **He has now been found, and he is not what this archive
expected the Urode to be.**

## The route

The death index this archive has read — fourteen Urode, 1805 to 1846 — belongs
to the **Umrli 1804–1861** book, and its earliest Uroda is 1808. A man dead
before February 1806 had to be in the **earlier** book.

**He was, and that book has its own alphabetical index which nobody here had
opened.** On film **DGS 5481649**:

T·U at 431, U·V at 432, V·X at 433.**

## Folio 67

The left-hand pages carry the even folio numbers — **62 at image 388, 64 at
389, 66 at 390** — so **folio 67 is image 390's right-hand page**, and that page
is headed ***Anno Domini 1791***.

Near its foot, under *Septembris · ejusdem*:

> ***Dñus Georgius Uroda**, munitus omnibus Sacramentis, [in domo] sua obiit,
> atque die **12ᵃ** sepultus est **in propria [sua Crypta ante] fores Ecclesiæ
> Parochialis** existente.*

**Dominus Georgius Uroda, fortified with all the sacraments, died in his own
house, and on 12 September was buried in his own crypt before the doors of the
parish church.**

## Why this changes the Urode

This archive has carried the Urode as **fishermen**. Ahnentafel 54's wife is
buried in 1834 as *«uxor **piscatoris** Georgii Uroda»*, and he himself in 1846
as *«Georgius Uroda annorum 72, **piscator**»*.

**The man who died in 1791 is styled *Dominus*, dies fortified with all the
sacraments in his own house, and is buried in a crypt his family owns, at the
church door.** That is not a fisherman's death. It is the death of a family with
standing in a small Military Frontier port — and the 1806 marriage confirms the
style, calling him *Dñi Georgij Uroda* fifteen years after he was buried, with
the **village judge** standing as witness to his son's wedding.

## What is proved, and what is not

**Proved, at the page:** a *Dominus* Georgius Uroda died at Karlobag on
10 September 1791 and was buried in a private crypt; a Joannes Uroda, his son,
married Maria daughter of Nicolaus Barletich on 10 February 1806.

**Not proved:** that **ahnentafel 54** belongs to this family. He is a
**Georgius**, a **fisherman**, born about **1774** by his death entry — so he
would be seventeen when the *Dominus* died, and could be a second son. But
*Uroda* may simply be a common Karlobag surname, and **a family that buries in
its own crypt in 1791 producing a fisherman by 1846 is a descent this archive
has not demonstrated.**

## What would settle it

**Ahnentafel 54's own baptism.** He was born about 1774, and Karlobag births
**1771–1804** are filmed on **DGS 5481648** with an index (*Kazalo rođeni A–V*).
If his father is named there, the join is made or broken in one entry — and the
book is the one this archive has already opened for other people.
