# Toma Žubrinić, Šumećica N° 2, 4 January 1838

15 September 2026. Work list row 28. **Ahnentafel 40 is read.** He was the
emptiest man on the direct line above generation five — a name, a year, no
media, state *named* — and he is now a register entry with a date, an age, a
cause, a burial and, above all, **a house number**.

## The entry

Otočac, *Umrli 1834–1858*, **page 45, entry 3 of 1838** — the right-hand page of
image **117**, under the ruled heading ***Anno Domini 1838***. ARK
`3:1:3QS7-L99C-PVR4`.

| Column | What it says |
|---|---|
| *Annus, Mensis, Dies Mortis* | **1838. Januarii die 4ta** |
| *Nomen, Cognomen Defuncti* | **Thomas Xubrinich**, *Inq: ~~Ottoèsanq~~ Centu-rio Confiniarius, ex Pago Shumcchicza N° 2.* |
| *Aetas Defuncti* | **An. 70** |
| *Morbus* | *Senectutis Debilitas* |
| *Sacramenta* | *Omnibus SStis morientium Sacramentis provisus, morte testimonio medico comprobata* |
| *Locus Sepulturæ* | *Cœmeterium Commune SStæ Trinitatis Ottochiæ* |
| *Sepeliens* | *Matthæus Mattasich, minist.* |

The surname is **underlined by the scribe**, as *Stanich* is in the entry below
it — in this book the underline marks the surname, not a by-name, and nothing
here belongs in the by-name list.

**The description is read as far as it is legible and no further.** The scribe
struck a word out — it reads *Ottoèsanq*, almost certainly a first attempt at
*Otočac* — and the surviving phrase runs *Inq: … Centu-/rio Confiniarius*. The
load-bearing words are not in doubt: ***Confiniarius, ex Pago Shumcchicza
N° 2***. The abbreviation *Inq:* and whether the broken word is *Centurio* or
*Centuriæ* are left open rather than guessed at.

## What it settles

**The tree said Šumećica 2 and had nothing to show for it.** Toma Žubrinić's
house number came into this archive from the GEDCOM alone. The register now says
it independently, in the parish's own hand, at his death.

**The age corroborates the birth year.** *An. 70* on 4 January 1838 puts his
birth in **1767 or 1768**. The tree says 1768.

**His widow is read too.** [Marija Findrić](otocac-marija-findric-1849.md) is
entered on **29 July 1849** as «*Maria Žubrinić, Udovica*», aged 69 — eleven
years and seven months after this entry.

**It strengthens the harder question — the one row 1 left open.** Ahnentafel 10,
Nicolaus, was baptised in 1834 into his father's household at **Šumećica N° 24**,
but his own children in **1856 and 1858 are at Šumećica N° 2**. Two readings
stood: he moved, or the man at house 2 is a different Nicolaus. **House 2 is now
documented as his grandfather's house**, eighteen years before his children were
baptised in it. A married grandson in the house his grandfather died in is the
ordinary shape of a *zadruga*; a coincidence of house numbers between two
unrelated Nicolaus Žubrinići is not. **This does not close the question — the
*Status Animarum* still does that — but the two readings are no longer level.**

## And a method the archive did not have

[The house-number note](otocac-house-numbers.md) established that **the baptism
books stop writing the household into the *Conditio* column from about 1836 to
1845**, and that this is the wall in front of question 4.

**The deaths book does not have that gap.** This 1838 entry writes *ex Pago
Shumcchicza N° 2* straight into the *Nomen, Cognomen Defuncti* column, in the
middle of the very years the baptisms go silent. **The Otočac deaths of
1834–1858 are therefore a house-number source for 1836–1845, where the baptisms
are not** — and that is worth more than one ancestor.
