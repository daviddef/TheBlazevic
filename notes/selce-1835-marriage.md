# Selce, 22 November 1835 — the scan that was in the house all along

Read 14 September 2026, from `photos/line/4505564-josephum-and-maria-marriage-1835.jpg`,
**2,987 × 2,163**, swept out of the MyHeritage media library and never opened.

No film viewer, no FamilySearch, no browser. The document was already on disk.

*Liber Copulatorum*, Selce, page 21–22, 1835. Ruled columns —
*Annus/Mensis et dies · Nos. Domus · Sponsus · Sponsa · Testes* — with the entry
written as continuous Latin inside them. An archivist's tab on the right margin
reads **SMEARED INK / RAZLIVENA TINTA**.

## The entry this archive needed

**Domus 51, die 22 9bris (November) 1835:**

> Præmissis inter Missarum solemnia 3bus Denuntiationibus et nullo detecto
> impedimento, Ego Vincentius Rombal Parochus Loci Copulavi **Josephum Antich
> Papa**, filium Denati **Joannis** et **Margarittæ**, cum virgine **Maria
> Jellicich**, filia Denatorum **Georgii** et **Margarittæ**, adstantibus
> testibus Simone Salov and Vincentio Rebar…

Read at 2.9×. *Josephum Antich Papa* is **underlined by the scribe**, the same
convention as the 1806 Vrbnik baptism.

That is **ahnentafel 24 marrying ahnentafel 25**, and it names **ahnentafel 48,
49, 50 and 51** — four direct ancestors documented in one line, all four already
dead in 1835.

## And the entry immediately above it

**Domus 80, the same day:**

> …Copulavi **Matthiam Jellicich** filium Denatorum **Georgii** et
> **Margaritta**, cum virgine **Euphemia Antich, aliter Papa**, filia Denati
> **Joannis** et **Margarittæ**…

Same two sets of parents, reversed. **A brother and sister of the Antići married
a sister and brother of the Jelličići on the same day** — a double sibling
exchange, entered one after the other in the same hand.

## What it does to question 5

The chain for *Papa* displacing *Antić* had a gap in the middle. It no longer
does:

    1806  Vrbnik   Antich Papa            underlined nadimak, ahn 24's baptism
    1835  Selce    Josephum ANTICH PAPA   ahn 24's own marriage — BOTH names
    1835  Selce    Euphemia ANTICH        his sister, same day
                   ALITER PAPA            the alias stated as a formula
    1838  Selce    ahn 12 born, carrying every form at once
    1885  Senj     his son's marriage enters the father as PAPA alone

**And the formula is not special to this family.** The same two pages carry five
more, in the same hand:

    Josephum DUDICH aliter ANTICH        (a different Joseph Antić)
    Franich aliter LONZARICH
    Lucia PUXICH aliter LONZARICH
    Marca BADROVICH aliter BAGATELLO
    Franciscum PENES aliter PASCHUL

So *aliter* is how this parish routinely wrote a household by-name beside a
surname. **Papa is not an oddity in this family; it is the local system**, and
the archive's reading of question 5 is now the ordinary explanation rather than
the clever one. It also shows the by-name attaching to **Antić** twice over —
once as *Papa* and once, on another household, as *Dudić*.

## A contradiction with the tree

The register says Maria Jelličić was *filia **Denatorum** Georgii et
Margarittæ* — the genitive plural: **both her parents were dead by 22 November
1835.**

    ahn 50  Georgi Juraj Jelličić   tree: died 1 April 1832    consistent
    ahn 51  Margarita Brozović      tree: died 8 May 1842       CONTRADICTED

The tree has ahnentafel 51 alive for another seven years. One of the two is
wrong, and the register was written at the time by the priest who married her
daughter.

## What this says about the rest of the library

This document had been in `photos/line/` since the media sweep, attached in the
tree to six direct ancestors, and counted by the coverage tool as *scanned* —
"an image is attached but has not been read out". **It contained four ancestors'
parentage and a seven-year contradiction.**

There are more like it. The coverage tool counts every *scanned* person, and
nobody has been through them.
