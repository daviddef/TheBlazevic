# Senj births, 1878–1894: the register for 3 March 1879

Waypoint 9R2D-JWL (Senj births 1878–1894, 269 images), read 24 November 2026: image 25 again (printed Str. 17, entries 15–21) and image 28 for the first time (Str. 20, entries 32–37); images 26 and 27 (entries 22–31) are the 25 February reading (`senj-births-february-1879-the-25th.md`).
Follows that note, which found Ferdinand Vjekoslav baptised on 3 March (entry 19); this note asks who else is born or baptised on 3 March 1879.

## The question

Is any child born on 3 March 1879, or baptised on 3 March 1879, in the Senj register, and is any of them a Perpić, Prpić or Tomljanović child of the household at Klaričevac 82?

## What the pages are

- The printed Croatian table as in the earlier 1879 notes: entries numbered from 1 each year, in order of baptism, some babies baptised weeks late.
  Image 25 is Str. 17 (entries 15–21, baptised 12 February to 16 March), image 28 is Str. 20 (entries 32–37, baptised 3 to 18 May). Both pages of each opening were read at full size.
- Months: ožujka = March, travnja = April, svibnja = May, veljače = February.

## What was found

- **Baptised on 3 March: one entry.** Entry 19, Ferdinand Vjekoslav (born 25 February; «Jandro Perpić» and «Ana rodj. Kovačević», house «Senj, Karlobag», baptiser Juraj Dobrović kapelan), as the 25 February note has it. Entries 17 and 18 are baptised on 2 March and entry 20 on 11 March.
- **Born on 3 March: none in entries 15–37.** Births in entries 15–31 are 9 Feb (15, 16), 25 Feb (17), 28 Feb (18), 25 Feb (19), 2 Mar (20), 15 Mar (21), 8 Mar (22), 27 Feb (23), 19 Feb (24), 20 Mar (25), 24 Mar (26), 2 Apr (27), 7 Apr (28), 15 Apr (29), 7 Mar (30), 16 Apr (31); entries 32–37 are 26 Apr, 29 Apr, 2 May, 9 May, 4 May and 13 May. The nearest to the date are entry 20 (Julius, born 2 March, baptised 11 March, father «Martin Matijević postolar») and entry 30 (born 7 March, baptised 23 April).
- **No Perpić, Prpić or Tomljanović in entries 32–37**, and none in entries 15–21 beyond the two already described (Matija, entry 17, Ivan Tomljanović and Lucia née Prpić, Klaričevac kb. 82; Ferdinand Vjekoslav, entry 19). Entries 15 and 16 are twins of Frane Grzin and Matija Prpić (born 9 February), a mother of the Prpić name who is not shown related to the Klaričevac household.
- **Where the late baptisms stop.** The longest delay in entries 15–37 is entry 30, born 7 March and baptised 23 April (47 days). In entries 32–37 every baby is baptised within ten days of birth. A birth on 3 March baptised by 18 May would be 76 days late; none is.

## What this does not show

- A birth on 3 March baptised after 18 May would stand at entry 38 or later; those entries were not read. Not a walk of the register or of 1879: entries 15–37 of images 25–28 only.
- This is a reading of the Senj births. It does not say where the Klaričevac couple's other children were baptised (Josephus 1876 and Pave 1881 are not in the Senj register at the dates read; the Krivi Put baptiser Simun Serdoc fits a baptism at Krivi Put itself, consistent with that, not proof).

## What follows

Surname and given-name forms met are those of the 25 February note; entries 32–37 name no Perpić, Prpić or Tomljanović, so nothing was added to the Atlas catalogues. The open question stays as it was: the tree's Ivan Tomljanović × Lucia Prpić (Matija) and Ivan Perpić × Luca of Krivi Put may be one man; that is for the tree's owner.
