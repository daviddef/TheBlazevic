## The entry

«Praemissis &c. nullo que canonico impedimento detecto ac habito mutuo consensu **Michaelis vidui Gargács et Marthae viduae defti Laurentii Kovacevich**, eos solemniter per verba de praesenti in facie Ecclesiae de licentia Parochi in Matrimonium conjunxit A.V. Pater **Maurinus Molinarich** Capucinus, praesentibus testibus **Martino Kovacevich** vigiliarum Magistro Confessionato, et **Joanne Naglich** hujatibus.» [«Gargács» for Gergacs; the page marks him «vidui»: a widower.]

## What it gives

* **Michael Gergacs was a widower on 8 October 1809.** His wife Lucia («vulgo Maja») had been buried at Karlobag on 19 September 1809 ([karlobag-deaths-1809-1828.md](karlobag-deaths-1809-1828.md)). He married again **nineteen days later**.
* **His second (or later) wife is Martha, a widow of a Laurentius Kovačević.** The witness Martinus Kovacevich, of the same surname, is probably her kin. The baptism of 19 January 1815 of **Antonia Vincentia, to Michael Gergach and Martha** (QKMX-YG2W) is now the child of this couple, as near as an index can say: same names, within six years.
* **It does not settle the age question** (one Lucia with a wrong age, or two). It shows only that the Lucia who died on 19 September 1809 was the Michael's wife of the day: a widower marrying so quickly is what a man whose wife has just died does, and the entry gives no wife's name before Martha.

## Caution

* «Gargács» is the page's spelling of the name; the identification with Michael Gerkacs (1762–1836, ahnentafel 110) rests on the name, the status of widower in the month after his wife's death, and the 1815 baptism. The entry gives **no father and no age**.
* The tree has Michael with one wife only (Lucia); Martha is not in it.
