# What FamilySearch actually holds — the catalogue, read properly

14 September 2026. Opened to find **Juraj Blažević's baptism at Krmpote**, which
this archive had written down as the next thing to do. It is not findable, and
finding out why turned into the largest correction to the holdings table since
it was written.

## The method that was missing

Two sittings have taken parish ranges from the **collection waypoints** — the
browse labels. Those are coarse and, as Krmpote already proved, sometimes wrong.
**The catalogue item is one level deeper and gives the contents of each film,
volume by volume, with the DGS number.**

    /service/search/catalog/v3/search?q.keywords=<parish>     -> koha:NNNNNN
    /service/search/catalog/item/koha:NNNNNN                  -> film_note[]

**`q.keywords` is the only query term that works.** `q.text`, `q.anyPlace` and
`q.placeString` are all rejected by that service — which is very likely why no
earlier sitting used it. Everything below came out of about six calls.
The result is in `sources/familysearch/holdings.psv`: **76 films, named.**

## Krmpote: worse than the table said

    Krmpote-Vodice · koha:719032 · DGS 5494763
    Rodjeni 1879-1896 — ONE film, ONE item, and nothing else.

Not "1815–1896 births only". **1879–1896 births only.** Juraj was fathering
children from 1892 and his wife was born in 1867; he was born around the 1860s.
**His baptism is not in this book, and there is no other Krmpote book.** The
plan that said "his own baptism is the reachable target" was wrong, and it was
wrong because the range came from a label.

## Rijeka — and question 13 is answered

The table carried Rijeka as **"unknown · NOT CHECKED"**. It is filmed, and
heavily: **Župa Rijeka, koha:722509, 1590–1920**, births running almost
continuously from **1594**, with alphabetical ***Kazalo*** indexes to births,
marriages and deaths.

**And the answer to question 13 is a negative.** The births go

    ... 5497933  Rodjeni 1880-1886
        5497934  Rodjeni 1886-1890
        5497935  Rodjeni 1890-1891
        5497937  Rodjeni 1900-1901

**There is no 1892–1899 birth register on the film.** Ljubomir was baptised at
Rijeka on the strength of a birth of **1 March 1892**, and that volume is in the
gap. The indexes do not rescue it either — the birth *Kazalo* runs 1800–1889,
then 1895–1902. **Ljubomir's Rijeka baptism cannot be reached on FamilySearch,
by image or by index.** It has to come from the Rijeka state archive or the
parish, which is exactly what the unsent letter in `requests/EMAIL-rijeka.txt`
asks for. **That letter is now the only route, and it should go.**

## Rijeka — and a target that IS reachable

    Vjencani 1866-1891 · DGS 5497940 · 720 images

Question 1b asks for **Juraj's marriage to Tereza Žubrinić before 1892**. This
register covers exactly that span, and the family was demonstrably in Rijeka by
March 1892. Fixed points read today:

    image 701  =  1891, May, entries 115-121
    image 720  =  the end of the book

**Images 699, 700 and 701 were read name by name** — Cesare, Kljun, Zadnik,
Martić, Leopar, Švić, Vrsalović, Mihalčić, Terzaga, Matejčić, Sirola, Larber,
Demarchi, Peharović. Rijeka town names, Italian and Slovene as much as Croatian.
**No Blažević and no Žubrinić in April–May 1891.**

**Images 702–720 — June to December 1891 — are the remaining window**, and they
are the last nineteen openings of the book. That is a bounded job, not a sweep.

**It should be attempted with the expectation of a negative.** A Lika man and an
Otočac woman would ordinarily marry in her parish or his, and Otočac marriages
on FamilySearch stop in **1872** while Krmpote has no marriage book at all. If
Rijeka does not have them, **the marriage is not on FamilySearch anywhere**, and
that is worth knowing definitively because it turns question 1b into a letter
rather than a sitting.
