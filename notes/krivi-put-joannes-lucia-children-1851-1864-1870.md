# Krivi Put: three more children of Joannes (Ivan) Perpić and Lucia nata Perpić — Maria 1851 (house 36), Catharina 1864 and Anna 1870 (house 135)

Read from the image on 23 November 2026: Krivi Put births 1847–1884 (DGS 5494757, FamilySearch waypoint 9R28-DP8, 382 images). Work list row 31.
The entries were **found through the FamilySearch index** (a search of the surname, exact spellings, narrowed to Krivi Put; each hit carries the
ark of its image, and the three arks agree with the known images 46 (Toma) and 78 (Stephanus) used as a control), and **then read from the image**.
This is a targeted read of three pages; it is **not** a walk, and no other entry on these pages was read for anything.

**Maria 1851.** The household is at **house 36**, exactly the house of the Maria baptised on 7 February 1847 to «Joannes Perpich and Lucia nata Perpich»
([that note](krivi-put-maria-perpic-1847-joannes-lucia-house-36.md)). The same given name, the same pair of parents, the wife's maiden name Perpić
written again, and the same house in 1847 and 1851: **very probably one household**. A second Maria four years after the first usually means
the first had died, but **no death of the first Maria is read here**, so that is a reading of the pattern, not a finding. The digits «36» were
read at about 9x; the slanted stroke before the 3 is the tail of the clerk's *N°*, not a 1. Godparents as written: «Kumovi biše Mate Perpić, i Mate
njegova Mara» (the words between the two names are not settled); the baptiser's name is not read.

**Catharina 1864 and Anna 1870** are at **house 135**, the house of Georgius (1868) and Joanna (1874)
([that note](krivi-put-the-younger-children-and-their-houses.md)), where those were «very probably» ahnentafel 7's brother and sister. The two pages
fill in the years between: **135 is written for this couple in 1864, 1868, 1870 and 1874**, with the same formula for the mother, «nata Perpić».
Ahnentafel 7 (1859) and her brother Stephanus (1861) are at **136**; so, between the 1861 entry and the 1864 entry the couple's number goes from
136 to 135 and stays there for twelve years. One number apart, never both numbers in the same year: **consistent with one household whose number
changed once** (a renumbering of the hamlet, or a move next door), and with nothing else the register says. Not proved.
Godparents of Catharina: Antonius Tomljanović and Anna Perpić, wife of Petri; of Anna: Andreas Perpić and Martha Tomljanović («uxor Mar…», the
word is not settled). The baptiser of both is Vincentius (the surname is not read), *parochus* in 1870.

**A later note on Anna's entry.** In the margin column of the 1870 entry, beside the baptiser, a later hand has written **«† 18.II.1948»**.
It is the only dated note on the entry, and a cross in this column normally records a death. **It is not shown to be Anna's death**: no
other record of it is held here, and a note in this column can also be a marriage or a correction of a different kind. If it is her death, she
died on 18 February 1948, aged 77.

## What this does and does not say

- It shows a **second** and a **third** child of the Joannes–Lucia pair at the two houses of the later years, and a Maria of 1851 at house 36
  with the same pair of names as the Maria of 1847. It does **not** show that this pair is the pair of ahnentafel 14 and 15: the evidence for that is the
  parents' names, the wife's maiden name «nata Perpić», and the house numbers 136 and 135 (one apart), which is the same evidence as before, now
  covering more years. The house numbers earlier (36 in 1847 and 1851, 177 in 1853, 111 in 1856) still do not sit in a line with 136.
- Nothing is changed in the tree. These are finds beside the tree (`sources/found.psv`).

## What was read and what was not

Three entries on three images, at about 4 to 9x. Other entries on those pages were looked at only to place the dates and the neighbouring
houses. The index returned **404 baptisms at Krivi Put (1849 to 1884) under the six spellings tried**; only the rows with a
father named Ivan or Joannes and a mother named Lucia (several spellings) were followed: these three. Not followed: rows where the index
gives the mother another surname, or no mother's name, or the names are in the genitive with no first name.
Terms tried (Atlas catalogue, Prpić family): Perpić, Perpic, Prpić, Prpic, Perpich, Prpich; given names Ivan, Joannes, Lucia (Lucije, Luce, Luccia, Luciae).
Images 9 to 23, 25 to 98 and 100 to 134 are not read.
