# We went to page 34 for one woman. The K section gave a household.

**22 September 2026.** The death index of Kranj–Šmartin for 1812–1835 (NŠAL sig. **04634**) has an
appendix at the back — *«Dostavek ženskih imen za leta 1812–1822»*, a supplement of women's names —
and under **1816** it reads **«Kozina Maria — 34»**. Martin Kozina's grandmother, **Maria «Mina»
Dortschen**, was the last person on the Carniolan line without a death. Page 34 was the obvious
place to go.

**She is not on it. Two other women of her name are.**

## Page 34, which is image 18

The 1812–1835 death register (sig. **00894**) runs **two book pages to one scan** — image 35 is
pages 68 and 69 — so **page 34 is the left leaf of image 18**, and the page number is written on it
in red. The year 1816 stands at the head of the first column.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Den 27 [Februar 1816]** · «Z[ubern]» 4 | **MARIA KOZINAR**, *Häubl.* · weiblich · **ALTER 78 Jahre** · *Altersschwäche* |
| **Den 31 [März 1816]** · Oberfeichting 9 | **MARIA KOZINAR**, *Häubl.* · weiblich · **ALTER 3/4 Jahr** · *Fraiß* |

Maria Dortschen was **twenty at her wedding in October 1800**, so in 1816 she was thirty-six.
A woman of seventy-eight and a baby of nine months are neither of them her.

**The index was not wrong.** It carries **two** «Kozina Maria — 34» lines, one directly under the
other. The first reading of that index had taken one line and stopped.

## The village on the 78-year-old's row is not read

At nine times magnification it is written the same way twice on this page, house 4 both times, and
it reads **«Zubern»** or **«Luberan»**. It is recorded as the clerk wrote it rather than resolved
into a modern name. **What matters for the argument is only that it is not Feichting**, and it is
not.

## The nine-month-old is Maria Dortschen's own daughter

The **baptismal** index for 1812–1831 (sig. **04631**, image 169) lists **«Kosina Maria — 44»**
under 1815. Book page 44 — image 23 of sig. **00885** — reads:

> **den 8ten [August 1815] · Haus 10 · MARIA** · katholisch · weiblich · **ehelich** ·
> Vater: **FRANZ KOSINAR**, *Häubler* · Mutter: **MARIA DORTSCHEN** · *um 10 Uhr Nachmittag*

Born 8 August 1815, dead on 31 March 1816. **Seven and a half months, which is what three quarters
of a year means in that column.** The woman we went looking for was not on page 34. **Her daughter
was.**

## Then the whole K section was read, because one page is how a search gets long

**Six Kozina in the death index 1812–1835**, and the K section runs across images 125 and 126:

|---|---|---|
| Kosina Elisabeth — 6 (1813) | p. 6, image 4 | **11 April 1813**, Oberfeichting b. St. Ursula 9, *Tochter*, **5 Monath**, *Fraiß* |
| Kozina Maria — 34 (1816) | p. 34, image 18 | **27 February 1816**, «Zubern» 4, **78 Jahre**, *Altersschwäche* |
| Kozina Maria — 34 (1816) | p. 34, image 18 | **31 March 1816**, Oberfeichting 9, **3/4 Jahr**, *Fraiß* |
| Kosina Matthäus — 64 (1819) | p. 64, image 33 | **16 April 1819**, Oberfeichting 14, *Keischler*, **ALTER 45**, *Rothlauf und Brand* |
| Kosina Valentin — 72 (1820) | p. 72, image 37 | **29 February 1820**, Oberfeichting 9, *Häubler*, **1 Woche**, *Fraiß* |

**The index is wrong once.** Margareth is on page **130**, one opening before the page it promises.
Every other reference in the section landed exactly. An index that is right fifteen times and wrong
once is still the fastest way in — **but the page number is a hint and not a fact.**

## What the six change

**1. Matthäus was Franz's brother, not his father.** He stood witness at Franz's marriage in 1800
and **signed with a cross**, and this archive wrote for a day that *nothing here says which*. His
death says which: **45 in 1819 is born about 1774**, and Franz was born 1775–76. Two men born a
year apart are not father and son. **He was twenty-six when he stood up for his brother, and he
could not write his own name.**

**2. Franz kept Oberfeichting 9 himself.** The baptism of **Valentin**, book p. 108 (sig. 00885,
image 55), reads **«Den 15ten Hornung 1820 · Ober-Feichting · 9 · VALENTIN · ehelich · Vater:
FRANZ KOSINA, *Häubler* · Mutter: MARINA DORTSCHEN»**. He was buried on the 29th — 1820 was a leap
year — aged one week. **It is the only record that puts Franz at that house as the householder**,
twenty-one years before his grandson Martin was born in it and twenty-nine before he died in it as
an *Ausnehmer*. The house was never only his son Johann's.

**3. Three of the five known children did not live.** Elisabeth five months, Maria nine months, Valentin
fourteen days, in 1813, 1816 and 1820. **Gertraud, born between two of them in 1814, lived to bear
Martin in 1841**, and Johann, born about 1802, kept the house. **Five is what is known, not what
there were** — three Kosina baptisms of these years are still unopened. That is not a remarkable household; it is an ordinary one, and it is the first
time this archive has been able to say so about anybody in Carniola.

**4. The surname is Dortschen.** Six clerks across thirty-three years: **«Dortshen»** 1800 ·
**«Dortschen»** 1812 · **«Do[m]shan»** 1814 · **«Dortschen»** 1815 · **«Dortschen»** 1820 ·
**«Tom[sh]shan»** 1833. **Four of the six read Dortschen**, and the two that do not are this
archive's own bracketed readings of a hand it found hard. The lead spelling on the site changed
that day, and all six are kept.

**5. Margaretha and the elder Maria are kin without a proof.** Margaretha died **unmarried at
forty-five in Franz's own house** — born about 1781, of his generation, most likely his sister. The
elder Maria was born about **1738**, a generation above him, and is a **candidate for his mother**.
Neither entry names a parent. **They are recorded as what they are and no more.**

## Where Maria Dortschen's death is not

**The search is now exhaustive for this parish**, which is worth more than another guess.

* **1812–1835** (sig. 04634) — the six above. **Not her.**
* **1836–1869** (sig. 04635) — Franz 1849 and Luzia 1850, and nobody else. **Not her.**

She was alive in **February 1820**, at about forty, because Valentin was baptised then and entered
*ehelich*. So she died **unindexed, after 1869 at eighty-nine or more, or in another parish**.

## Method, for the sister archives

**Do not read the one page an index sends you to. Read the whole letter.** This sitting was asked
for a single entry on a single page. Reading the section around it cost four more downloads and
turned one negative into **six people, a house, a corrected kinship and a corrected spelling** —
and the negative it was asked for got *stronger*, not weaker, because it became exhaustive instead
of partial.

**And read the index's own appendices.** The women of 1812–1822 are in a supplement at the back of
this one, under a Slovene heading, **not in the main alphabetical run**. A K section read without
it would have missed both Marias and Elisabeth — three of the six.
