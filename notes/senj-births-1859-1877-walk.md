# Senj births, 1859–1877: the whole book, every entry (walked 26 November 2026)

Waypoint 9R2D-JW5 (Senj births 1859–1877, 400 images; Povijesni arhiv Rijeka 275, svezak V, inventory number 498), read 25–26 November 2026 from the archive's own private copy of every leaf.
Follows the smaller readings in this register (`senj-births-july-1876-josephus.md` and the Perpić/Tomljanović notes), which looked at single dates; this one reads every entry.

## What was done

- **All 400 images were looked at, both pages of every opening.** Images 1–4 are the film's slate, a resolution target, the cover and the inside cover with the volume label («Matična knjiga rođenih (1859–1877)», Senj, inv. br. V (498)).
  Images 5–393 hold the entries (381 openings; five are second exposures of a neighbouring opening and carry no new rows); images 394–400 are an endpaper, slates, a target and two blank leaves.
- **2,447 entries are captured**, one row each, in `sources/senj-entries-9R2D-JW5.tsv`: the number as written, born, baptised, child, legitimacy, parents (cell as written, then split), occupation, house, godparents, baptiser, later notes, a reading grade and a note on what the page shows.
  Names are copied as written (no modernising); a word that could not be settled is `[…]` with the grade in `conf`. The per-image ledger is `sources/senj-walk-ledger-JW5.json`.
- **The book begins at entry 8** (24 January 1859): the number on the first row is a bold 8 over a faint, erased 7 (zoomed), and the leaf before it is the inside cover. Entries 1–7 (1 to 23 January 1859) are **not in this book**: they were not found on this film and are not a gap in the walk.
  Entry numbers start again at 1 each year and the book ends with entry 119 of 1877 on image 393 (printed page 381); the film continues on another roll.

## How it was checked

- Six readers took disjoint ranges (images 5–70, 71–136, 137–202, 203–268, 269–334, 335–400). Every ledger line's count equals its rows; every row has all its fields; no cell holds a question mark.
- **Every number gap was looked at on the page.** Where the scribe's numbering slips, no entry is missing: on image 303 the rows run 44, 46, 46, 47 (the first 46 stands where 45 belongs), and across images 304 and 305 they run 52, 53, 53, 55 (the second 53 stands where 54 belongs). Where a number is lost under ink, under repair paper or at a torn edge, the row is in the table with its number cell empty or marked `[…]`; on image 346 the pencil numbers are one short from the sixth row (the next opening starts at 75).
- **A second, blind reading of 15 images** (spread across the book: 15, 39, 73, 100, 125, 151, 174, 211, 235, 263, 286, 299, 339, 367, 392; 98 entries) was made by readers who had not seen the table. Row counts and entry numbers matched on every one of the 98 entries; names and dates agreed on all but a few cells in each column, nearly all of them a word unsettled in one reading and settled in the other, or a cell split differently.
  What the comparison changed: a baptism day left open where the figure is faint (images 15 and 286), and two dates where the table had filled in a year the page does not write (images 39 and 211). An earlier slip, a baptism of 29 June 1876 first read as the 20th (image 363), was found against the 23 November reading of the same page.
- Known weak spots: godparents' names (many signatures and pencil lines are partly read), parents' surnames under ink, and the day figures of faint entries. A name left as `[…]` is a reading not made.

## What is in it for the families

- **Two households headed by an Antonius Blažević, merchant** (house «Segnia»): with Anna née Trbuhović, Georgius Albertus (born 22 April, baptised 28 April 1861, image 55, entry 55; the tree dates him 12 April);
  with Francisca née Gosler (Goßler), Maria Franciska Amalia (28 July 1867, image 199, entry 79), Emericus Joannes Andreas (22 November 1868, image 222, entry 101, the tree's «Mirka», who was Mirko), Francisca Helena (30 July 1870, image 252, entry 62) and Antonius Vincentius Ignatius (11 July 1874, image 329, entry 72).
- **Theresia, widow Blažević, née Čačić** (house «Segnia»): a son, Christophorus, entered as illegitimate, born 10 and baptised 14 September 1861 (image 63, entry 107; no father is named; the surname is split over the line, «Blo-/ževic» in the table, in a hand whose a and o are hard to tell apart). The tree holds him without a surname.
- **Joanes Žubrinić, «vigil policiae», and Mattoia née Sekulić**: Martha, born 24 April, baptised 26 April 1861 (image 55, entry 54); the tree has 21 April. The other Žubrinić/Zubrinić entries (as father in 1859 and 1863; as mother, godparent or in the parents' cell in the rest) are in the table.
- **Corrected later on 26 November 2026** (`senj-blazevic-households-1858-1888.md`): this note first said that Emericus and Christophorus were not in the tree and gave one Antonius two wives in turn. All three were wrong or unsupported. It also read Martha's mother as «Seškalić» and left the first letters of Anna's surname unread.
- A Blažević or Blasevich also stands as godparent or maiden name in several entries between 1859 and 1875; Martin Kosina is godfather twice (1863, 1864); a Petar Uroda is godfather in 1871 and Franciska née Uroda is a mother in 1873.
- **Tomljanović, Perpić/Prpić, Šojat, Papić, Dobrila and Franceskini** run through the whole book. Tomljanović is often written «Tom-Gánović» or «Tom[…]anović» from 1863: the capital reads G at zoom, and it is copied as written. Whether that is the same name is for the Atlas catalogue to decide, not this table.
- The tree's Senj births of 1859–77 can be matched against the table by parents and date: several agree to the day (for example Christina Maria Boras, 27 May 1862; Blasius Boras, 2 February 1864; three Papić children; Martinus Prpić 11 November 1863), which is a control on the reading.

- **Matched on the page, later on 26 November 2026:** `senj-births-1859-1877-matches.md` reads the families' own entries again, with the dates that differ from the tree and six children it does not give to their parents.

## What this does not show

- It is a reading of the whole register by readers working from a copy of the film; the grades in the table are the reader's own. A nil for a name in this book is a nil for the forms typed against the table, not for a word the table holds as `[…]`.
- **Krivi Put and Mrzli Dol are not covered by this.** The Krivi Put parish books (which also enter Mrzli Dol) are separate books, not yet read.
- Nothing in the table is about a living person: the youngest entry is 1877.
