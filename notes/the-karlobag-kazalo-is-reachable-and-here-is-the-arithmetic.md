# The Karlobag Kazalo is reachable, and here is the arithmetic

**21 September 2026.** [Row 26](/worklist/)'s last ancestor is **Catherina «Catta» Luckinić,
ahnentafel 105, baptised 30 March 1727 at Karlobag** — and the row calls reaching her
«a walk, not a lookup», because the FamilySearch *record* index holds seven Luckinić
entries in the whole parish and none is hers.

**The book has an index of its own.** This is about reaching that one.

## The arithmetic nobody had

[Row 54](/worklist/) records the 1691–1770 volume by its **waypoint** —
`9RK1-3Y9:391644801,391849401,391849402`, «327 images» — with three known ARKs at waypoint
images 217, 250 and 310. The **film** is a different numbering, and nothing connected them.

    DGS 005481648 has 596 images.
    WAYPOINT IMAGE N  =  FILM IMAGE N + 269

**Verified on two independent ARKs before use**, not inferred from one:

| waypoint | ark | film image |
|---|---|---|
| 250 | `3:1:3QS7-L99C-L99J-1` | **519** |
| 310 | `3:1:3QS7-L99C-L99Q-L` | **579** |

So row 54's «image 310 is inside the Kazalo» is **film image 579**, and the Kazalo occupies
roughly **film 569–596**.

## What the Kazalo actually is

Read at 4×, film images 579 and 581:

* **Organised by initial letter, then chronologically within the letter** — not
  alphabetically within it. The L column runs *Luttich · Lucsich · Lenach · Lazanich ·
  Lazanich · Lenacz*, which is date order and not name order.
* Each entry is **surname · given name · day and month · FOLIO**.
* **There is no year.** «21 May — 109» is a day, a month and a leaf of the register.
* The opening carries **five columns** — two on the left page, three on the right — each a
  continuation of the last.
* Film 579 is the **I/J** section: *Jellicich Vincen:, Jellinich Georgius, Jerko Joañes
  Nepom:, Ivankovich Anna*. Film 581 carries **L**, at folios **109–113**.

## So she is reachable, and the remaining work is bounded

**The route is now three steps instead of a walk**:

1. Scan the **L column(s)** for the block whose folios put it in **1727** — the Kazalo gives
   day and month, so the year comes from the folio, and folios 109–113 are already a known
   fixed point to interpolate from.
2. Take the **folio** off her line.
3. Open that folio in the 1691–1770 volume and read the entry.

**What stopped this sitting was not reachability.** Step 1 is a scan of a long column at
magnification, and this session had already spent its window; **the instrument is proved and
the arithmetic is written down**, which is what the next sitting needed and did not have.

## And the method that made it possible

This film was opened **without an ARK for any of the pages read**. The film enumerates in
one call and an image is fetched by `apid` at `<das>/v2/<apid>/dist.jpg` — see
`notes/image-75-reads-vivoz-and-the-film-enumerates.md`. **Every Kazalo page in this note was
reached that way**, including film 581, for which no ark exists anywhere in this archive.
