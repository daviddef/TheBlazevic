## What it gives

* **The Josephus of 1836 and the Theresia of 1841 are children of a Michael and a Catharina who was herself born a Žubrinić**, living at **Prazor, house 7**. Both given names are in the tree under Michael b. 1793 (Josephus 1836; Theresia, which the tree dates 1844, three years late).
* **The wife is not Maria.** The tree's Michael has one wife, Maria (b. 1793, d. 1831), who could not bear a child in 1836 or 1841. **A second wife, Catharina née Žubrinić, is the mother** if this is the tree's Michael. She is a Žubrinić by birth: a marriage inside the surname, which this village does repeatedly.
* The year of the tree's Theresia (1844) looks like a **misdate of 1841**.

## Not settled

* **Whether this Michael is the tree's.** Otočac had several Michael Žubrinić households in 1834–46 (the index names wives Orešković, Dražanović, Attalić, Marković). The two entries tie Josephus and Theresia to one couple, Michael and Catharina; they do not give his age or his first wife, and the **Stephanus of 1833** is before this book begins. Reading the marriage of Michael and Catharina (or his first wife's death in 1831, in the Otočac books before September 1834, which this film does not reach) would settle it.
* The tree is not changed.
