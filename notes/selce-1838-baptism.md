# Selce, 11 March 1838 — the last document question 5 wanted

Read 14 September 2026. *Liber Baptizatorum in Ecclesia* [Selce], **image 186**
of the Selce births film, ARK `3:1:3QSQ-G99C-5963-L`. Right-hand page, first
entry, *Annus 1838*.

> **Die 11ª Martii 1838 · Domus 51** — *Ego Vincentius Rombal Parochus Loci
> Baptizavi **Gregorium** … conjugum **Josephi Antich Papa**, filii Denati
> Joannis et [Lon]csarich, et **Mariæ**, filiæ Denatorum **Georgii** et
> **Margarittæ**…*

## The identification is certain

Every load-bearing detail matches the parents' own marriage entry, read the same
week:

    date    11 March 1838          the tree's date for ahnentafel 12, exactly
    house   Domus 51               the same house as the 1835 marriage
    priest  Vincentius Rombal      the same priest
    father  Josephus ANTICH PAPA, son of the late Joannis and …csarich
                                   = ahn 24, ahn 48 and ahn 49 (Lončarić)
    mother  Maria, daughter of the late Georgius and Margarita
                                   = ahn 25, ahn 50 and ahn 51

This is the child of ahnentafel 24 and 25 born on the day the tree gives, in the
house the tree's own documents put them in. **There is no doubt whose entry it
is.**

## But the given name is not what the tree says

The tree carries ahnentafel 12 as **Georgius Juraj "Jure" Antić Papić**.

The register, underlined by the scribe and read at the highest magnification the
scan allows, says **Gregorium** — G‑r‑e‑g‑o‑r‑i‑u‑m. That is **Gregorius**,
Croatian **Grgur**. It is not *Georgium*, which would be G‑e‑o‑r‑g‑i‑u‑m and
which this hand writes differently: *Georgii* appears twice in the very same
line, for the bride's father, and does not look like this word.

**Stated as a reading, not yet as a correction.** Against it: the family called
him *Jure*, which is Juraj — Georgius — and not Grgur, whose hypocorism is
*Grga*. A man known lifelong as Jure is unlikely to have been christened Grgur.

So one of three things is true, and this archive does not yet know which:

1. The tree misread *Gregorium* as *Georgium* when it was built, and ahn 12 was
   christened **Grgur** but went by a different name all his life.
2. The priest wrote the wrong name, and it was corrected in practice.
3. My reading is wrong.

**What would settle it:** ahn 12's **marriage** to Matia Pilipić, and his
**death entry of 29 January 1912**, each of which names him again in a different
hand. Selce marriages run to 1878 and are reachable.

## What it does for question 5

Whatever the given name, the entry adds the **fourth** and final step to the
chain, and it is the step this archive said it was missing:

    1806  Vrbnik  baptism   ahn 24 entered Antich Papa, underlined
    1835  Selce   marriage  ahn 24 entered Josephum ANTICH PAPA, underlined
    1835  Selce   marriage  his sister entered Antich ALITER Papa
    1838  Selce   baptism   ahn 12's father entered JOSEPHI ANTICH PAPA
    1885  Senj    marriage  ahn 12 himself entered as PAPA alone

Five documents, four of them now read at full resolution, and the by-name
travels the whole way.
