# The Otočac baptisms, and where the house number actually lives

14 September 2026. Written after the *status animarum* failed to hold any
Žubrinić at Šumećica, and the next sitting was redirected onto the baptism
registers on the strength of one sentence: *every entry writes the household
into the* Conditio *column in the form «e Šumećica N° X»*.

**That is true of one of the two books and not the other.** The redirection
needs narrowing before anyone spends a day on it.

## The index gives the image, which is the real unlock

The record search service is

    /service/search/hr/v2/personas?count=100&f.collectionId=2040054
      &offset=N&q.surname=Zubrinic&q.birthLikeDate.from=1834&q.birthLikeDate.to=1862

and each entry's GEDCOM-X payload carries **the image ARK** (`3:1:…`) alongside
the child, the date and both parents. **So an indexed entry can be opened at the
page directly, without hunting a film for it.** That is worth more than any
single reading and no earlier sitting used it.

Two cautions. The place filter **does not bind** — `q.birthLikePlace` is
ignored, and the results have to be filtered in hand on the place string, which
for this parish is **`"Otočac, Küstenland, Austria"`**. And **every baptism is
indexed twice**, once per parent role, so a raw count doubles.

**276 Otočac Žubrinić entries, 1834–1862 — about 138 baptisms.**

## The fathers, counted

Normalising the spellings (*Zubrinić · Žubrinić · Xubrinić · Žubrinich ·
Zabrinić · Dubrinić · Bubrinić* are all the same surname, as the index's own
scatter shows):

    Joannes      ~22 baptisms        Michael / Michaël / Miha / Miho / Miko  ~23
    Georgius       5                 Franciscus    6        Thomas    5
    Matthaeus      7                 Josephus      4        Philippus 3
    Stephanus      4                 Casparus      3        Elias     2
    Mathias        3                 Jacobus       3        Nicolaus  2

**Nicolaus has exactly two**, and they are the two read at the page this
morning — *Stephanus* 3 July 1856 and *Maria* 15 September 1858, both at
**Šumećica N° 2**.

**Michael has twenty-three, to at least eight different mothers** — Maria
(no surname, 1839–1845), Catharina, Magdalena, Helena, Maria Marković, Maria
Rogić, Magdalena Draženović, Anna Bobinac, Maria Maković, Helena Orešković.
**That is not one man. It is a village full of Michaels**, and it is exactly
the trap question 4d is about.

## And here is the problem with the plan

**The 1834–1846 book does not record house numbers.** Its columns are

    Annus, Mensis, Dies et Locus administrati Baptismi | Nomen Prolis, Sexus
    et Religio | Conditio | Parentes eorumque Religio | Patrini · Commorantes

and the ***Conditio* cell holds a single status word** — *Confiniarius*,
*Confiniaria*, *Decurio*, *Limitaneus* — **with no village and no number.**
Checked at two points: **December 1839** and **March 1845**. The place column
gives only *«Ottoccii»*, the place of baptism, not of residence.

**Ahnentafel 10's own baptism of 24 January 1834 is the exception**, and it now
looks like an early-book habit: *«Confiniarius, ex pago Shumechicza N° 24»*.
**Somewhere between January 1834 and December 1839 the parish stopped writing
the household into that column.**

**The 1846–1858 book puts it back.** Its *Conditio* reads *«Rustici Confiniarii
e Šumećica N° 2»*, and that is where this morning's two readings came from.

## So the redirection, corrected

**The house numbers are readable for 1846–1858 and for the first years of the
1834 book. They are not readable for roughly 1836–1845.**

That still leaves the useful decade. **Twenty-three Michael baptisms and two
Nicolaus ones**, each openable at the page by its indexed ARK, would give the
Šumećica Žubrinić households **by house number across 1846–1858** — enough to
say who else is at N° 2, whether anyone is still at N° 24, and which Michael is
which.

**It is a sitting of about a dozen page-reads, and every one of them is a
direct hit rather than a search.** The arks are in the index; the method is
above.
