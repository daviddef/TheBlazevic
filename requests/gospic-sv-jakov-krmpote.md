# Državni arhiv u Gospiću — parish of Sv. Jakov, Krmpote

**Status: drafted, not sent.**

## What is wanted, in one line

The **baptismal entry of Juraj (“Jurko”) Blažević**, or his **marriage to Tereza
Žubrinić**, in the registers of the parish of **Sv. Jakov, Krmpote** — either of
which names his father.

## Why this parish

The Senj death register for 1907 (p.225, entry 60) gives the birthplace of
**Toma Blažević**, *nadničar*, as the parish of ***Sv. Jakov Krmpote***. That is
the register covering the village of **Smokvica Krmpotska**, and therefore the
book that should hold every Blažević generation above Toma.

## What is already known, so the search can be narrow

| | |
|---|---|
| **Juraj “Jurko” Blažević** | *seljak*. Married **Tereza Žubrinić** (b. 29 Nov 1867, Otočac); the tree gives the marriage as **1888**, unsourced. Dead before **December 1920**, when his son's marriage entry annotates him *mrt.* Living at **Smokvica kbr. 114** in 1895. |
| **Children, all baptised in this parish** | Ljubomir (b. Rijeka, 1 Mar 1892), **Anka (1893)**, **Marijan (b. 12 Apr 1895, entry 26, p.168)**, **Ruža (1897)** |
| **The household** | Smokvica **house 114**, shared across three generations of the descendants of **Ilija Blažević** (b. *c.* 1790) — a *zadruga*. Also recorded there: **Toma Blažević & Terezija Pećanić** (1882, 1884, and retrospectively 1912) and **Pavao Blažević & Marija Tomljanović** (1890). |

## The three documents wanted, in order of value

1. **Baptism of Juraj / Jurko Blažević**, Sv. Jakov Krmpote, most likely
   **1855–1870**. Names his father outright.
2. **Marriage of Juraj Blažević and Tereza Žubrinić**, most likely **1885–1892**,
   at Krmpote, Senj or Otočac. Names both fathers.
3. **Death of Juraj Blažević**, Krmpote or Senj, **before December 1920**.

## Also wanted if the same volumes are open

- The **death entry of Tereza Žubrinić**, given in the family record as **1931**.
- The **birth entries of Ruža (1897) and Marijan (1895)** at Smokvica 114, to
  confirm the house number across the family's own children.

## Why it matters, briefly

Everything above Juraj — five further generations, to a **Mateša Blažević born
about 1750** — hangs on a link that no document yet states. Juraj is named as a
parent by two independent marriage registers (Senj 1919 and Senj 1920) and placed
at Smokvica 114 in 1895, and his son's godparents were Toma's own children. That
is a strong circumstantial case and it is not a sentence naming his father.

*Enquiries may be made in Croatian or English. A negative result is worth having
and will be recorded as one.*
