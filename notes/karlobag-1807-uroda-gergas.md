# The marriage that was expected to be a null

15 September 2026. Work list row 2 said, in as many words: *«Expect a negative,
and record it as one.»* It is not a negative.

**Ahnentafel 54 married ahnentafel 55 at Karlobag on 17 June 1807**, and the
entry is on **folio 4, image 231** of film 005481649 — the *Vjenčani 1736–1804 /
1804–1861* volume — under the heading **Anno Domini 1807**:

> «Præmissis &c. nulloque impedimento detecto ac habito mutuo consensu
> ***Georgii filij defti Georgii Uroda*** et ***Mariæ filiæ Michaelis Gergas***
> hujatium, eos solemniter in facie Ecclesiæ Matrimonio conjunxit idem præmissa,
> præsentibus testibus ***Caspare Jerko viduo*** et ***Joanne Fero…***»

The date column reads **17**; the month column is a **ditto for the entry above
it**, which is *16 Junii*. The page runs 7 January · 14, 22, 26 *ejusdem* ·
3 February · 7 May · 16 June · **17** · 8 July · 20 September, so the ditto can
only be June. **17 June 1807.**

## What it settles

**The two brothers are proved.** This archive already had one Uroda marriage,
**10 February 1806**: *«Joannis filii defuncti Dñi Georgij Uroda ac Mariæ filiæ
Nicolai Barletich»*. It was read then as probably a **brother** of ahnentafel 54
rather than the man himself, because ahnentafel 55's death entry of 1834 calls
her husband a **Georgius**. That reading is now confirmed from the other side:
**Joannes and Georgius are both sons of the same dead Georgius Uroda**, and they
married sixteen months apart in the same church.

**Ahnentafel 108 gains a second document.** Georgi Uroda — *Dominus*, buried
10 September 1791 in his own crypt before the doors of the parish church — is
named here as **«defti Georgii Uroda»**, the groom's late father, which is the
independent confirmation that the 1791 burial and the 1806/1807 marriages are
one man.

**Ahnentafel 55 gains her father at the page.** *Michael Gergas* is already in
the tree as **Michaeli Gerkacs**; this is the first document that puts him
there, spelled as the parish clerk spelled it.

## Two cautions this cost

**Folio 6 was the wrong folio, and the index sent us there.** The volume's own
alphabetical index places a *Georgius Uroda* on **folio 6**. Folio 6 — image 232
— has now been read **end to end, both pages, 1809 and 1810 through to the
«Finis»**, and it holds **no Uroda at all**. The index's *folium* column cannot
be trusted to point at the leaf the entry is on. The marriage was on **folio 4**,
which the index gave as a separate line.

**One surname is settled that had been carried as a doubt.** The register row for
the marriage index carried *«18 January 1809, a Miletich marrying Matthææ filiæ
defuncti Martini U[roda?] — surname unconfirmed»*. Read at five times full
resolution it is unambiguous: ***Uzelac***. Not Uroda, and not a lead.

## And one thing for row 5

The second entry of folio 4, 14 January 1807, marries a *Georgius Scatoli* to
**«Martha filia defti Josephi Smoyver»** — a Josephus Smojver dead by January
1807. Ahnentafel 53 is **Oliva Maria Smojver, born March 1781 at Karlobag**, and
her father is not yet known. A dead Josephus with a marriageable daughter in 1807
is the right shape. **Not a claim — a place to look.**
