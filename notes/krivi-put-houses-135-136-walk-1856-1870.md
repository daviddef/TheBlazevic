# Krivi Put houses 135 and 136, 1856–1870: who stood there, and where Joannes and Lucia Perpić are among them

23 November 2026. Work list row 31, the walk. Krivi Put births 1847–1884 (DGS 5494757, FamilySearch waypoint 9R28-DP8, 382 images).
The question was the one the index could not answer: **was ahnentafel 7's house, 136, also the house of other Perpić couples, and did Joannes and Lucia have
children there before 1859 or between 1862 and 1873 that the index does not carry?** The method was a walk of the **house column**
(the «Krivi put N°» cell) of images 10–134, then a close read of every row that showed 135 or 136.

**This is a walk of one column, not of the pages.** Only the house cell was read for every row of every image the pane could show; the rest of a row was read only
when its house number was 135 or 136. The limits are listed at the foot. Every entry below is kept as a snapshot
([the whole set](/snapshots/#krivi-put-baptism-1856-marko-perpic) starts at Marko 1856).

## What this shows

* **136 is a hamlet-sized number, not a household.** Five Perpić couples are written at 136 between 1856 and 1869, and 135 holds two more besides
  Joannes and Lucia. The house number alone never identified the couple; it identifies a cluster of Perpić households at Krivi Put (and two or three of them
  write the wife as «nata Perpić», so Perpić marrying Perpić is common here).
* **No other child of Joannes and Lucia stands at 136 or 135 before 1859 in the pages read** (images 44–103 for 1856–65, plus the 1847–55 images screened
  earlier). The elder-sibling question therefore still rests on the two entries at **house 36**, Maria 1847 and Maria 1851: nothing in the house column adds to them or removes them.
* **Between 1861 and 1864 the couple stands at 136 and then at 135, and the other households do not move.** Antonius and Catharina stay at 136 through 1866;
  Petrus and Anna through 1869. That supports a move next door (or a renumbering of this couple's house alone), and is against a renumbering of the whole hamlet.
  Not proved: the register never says so.
* **Lucia, wife of Joannes, is a godmother at two 136 baptisms: Antonius' Rozaria in April 1866 and Petrus' Gregorius in January 1869, each time with a Michael Perpić**
  («Michael Perpić et Lucia Perpić uxor Joannis»). It ties Joannes and Lucia to the 136 households after they are written at 135, and shows the two houses
  as neighbours who stand godparent for one another. It does not say whose relatives they are.
* A mother's maiden name is not stable: Petrus' Anna is «nata Perpić» in 1862 and 1866 and «nata Tomljanović» in 1856 and 1869. One reading fits all four entries (one wife whose
  name the clerks wrote differently); two Annas at 136 would also fit, and the register does not choose.
* **Spelling met:** the clerks write Krmpotić as «Kermpotić», «Krmpotić», «Kempotić», «Krempotić». **Perpić** is read as Perpić throughout; the Latin hand's last letter sometimes looks like a doubled *i*.

## What was read and what was not

* **Read for every row:** the house column of images 10–134 at screen strips (level 12); images 10–39 at a lower resolution than the rest, so a three-digit
  number there may be misread; 40–63 as margin strips and tables; 64–134 as column strips. **Every 135 or 136 was then zoomed and the row read in full.**
  The look-alikes zoomed and found not to be 135/136: 133, 137, 142, 143, 129, 151 and 132.
* **Not read:** the right-hand house column of image 134, and the left-hand pages of images 114–115 and 130–131 (loose slips lie over them); images 104–105 (slips lie over most of the rows: read only where the print shows); the right-hand page of 89 (a slip over most of it)
  and of 109, the top of the right-hand page of 113 (a pasted document), image 110's left page (a pasted extract) and the right-hand columns of 106–107 (clipped by the page's edge). Where another exposure of the same opening exists it was used (28/29, 34/35, 54–56, 69/70, 75/76, 79–81, 102/103, 113–115, 118/119, 122/123, 126/127); the rest is **not read**.
  A row under a slip may hold a 135 or 136 that was not seen.
* **Images 1–9 and 135–382 are not part of this walk.** Images 1–8 were read earlier; 135 (1870) onward is not walked.
* This is a walk of the house column and a read of the rows at 135 and 136, **not a walk of every entry**: the register is not called walked, and no Perpić baptism at another house was read for anything.
* **Terms:** the house column was read for the number alone, whatever the surname; the rows at 135 and 136 are all Perpić (or the mother's or godparents' names listed above). The earlier index query by the parents' names (Joannes, Ivan, Ivana, Lucia, Lucija with the six spellings of Perpić) is not what this walk rests on.
  Near names met at 135/136 and not counted: Perpić households of other couples, listed in the tables above; Sojat, Solić, Tomljanović and Krmpotić only as mothers or godparents.
