# Senj births, 1878–1894: the register for 2 March 1879 (Matija, daughter of Ivan and Lucia née Prpić, Klaričevac 82)

Waypoint 9R2D-JWL (Senj births 1878–1894, 269 images), read 23 November 2026 at images 24–25 (the openings holding the baptisms of February and March 1879).
Follows `krivi-put-houses-1874-1885-walk-images-176-377.md`, whose Krivi Put register (image 269, entry 27) writes the father «Jvana Perpića» while the Senj index extract gave «Ivan Tomljanović».

## The question

Which surname does the Senj register give the father of the child baptised on 2 March 1879 at Klaričevac 82, and is the child the one in the Krivi Put row?

## What the pages are

- The book is the printed Croatian table (Broj tekući; Godina, mjesec, dan: kad se je rodio, kad je krščen; Krštenik: ime mu, je li zakonit; Roditelji: ime, prezime, stališ, vjera; Mjesto gdje stanuju i pod kojim kućnim brojem;
  Ime, prezime, vjera i stališ njegovih kumova; Služba onoga koji ga je krstio; Opazke). Entries are numbered from 1 each year. Image 25 is printed page 17 (entries 15–21, 9 February to 15 March 1879); image 26 is page 18 (entries 22–23 seen at page scale).
- The baptisers on page 17 are Mirko Babić (kapelan) and Juraj Dobrović (kapelan); Ante Bosnić signs once.
- The month is written «ožujka» (March) and «veljače» (February). The index extract gives the baptism as «Feb 1879»; the register says 2 March: the index's known month slip for these years.

## What was found

- **Entry 17, image 25 of the waypoint's list (ark 3QS7-L99X-17NV): «25 veljače / 2 ožujka 1879» — Matija — zakonita (a girl) — «Ivan Tomljanović poljodjelac i Lucia rodj. Prpić» — rim. kat. —
  «Senj, Krivi put, Klaričevac kb. 82» — kumovi Vincenc Durbešić and Pepica Glavičić — Juraj Dobrović kapelan («po meni»); Opazke empty.** Snapshot `/snapshots/#senj-baptism-1879-03-02-matija-tomljanovic`. The father's name was zoomed
  (Tomljanović, with the mother written Prpić).
- **It is the same baptism as the Krivi Put row** (image 269 of the Krivi Put births, entry 27): the same birth and baptism days, house, godparents and baptiser. There it is «Jvana Perpića i Luce rodjene Perpić, seljaka»,
  with the note «u Senju krštjena» (baptised in Senj). So one baptism is written with two surnames for the father: Tomljanović at Senj, Perpić at Krivi Put.
- **The index extract was right about the register** (father Ivan Tomljanović, mother Lucia Prpić), and wrong only in the month (Feb for 2 March).
- On the same page, two entries earlier: twins Filip Anton and Filipina Apolonia (9 February, baptised 12 February 1879), children of Frane Grzin and Matija Prpić.

## What this does not show

- **Which surname the father bore is not settled.** A Tomljanović husband with a Prpić wife is as natural a reading as a Prpić couple; the Krivi Put copy and the Senj entry cannot both be literal. Nothing here says whether Josephus (1876) and Pave (1881) at Klaričevac 82 are
  children of this Ivan; they are at the same house with a father written Perpić and a mother Luca née Perpić.
- Not a walk: entries 15–21 of 1879 (image 25) were read; image 24 (entries before 15) and the rest of the volume were not.

## What follows

Josephus (1876) was read at Senj afterwards (`senj-births-july-1876-josephus.md`): entries 67–79 name no Perpić or Tomljanović and no Josephus. The Senj entry for Pave (born 12 September 1881; Senj births 1878–1894) would show which surname the register gives for the youngest child.
Surname and given-name forms met: Tomljanović/Tomjanović, Prpić/Perpić, Durbešić, Glavičić, Dobrović, Grzin; Ivan/Jvan, Lucia/Luca/Luce, Matija (a woman's name here, as in the index). None is new to the Atlas catalogues.
