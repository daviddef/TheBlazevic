# Karlobag 1727: no Luckinić baptism on 30 March, and Vicko Luckinić's wife is Morica

9 October 2026. Work list row 26, the last ancestor it still lists: **ahnentafel 105, Catherina «Catta»
Luckinić**, whom the tree gives as born **30 March 1727** at Karlobag, daughter (per the tree) of Vicentius
Luckinić. Her death (29 May 1790, «annorum 70») is read; her baptism is not. This sitting went to the baptism
book directly, by year, instead of through the Kazalo.

## What image 49 holds for March 1727

The entries run 14 March, **20 March** (a son, the father's name read as Petar Bulanich), **30 March** («Lovri Bulanichia … imenom Jella»,
godparent Ivan Amoich?), then 1 April («Lesi Lukatalichin … imenom Madalenu») and 6 April. **None is a Luckinić.**
The surnames read on the January–April page (Lukatalich, Kintelich, Bartula Jurse, Bulanich, Zubrich, Dobričich) do
not include one.

## The one Luckinić there is in 1727

On image 49's right-hand page, **24 August 1727**: «…a kuma [gospoja] xena **Vicka Luckinicha** imenom **Morica**» —
the godmother is *the wife of Vicko Luckinich, named Morica* (a form of Marija, as read). So **Vicko Luckinić is alive
at Karlobag in 1727 and his wife is Morica.** That agrees with the tree and with the 1725 baptism of *Mare
Luckinicha* (index, father «Vicka Luckinicha», no mother named). It does **not** make Morica Catherina's mother.

## What this changes, and what it does not

* **The tree's 30 March 1727 is not confirmed.** There is no Luckinić baptism on that date in this book. The tree's
  date, or its year, is either wrong or taken from a source that is not this register.
* **Her baptism has not been found**, so row 26 stays open. The Kazalo route (the L column, a folio for her) was not
  used and is still the way to settle it: it names the folio whatever the year is.
* **A candidate mother:** a *Morica* wife of Vicko Luckinich in 1727. Recorded in `found.psv` as a godmother, not
  as a mother.

## What was not done

* Only the January–April left page of image 49 was read entry by entry for the surname; May–December 1727 and 1726
  and 1728 were not searched, so a Luckinić baptism on another date in 1727 is not excluded.
* Surnames are spelt variously in this hand (Lukatalich, Lukinić, Luckinich, Lukanić); a Luckinić under an unfamiliar
  spelling could have been missed.
* The Kazalo (the register's own index) was then read for the L column: see below.

## The Kazalo, read afterwards

The Kazalo's L section is on waypoint image **312** (K on its left page, L on its right page, M from 313). The L column lists
name, day, month and **folio**, in date order; its first column runs folio 1 to about 101 with these spellings: *Locatelli,
Lukšetich/Lukšić, Lazarich, Lenach, Lulich, Lucich.* Folio **44** (15 February) and **47** (29 June) are 1727, matching the
leaf numbers on images 49 and 50. **There is no 30 March near folio 44, and no Luckinić at all** — nor an entry for Mare's
13 April 1725 baptism (about folio 37), which the FamilySearch record index lists. The Kazalo therefore files this
family under a different letter or spelling, or omits it, and **cannot be used to find them under L**. Not done: the second
and third L columns (folios about 109–208) were read only for the surname, and no other letter was tried.

## And May to August 1727, afterwards

The right-hand page of image 49 (9 May – 19 August 1727) was then read for surnames at moderate zoom: Lukatalich, Bulanich,
Dosetich, Gricikik, Jezicka, Humorovic and others, **no Luckinić except the godmother entry of 24 August**. September–December
1727 (image 50, left page) and 1726 and 1728 were not read for the surname.

## 10 October 2026 — 1726 and the rest of 1727 read; a gap in the book

Images 47 (right page), 48 (both pages), 49 (both pages) and the left page of 50 were read entry by entry at about 4x. **No Luckinić child is baptised in January–May 1726 or January–September 1727**; the Luckinići there are sponsors: *Vicko Luckinich* as godfather on 21 December 1725 (image 47), *Mara Vicha Luchinicha* as godmother on 10 March 1726 (image 48), *Morica* wife of Vicko on 24 August 1727. So Vicko's wife is called Mara in 1726 and Morica in 1727 — one woman by two forms of Marija, or two wives; the book does not say.

**The register has a gap.** Image 48's right-hand page ends with an entry of 4 May 1726; the leaf's back (image 49, left page) begins on 11 January 1727, and the January 1727 entry shows through at the foot of the earlier page. **Nothing from May to December 1726 is written between them.** If Catherina was born in the second half of 1726 her baptism is not in this volume's sequence, which would also explain why neither 30 March 1727 nor the Kazalo has her. That is a hypothesis, not a finding: the film may differ from the book, and the missing months may be written elsewhere.

Image 50 was then read too: its right-hand page carries November 1727 to July 1729 in about ten entries, none a Luckinić child, with the lower third faded and not legible. **Not done:** anything after July 1729, the faded entries of 1728–29, and a search for where the May–December 1726 baptisms went.
