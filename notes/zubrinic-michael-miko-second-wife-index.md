# Michael Žubrinić (b. 1793): the children after his wife Maria died in 1831 point to a second wife, Catharina — an index reading

17 October 2026. Index only (FamilySearch *Croatia, Church Books*, Otočac baptisms from 1834; no page opened). From the integrity triage ([integrity-audit-triage-2026-10-17.md](integrity-audit-triage-2026-10-17.md)): the tree has **Michael Miho Miko Žubrinić Xubrinich (b. 1793)** with one wife, **Maria (b. 14 March 1793, d. 1831)**, and children **1822, 1825, 1828, 1833, 1836 and 1844**, all «of Maria». Three of the six are born after she died.

**What the index shows for Otočac, 1832–1846** (a surname search on Xubrinich/Zubrinich/Žubrinić with fathers named Michael). There are **several Michael Žubrinić households** at Otočac, with wives named Maria *Orešković*, Helena *Orešković*, Magdalena *Orešković*, Magdalena *Dražanović*, Rosalia *Attalić*, Maria *Marković* and **Catharina**. The ones with a wife **Catharina**:

The tree's **Josephus (1836)** and **Theresia (1844 in the tree, 1841 here)** have the same given names and a Michael as father; the tree's **Stephanus (1833)** is not in the Otočac index (the film begins in September 1834).

**What this suggests, and what it does not.** If this Michael is the tree's, **his later children were borne by a Catharina, not by Maria**, who had died in 1831: the tree's single wife slot holds two women, and a second wife is missing from it. **It is not proved**: Otočac had several Michaels, the index gives no ages and no house numbers, and the tree's Theresia is dated three years off. A page read (the Otočac births of 1836 and 1841, with the house number) would tie the family.
