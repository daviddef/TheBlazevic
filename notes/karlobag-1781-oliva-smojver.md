# Ahnentafel 53, at the page: 11 March 1781

15 September 2026. Work list row 5. The tree carried **Oliva Maria Smojver,
born MAR 1781, Karlobag** — a month and no day, and no document. Here she is:

> «Ejusdem — Ego Joës Antonius Frankovich Parochus Loci baptizavi Infantem
> ***die 11ma*** hujus natam ex ***Vincentio, et Oliva Smoyver, vulgò Vukić***,
> Conjugibus hujus Civitatis, cui imposui est nomen ***Oliva Maria***. Patrini
> fuerunt Philippus Lupsich(?) et Catharina uxor Francisci…»

**Page 65, ark `3:1:3QS7-L99C-L998-5`, image 217.** The month is *Ejusdem* — the
entry above it is *Martij*, and the next opening's page 66 begins at 19 March —
so **11 March 1781**.

## What it gives

**A day where there was a month.** Born **11 March 1781**, baptised the same
month.

**Both parents, confirming the tree from the register side.** *Vincentius* and
*Oliva* — ahnentafel **106** (Vicentius Fabiano Smojver Vukić, b. 18 Jan 1754)
and **107** (Oliva Andreana Bacchi, d. 15 Sep 1795). The tree had them; nothing
had ever put them on a page. Now a single line names the couple and the child
together.

**And a by-name, in a parish that had none.** The register writes
***«Smoyver, vulgò Vukić»***. This archive's by-name file was built out of Selce
and its neighbours, where the connectors are *aliter*, *dictus*, *dicti* and the
scribe **underlines** the pair. This is **Karlobag**, the connector is
***vulgò***, and it is **not underlined**. The practice is wider than one
district and it is written differently in each.

It also explains a name the tree has been carrying without comment: ahnentafel
106 is recorded as *«Vicentius Fabiano Smojver Vukić»*, as though **Vukić** were
part of the surname. It is not. It is the household name, and the parish clerk
said so.

## How the page was found, which is the transferable part

**The film number was wrong and the note about it was wrong.** `holdings.psv`
gives DGS **5481648** as *«Rodjeni 1691-1770, 1771-1804 · KAZALO»*, and
`notes/karlobag-films.md` describes that film as **327 images** with *«image 220
= Anno Domini 1782, page 70»*. Neither survives contact: film 005481648 has
**596 images**, and its image 216 is a tabular register headed **«Godine 1845»**.

The 1771–1804 baptisms sit under a **different waypoint** —
`wc=9RK1-3Y9:391644801,391849401,391849402` — and the way in was not the film
number at all. It was **the record index**: a search for any Karlobag surname,
filtered by hand to 1781, returned *Antonium Devcich, 7 June 1781*, whose image
ARK opens the right volume at page 66. One click of **Previous Image** gave
page 65, and the March entries were there.

**Two mechanical notes for the next sitting.** Changing `&i=` on an ARK URL does
**not** move the viewer — use the *Previous Image* / *Next Image* buttons. And
the viewer's own number input holds the image number while `document.title`
holds the current ARK; that pair is how to build a map of a film this archive
has no map for.
