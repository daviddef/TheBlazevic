# Antonija Perpić's baptism, Krivi Put, 15 May 1859 — read from the image (it had been INDEX ONLY)

11 October 2026. Ahnentafel 7 (Antonija «Tonka» Perpić). The reading row for her baptism said *INDEX ONLY — the page has not been opened*; the page is now opened and kept as a snapshot.

**Where.** Krivi Put births 1847–1884, DGS 5494757, FamilySearch waypoint **9R28-DP8** (years 1847–1884 contain 1859). Image **64, left-hand page** (printed page number 110, entries 48–57, May–June 1859), ark `3:1:3QSQ-G99X-RNXT`.

**The entry (no. 51):** born **12 Maji 1859**, baptised **15 Maji 1859**, **Antonia Perpić**, *legit.* (legitimate), parents **Joannis** and **Lucia Perpić**, *filia [Confinii?]* — the last word of the mother's line is read at one zoom — religion *Cath.*, house **Krivi put No 136**, godparents **Lucas and Anna Tomljanović** (as read).

**What it settles and what it does not.**
* The index was right to the day, and the page agrees with the tree's 12 May 1859.
* **House 136** is the house the Perpić family of ahnentafel 14 and 15 is recorded at in the baptisms of Stephanus (1861) and the younger children — so the daughter and her brothers are one household, now on the page and not by inference from an index.
* **The mother's maiden name is still not given.** «Lucia Perpić» is her married name, as in the index and in every later entry; nothing on this page says whose daughter Lucia was. (The reading of «filia» and the word after it is the least certain part of the line.)
* **Not done:** the page's right-hand neighbour, and the 1859 marriage-register search for Joannes Perpić × Lucia, which would name her parents.

## Added the same day: her mother's maiden name is on her marriage

The baptism page gives the mother only as «Lucia Perpić». **Her marriage entry does name her**: Senj marriages, 3 May 1885 (entry 5, image 196 of waypoint 9R2D-N3F), where Antonia's parents are «Ivan Perpić, seljak, i Luca **rođena Perpić**» — Lucia née Perpić, as this archive had already read on 13 September (notes/reading-2026-09-13-familysearch.md). Both pages of that marriage are now kept as a snapshot: [senj-marriage-1885-papic-perpic](https://daviddef.github.io/TheBlazevic/snapshots/#senj-marriage-1885-papic-perpic).
