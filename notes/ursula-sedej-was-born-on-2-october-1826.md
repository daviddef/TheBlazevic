# Ursula Sedej was born and baptised on 2 October 1826, to Andreas Sedej and Gertrudis Roje

8 October 2026. Work list row 78. Her marriage of 28 May 1873 gave her age as 46 and her
parents as «Andrej Sedej, gruntar na novi Oslici» and «Jera Roje»; the baptismal register of
Nova Oselica that the first walk used (sig. 01584, 1826 onwards) had no Ursula in it. **She is
in the volume before it.**

## The entry

Matricula Online, Nova Oselica, *Krstna knjiga / Taufbuch 1785–1826*, sig. **01583** (136
images, the last of them the back cover), **image 134, left-hand page**, the lowest entry,
under *Anno 1826*:

> «[village, read *Habouske*, the first letters uncertain] Nro 8 · **Die 2da Octobris 1826.
> Ursula filia legitima Andreae Sedej rustici et ejus conjugis Gertrudis natae Roje**, catholici
> rustici [a word crossed through], nata hodie mane hora 9ª, baptizata a me Joanne Nemitz
> Parocho … Patrini levantes eam ex sacro fonte fuere Andreas Pod[…]ik et Maria Po[…]ig pariter
> catholici et rustici ex loco [village]. Obstetrix Maria Sedej ex Podlani[…].»

## What it settles

* **Her birth: 2 October 1826**, the same morning as the baptism («nata hodie mane hora 9ª»).
  Her age of 46 in May 1873 is **exact**, which is more than the 1873 marriage was expected to
  give.
* **Her parents: Andreas Sedej, a farmer (*rusticus*), and Gertrudis née Roje.** *Jera* is the
  Slovene short form of *Gertruda*, and «Roje» is the mother's own surname in both entries. It
  is the 1873 marriage read from the other end.
* **It sinks the first reading of the Maria Briz entry.** The 1829–30 baptism that names an
  «Andre Sedej, ½ Hüblor» with a wife **Maria Briz** was read as possibly an earlier wife of
  Ursula's father. **Gertrudis is Andreas's wife in October 1826**, and that child is of about
  1829–30, so Maria Briz cannot be an earlier wife. **It is either a different Andrej Sedej of
  the same parish and generation, or Gertrudis died between 1826 and 1829 and he married again.**
  Neither is proved.
* **A *Maria Sedej* is the midwife**, which fits the family being thoroughly resident, as the
  walk of 28 September already showed.

## What was not done

* The village name, the house number, the godparents' and midwife's names are **as read and
  not all certain**.
* Neither parent's own baptism or marriage was looked for; nor were any siblings of Ursula in
  1827–1834, nor her father's death, nor a marriage to Maria Briz.
* **Her marriage to Johann Kozina is still not found** (not in Kranj–Šmartin, not at Nova
  Oselica 1851–1853: [ursula-sedej-marriage-not-at-nova-oselica.md](ursula-sedej-marriage-not-at-nova-oselica.md)).
* It is not known why the first walk began at sig. 01584 and not at the end of 01583: the
  catalogue labels 01584 «1826–1862», and 01583 runs to October 1826, so **the two volumes
  overlap by the last months of 1826 and anyone looking for a baptism of that year has to
  open both.**
