# The marriage of Juraj Papa and Matia Pilipić is not on the Karlobag or Selce pages for 1856–1857

8 October 2026. Work list row 4, the marriage of ahnentafel 12 (Juraj Papa, b. 11 March 1838
at Selce, house 51) and ahnentafel 13 (Matia Pilipić, b. 18 September 1838, Karlobag). Their
son Josip was baptised at Senj on 1 November 1857, so they married in 1856 or early 1857 at
the latest. The Senj marriage index does not hold it (five queries, row 4), so this sitting
read the other two parishes the couple belong to.

## What was read, and what it shows

**Karlobag marriages 1804–1861** (DGS 5481649, waypoint **9RK1-HZK**): image 250, right
page (late 1855 and the 1856 entries of January, May and July) and
image 251, both pages (the rest of 1856, to 26 November, and 1857 to its first entries of
1858). The book is in Croatian from about 1855 («Poslie učinjenoga zasobe u tri razlikita
blagdana proglasjenja…»). **No entry has a groom named Juraj or Papa/Papić, and none has a
bride Pilipić/Pilepich.** Names noted on those pages include Luketić, Tomljenović, Kačunić,
Babić, Fabijanić, Perpičić and Franović.

**Selce marriages 1815–1878** (DGS 5497948, waypoint **9R2D-N35**, 66 images, about one image
for each year to 1850 and then two pages to a year): image 44, right page, printed page 75
(1855 and the 1856 list, closing «Godina 856 Vjenčanje parova 8»), and image 45, the 1857
page, printed page 76, of about eight couples. **Every groom and bride is a Selce Antić,
Lončarić, Jelčić, Bonić or Tobor, and none is the Juraj Papa of house 51.**

## What this changes

It narrows the place. **Not Karlobag and not Selce in 1856–57**, in these pages. What is left
is **Senj**, where the couple were by November 1857 and where the Senj marriage book of
1820–1858 (waypoint **9RK1-W3P**, 592 images) is **not in year order**: it holds several
sequences, Latin and Croatian, whose entries for 1832, 1836, 1841, 1848 and 1856 turn up
together on images 304–322. A reader cannot go to «1856–57» by interpolation, and the book has
to be walked. This sitting mapped it (images 296–366 are mostly entries with blank-form leaves
between; the marriages end and the deaths begin somewhere near the end of that range) and read
nothing in it for the couple.

## What was not done

* **Not every entry at Karlobag and Selce was read at the highest zoom.** Groom and bride names
  were read at about twice the spread's resolution, and a spelling not recognised on sight
  (Papić, Papa, Antić, Pilepich, Pilipić) could be missed on a dense line.
* **Karlobag before late 1855 and after early 1858, and Selce 1858 onward, were not read**, and
  neither was the Karlobag marriage KAZALO.
* **The marriage may be outside all three parishes** (their son's baptism names no place of
  origin for the parents).
* The whole of the Senj marriages 1820–1858 was left unread.
