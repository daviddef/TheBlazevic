# Senj baptisms 1826, 1828 and 1832: the older children of Augustinus Babić and Maria — and the mother's name written «Jur-csich»

Read from the images on 23 November 2026: Senj births 1756–1858 (FamilySearch waypoint 9R2M-L27, 589 images), the cathedral's baptism register.
Work list row 29 (the page half), done **by the parents' names**: the FamilySearch index was asked for the children of a father Augustinus and a mother Maria
under the surname Babich, 1824–1842, and each hit was opened at its image. It is the same family as [the 1834 and 1840 note](senj-baptisms-1834-1840-babic-siblings-maria-jurcic.md);
the sibling list of Ante Babić (baptised 17 May 1837, [that note](senj-baptism-1837-ante-babic.md)) is now read from the page for **every child the index gave, 1826 to 1840**.
**Control:** the same query returned the 1834 and 1840 children at images 251 and 259, as already read; the new entries are at images 244, 245 and 249.

**Reading limits.** The 1826 entry is on faded paper (the Matricula's own «Faded document» slip lies over the margin): the date, *Hellenam Franciscam*, *Augustini et Maria* and the
ending *-bich* are clear; the first letter of the surname is weak, and the second godparent's surname ends *-tich*. The 1828 entry is clear. In the 1832 row the godmother's surname
(*Kuzin*) and the priest's (*Jergollem*) are read with a query, and the house figure **455** after «sub Nro Dmi» is read at about 4x (the last two figures have the same compact form).

## What the reading settles

- **The mother's maiden name is written «nata Jur-csich» in 1832**, divided across two lines: *Jur* and then **csich** — the letter after the *c* is an *s* (or a long *s*) on balance, not an *e*, and the
  initial is a clear *J*. It is the tree's **Jurčić** again (1834 «Jur-cicz», 1837 and 1840 «Jurcich»), and **not** the index's «Turcich». The index has the twins' mother only as «Maria».
  **The Hungarian *cs* for *č* is a new written form for this name**; it goes into the Atlas catalogue's Jurčić family.
- **The 1826 and 1828 entries do not write the mother's maiden name** (the prose entries of the 1820s give the first names of the parents and the surname only). They are entered as this family's on four things:
  the father's name Augustinus, the mother's first name Maria, the surname Babich, and the parish; the maiden name is first written in 1832. That is a resemblance of two first names and a surname, so they are **probably, not provably**, the older children of the pair
  of 1832–1840.
- **The father's trade.** The 1832 row writes *Bajulus* (a porter or carrier) in the column of the father's trade, as the 1834, 1837 and 1840 entries do.
- **A house number.** «sub Nro Dmi 455» looks like the Senj house number of the family in January 1832. It is the only house figure these entries give; the 1834 and 1840 entries carry none.
- **A godfather and a surname.** Antonio Babich stands godfather in 1828 beside **Maria Lenacz**; the register of 1839–1843 writes an Antonius Babich's wife **Mathia Lenacz**. A resemblance only: the godfather
  may be that man, and nothing here says how he is related.

**The sibling list of Ante, now.** Hellena Francisca (28 March 1826), Joannes (23 June 1828), Gasparus and Paulus (4 January 1832), Nicolaus Ambrosius (6 November 1834), **Ante (17 May 1837)**, Philipina Jacobina (26 April 1840):
all read from the page. A seventh (Theresia, b. 1842) is in the index and not read.

## What was not read

Three images, one entry each (the twins' entry holds two children); the rest of these pages was looked at only for its headings, dates and the neighbouring rows. Images 450–461 (the Babić families of the 1840s), 481, 503 and 529 (Špalj × Perpić, see
[the next note](senj-spalj-perpic-1854-1857-daniel-and-franciscae.md)) and the Tomljanović and Krmpotić entries of 1820–1850 are **not read**; **the tree names no parents for any Tomljanović or Krmpotić of Senj in these years**, so there is no pair of names to ask the index for, and they stay a walk.
The index returned only the children it had; entries of Ante's family that it does not hold (Ante's own baptism is not indexed, and there may be others) are not excluded.

**Terms tried (the Atlas catalogue's Babić family):** Babich, with the father Augustinus (written Augusti, Angustinus in the index) and the mother Maria; Babić, Babic and Babicz were read on the pages. Mother's maiden name: Jurčić, Jurcic, Jurcicz, Jurcich, Jurcsich, Turcich (the index's misreading). Near names seen on the pages, not counted: none.
