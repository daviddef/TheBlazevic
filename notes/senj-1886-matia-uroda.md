# Ahnentafel 27, at the page — Senj, 9 February 1886

15 September 2026. Work list row 30. The Senj death register, ark
`3:1:3QS7-899X-1HVP`, entry **13** of 1886:

| column | as written |
|---|---|
| *Annus, Mensis, Dies obitus* | **1886, dne 9 veljača** |
| *Nomen, Cognomen, Conditio* | **Matija PILIPIĆ**, *žena* |
| *Parentum vel Conjugis* | **Vicko PILIPIĆ**, *fin. stražar u miru*, njezin muž |
| *oriundus* | **Karlobag** |
| religio · aetas | rim. kat. · **78** |
| *morbus* | **Marasmus senilis** |
| | *Providjena* |

**This is ahnentafel 27**, and three things in it are new.

**Her husband gets an occupation.** Ahnentafel 26 is written here as *«Vicko
Pilipić, **fin. stražar u miru**»* — a **retired finance guard**, a customs or
excise man of the Military Frontier's civil successor. Nothing in this archive
had said what he did.

**Her origin is recorded as Karlobag**, which independently confirms the
birthplace the tree carries. She is the daughter born **23 April 1810** whose
birth date fixed her parents' marriage to before 1810 — and that marriage was
found on 15 September as **17 June 1807** (`notes/karlobag-1807-uroda-gergas.md`).
Karlobag to Senj is the move her life makes, and the death register is the first
document to state it.

**And she is indexed under the wrong surname**, which is why she was missed. The
FamilySearch index files her as **Pilipić**, her *married* name; a search for
*Uroda* returns nothing at Senj. **A married woman's death is indexed under her
husband's surname**, and any sweep for a maiden name will walk straight past her.

## One thing recorded as read rather than as fact

The register gives her age as **78**. Born 23 April 1810 and dead 9 February
1886, she was **75**. A two- or three-year overstatement is ordinary in these
columns — the age is what the informant said, not what a document said — so it
is recorded as written and not treated as a correction to the birth date, which
has a baptism behind it and the age has not.

# Ahnentafel 8, at the page — Senj, 26 July 1907

Same row of the work list, same sitting. Senj deaths page **225**, entry **60**,
ark `3:1:3QS7-899X-14XR`:

> «**1907 srpnja 26** · **BLAŽEVIĆ Toma**, *nadničar* · **Udovac iza pok.
> Tereze** · Senj · R.K. · **69** · **Tuberculosis pulmonum**»

**The tree is exactly right and now it is evidenced.** Ahnentafel 8 was born
**27 January 1838** and the register gives his age as **69** — which is precisely
right for a death on 26 July 1907, and worth saying because the age column so
often is not. The date matches to the day.

**He was a day labourer.** *Nadničar* — nothing here had recorded an occupation
for him. Read beside ahnentafel 26, the *retired finance guard* on the 1886 page,
these two Senj households sit either side of a line: a pensioned state servant
and a man paid by the day.

**And he was a widower.** *«Udovac iza pok. Tereze»* — **ahnentafel 9, Tereza
Pećanić, was already dead in July 1907.** That is the first document of any kind
to touch her; the tree has no birth, no place and a death recorded only as
**«BEF 1850»**.

## A contradiction this sharpens without solving

**«BEF 1850» cannot be right.** Ahnentafel 8 was born in 1838. A wife who died
before 1850 would have been married to a boy of eleven. The entry proves only
that she died **before 26 July 1907** — a window fifty-seven years wide — so it
does not fix the date, but it does condemn the one the tree carries.

This belongs to work-list rows 36 and 38, the contradictions in
`consistency.json`, and it is a good example of the class: the error is not in
the register, it is in the tree, and the register is what exposes it.

## One loose end recorded rather than resolved

The FamilySearch index files this entry as a **burial on 28 August 1907**. The
register column it sits in is headed **«Godina mjesec i dan smrti»** — year,
month and day of *death* — and reads **26 July**. A month between death and
burial is not ordinary, so either the index is wrong or there is a separate
burial column further right that was not read. Recorded as a discrepancy, not
smoothed over.
