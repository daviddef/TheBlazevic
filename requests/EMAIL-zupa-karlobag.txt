I am researching the family of my grandmother, HEDVIGA "SEKA" BLAŽEVIĆ, born at
Senj in 1926. One of her ancestors on her mother's side (the PAPIĆ line) was CATHERINA
"CATTA" LUCKINIĆ of Karlobag. The work is published, free and without
advertising, at:

    https://blazevic.defranceski.com/

Anything you send would be published there in full and credited. A negative
answer is worth having too, and I publish those as results.

WHAT I HAVE READ. Your death register records her on 29 May 1790 as
"Catharina annorum 70, vidua defti Jacobi Pilepich" — the widow of the captain
of the port. A family tree gives her birth as 30 March 1727, and her marriage to
Jacob Pilepich as 22 November 1747; I have found no document for the birth.

===========================================================================
1. Her baptism
===========================================================================

I read the baptismal book for 1691-1770 on the FamilySearch film (Hrvatski
državni arhiv). The year 1727 runs from image 49 to image 50. There is NO
Luckinić baptism on 30 March 1727 (the entry for that day is a daughter of Lovri
Bulanich), and none anywhere in 1727. The book's own alphabetical index has no
Luckinić at all.

  1a. The baptism of CATHERINA LUCKINIĆ, with her parents' names. I have now read
      every baptism from June 1717 to the middle of 1732 for her and she is not
      among them; her death entry of 1790
      gives her age as 70, which points to about 1720. If the filmed copy has
      missing leaves, or if her entry is in another volume, I would be grateful
      to be told.

  (For the record: I have found her MARRIAGE, 22 November 1747, in the marriage
  register of 1736-1804, where she is "Katta, daughter of the late Vicko
  Luckinich", so it is only her baptism that is missing.)

  1b. A GAP I FOUND WHILE LOOKING. In the filmed copy the last entry before
      the turn of a leaf is dated 4 May 1726, and the next entry, on the back
      of that leaf, is dated 11 January 1727. Nothing from May to December 1726
      is written between them. Are the baptisms of those eight months in the
      original (on a loose leaf, in another volume, added later), or were none
      entered? A baptism in that window is a place Catherina could be.

2. A Luckinić household at Karlobag in 1727
===========================================================================

On 24 August 1727 the baptismal book names "the wife of Vicko Luckinich, called
Morica" as a godmother (and on 10 March 1726 a godmother "Mara, wife of Vicko Luckinich"), so a Vicko Luckinić and his wife were living at Karlobag
that year. The FamilySearch index lists a Mare Luckinić baptised on 13 April 1725 to a
Vicko Luckinić. I have now read that entry, near the foot of a page that is out
of date order: it appears to record a daughter ANA to Vicko Luckinić and his wife
MARE, so the index may have taken the mother's name for the child's.

  2a. The marriages of VICKO LUCKINIĆ, before 1725 — the marriage register on
      film begins in 1736. The baptismal book names his wife JELENA as a
      godmother on 23 January 1718 (and, less certainly, on 11 August 1722), and
      a wife MARA or MORICA in 1726 and 1727, so he may have married twice; a
      second marriage would be about 1722-1726.
  2b. The deaths of Vicko and Morica, which would give their ages.

4. A family called GERGACS / GERKACS / GERGACH, at Karlobag by 1753
===========================================================================

Another ancestor on the same side, NICOLAUS GERKACS, died at Karlobag on 30 October
1783 — your death register, folio 39: "Nicolaus Gergacs annorum 66 ... obiit in domo
filii sui Antonii". His son ANTONIUS married Francisca, daughter of Michael
Berkliacich, on 26 November 1769 ("Antonij filij Nicolai Gergacs"), and his son
MICHAEL married Lucia Tomljanović on 2 November 1785 ("filii defuncti Nicolai"). I
cannot find where the family came from, and I have now read the filmed books
for it.

What the films show: no Gergacs/Gerkacs baptism at Karlobag in the whole baptismal
book 1691-1770 EXCEPT ONE, on 9 August 1753 (book page 213): "Lovru sina Mikule y
Marde Gergach", godparents Marko Milletich and Gele Kriskovich. And a marriage on
29 June 1766 (marriage book, page 21) of Antonius Carnkovich to Mattia, "filiam
Nicolai Baretich alias Gergach". No marriage of a Gergach at Karlobag between 1741 and
1766. Nicolaus's own baptism (about 1717) and Antonius's (before 1748) are not in the
Karlobag book, and I have not found them in the Mrežnica books either, so I think
the family arrived from another parish between February 1751 and August 1753.

  4a. Whether any record in the parish or the diocese — a list of parishioners, a
      note in the margin of the 1753 entry, a letter of transfer, a dispensation
      for a marriage — says WHERE the family of MIKULA / NICOLAUS GERGACH came from,
      or what his wife MARDA's family was.
  4b. The meaning of "Baretich alias Gergach" in 1766. Is Baretich a house name, a
      second family name, or a different man? Is there a Baretich family at Karlobag
      (a Georgius Baretich and his wife Barbara have children baptised 1754-1757)?
  4c. The baptisms of ANTONIUS (born before 1748) and MICHAEL (the family tree says born about 1762; I have no document for it) —
      if they are anywhere in a register you hold that the film does not carry. I
      noticed that the filmed baptismal book has no entries between 20 January and
      19 February 1738, and that a loose slip of 12 September 1762 mentions a
      "Mihat Gargay alias Bazetich u Šibniku" (baptised at Šibenik) — I do not know
      whether that is the same family.
  4d. The marriage of Mikula and Marda, if the parish has it (I did not find it at
      Karlobag).
