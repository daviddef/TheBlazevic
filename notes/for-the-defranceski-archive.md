— they belong to the Defranceski line — **and that the pages should exist there.** This file is the
hand-off.

## Two cautions before publishing any of them

**1. Most of the pre-1800 ones hang off a sorting bucket.** Their recorded parent is
*«Gologorica Defranceschi»*, which is a **filing label, not a person** — the same class this estate
gates against publishing. The people are real; **the parentage is not evidence** and should not be
drawn as a link.

**2. Duplicate given names are expected and are usually not errors.** Four *Gian Giovanni Ivan
Battista*, four *Guiseppe Josephus Josip*, three *Maria*. In these parishes a child who died was
very often replaced by a later child of the same name. **Do not merge them on a name.**
