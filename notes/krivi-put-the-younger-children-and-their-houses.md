# The younger children of Joannes and Lucia Perpić, and their houses

9 October 2026. Work list row 31, third part. The index lists a Georgius (1868), a Joanna (1874) and a
Josephus (1876) as children of «Joannes Perpić × Lucia Perpić» at Krivi Put, and `found.psv` carried them
as candidate younger siblings of ahnentafel 7. Ahnentafel 7 (1859) and her brother Stephanus
([born 13 December 1861](stephanus-perpic-was-born-in-december-1861.md)) are at **Krivi Put 136**, so the
house number is again the test. Krivi Put births 1847–1884, waypoint 9R28-DP8.

## What this shows

* **Georgius and Joanna are very probably her brother and sister.** The parents' names are the same pair, the
  house is **135**, one number from 136, and the house is the same in 1868 and 1874. A renumbering of a
  neighbour is possible, and nothing in the register says 135 is 136, so the word is **probable**.
* **Josephus is not shown to be one.** Same parents' names, but a different place and number
  («Klaričevac», the hamlet name uncertain), godparents Josephus and Mathia Perpić, and a later note
  «umro 3.12.85» (died 3 December 1885, aged nine).
* **All three dates agree with the index.** (This is a different volume from Senj, where the tree was right against the index for Milka; neither source is reliable in general.)

## What was not done

* The 1862–1873 children of houses 135 and 136 were not listed, so there is no household list and no
  explanation of 135/136.
* Lucia's surname in 1876 is cut off at the cell edge.
* The marriage of Joannes and Lucia is not in a filmed Krivi Put marriage book (1871–1894 only).
