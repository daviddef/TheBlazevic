# Daniel Špalj: three children at Senj (1852, 1854, 1857), no baptism of his own, no marriage — an index search, 17 October 2026

The tree's **Daniel Špalj** has no dates and no place, a wife **Francesca Prpić**, and children **Margaritte (1871, born at Krmpote 152), Stanislava (1873) and Mathea (1875)**. This archive's earlier reading ([senj-baptism-index.md](senj-baptism-index.md), item 6) found him at Senj in 1854 and 1857. This note records what the full search for **his own baptism and his marriage** found. Index only for the baptisms; the marriage volume is the archive's own Senj walk.

## What the index holds for a father Daniel (Danielis) Špalj

| baptised | child | parents | index record |
|---|---|---|---|
| **26 February 1852** | **Matthia** (the first of the name) | Danielis *Spaly* × Francisca *Penpic* [Prpić] | QKMJ-S8Y7 / S8YQ |
| 1 December 1854 | Lucia | Danielis Spalj × Franciscaina Perpić | QKMJ-SSNK |
| 22 February 1857 | **Mathia** (the second) | Danielis Spalj × Francisca Prpić | QKMJ-S31G |

**This adds a third child and moves the marriage earlier:** the Matthia of **1852** is under a spelling («Spaly», «Penpic») the earlier sweep did not capture, and the **Mathia of 1857 is a second child of the same name**, so the first died or the name was reused. **Daniel and Francesca were married before February 1852** and were at **Senj** in 1852, 1854 and 1857.

## What the index does not have

* **No baptism of a Daniel Špalj** in the collection, searched under **ten spellings** (Spalj, Špalj, Spal, Spaly, Spaljich, Špaljić, Spalich, Szpalj, Spalyj) with the given name Daniel/Danielis/Danijel, births **1815–1845**, anywhere: nothing. The Senj baptisms of 1820–1858 are indexed, so **he was not baptised at Senj in those years**. The only Špalj baptisms of 1825–1840 are at Bribir, Smiljan and Gospić, none a Daniel.
* **No marriage of Daniel and Francesca** in the Senj marriage volume (1820–1858, read entirely in the walk of 13–15 October; the walk's negatives are weak). The Senj marriage volume holds Špalj entries of 1747, 1814 and 1820 and deaths of Špalj in 1820, 1828, 1831 and 1836, none of them his.
* The **Krmpote births begin in 1879** and the film for Krmpote earlier does not exist, so a baptism at Krmpote or Ledenice before the 1850s is on no film.

## Result

**Daniel Špalj's baptism is not reachable on any film or index this archive can open**, and the better reading is that he was born about 1820–1830 at **Krmpote, Vodice or Ledenice** (where his children later lived: Krmpote 152) and came to Senj before 1852. Two routes remain: the Senj marriage entry of about 1850–51 (a second read of the 1850–51 pages of the Senj volume, which the walk read once) and an entry in his children's later registers that names his parents (the Krmpote births from 1879 name grandparents only by chance).
