# Stephanus Perpić was born on 13 December 1861 — the index was right and the tree was wrong

9 October 2026. Work list rows 29 and 31. On 20 September the index gave Stephanus Perpić (a
son of ahnentafel 14 × 15 and so a brother of ahnentafel 7) as **13 December 1861, baptised 16
December**, where the tree has **13 October 1861**. A test against the archive's own page images
then went against the first theory about it, and this case was left "open and standing alone"
([the-month-is-wrong-and-the-day-is-right.md](the-month-is-wrong-and-the-day-is-right.md)). It is
now read from the page.

## The entry

Krivi Put births 1847–1884 (DGS 5494757, waypoint **9R28-DP8**), **image 78, left-hand page,
entry 109 of 1861**: born «13. 10bris 1861», baptised «16. 10bris 1861», **Stephanus Perpić**,
*legit.*, parents **Joannes Perpić & Lucia Perpić**, **Krivi put N° 136**.

## Why 10bris is December

The clerk writes the months as Latin numerals with *bris*: 7bris September, 8bris October, 9bris
November, 10bris December. The entries on the page run in date order, and they say it themselves:

| entry | born | baptised |
|---|---|---|
| 105 | 30 «9bris» (November) | 30 «9bris» |
| 106 | 30 «9bris» | 1 «10bris» (December) |
| 107 | 7 «10bris» | 8 «10bris» |
| 108 | 13 «10bris» | 15 «10bris» |
| **109 — Stephanus** | **13 «10bris»** | **16 «10bris»** |
| 110 | 26 «10bris» | 28 «10bris» |
| 111 | 29 «10bris» | 29 «10bris» |
| **«Annus 1862», entry 1** | 4 January 1862 | 5 January |

An October entry could not stand between 30 November and 26 December. The year's 111 entries
also put entry 109 at the very end of 1861 (June is about entry 59, August about 70).

## What it settles

* **Born 13 December 1861, baptised 16 December**, at **Krivi Put 136** — ahnentafel 7's house.
* **The index was right and the tree wrong.** The day (13) is the tree's; only the month differs.
* **The earlier theory is dead in both directions.** 20 September's hypothesis was that the
  tree loses Roman-numeral strokes (*XII → X*). Milka's case went against the index, and this one
  goes against the tree, so neither source is the careless one in general. **Each of the two
  disputed dates simply has to be read.**
* **A second household at house 136:** entry 1 of 1862 (4 January) is a *Josephus Perpić*, son of
  **Petrus Perpić & Anna Perpić**, also **Krivi put N° 136** — a different couple in the same
  house, which is what a *zadruga* would look like in a register.

## What was not done

* The digits **13** and **136** were read at about three times the page's resolution; the *month*
  rests on the sequence of entries, not on one stroke, which is why it is firm.
* The tree entry was **not edited** (this archive does not edit the tree); the disagreement is
  recorded in [/corrections](/corrections/) and `readings.psv`.
* The other children (Georgius 1868, Joanna 1874, Josephus 1876) were not opened.
