# Senj births, 1859–1877: the register for 7 July 1876 (Josephus, Klaričevac 82/163)

Waypoint 9R2D-JW5 (Senj births 1859–1877, 399 images), read 23 November 2026 at images 363–364 (the two openings that hold the baptisms of 29 June to 23 July 1876).
Follows `senj-births-march-1879-matija.md`, which left the Senj entry for Josephus open.

## The question

Josephus, son of Joannes Perpić and Lucia née Perpić, stands in the Krivi Put births at image 226 with the date «7 July 1876», house Klaričevac 82 / 163, godparents Josephus Perpić and Mathia Perpić, baptiser Simon Serdoc parochus.
Is there a Senj entry for him, and does it write the father Perpić or Tomljanović?

## What the pages are

- The book is the printed Latin table (Numerus currens; Annus, Mensis, Dies: Nativitatis, Collati Baptismatis; Baptisati: Nomen, legitimus vel illegitimus; Parentum: Nomen, Cognomen, Conditio, Religio; Locus domicilii et numerus domalis;
  Patrinorum nomen; Baptisantis nomen; Observationes). Entries are numbered from 1 each year.
- Image 363 is printed page 353 (entries 67–73, born 27 June to 6 July, baptised 29 June to 9 July 1876); image 364 is page 354 (entries 74–79, born 5 to 22 July, baptised 12 to 23 July 1876).
  Both openings were read at full size, left and right pages. The openings before and after (images 362 and 365) were seen only to place the dates.
- The month is written «Julii» / «Junii». The baptisers are Emericus Babić (cooperator) and Antonius Bosnić.

## What was found

- **No entry is dated 7 July 1876 as a birth or as a baptism.** The births in entries 67–79 are dated 24 June to 22 July; the baptisms 29 June (67), 2 July (68–70), 9 July (71–73), 12 July (74), 16 July (75, 76), 18 July (77) and 23 July (78, 79).
- **None of the thirteen entries (67–79) names Perpić, Prpić or Tomljanović**, in the parents, the godparents or the house column; none is at Krivi Put or Klaričevac; none is a Josephus (the boys are Paulus, Joannes Petrus, Joannes Isaias, Henricus, Antonius Ludovicus, Franciscus, Vincentius, Jacobus).
- Snapshots: `/snapshots/#senj-births-1876-07-entries-67-73` (image 363) and `/snapshots/#senj-births-1876-07-entries-74-79` (image 364), each the left page with the right page beside it.
- On image 363, entry 68 (Anna, daughter of Georgius Špalj and Catharina née Butorac, house Krmpote N° 152) has godparents «Thomas Thian et Anna Babić». The same name stands godfather to Michael at Senj in January 1874; the entries do not show it is the same man.
  Near-name seen on the page, not counted: **Špalj** (the 1875 slip at Krivi Put names a Lucia née Špalj, wife of a Josephus Prpić).

## What this does not show

- **It does not show Josephus is absent from the Senj register.** The Krivi Put entry names Simon Serdoc parochus as baptiser: Josephus was most likely baptised at Krivi Put itself, not at Senj (as Matija was, «u Senju krštjena»). The Senj pages here are consistent with that, and no more.
- Only entries 67–79 were read: the baptisms of 29 June to 23 July. A baptism at Senj of a child born in the first week of July, entered late, or an entry before or after this stretch, was not looked for. This is not a walk of the register or of 1876.
- The Krivi Put date «7 July 1876» is a single date in the table; whether it is the birth or the baptism was not settled here.

## What follows

Pave (born 12 September 1881) was read afterwards (`senj-births-september-1881-pave.md`): nothing in entries 71–84. Of the couple's children the Senj register has Michael (1874, father written Perpić) and Matija (1879, father written Tomljanović); nothing for Josephus or Pave.
Surname and given-name forms met: Prpić/Perpić (cover run, no new form), Tomljanović, Špalj, Jurčić, Radošević; Josephus/Josip (in the catalogue). None is new to the Atlas catalogues.
