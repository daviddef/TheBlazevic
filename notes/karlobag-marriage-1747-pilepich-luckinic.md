# Jakov Pilepich × Katta Luckinich, Karlobag, 22 November 1747: the marriage of ahnentafel 104 and 105 is on the page, and it names Katta's father as the late Vicko Luckinich

11 October 2026. Work list rows 26 and 34. Karlobag marriages 1736-1804 (DGS 5481649, waypoint 9RK1-HZK, the film's
old marriage book at images about 275-300: 1744 at image 282, 1746-1748 at 283-284, 1755 at 290, 1760 at 293, 1763 at 296).
The book is in Croatian until the late 1740s and then in Latin; the entries are short — the priest, «sakupih u S.
Matrimonij» (I joined in holy matrimony), the two spouses, sometimes the bride's father, and the witnesses.

## The entry

Image 283, right-hand page, headed «Letta 1747», the fifth entry: «Dan 22. Novembra Andrij Kollekar Clovan
sakupih u S. Matrimonij **Jakova Pillepicha** ij **Kattu heer pokoin[og] Vicha Luckinicha**. Biha svidoki Pop Nikula
Jurich ij Sig[nor] Mattio Borri.» — «On the 22nd of November, Andrija Kolekar, parish priest, I joined in holy
matrimony Jakov Pilepich and Katta, daughter of the late Vicko Luckinich; witnesses the priest Nikola Jurić and
Signor Matteo Borri.»

## What it gives

* **The tree's marriage date, 22 November 1747, is right**, and it is now read from the register.
* **Catta's father is named: VICKO LUCKINICH, and he was already dead («pokojnog») in November 1747.** That
  is ahnentafel 210, who had a name and no document. It also fits the sponsor entries of 1718-1727 (Vicko Luckinich
  and his wives Jelena, Mare), so the Vicko of those pages is almost certainly her father, dead between 1727 and 1747.
* **Her baptism is not in the marriage entry's reach, and her age «70» in 1790 (born about 1720) is untouched:**
  nothing here gives a birth year. She is «Katta» here and «Catharina» in her 1790 death entry.
* Her mother is not named; ahnentafel 211 (Maria in the tree) is still unproved. Of Vicko's wives in the baptism
  book, Jelena (1718, 1722) is the one likely to be Catta's mother if she was born about 1720, and Mare/Mara/Morica
  (1725-27) if she was born later; nothing in the marriage settles it.

## Caution

* «heer pokoin[og]» is read at one zoom, the second word of the groom's wife's patronymic is cut by a line end at
  «Vicha / Luckinicha»; the witnesses' names are read at one zoom.
* Jakov is written «Pillepicha» (genitive/accusative), the same family as the Pilipići of Karlobag; his own father is
  not named.
