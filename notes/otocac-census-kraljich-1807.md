# The Kraljich brothers in the Otočac status animarum: «sons of the late Jacobus», adopted into house No. 53 (17 October 2026)

**The question.** The tree's Nicolaus Kraljich (b. «29 September 1807», d. 1845) has a father Jacobus, an «Anna, b. 1809» as his mother (which is his wife's birth year, see [integrity-audit-triage-2026-10-17.md](integrity-audit-triage-2026-10-17.md)), brothers Mattheus (1812) and Stephanus (1817), and a sister Francisca (1820). [boras-kraljich-source-search-2026-10-17.md](boras-kraljich-source-search-2026-10-17.md) looked for their baptisms in the Senj births of 1807–1820 and found no way in. **That search was aimed at the wrong parish.** The tree's own source for Nicolaus's birth is the **Otočac** church census, image 76 of 384 (waypoint 9R2D-16X; image list `9R2D-16X:391644801,391674001,391979501`), which the earlier sessions had never opened for him. This note reads two pages of it: **image 76 (house No. 40) and image 86 (house No. 53).** Image n is archive frame n + 243 (76 = 319, 86 = 329).

## What it shows

1. **Nicolaus and Stephanus are «filius defti Jacobi», sons of the late Jacobus** (*defti* = *defuncti*). So the tree's father **Jacobus is a real entry on a document and was dead when the line was written**. No mother is named on either page. Matthaeus is in the same run of struck rows with the father's name left to the dittos; that he is a third son of Jacobus is a reading of the layout, not a statement.
2. **They did not stay in house No. 40.** Their rows are struck there, carried to **No. 53**, and at No. 53 Nicolaus is entered as **«adopt[atus] Kraljić ex N° 40»**: **adopted into the Dujmović household from the Kraljić house**, with his brother Stephanus beside him. (The census marginalia elsewhere use «adoptata ex 35 Dubravo» in the same sense.) Nothing on the page says why; it is the pattern of a Military Frontier house taking in heirs. The «845» beside both brothers at house No. 40 is probably the year of the move.
3. **Nicolaus's date is «9bris» = November, not September.** The months from September run 7bris, 8bris, 9bris, Xbris (September, October, **November**, December). At house No. 53 the glyph of Nicolaus's «29 9bris 1807» is the same as Vitus's «1 9bris», and **Stephanus's «7 7bris 1817» two rows below is visibly a different glyph (a 7)**, so the scribe's 7 and 9 can be told apart on this page. The tree's «29 September 1807» is therefore probably **29 November 1807**. (An earlier version of this note read Stephanus's month as 9bris as well; that was wrong, and is corrected: **Stephanus was born 7 September 1817**.) Only a baptism entry would settle it.
4. **Nicolaus's children are in house No. 53 with him:** Georgius (19 April 1836), Petronilla (1838), Vincentia (12 March 1840), as the tree has them.
5. **The wife: the tree is right about Anna.** The tree has **Anna Žubrinić Xubrinich, b. 5 September 1809**. The census (image 76 right, house No. 40) has «**Anna uxor Nicolai**, 5 7bris 1809» (struck) with no maiden name, to the day of the tree's date; and **the baptism of her son Georgius on 19 April 1836 names her «Anna uxor ejus nata Xubrinich», at Prozor N° 40** ([otocac-death-1821-jacobus-kralich.md](otocac-death-1821-jacobus-kralich.md)). So house No. 40 is **Prozor No. 40** and the tree's wife stands on two documents. (An earlier version of this note said the Žubrinić was unsupported; that was written before the baptism was read, and is withdrawn.) At house No. 53 another woman is entered as wife of a Nicolaus, **«Felicitas uxor Nicolai nata Banich»**, and the only «nata Žubrinić» in the household is **Mathia, wife of Stephanus**. Which Nicolaus is Felicitas's husband is not stated (the other Nicolaus there was born in 1837), so she is probably a later wife of Nicolaus Kraljić after Anna was struck; that is not proved.
6. **Stephanus is one man in the tree twice.** The tree's «Stephanus Kraljich, husband of Mathia Žubrinić, father of Maria (1848)» and its «Stephanus Kraljich b. 1817» are almost certainly the brother who is «Stephanus frater Nicolai» here, with «Mathia uxor Stephani nata Žubrinić» and «Maria filia Stephani» (July 1848) in the same household. The tree is not edited; it is noted.
7. **The family is Otočac's, so the Senj books of 1807–1820 were never the place to look**, and the Senj film (9R2M-L27) has nothing for them ([senj-births-1756-1858-image-map.md](senj-births-1756-1858-image-map.md)). The baptisms of 1807–1817 are in books before the Otočac film begins (September 1834).

## What is still unproved

* **Who Jacobus's wife was.** No mother is named on either census page. The Otočac deaths of 1821 have a «Maria uxor Jacobi Kralich», aged 36, on 6 February, six months before Jacobus's own burial (1 August 1821, aged 41): [otocac-death-1821-jacobus-kralich.md](otocac-death-1821-jacobus-kralich.md). She is the probable mother; her maiden name is not given. The tree's «Anna, b. 1809» as mother is Nicolaus's wife attached to the wrong person; its «Vincentia, b. 1811», second wife of Jacobus, is on no page read.
* **Whose daughters Petronilla and Vincentia are** (Anna's or Felicitas's): no mother is stated.
* **Francisca, b. «1820»** (the tree) is not among the rows read.
* **Year digits** of Georgius (b. 1778), Franciscus and the second Georgius at house No. 40 and of Vitus at house No. 53 were read once at about 0.55x and are marked not firm.
* **The houses between:** the page of house No. 40 (image 76) and house No. 53 (image 86) are the two read; images 77, 82, 85 were looked at only for the house numbers (77 = about No. 42–43, 82 = No. 46–47, 85 = No. 50–52, 86 = Nos. 52–54).

## The letter

The questions the pages cannot answer (the baptisms of 1807, 1812 and 1817, the parents' marriage, whether the Jacobus and Maria of the 1821 deaths were of Prozor No. 40, the adoption into the Dujmović house, and Nicolaus's marriage or marriages) go to the **Otočac parish**, not to Senj, as **section 4 of the Otočac draft** on the [errands page](/errands/): *EMAIL-zupa-otocac.txt*, not sent.
