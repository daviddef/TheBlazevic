# Michael Gerkacs × Lucia Tomljanović, Karlobag, 2 November 1785: ahnentafel 110 and 111 married, the groom's father is the late Nicolaus Gerkacs, and Lucia's father Paul Tomljanović is «of Montani Stanište»

11 October 2026. Work list rows 24 and 34. Karlobag marriages 1736-1804 (DGS 5481649, waypoint 9RK1-HZK), image 324,
right-hand page (the left page and the page's head carry «Anno Dni 1785»; image 320 is January 1782, 322 is 1783, 323
is 1784 and 326 is 1786). The entries are Latin, one paragraph each, the fathers named.

## The entry

«2. Novembris. Praemissis denuntiationibus, nullo impedimento detecto, ac habito mutuo consensu **Michaelis filii
defuncti Nicolai Gergacs et Luciae [filiae] Pauli Tomljenovich, loci Mostani [Montani] Stanize**, eos per verba de
praesenti solemniter matrimonio conjunxi ego Joes Antonius Frankovich Parochus Carlobagensis, praesentibus Antonio
Gergacs et Joanne Kovacevich.»

## What it gives

* **Ahnentafel 110, Michael Gerkacs, is the groom, and his father is the LATE NICOLAUS GERKACS** — ahnentafel 220,
  whose death at Karlobag on 30 October 1783 is already in the tree and fits «defuncti» in 1785.
* **Ahnentafel 111, Lucia «Maja» Tomljanović, is the bride, and her father is PAUL TOMLJANOVICH, of «Montani
  Stanize»** — the herders' settlement the tree gives for her and for ahnentafel 222. That makes the place
  **a document and not only the tree's word**, and the first page-evidence for the Montani Stanište household
  (the work list's row 34 had it as «a gazetteer gap»). Her mother is not named.
* The marriage date, 2 November 1785, agrees with the tree's era (the first child of this couple is baptised in
  1787) and with her death entry at the 1809 «annorum 34», which cannot be right for a marriage of 1785 and so
  stays unresolved.
* Witnesses: Antonius Gergacs (a relative or namesake) and Joannes Kovacevich; the priest is Joannes Antonius Frankovich,
  parish priest of Karlobag.

## Caution

* «Mostani Stanize» is the place word at the line end, read once at about 6x; the identification with Montani
  Stanište is the obvious one and the tree's, and is not independently checked.
* The month and day were read from the margin («2. Novembris») and the entry's position between October and
  24 November.
