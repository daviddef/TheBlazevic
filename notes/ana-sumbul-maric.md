# «Ana Shambul Marić» — the name is two names, and one of them is not a surname

*Work list row 51, 15 September 2026. Read out of the GEDCOM alone; no register
was opened, and nothing here is evidence of the kind this archive collects.*

Ahnentafel 11 is the emptiest person on the direct line: a name, two tree links
and nothing else. Her father, ahnentafel 22, is **a surname with no given name**.
Her mother is not in the tree at all.

Before opening a book for her it is worth knowing **what to look for**, and the
GEDCOM answers that on its own.

## Four things the tree says

**1. «Shambul» is in the surname field, not the given-name field.**

    0 @I506654@ INDI
    1 NAME  /Shambul Marić/
    2 SURN Shambul Marić

The given name is *empty*. So this was never «a man called Shambul whose surname
was Marić». The whole string arrived as a surname, and MyHeritage has simply
carried it.

**2. Her two siblings are plain Marić.** Mathias Marić and Joannes Marić, same
father, same family record, no second word. A surname does not attach to one
child of three. **A by-name does** — it follows a household, a house or a man,
and the priest writes it when he needs to tell that Marić from another.

**3. The two sources disagree with each other, and neither is a document.** Ana's
record cites a MyHeritage Smart Match (QUAY 3) whose URL reads
`ana-žubrinić-rođen-**shumbul**-marić`, and a Geni world tree (QUAY 4) reading
`ana-zubrinic-born-**shambul**-marić`. **Šumbul in one, Shambul in the other**,
and both are other people's trees rather than a page anybody has read.

**4. Her married name is recorded separately.** `2 _MARNM Žubrinić` — the tree
knows the difference between the name she was born with and the name she was
buried under, which is the same distinction the Senj stones forced on
[the grave matcher](../site/src/pages/stones.astro).

## What follows

**The surname to search is Marić.** Šumbul is the discriminator, not the name —
the thing that will separate this family from every other Marić in Lika once a
register is open, exactly as *Papa* separates one Papić household at Selce and
*Smojver* one Vukić household at Karlobag.

**It stays out of `sources/bynames.psv`.** That file holds phrases **read off a
page** — *Antich Papa*, *Jellichich dictis Jurešina*. This one has been read off
a URL. It is a hypothesis with a reason, and it is filed here rather than there
for exactly that reason.

## The book

Ana's husband is Nicolaus Žubrinić, born 24 January 1834 at Otočac. Their
children are **Lucca (1853)**, **Marija (about 15 September 1858)**, Tereza
(29 November 1867) and Jacobus. Otočac births are filmed **1834–1858**, so
**Lucca's and Marija's baptisms are both inside the film** — and a baptism names
the mother, her father and usually the house number.

Two entries would settle her father's given name, her mother's existence, and
whether the priest wrote Šumbul beside Marić.
