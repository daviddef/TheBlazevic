# Brinje — the parish this archive has never opened

**Status: drafted, not sent.** Written 13 September 2026, the day it became
obvious that we had been searching the wrong side of a mountain.

**To:** Državni arhiv u Gospiću, Kaniška 17, 53000 Gospić —
**zahtjevi@arhiv-gospic.hr**
*(Brinje is in Lika-Senj county and falls under Gospić.)*

## What we got wrong

Our archive treats the **Kalanj** family as a **Klenovica** family. Klenovica is
on the coast, and we hold 216 Kalanj records, 119 of them at Klenovica across
houses 22, 23, 36, 37, 38 and 59.

Their own baptismal entry says otherwise, and we had never read that line
properly. At the baptism of **Ana Kalanj, Klenovica N° 22, 3 January 1864**, the
parents are entered as:

> *Josephus Kalanj et Rosalia nata Perković ejus uxor,*
> **Brinjensis parochiae ruricolae**

— **country people of the parish of Brinje**. Brinje is inland, over the Kapela,
about forty kilometres from Klenovica. Our own tree independently records
**Rozalija Perković** as being *of Brinje*.

So the Klenovica Kalanji are **Brinje people who came down to the coast**, and
every search we have run has been on the coast.

## Three surnames meet at Brinje, and we did not see it

| Person | Record | Where this comes from |
|---|---|---|
| **Rozalija Perković** | wife of Josip Kalanj of Klenovica 22; recorded *of Brinje* | our tree |
| **Josip Kalanj & Rozalija** | *Brinjensis parochiae ruricolae*, 1864 | Klenovica baptism |
| **Jure Borić** | *of Brinje* | our tree |
| **Ana Borić**, b. **12 Feb 1885**, Brinje | daughter of Jure; **married Frane Papić** (b. 27 July 1873, **Senj**) | our tree |
| **Stipe Kalanj**, b. 1885 | emigrated **1910**, residence **Brinje**, met in New York by his **cousin Juro Borić** | US passenger manifest, *La Bretagne* |
| **Mile Kalanj**, b. 1889 | emigrated **1909**, met by his **uncle Jure Božić** | US passenger manifest, *Lucania* |

**Kalanj, Perković and Borić are all at Brinje**, and a Borić of Brinje married a
Papić of Senj — Papić being the family of Hedviga's own mother.

We are **not** claiming these are all one kin-group. We are saying that the
parish where three of our surnames overlap is the one parish we have never
opened.

## What we are asking for

1. **Brinje marriages, roughly 1840–1865** — for the marriage of
   **Josip (Josephus) Kalanj**, born 27 August 1826 at Klenovica, to
   **Rozalija Perković** of Brinje. She died 25 September 1917. This is the
   marriage that would tell us which Brinje household she came from, and it is
   the single entry we want most.
2. **Brinje baptisms, roughly 1820–1835**, for **Rozalija Perković** herself.
3. **Any Brinje register decade 1850–1890** in which the surnames **Kalanj**,
   **Perković** and **Borić** can be seen together — house numbers especially.
   We have learned to read house numbers as kin-groups
   (*zadruge*) and they have done more for us than names.

## What we can offer in return

We hold, and will share, a transcribed list of **81 Kalanj emigration records**
from US and Australian passenger manifests, 1891–1956, including residences,
ships and the relatives each emigrant named. Several give **Brinje** and
**Plaščica** as the last residence. If your reading room has any interest in the
Lika emigration, that list is yours.

## What we would do with it

Publish it in full, with the shelfmark, at **daviddef.github.io/TheBlazevic**,
and say plainly that we had the parish wrong for the life of the archive.

*Hvala vam unaprijed na svakoj pomoći.*
