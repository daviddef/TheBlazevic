# Vrbnik or Selce? Four registers, one family of priests, two labels

Read 14 September 2026 from `photos/line/4505556-josephum-antic-baptism-1806.jpg`,
**2,962 × 2,167**, on disk since the media sweep.

## The entry, confirmed at full resolution

> **Die 14. Martii Aº 1806. baptizavi ego Michael Rombol C:L: infantem lege
> natum patre Joanne, Matre Marthæ <u>Antich Papa</u>, nomine Josephum,
> susceptoribus conjugibus Judice Mathia Jellichich et pia consorte Margarita.**

*Antich Papa* is underlined, exactly as this archive has always said. Two things
it did not have:

* the godfather is **Judex Mathias Jelličić — the village judge** — and a
  Jelličić. Ahnentafel 24's godfather is of the family he would marry into
  **twenty-nine years later**.
* the priest signs **C:L:** in 1806 and **Parochus Loci** in 1809 — the same man,
  promoted.

The entry below it confirms the other two by-names, both underlined:

> **Die 22. Martii 1806. Rñdus Dñus Nicolaus … *Canonicus Dobrig[nensis]*
> baptizavit infantem … Matre Maria <u>Lonzarich Franich</u>, nomine Josephum,
> susceptoribus conjugibus Vincentio et Maria <u>Antich Dudin</u>.**

## And now the problem

The page header reads **`Anno Dñi 1806`** in the original hand. Beneath it, in
modern block capitals and a different medium, someone has written
**`* 40 NOVORODJENIH`** and **`VRBNIK`**. Those are **finding-aid annotations,
not the register's own statement** — but a cataloguer's annotation carries
weight, and it is corroborated: the second entry's officiant is a **canon of
Dobrinj**, which is on the island of **Krk**, as Vrbnik is.

So this archive holds four registers in the hands of one family of priests:

    1806  baptism of ahn 24   Michaël Rombol, C.L.        labelled VRBNIK
    1809  baptism of ahn 25   Michaël Rombol, Parochus Loci   — no parish stated
    1835  marriage, ahn 24×25 Vincentius Rombal, Parochus Loci  labelled Selce
    1838  baptism of ahn 12   Vincentius Rombal, Parochus Loci  Liber Baptizatorum, Selce

**And the same by-name pairs run through all of them** — *Antich Papa*, *Antich
Dudich*, *Lonzarich Franich* — on Krk in 1806 and on the mainland in 1835.

## Two readings, and this archive cannot choose yet

**Either** the family moved from **Krk to the mainland** between 1809 and 1835 —
which would make the tree's birthplace of *Selce* for ahnentafel 24 **wrong**,
and would explain the by-names travelling because the families carried them.

**Or** all four registers are **one parish**, and the `VRBNIK` pencil is a
mislabel — in which case the tree is right and the Dobrinj canon was simply a
visiting officiant, which is entirely ordinary.

**The archive currently asserts both** without noticing they conflict: `/searched`
calls the 1806 scan *Vrbnik baptisms*, while `ancestors.json` gives ahnentafel 24
a birthplace of *Selce*.

## What would settle it

* The **1809 register's own parish heading** — it is the same book series and
  nothing on the page read so far names the parish.
* **Vrbnik's registers**, which FamilySearch holds: if the 1806 entry is in the
  Vrbnik film at this page, the question is closed in one look.
* The **1835 marriage's *origo* columns**, if that register has them — it is a
  prose entry and may not.
