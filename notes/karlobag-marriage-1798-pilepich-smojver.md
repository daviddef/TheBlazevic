# Josephus Pilepich × Maria, daughter of Vincentius, Karlobag, 14 February 1798: the marriage of ahnentafel 52 and 53, probable, with the bride's father named

11 October 2026. Work list rows 26 and 34. Karlobag marriages 1736-1804 (DGS 5481649, waypoint 9RK1-HZK), image 343,
right-hand page, headed «Anno Dni 1798» (the film's image 342 is January 1797 and 345 is May 1799). By 1798 the book
is Latin, tabular (day, month, text), each entry giving the dispensation, the consent and the two spouses.

## The entry

Day 14, the month column reading «Feb[ruarii]» as the page's second entry: «Ex dispensatione Revmi Dni Archidiaconi
Joannis Baptae de [Stefanis] in duabus denuntiationibus, et facta una publicatione, quod nullo impedimento detecto ac
habito mutuo consensu **Josephi viduei [Pilepich?] et Mariae filiae Vincenti [Smojver?]**, in facie Ecclesiae de
licentia Parochi matrimonio conjunxit M.R.D. Andreas Bagetich Localis Capellanus, … testibus praesentibus Michaele
Barach et Josepho Lazarich.»

## What it gives, and what it does not

* **The date, 14 February 1798, is the tree's date for ahnentafel 52 and 53**, and the groom is a Josephus whose
  surname is faintly written and underscored (read as Pilepich); **the bride is «Maria, daughter of Vincentius»** —
  ahnentafel 53 is Oliva *Maria* Smojver, and her father is Vincentius Fabiano Smojver Vukić (ahnentafel 106).
  So the entry fits on name, date and father, and it **would be the first document for the marriage and for the
  link of 53 to 106**.
* **The groom is written «viduei» — a widower.** Josephus Pilipić, b. 1768, was about thirty; a first wife is not in
  the tree and is not in this archive.
* **Why «probable» and not «read»:** the groom's and the bride's SURNAMES are the two words this hand makes least
  legibly (the groom's is overwritten, the bride's is absent or cut), so the identification rests on the day, the
  month, the first names and the father's given name. Re-reading at a higher zoom, or the same entry in a second
  copy, would close it.

## Caution

* The month word was read once at about 6x; the archdeacon's and chaplain's names are as read.
* Witnesses Michael Barach and Josef Lazarich are not otherwise known in this archive.
