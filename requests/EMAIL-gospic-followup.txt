Dear Ms Fajdić,

Thank you for confirming on 14 September that my request was received and queued.
I am not asking you to hurry it. I am writing to NARROW the first of its three
questions, because since then I have read everything on that question that I can
reach, and I now know where the entry is not.

WHAT I HAVE READ SINCE. The FamilySearch films of the Brinje registers
(Hrvatski državni arhiv) cover births and marriages 1888-1899 and deaths
1888-1899. I have now read every page of all three volumes, looking for Kalanj,
Perković and Borić: about 500 people, 48 deaths among them. I found Kalanj and
Borić households in plenty and some Perković households and witnesses, but
**no marriage of a Kalanj to a Perković and no Perković death**, and no entry
that names Klenovica, Senj or America.

SO THE QUESTION IS NOW EXACTLY ONE YEAR-RANGE.

  1a. The MARRIAGE of JOSIP (Josephus) KALANJ, born at Klenovica on 27 August
      1826, and ROZALIJA PERKOVIĆ of Brinje, between about 1840 and 1865. It is
      before the filmed registers begin in 1888, so it can only be in a Brinje
      volume of those years.

  1b. The BAPTISM of ROZALIJA PERKOVIĆ, roughly 1820-1835.

  1c. A plain answer to a plain question: **do the Brinje registers before 1888
      survive, and where are they?** If they are at the parish and not at your
      archive, the name of the parish office would let me write there instead.

The other two questions of 13 September are unchanged: the Otočac baptisms for
Prozor 1875-1895, and the one-man-or-two question about Michael and Nicolaus
Žubrinić. If one volume is quicker to look into than another, I would take any
answer, including "this book does not survive" — I publish negatives as results.

I am glad to pay for copies or for an hour of an archivist's time.
