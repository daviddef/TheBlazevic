# The manifests the trial did not reach — and a third spelling

*Work list row 57, item 2. 16 September 2026. The trial read 106 of 119
`ZUBRINIC` index rows and most of the `ELLIS2` transcripts; these are the ones it
did not open, plus a spelling nobody had tried.*

---

## The spelling question is now closed

The trial's central method finding was that **`ZUBRINICH` returns 191 records and
gives Australia; `ZUBRINIC` returns a different 119 and gives Lika.** The
obvious worry was how many more spellings were out there. Tested today:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Zubrinich** | **191** |
| **Zubrinic** | **119** |
| **Zubrinec** | **1** |
| Subrinic · Xubrinich · Zubrinicz | **0** |

**Three spellings, and no more.** That is worth as much as a find: the surname
sweep is complete and does not need re-running on a hunch.

## SINAC — a parish this archive catalogued and had nobody for

The single `Zubrinec` record is the one that matters.

> **Franc Zubrinec**, b. **1887**, age 24 · ship **Adriatic**, Southampton →
> New York, **1911** · Nationality *Austria, Croatian* ·
> **Residence: «Sinae, Hungary»** · departure relation **(FATHER): Pao?
> Zubrinic** · arrival relation **(BROTHER): Stipan Tonkovic**

**«Sinae» is Sinac**, the village on the Gacka a few kilometres from Otočac.
**`parishes.psv` has carried Sinac since 14 September** — catalogue-verified,
three films, `koha:837243` — and **not one person in this tree has ever been
placed there.** It was a parish the archive had no reason to open.

**It has one now, and the book is in range.** Sinac births are filmed
**1834–1901**, so **Franc's 1887 baptism is inside the film**, and it will name
his father *Pavao* and the house number.

**And he did not travel alone.** His record is `…ELLIS2/003960574` and
**Anton Zubrinic's is `…ELLIS2/003960577`** — three apart, the same ship, the
same year. One man off the manifest page is from **Sinac**; the man beside him is
from **Prozor**.

## The two that were simply unread

> **Eva Zubrinic**, b. **1880**, age 27 · ship **Ultonia**, **departure port
> FIUME** → New York, **1907** · **Residence: Prozor** · arrival relation
> **(BROTHER-IN-LAW): Joso Gomercic**

**Every other departure in this material is Southampton, Rotterdam or
Liverpool.** Eva sails from **Fiume** — from Rijeka itself, three hours from
home, on a Cunard ship running the Adriatic emigrant trade. A woman of
twenty-seven leaving Prozor for a brother-in-law in New York.

> **Anton Zubrinic**, b. **1891**, age 20 · ship **Adriatic**, Southampton,
> **1911** · **Residence: «Prozov, Hungary»** · departure relation
> **(FATHER): Marco Zubrinic** · arrival relation (FRIEND): Foica Grevic

**This is not the Antoni already recorded, and the archive should not merge
them.** The trial read *Antoni Zubrinic*, 20, **1911**, ship **Saint Louis**,
residence **«Otochetz, Austria»**, **father Marko Zubrinic**. Same age, same
year, the same father's name — and **a different ship and a different village**.
Two cousins, or one man indexed twice from two manifest pages. **Written down as
a question.**

## GOMERČIĆ is the second family married in

The trial found **Kostelac**: Mate Žubrinić met by a *nephew* Stipan Kostelas in
1911, Miko met by an *uncle* Josef Kostelac in 1910 — kin by blood, and not in
this archive at all.

**Gomerčić is the same shape, three times over.**

* **1907** — Eva Žubrinić of Prozor is met by **brother-in-law Joso Gomerčić**.
* **1907** — Mjos Žubrinić of Otočac is met by **Jeso Gomerčić**, «acquaintance».
* **1911** — **Mijo Žubrinić is recorded as UNCLE to the passenger Pavdo
  Gomerčić**.

A brother-in-law in one direction and an uncle in the other is **a marriage
between the two families**, not a village acquaintance — and the 1907 record
that calls Jeso an acquaintance is the one to distrust.

**Neither Kostelac nor Gomerčić is in this tree.** Two families, both reached by
blood, both invisible.
