# Otočac baptisms matched to the tree (28 November 2026)

Two Otočac birth books were first copied privately and FamilySearch's name index of them set beside the tree. The index only says where to look: every entry below was then read on the page image at full size, and what is written here is the page. The books were not walked for this, and a person not listed has not been shown to be absent. Many of these openings had been transcribed once before for the surname Žubrinić (`sources/otocac-zubrinic-table.tsv`); the entries here were read again on the page.
Snapshots: `/snapshots/`, one per entry. Disagreements with the tree, and the questions below, are on `/corrections/`. The tree is unchanged.

## Questions for the tree's owner

Nothing here has been merged.

- **Nikolas Žubrinić** — One child entered three times? One Nicolaus was read on 3 August 1847, of Thomas Zubrinich and Joanna née Ratkovich, Dubrava 60. A daughter of Toma and Ivanja née Ratković of the same house was baptised in 1851.
- **Joannis Žubrinić** — His mother, and whether he is entered twice. The entry of 30 May 1848 names the mother Maria née Kostelac. The entries beside it are dated 29 May and 6 June, so no second baptism of that day stands there.
- **Gregorius Žubrinić Xubrinich** — One child entered twice? One entry was read for that day: «Gerga», son of Ive Žubrinić and Martha née Barković, Prozor 49.
- **Magdalena Žubrinić** — Which couple she belongs to. The entry of 9 May 1847 names Philippus Žubrinich and his wife Maria née Begovich, of Prozor 17, and calls neither dead.
- **Pave Talić** — One couple entered twice? Baptisms of 1850 to 1857 at Šumećica 13 name Pave or Paulus Atalić and Maria née Žubrinić.
- **Stipan Kostelac** — One couple entered twice? Baptisms of 1851, 1853 and 1856 at Prozor 27 name Stipan or Stephanus Kostelac and Bare or Barbara née Žubrinić.
- **Franciscus Frane Žubrinić** — One couple entered twice? Baptisms of 1849 to 1855 at Prozor 1 name Frane or Franciscus Žubrinić and Luce or Lucia née Šimatović.
- **Joannes Juan Ivan Ive Žubrinić** — Which Joannes of Dubrava 41? Baptisms of 1848, 1853 and 1854 at Dubrava 41 name Joannes Žubrinić and Maria née Kostelac.

## Where the index differs from the page

- Image 18 of 9RK1-N3B: the index has a «Lucas Mlinarich»; the page has a girl, Lucia.
- The index writes Gerga's mother «Mathia»; the page has Martha.
