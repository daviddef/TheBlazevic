# Vincentius Smojver × Oliva Bacchi, Karlobag, 2 May 1774: the marriage of ahnentafel 106 and 107, with the bride's father already dead — which breaks the tree's Caetano Bacchi of 1779

11 October 2026. Work list rows 24, 26 and 34. Karlobag marriages 1736-1804 (DGS 5481649, waypoint 9RK1-HZK), image 306,
right-hand page; the page's head carries «Anno Dni 1773» and a second heading «Anno Dni 1774» near the top, and this
entry stands below it (day column «2 Maij»). The following entries are of 26 June and 10 July 1774.

## The entry

«2. Maij. Praemissis praemittendis et habito mutuo consensu **Vincentii filii Josephi Smojver, vulgo nuncupati
Vukich** [Vukić], ac **Olivae filiae defuncti Caetani Bacchi**, eos per verba de praesenti solemniter conjunxi,
praesentibus Dno Nicolao Jurich et Francisco [Lukulich?].»

## What it gives

* **The marriage of ahnentafel 106 (Vincentius Fabiano Smojver Vukić, b. 1754) and 107 (Oliva Andreana Bacchi)**: 2 May
  1774, he about twenty, she «daughter of the late Caetanus Bacchi».
* **ahnentafel 106's father is named: JOSEPHUS Smojver (the tree's ahnentafel 212, Josiph Smojver Vukić, b. 1731, d.
  16 May 1776)**, and the surname is written «Smojver, vulgo nuncupati Vukich» — the book itself says Vukić is the
  by-name, the same pattern as the Antić «Papa».
* **ahnentafel 107's father is named, and was dead by May 1774: CAETANUS BACCHI (ahnentafel 214).**
* **Ahnentafel 53 (Oliva Maria Smojver, b. March 1781) is therefore not their first child** — they had been married
  seven years.

## A conflict this opens

The tree gives **Caetano Bacchi (ahnentafel 214) as born 12 January 1779 (marked «maybe»)**, the son of Angelo Bacchi and
Catharina (ahnentafel 428 and 429). A man born in 1779 cannot have been the dead father of a bride in 1774. **The 1779
baptism in the Karlobag index is a different, later Caetano, and the tree has attached the wrong one** — and with him
ahnentafel 428 and 429, whose only support was that baptism. The marriage entry gives no father for the elder
Caetanus, so ahnentafel 428 and 429 revert to «not known». On /corrections.

## Caution

* The bride's surname «Bacchi» is as read (the final letters are cut by a line end); the witnesses are read at one
  zoom; «Vukich» is the word after «nuncupati» (read at about 6x).
* The year 1774 is read from the page heading above the entry; the entries below it carry 26 June and 10 July.

## Added later the same day: the elder Caetanus Bacchi was dead by October 1760

Walking the marriage book backward for another search (image 293, left-hand page, year-heading «Anno Domini 1760»),
the entry of **20 October 1760** reads, after «Idem eadem die»: «matrimonialiter copulavi **Paulum Perpich et Annam
viduam Caetani Bachi**. Praesentibus testibus Joanne Mat[hosich] ac Petro Bovandich.» So a Caetanus Bacchi had **died
before 20 October 1760**, leaving a widow ANNA who then married Paulus Perpich — a better fix on his death than the
«before 2 May 1774» of the 1774 entry, and nothing like a man born in 1779.

* It is **probably** the same Caetanus: Bacchi is a rare surname in this book and the 1774 bride's father is a dead
  Caetanus Bacchi. NOT PROVED, because neither entry names the other, and Oliva is not called Anna's daughter anywhere.
* If Oliva was Anna's daughter she was born before the father's death, so by about 1760 at the latest; she was married in 1774.
* Perpich is spelt «Cerpich»/«Perpich» in the hand and was read at one zoom (the line is clear).

## Added later the same day: the elder Caetanus Bacchi alive in 1753–55, with a wife Anna and a son Silvestro

Reading the Karlobag baptism book for 1750–1759 for Oliva's own baptism (Karlobag births 1691-1804, DGS 5481648, waypoint
9RK1-3Y9, book pages 200–241; the viewer helper's image numbers 112–135) turned up the family instead:

* **18 March 1753** (book page 211, helper image 117 right): at the baptism of a daughter of Petar Tomljenovich, «bihu
  kumi **Caietan Bachi, svojom xenom Annom**» — Caetanus Bacchi as sponsor with his wife Anna.
* **27 May 1753** (page 212, image 118 left) and **late October 1755** (page 221, image 123 left, the line partly cut by the
  page edge): «Caietan Bachi y Anne» again among the sponsors.
* **2–3 January 1754** (page 215, image 119 right, the entry after 1 January): «krstih **Antola [Antonu?] Sylvestra,
  sina Gajetana y Anne Bachi**», sponsors «Jose y Venera Dur[…]ch» — a **son, Sylvester, of Caetanus and Anna Bacchi**.
  (The 18th-century hand writes the surname «Bachi»; the index has none of these.)

So **Caetanus Bacchi was alive from March 1753 to at least October 1755 and dead by 20 October 1760, with a wife Anna** —
the same Anna who is his widow in 1760. Together with the 1774 marriage this makes a consistent family: Caetanus and
Anna, a son Sylvester (b. January 1754), a daughter Oliva (married 1774, so born before about 1759). **Oliva's own baptism
is not on the pages read** — 1750 to about the end of August 1759 (flaps of cardboard and a loose slip hide parts of book
pages 226–227 and 235 in some frames; the same pages show in the neighbouring frames; helper images 124 and 125 are the
same pages twice). She may be older than 1750 (baptised 1745–49, unread) or younger than the pages read, or
baptised elsewhere. Read at one zoom, names in the Croatian hand of 1750–57 and the Latin of the later pages.

## Added later the same day: the marriage of Caetanus Bacchi and Anna Lukatelli, 4 February 1753 — and a correction to the paragraph above

Karlobag marriages 1736-1804 (DGS 5481649, waypoint 9RK1-HZK), image 287 right-hand page (the heading «Anno Domini 1753»
opens at 15 January; the entries run 15 January, 23 January, 4 February): «**Die 4ta Febr:** … *Idem eadem die
matrimonialiter copulavi ad faciem Ecclesiae* **Cajetanum Bachi et Annam filiam defuncti Alexii [Lukatelli]**.
Praesentibus Wolffgango Suvich ac Joanne Radkovich.» The father's surname is read from the full-page view
(«Lukatelli», a Karlobag surname that fills the book) and the day as the 4th at about 6x.

* **Caetanus Bacchi's marriage: 4 February 1753, to Anna, daughter of the late Alexius Lukatelli.**
* It makes the 1753 sponsor «Caietan Bachi, svojom xenom Annom» (March 1753) a newly married couple, and the son Silvester of
  January 1754 a child born eleven months after the wedding (no earlier child of theirs was seen).
* He appears in the same book as a **witness** at marriages of **10 February 1754** (Joannes Basletich × Lucia Muhvach) and
  **30 January 1755** (Michael Dieich × Matthia, widow of Jacobus Vorban) — an adult man living at Karlobag.
* **Correction to the baptism paragraph above:** I wrote that Oliva could be older than 1750. If she is Anna's daughter
  she was born **after the wedding of February 1753 and, because Silvester was born in January 1754, from about
  October 1754 and before about 1760** — inside the pages I read for her (1754 to August 1759), where she was not seen at one
  zoom, or in September 1759 – 1760, unread. She was then **14–19 at her wedding in 1774**. A child of an earlier marriage
  of Caetanus is not excluded and would be older than 1753; none is known.
* One entry looked like a second marriage and is not: on 12 August 1753 (image 288) the clerk wrote «Caietanum Bachi» and
  struck it through before «Nicolaum Braikovich et Dorotheam filiam Marci Stoiavich».

## Uroda leads from the same pages

«Mathiam Kriskovich et Magdalenam filiam **Dominici Uroda**» married on 5 February 1755 (image 289 right), and «Jacobus Uroda»
is a witness on 28 July 1754 (image 289 left); the November 1767 bride of the entry noted on the work list was also
«filiam defuncti Dominici Uroda». **A Dominicus Uroda, dead by 1767, with at least two daughters, is a Karlobag man of the
right age to be a relative of Georgius Uroda (ahnentafel 108)** — a lead, not a link: nothing read says Georgius is his son.

## Dominicus «Dume» Uroda was a regular marriage witness, 1744-1749

Reading the marriage book back to January 1742 (images 280-286) for the Bacchi and Uroda names found no Bacchi (the 1753 wedding is the
first) and no Uroda groom or bride, but **«Dume Uroda» / «Dominico Uroda» witnesses marriages again and again between 1744 and 1749**:
in the Croatian-language pages «Dume Uroda» (January and February 1744, January, February and June 1745, 1746, January 1747) and, when the
book turns to Latin in April 1748, «Dominico Uroda» (8 May, 5 and 7 October and 17 November 1748), roughly ten entries I counted by eye.
So **Dominicus Uroda was an adult, settled Karlobag man from at least 1744 and dead by November 1767** (his daughter Maria's
marriage then calls him «defuncti»), and his daughter Magdalena married in 1755. **He is the right age to be the father of Georgius
Uroda (ahnentafel 108, b. about 1745-55) or of an older Georgius** — but not a word read says so, and Georgius's own baptism
and marriage are still unfound. A baptism of a «Georgius» to Dominicus Uroda and a wife in 1745-55 would settle it; it was not looked for in
the 1750-59 baptism pages read earlier except by eye for Bacchi, so **a targeted re-read of those pages for Uroda is the next test.**

**Narrowing the Uroda marriage (index, 11 October 2026).** The FamilySearch index already lists a **Dominicus**, son of Georgius Uroda and Maria,
baptised **6 May 1764** — the first known child. A boy named Dominicus at the first-born position fits the habit of naming a son
after the father's father, which supports Dominicus «Dume» Uroda as Georgius's father (still not proved). It also means **the marriage of Georgius and Maria
falls before about August 1763**, not «about 1771-76» as the work list first assumed — and the marriage book read for 1742-1776 shows no such entry, so it is
either on one of the crossed-through or unread pages (1736-41; 302 left; 305 right), hiding behind a spelling of «Uroda» not recognised at one zoom, or
outside Karlobag (the bride's parish). The 1756-1763 pages (images 290 right to 298) are the ones to re-read slowly.

**Re-read of 1756-1763 (same day).** Images 290 right to 295 right (January 1756 to October 1763) were read again slowly, both pages of each opening: **no Uroda groom
or bride, and no «Georgius» marrying a Maria**. Together with 1742-1755 earlier the same day, **the Karlobag marriage book holds no Georgius
Uroda wedding from January 1742 to October 1763**; the unread parts are 1736-41 (images 278-279), the last weeks of 1763, and the two crossed-through pages.
**CORRECTION (same day, after the page itself was captured).** An earlier version of this paragraph said Georgius Uroda's death entry of 1791 gives his age as 72, so he
was born about 1719 and Maria was probably a second wife. **That was wrong.** The «annorum 72, piscator» is the death entry of **ahnentafel 54** (Georgius, d. 1846); the 1791 entry
for the *Dominus* Georgius Uroda (ahnentafel 108) **carries no age at all** — see the snapshot `karlobag-death-1791-uroda`. So **nothing is known of ahnentafel 108's age, and the
«second marriage» guess has no support.** What stands is the narrowing from the baptisms: the first known child, Dominicus, was baptised 6 May 1764, so the marriage was before about August 1763, and the
marriage book read for 1742-1776 shows no such entry — so it is behind an unrecognised spelling, on a page not read, or **outside Karlobag** (the bride's parish).

* **6 May 1764 (image 151 left, book page 270):** the first child is **Joannes Dominicus** (the index has «Dominicum»), godparents **Antonio Barach of Šibenik and Vincentia, wife of Joannes Margvart** — [snapshot](https://daviddef.github.io/TheBlazevic/snapshots/#karlobag-baptism-1764-dominicus-uroda).

## Karlobag births read entry by entry, 1750 – June 1752 (images 112–116), 11 October 2026

Both pages of images 112, 113, 114, 115 and 116 were read in half-page strips at about 2x (January 1750 to 9 June 1752). The baptisms are in Croatian until about 1753.
**No Uroda appears as a PARENT on any of them.** Georgius Uroda is not baptised there and no baptism of a child of a Dominicus or a Georgius Uroda falls in these two and a half years. What does appear is a **widow, Jerolima ("Gjerolima udova … Dume Uroda"), godmother again and again** — 23 April 1750, 25 April 1751, 21 March and 3 April 1752 — and on **12 March 1752 a «Jure Uroda» (Georgius) is godfather beside her**. Snapshots: [karlobag-baptism-1750-04-jerolima-uroda](https://daviddef.github.io/TheBlazevic/snapshots/#karlobag-baptism-1750-04-jerolima-uroda); [karlobag-baptism-1751-04-jerolima-uroda](https://daviddef.github.io/TheBlazevic/snapshots/#karlobag-baptism-1751-04-jerolima-uroda); [karlobag-baptism-1752-03-12-jure-uroda](https://daviddef.github.io/TheBlazevic/snapshots/#karlobag-baptism-1752-03-12-jure-uroda); [karlobag-baptism-1752-03-21-dume-uroda](https://daviddef.github.io/TheBlazevic/snapshots/#karlobag-baptism-1752-03-21-dume-uroda); [karlobag-baptism-1752-04-03-jure-uroda](https://daviddef.github.io/TheBlazevic/snapshots/#karlobag-baptism-1752-04-03-jure-uroda).

* **Dume (Dominicus) Uroda was dead by April 1750.** This archive had him as a regular marriage witness 1744–1749 and as «defuncti» only in 1755 and 1767. The widow's title at the first baptism of 1750 shortens that to a death between about the last witness entry (1749) and 23 April 1750. (His death entry was not looked for.)
* **A Jure Uroda is an adult Karlobag man in 1752 and stands with Dume's widow.** That fits Georgius (ahnentafel 108) being Dume's son, and it fits nothing else seen. It is **not proof**: Jure is the commonest given name in the book, and the only link is that two godparents of one child share a surname.
* **It also changes the chronology.** If this Jure is ahnentafel 108 he was an adult by 1752 (a godparent is at least a teenager, probably older), so he was **born no later than about 1735**, which makes Maria (his wife by 1763) a **second** or late marriage, or him already about 30 at the first known child in 1764. The 1791 death entry gives no age; ahnentafel 54's father could be much older than the 1745–55 birth I had assumed.
* **Unread:** images 117–143 (June 1752 – 1762) were read only for Bacchi; 100–111 (before 1750) not at all.

## Karlobag births read back from October 1749 to November 1744 (images 98–111), 11 October 2026 (night)

Both pages of images 98–111 were read in half-page strips at about 2x (the hand is large and legible, Croatian). **The session ended in the middle of the next stretch (images 97 and 96 were being opened and were NOT read).**
* **No baptism of a Georgius/Jure Uroda, and no Uroda parent other than Dume, in these fifty-one months.**
* **Dume (Dominicus) Uroda and his wife Jerolina are the parish's commonest godparents** — «bihu kumi Dume Uroda i Jerolima xena Dume Uroda» turns up on almost every page from 1745 to 1749, and «Dominik Uroda» as kum in 1746 and 1748. So a Uroda in a godparent line is NOT evidence of kinship; it is the usual sound of this parish. Dume is still «xena Dume» (Jerolina his wife, not widow) in December 1748; **«udova» first in April 1750**, so he died between those dates.
* **One baptism of their own child: 11 August 1745, image 100 left, «Dume urode i xene Jerolime» — a daughter, name read as Marta Franceskina.** Snapshot [karlobag-baptism-1745-08-marta-uroda](https://daviddef.github.io/TheBlazevic/snapshots/#karlobag-baptism-1745-08-marta-uroda).
* **What this does to the «Jure Uroda» of 1752:** he is not Dume and Jerolina's baby baptised in 1744–49, and nothing here says he is their son either; if he was born before November 1744 his baptism is earlier still (images before 98).

### Continued: images 94–97 (June 1743 – November 1744), read the same night
* **Dume Uroda and Jerolina again serve as godparents** all through 1743–44 («Dume urode … Jerolina xena Dume urode»).
* **A SON of theirs is baptised on 10 May 1744** (image 96 left, book page 170): «Korstih sina Dume urode i njegove žene Jerolime imenom [Gratiova?]». The name is not read. * **Not yet read: images before 94 (before June 1743).** The next stretch is where a Jure Uroda baptism of about 1725–40 could be.

### Images 90–93 (January 1742 – June 1743), read the same night — a possible baptism of a Georgius, son of Dume Uroda
* Dume Uroda is again a godparent through 1742–43 («Dume urode» as kum 14 February 1742, 14 October 1742; «Jerolima xena Dume urode» in January 1743).
* **Image 91, right page, last entry (a 4th of a month in mid-1742): «Korstih sina Dume urode i njegove xene … imenom [Cvidora?] Juria.»** A son of Dume Uroda, and the name ends in what I read as **Juria** — Jurja, Georgius. [Snapshot](https://daviddef.github.io/TheBlazevic/snapshots/#karlobag-baptism-1742-son-of-dume-uroda). The first word of the name is unclear (it could be an Isidor-type name, or a second name), and the month is a doubtful reading, so **this is a lead, not an identification.**
* **If it is Georgius, it fits everything known:** born mid-1742; father Dume (Dominicus) — whence his own first son **Dominicus** in 1764; a young man of 21 in 1763 and 22 at his first child; dead in 1791 at about 49 (the 1791 entry gives no age). Dume and Jerolina would then have a son in 1742, another in May 1744 and a daughter in August 1745. (Magdalena, who married in 1755, and Maria, who married in 1767, are daughters of a Dominicus Uroda too, but whether of this Dume is not established.)
* **What it does NOT fit is the «Jure Uroda» godfather of 12 March 1752**, who would then be a ten-year-old; that entry must be a different, older Jure (or misread).
* **To close it:** read the line again at higher zoom (the tiles go no further than the image's own resolution, so the month may stay uncertain), and look for other mentions of this son (a godparent entry, a marriage, the 1764 godparents Antonio Barach and Vincentia Margvart).

### Re-read of the 1742 line at higher magnification (11 October 2026, late)
At about 1.3x the entry reads: «Na 4: [month: Ajmila/Apmila — NOT read] 1742 · [K]orstih **sina Dume urode** i njegove xene … imenom **[Gvidora / Evidora] Juria [Furia]**, bi kum Milo Bulinc … a kuma Marija xena Martina …». **«Sina Dume urode» — a son of Dume Uroda — is clear**; the child's name is **two words, the second read as Juria/Furia, the first as Gvidora/Evidora**, neither certain. The month cannot be April (the entry follows 3 and 4 June 1742 on the same page) and is not read. **So the lead stands at «a son of Dume Uroda, baptised in the second half of 1742, whose name may end in Jurja»; it is NOT shown to be Georgius.** A name like «Guido Jure» would be unusual in this parish, which is itself a reason for caution.
* The FamilySearch index lists none of Dume's children (it knows Uroda baptisms in Karlobag only for 1733 — Antona, daughter of Sime Uroda and Eva — and from 1764); so the 1742, 1744 and 1745 baptisms are unindexed, and no index will confirm them. A **Pompeo/Pompio Uroda, husband of Maria Anna Bachi,** also has children in the index (1760s–70s): a Bacchi marriage into an Uroda family, noted, not followed.

* **So Dume and Eva: Antona 1733 (if «Sime» is Dume), a child 1734, Franica December 1739; then Jerolina is his wife (1742, 1744, 1745).** The 1733 baptism puts Dume's marriage to Eva at 1732 or earlier, so he was born about 1700–1710 and was about 40–50 when he died by April 1750. A Georgius born in 1742 would be a child of the second marriage, a generation after the first children, which is odd but possible.
* **No other Uroda parent in images 56–74.** The index's Antona is the only 1733 Uroda baptism; a «Sime Uroda» is not seen as a separate person anywhere else in the book.
* **Not resolved by this stretch:** Georgius's baptism, Dume's marriage (Karlobag's marriage book), Dume's own baptism and parents (before 1733), or a Sime who is distinct from Dume.

### Items 1–5 of the follow-up list, 11 October 2026 (late night)

**1. Dume's own baptism — images 12–17 (1706–1712) and 52–55 (1729–1732) read; nothing found, and a reason to doubt the method.** Both pages of each, in half-page strips at about 2x. Image map: 12 = Aug 1706 – Jan 1707 (book page 8–9), 13 = Nov–Dec 1707 on the left and 1706 on the right (book pages 18–19, out of order), 14 = Nov 1709 – Aug 1710, 15 = Sep 1710 – Apr 1711, 16 = May 1711 – Jan 1712, 17 = Jan – Oct 1712; 45 = 1724, 52 = Oct 1729 – Jan 1731 (book pages 92–93), 53 = 1730, 54 = Jan–Jun 1732, 55 = Jun – Dec 1732; image 18 ≈ 1712, 28 ≈ 1714, 38 ≈ 1721. **No Uroda parent in any of them.** In the 1706–1712 pages the families carry patronymic surnames (-ović, -ić) and there is no «Uroda» at all; the name first turns up in the 1730s as a **by-name** («Dume urode alliti Horaca [Kovaca?]», 1734). **So a baptism of Dume would be written under a different surname, and a blind read for «Uroda» in the older images cannot find it.** Not read: images 1–11 (1691–1706), 18–51 (1712–1729).

**2. The marriage books — Karlobag 1736–44 (images 278–281).** The marriage book starts on 1 February 1736, so **Dume's marriage to Eva (before January 1733) is not in it**, and his marriage to Jerolina (between December 1739 and mid-1742) does not turn up in 1740–42 either (read at about 2x; a probable negative). **What does turn up is this: on 7 June 1739 Martin Svjata married «Simonu pokoi[nog] Dume urode od Šibenika» — Simona, daughter of the late DUME URODA OF ŠIBENIK**, witnesses Tavia Kovačević and Bartul Mišić. [Snapshot](https://daviddef.github.io/TheBlazevic/snapshots/#karlobag-marriage-1739-06-svjata-simona-uroda-sibenik). So there was a **second Dume Uroda, from Šibenik in Dalmatia, who was already dead in 1739** while the Dume with Eva and Jerolina was alive and baptising children. That is the first evidence of **where the name came from**, and it points to Dalmatia. Dume the living is a witness at weddings on 15 and 17 Feb 1744 («Dume Uroda»). **Whether the dead Dume of Šibenik is the living Dume's father, an uncle, or a namesake is not established**; Simona would be his sister if he is the father.

**3. Dume's death — not in a filmed book.** Karlobag's filmed volumes are births 1691–1804, marriages 1736–1804, deaths **1774**–1804 (+ the 1804–61 books) and a Stanje duša (church census, 122 images, a nineteenth-century table). **There is no death book before 1774, so his death entry cannot be found there.** Bounds from the births: alive in December 1748 («xena Dume»), «udova … Dume Uroda» from April 1750.

**4. Pompeo/Giampaolo Uroda × Maria Anna Bachi — a Dalmatian family, not a Karlobag one.** The FamilySearch index has the couple's baptisms at **Drniš (Šibenik district)**: Josepha, 13 March 1770 (father «Janpao», mother Marana Bachi); Dominica Oliva, 5 April 1771 («Ganpas», Mariana Baich); Julia Magdalena, 26 August 1778 («Pompio», Maria Anna Bachi); Joseph, 23 October 1780 («Tomaso», Maria Anna Bauhi). The four fathers' names are indexers' readings of what is probably one man and one wife. **Nothing links them to the Bacchi of Karlobag**; the Bachi marriage is Dalmatian. Other Uroda clusters in the index: **Betina, Šibenik** (Simeone Uroda × Giovanna Barran/Magapin/Majazin, children 1835–1854; Antonio × Anna 1849; Giovanni × Chetanina Magapin 1850; Pietro «Urodan» × Domenica Cucia 1829). **«Sime» is the commonest Uroda given name at Betina**, which makes the «Sime Uroda» of the 1733 index entry look like the same family type as Dume of Šibenik. The reading of the Karlobag 1733 line (Sime or Dume) is still not settled.

**5. The 1764 godparents — ordinary Karlobag households.** **Barach** is a large Karlobag family (Martin, Jure, Michael, Jacob, Georgius; baptisms from 1751 to 1825). **Margvart:** the index shows one household, **Joannes Margvart × Vincentia**, baptising a daughter on 7 November 1761 — so Vincentia Margvart is a married woman of the town, not an Uroda relation. **Neither names Maria's maiden name, but godparents are often the wife's kin, so Barach or Margvart are candidate surnames for Maria (ahnentafel 109)**; nothing proves it.

**What the five items did to the Uroda picture: there are at least six «Uroda» groups, and they must not be merged** — see the work list (row 81).

### The Drniš originals opened (11 October 2026, night): a soldier's household, one man in four index spellings
The Drniš baptisms of the «Pompeo × Bachi» family were opened on FamilySearch rather than taken from the index. **The father is a soldier.** [1770](https://daviddef.github.io/TheBlazevic/snapshots/#drnis-baptism-1770-josepha-uroda): «Pampao Uroda et Mariana Bachi, Militum …», godparents «omnes Militas»; [1778](https://daviddef.github.io/TheBlazevic/snapshots/#drnis-baptism-1778-julia-magdalena-uroda): «**Caporal** Pompeo Uroda et Maria Anna Bachi», the godfather a *Serdar*; [1780](https://daviddef.github.io/TheBlazevic/snapshots/#drnis-baptism-1780-joseph-uroda): «Domino **Caporal Pompeo** Uroda et Domina Maria Anna Bauchi». **The index's «Tomaso» of 1780 is Pompeo on the page** — so the four fathers of the index are one man, a Dalmatian corporal. This strengthens the picture that this is a **Venetian-Dalmatian garrison family** with no sign of the Karlobag Urode (a Military Frontier town); the only shared thing is the Šibenik district that Dume's «od Šibenika» names. The 1771 Dominica Oliva is still index only.

### Maria's maiden name, Georgius × Maria's marriage, and Dume's baptism — what the last round found (11 October 2026, night)
**Correction first.** An earlier note here says the FamilySearch index lists none of Dume's children. **It does — under the spelling «Urode», which a search for «Uroda» misses:** Franica 17 Dec 1739 (parents «Eve, Dume Urode»), **Giakova 10 May 1744** («Jerolime, Dume Urode»), Marta 11 Aug 1745, and **Lucia 17 Dec 1746**, the last not seen before. **The son of 10 May 1744 is therefore «Giakova» — Jakov — not a Georgius.** The 1742 child, the only one whose name ends «Juria», is still unindexed; Georgius is unchanged as a lead and no more.

**Maria's maiden name: not found.** The six unread baptisms of Georgius and Maria's children were opened and the godparents read — [1767](https://daviddef.github.io/TheBlazevic/snapshots/#karlobag-baptism-1767-petrus-jacobus-uroda) the criminal judge Antonius Dorliga and the wife of Antonius Iurich; [1769](https://daviddef.github.io/TheBlazevic/snapshots/#karlobag-baptism-1769-heva-joanna-uroda) the Chancellor Josephus Kumanović and Sancina, wife of Antonius Dorligo; [1772](https://daviddef.github.io/TheBlazevic/snapshots/#karlobag-baptism-1772-maria-catharina-uroda) Kumanović again, with his wife Joanna; [1773](https://daviddef.github.io/TheBlazevic/snapshots/#karlobag-baptism-1773-maria-heva-uroda) Antonius Pomsick and Aloysia, wife of Josephus Collini; [1780](https://daviddef.github.io/TheBlazevic/snapshots/#karlobag-baptism-1780-antonius-uroda) the standard-bearer Antonius Klein and Catharina, wife of the pro-tribune Joannes Kallinich de Zirfeld; [1784](https://daviddef.github.io/TheBlazevic/snapshots/#karlobag-baptism-1784-dominicus-christianus-uroda) Nicolaus Samanich and Heva, wife of Joannes Butorović. With 1764 (Barach and Margvart) and 1777 (a colonel) that is **no godparent who stands twice but the Chancellor Kumanović, and no sign of kin.** What it does show is the household's standing: **«Dominus Georgius» and «Domina Maria», «Cives» of the town, whom the Chancellor, a judge and army officers stand godparents to.** Maria's maiden name must come from the marriage record, which is not in Karlobag.

**The marriage of Georgius and Maria: not in the index.** A wildcard search of the whole collection (surname «Urod*», marriages 1745–1766) returns baptisms only and no marriage. It is in an unindexed register or outside this collection.

**Dume's baptism: no hit, but a sharper lead.** The 1734 by-name re-read at 3x is **«alliti kovaca» — Kovač, a smith — not «Horaca»**. The Karlobag book of 1706–1712 is full of a **Dujam (Dume) Kovačević**, the commonest godfather, and Simona's marriage of 1739 has a **Tavia Kovačević** as witness. So the working hypothesis is that **Dume Uroda was also called Kovač, and belongs to the Kovačević stock of Karlobag**, whose baptisms would be under that name. That is a lead, not a link; the index (which spells these baptisms in the dative: «Duima …») gives only three Dume baptisms of April 1700 and one of 1706, none Kovačević or Uroda.

**Correction to item 5 above, from re-reading the 1764 line at 3x:** the godfather is «**Antonio Barach è Sibenik**» — a Barach **of Šibenik** — and not an ordinary Karlobag townsman (the other Barach of the town are a large local family). So Georgius and Maria's first child has a godfather from the same Dalmatian town as the dead «Dume Uroda od Šibenika» of 1739. **That is the first thread between Georgius's household and Šibenik**; it is drawn on /lines/ as a dotted join, because a godfather proves a connection and not a descent. It also makes Maria's maiden name **Barach** a stronger candidate than before (a godparent of the wife's family, from her home town) — still only a candidate.

### The lines for Smojver, Pilipić and Bacchi (11 October 2026, night) — and a candidate home for Caetanus Bachi
The index has a JSON interface that returns the place and year of every hit, so each surname was counted by place (exact spelling, since the default search is phonetic and returns 20,000 «Gerkacs»). **Smojver** (522 lines): Cesarica 138 (1810–1858), Karlobag 67 (1768–1861), later scatterings at Vitipolis, Jablanac, Fiume and Senj. **Pilipić:** the name is old at Rijeka/Fiume (1626) and Kastav, big at «Vitipolis», present at Perušić and Pakrac — none shown to touch the Karlobag captain of the port. **Gergacs** (25 lines): 17 of them at Karlobag 1786–1855, the rest scattered — **a Karlobag name, one lane, no second family to be told apart from.** **Luckinić:** twelve exact lines, ten at Karlobag. So Gerkacs and Luckinić get no figure until a second family turns up.

**Bacchi: Pag.** The exact surname «Bachi» has twelve lines at **Pag** (1725–1778) and two at Karlobag; one is **[Caietanus Antonius Gregorius, baptised at Pag on 14 March 1728, son of Dno Angelo Bachi, patrician, and Dna Oliva](https://daviddef.github.io/TheBlazevic/snapshots/#pag-baptism-1728-caietanus-bachi)** — read from the page. **A candidate for the Caetanus Bachi who married Anna Lukatelli at Karlobag on 4 February 1753** (aged 24 if so): the rare given name, the age, the island across the channel, a daughter at Karlobag called Oliva like the Pag mother. **NOT PROVED:** no Karlobag entry names his father or his town. **And the tree's Angelo is not evidence:** he comes only from the Karlobag baptism of **12 January 1779** — [a Caetanus Antonius, son of Angelo and Catharina Bachi, with Maria, wife of Dominus Georgius Uroda, as godmother](https://daviddef.github.io/TheBlazevic/snapshots/#karlobag-baptism-1779-01-caetanus-antonius-bacchi) — a **second Bachi household at Karlobag**, which the tree welded onto the elder Caetanus. So there are two Bachi lines at Karlobag (Caetanus × Anna Lukatelli; Angelo × Catharina) and a Pag family that may stand behind one or both. The Pag mother **Oliva died on 9 December 1730**; Angelo then has three more children by Hijeronima (1732, 1735, 1737).

**Smojver, Paulus.** [A Paulo and Barbara Smojver baptise Vitus at Karlobag on 17 May 1780](https://daviddef.github.io/TheBlazevic/snapshots/#karlobag-baptism-1780-05-vitus-smojver) (1763: Paulus and Ursula) — written without «Vukić», so a second Smojver household in the town, which the lines page keeps apart from the Vukić line of ahnentafel 106 until something joins them.

All three are drawn on **/lines/**.

### Dume's father, the Kovačević lead (11 October 2026, night)
The index's search interface (father's given name «Duim*» and surname «Kovac*») returns a **Duim (Dume) Kovačević, a father at Karlobag from 1697 to 1720**: Marta 20 Dec 1697, Rozana 10 Mar 1699, Petra 12 Dec 1699, Maria 2 Feb 1703, Maria 14 Oct 1705, Ivanica 27 Jun 1713 and Petar 1 Jul 1720 — all indexed under the dative «Duimu/Duima Kovačevicu». **Dume Uroda's by-name is «Kovaca»** (1734), and a **Tavia Kovačević** witnesses the 1739 marriage of Simona, daughter of the late Dume Uroda of Šibenik. **Hypothesis, no more: the dead «Dume Uroda od Šibenika» of 1739 is this Duim Kovačević (a smith who came from Šibenik and was called Kovačević in the Karlobag books), and the living Dume Uroda alias Kovač is his son**, baptised between 1700 and 1712, i.e. in the stretch of the births book whose baptisms the index does not cover. Against it: none of the indexed children is a Dume or a Simona. **Next test:** read Karlobag births 1700–1712 (images 5–17) for a «Duima» son of Duim Kovačević and a Simona of 1710–20, which needs a read of the page, not the index. Also seen: a **Duim Smojver** is a Karlobag father in 1718–1721 (Marta 1718, Jakov 1721) — an older Smojver household, of the by-name-less kind.
