# Milka's first marriage is in an index, and it disagrees with the archive
# about her mother-in-law's name

*20 September 2026, found while correcting the indexed-coverage table. The
record that disproved «no marriage register is indexed» is this family's own.*

---

## The record

**«Hungary, Croatia-Slavonia, Marriages, from 1678 to 1927»** · `1:1:X9D5-DTRW`

> **Zvonimir Vukelić**, b. 1883 — son of **Petar Vukelić** and **Vjekoslava
> Pulanić**
> **Milka Papić**, b. 1886 — daughter of **Joso Papić** and **Tonka Papić**

That is the **Senj marriage of 4 November 1907** — [question 1c](/open-questions/) —
indexed, with **both sets of parents**, in a collection this archive had never
searched because the first coverage probe reported that marriages were not
indexed at all.

And his baptism is in the index too: `1:1:X9DP-3Y7X`, **«Zvonimir Matej Vukelić,
baptised 25 February 1883 at Senj, Petar Vukelić × Lojza Pulanić»** — which
confirms to the day what this archive read off the film, and confirms that
**Lojza and Vjekoslava are one woman**, the Croatian and the German-Latin form
of the same name.

## The disagreement, and it is not settled here

`site/src/data/corrections.json` carries this, from 13 September:

> At **5,812 pixels** the row reads *«Vukelić pl. Petar gostioničar, i †
> Vjekoslava Putnić»* … **Her surname, Putnić or Putinić, is still not
> settled** and is written here as unsettled.

**The index says PULANIĆ, twice, in two different records.** The archive's own
full-resolution reading says **Putnić**. Those are not variant spellings of one
name — *Putnić* and *Pulanić* differ in the middle by a letter the eye can
confuse but the hand cannot: **tn** against **lan**.

**This archive does not settle it in the index's favour, and the reason is
today's other finding.** The same Karlobag index names the wrong mother for
ahnentafel 106 — *Marie* where the register plainly reads *Antonie* — so an
index transcription is not evidence against an image read at 5,812 pixels.
See `notes/karlobag-index-is-wrong-about-ahn-106.md`.

**What the index does contribute is that it is TWO transcriptions, not one**,
taken from two different entries — the 1883 baptism and the 1907 marriage —
and both say *Pulanić*. That is enough to make the question live again and not
enough to answer it.

**What would settle it**: the 1883 baptism at full resolution. The archive has
read the 1907 marriage row and not the baptism, and the baptism is the entry
where her name is written as a bride's rather than as a dead mother's.
