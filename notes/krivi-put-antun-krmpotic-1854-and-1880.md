# Antun Krmpotić: baptised 11 June 1854 at Krivi Put, son of Leo Krmpotić and Tereza née Biondić; married Marija Perpić there on 3 October 1880

17 October 2026. Krivi Put births 1847–1884 (FamilySearch waypoint 9R28-DP8, 382 images) and the Krivi Put marriage register of 1880 (the same parish, image ark 3:1:3QSQ-G99X-R3C). The tree has **Anton Antun Krmpotić** (no dates, «Krivi Put») married to **Marija Prpić**, with children Mile Vatroslav (1881), Anton Frane (1883), Slavka Katarina (1885), Marijan (1888), Anton (1890) and Leon Davorim (1894), and **no parents**. The Senj baptism index gave his children; his own entry was not in it.

## 1. The marriage, 3 October 1880 (the way in)

Krivi Put marriage register, spread of 1880, left and right pages, entry **14**: «1880 Listopada dne 3 — **Antona Krmpotić**, opančar [a cobbler], of Veljun [house number read 157], age **26**, *neoženjen* [unmarried]; and **Marija Perpić**, of Krivi Put, house 117, age **19**, *neudata* [unmarried]». The right-hand page names the parents: the groom's, **«Leo Krmpotić i Tereza rod. Biondić, seljaci»**; the bride's, **«Mate Perpić i Kate rod. Perpić, seljaci»** (peasants); witnesses are named in the next column. *Listopada* is **October** (the index reads it as November).

## 2. The baptism, 11 June 1854

Krivi Put births, image 39 (right-hand page), protocol entry **57**: «**1854 dne 11. Lipnja** Kod B.D.M. od Sniga Karstjen jest **Anton** zakoniti Sin **Leona Kermpotić i Therese rodjene Beondić**. Kumuvahu Mile Kermpotić i Luce Kermpotić — po Mirku Mareš Upraviteljn.» The margin reads «Iz Veljuna N° 132». *Lipnja* is **June** (the index has 11 July).

[Snapshot](https://blazevic.defranceski.com/snapshots/#krivi-put-baptism-1854-antun-krmpotic). The index record is XQM9-NPZD, under «Anton Kermpotić», father «Leopa», mother «Thereze Beondić»; it was found by searching the **spelling Kermpotić**, which the exact-surname search for Krmpotić had missed.

## What it gives

* **Antun Krmpotić's parents are Leo Krmpotić and Tereza Biondić**, and his birth year is 1854 (age 26 in October 1880 agrees). His **wife is Marija Perpić, aged 19 in 1880 (born about 1861), daughter of Mate Perpić and Kata Perpić**; the tree calls her Prpić. Neither Leo nor Tereza is in the tree.
* **His siblings (same parents, from the Krivi Put index):** Maria (baptised 31 January 1858, XQM9-ZT6B), **Mile** (b. 1863, d. 1881, XQM9-DM5J: Antun's first son Mile Vatroslav, b. 1881, is named after him) and Marthinus (b. 8 November 1867, XQM9-NPHZ). The godparents of 1854 are a Mile and a Luca Kermpotić.
* He was a **cobbler** (*opančar*), not a peasant. The village is written **Veljun** both times (houses 132 and 157), which is not the Krivi Put house of his wife (117) and which the archive has not placed.
* The Senj entries of his children (1883–1894) and the Krivi Put marriage now join up: Antun, son of Leo, living away from Krivi Put at Veljun in 1854 and again in 1880.

## Not settled

* «Veljun»: whether the margin word is Veljun (a village in the Karlovac region) or a Krivi Put hamlet; the two house numbers differ (132, 157) and may be misread.
* Leo's own baptism, his wife's parents and the dates of both deaths.
