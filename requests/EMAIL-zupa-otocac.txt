I am researching the family of my grandmother, HEDVIGA "SEKA" BLAŽEVIĆ, born at
Senj in 1926. Her father's mother was TEREZA ŽUBRINIĆ, born at Otočac on
29 November 1867. The work is published, free and without advertising, at:

    https://blazevic.defranceski.com/

Anything you send would be published there in full and credited. A negative
answer is worth having too, and I publish those as results.

WHAT I HAVE ALREADY READ. Tereza's parents were NIKOLA (Nicolaus) ŽUBRINIĆ,
born at Otočac on 24 January 1834, and ANA, his wife. In your baptismal
register, read from the filmed copy, they are at ŠUMEĆICA No. 2:

    3 July 1856      Stjepan — mother entered "Anna nata Štrumbulko"
    15 September 1858 Marija — mother entered "Anna Marić"

The same woman is written with two different family names two years apart. I
think the first is a house name (a nadimak) and the second her surname, but that
is an inference from two entries, not something any document says.

===========================================================================
1. Ana's own baptism — the one thing that would name her parents
===========================================================================

Ana had a first child in 1853, so she was born between about 1830 and 1836. The
filmed copy of your baptismal register begins in SEPTEMBER 1834, so a baptism
before that month is on no film I can reach, and I have looked at the first
months after it without finding her.

  1a. The baptism of ANA MARIĆ ("Štrumbulko"), Otočac, about 1830-1836. It
      would give her father's given name, her mother's name, and the house.

===========================================================================
2. Their marriage
===========================================================================

  2a. The marriage of NIKOLA ŽUBRINIĆ and ANA MARIĆ, about 1850-1852. I cannot
      find it in any index, and the marriage register on film for your parish
      begins in 1859. It would name both sets of parents.

===========================================================================
3. If it survives
===========================================================================

  3a. A status animarum (stanje duša) for Otočac after 1846, or any household
      list that shows ŠUMEĆICA No. 2. It would show who lived there and when
      they were born, and whether the name Štrumbulko was kept beside Marić.

===========================================================================
4. A second family, an earlier generation: the Kraljić (Kraljich) brothers
===========================================================================

I have also read your Status animarum on film (images 76 and 86, printed
numbers 319 and 329). Image 76, house No. 40, lists a household of GEORGIUS
KRALJICH, and in it, each with a birth date and the words «filius defti
Jacobi» (son of the late Jacobus):

    NICOLAUS KRALJICH   «29 9bris 1807»   (I read the month as November; the
                                           family tree I inherited says
                                           29 September)
    STEPHANUS KRALJICH  «7 7bris 1817»    (7 September)

and, between them, a MATTHAEUS «5 Febr 1812» whose father's name the page
leaves to ditto marks. Their lines are struck through and carried to house
No. 53 (image 86), the household of MATTHIAS DUJMOVIĆ (b. 31 January 1768),
where the page enters «Nicolaus adopt[atus] Kraljić ex N° 40» and «Stephanus
frater Nicolai», with Nicolaus's children Georgius (19 April 1836), Petronilla
(1838) and Vincentia (12 March 1840), and two wives: «Felicitas uxor Nicolai
nata Banich» and, on the earlier page, «Anna uxor Nicolai» (5 September 1809).
Georgius's baptism is on the film. The baptisms of the three brothers are not:
your baptismal register on film begins in September 1834.

  4a. The baptism of NICOLAUS, 1807 (29 November, or 29 September if I have
      misread the month). It would name his father Jacobus and his mother.
  4b. The baptisms of MATTHAEUS (5 February 1812) and STEPHANUS (7 September
      1817), and of FRANCISCA (about 1820, a date I have only from a tree),
      to see whether one couple had them all.
  4c. The marriage of JACOBUS KRALJIĆ and his wife before 1807. Your death
      register has, in 1821, «Maria uxor Jacobi Kralich», aged 36 (6 February,
      image 264) and «Jacobus Kralich», aged 41, who died «sine Sacramentis
      ob negligentiam Domesticorum» (1 August, image 266). Those years carry no
      village or house; I should be glad to know whether they were of Prozor
      No. 40, and Maria's maiden name.
  4d. The adoption of NICOLAUS and STEPHANUS into the Dujmović house
      (about 1820-1830?), if a note of it was kept.
  4e. The marriage or marriages of NICOLAUS KRALJIĆ, about 1830-1840: to ANNA
      née ŽUBRINIĆ (born 5 September 1809; the baptism of their son Georgius,
      19 April 1836, Prozor No. 40, names her; your death register has «Anna
      consors Nicolai Kraljić», aged 33, on 27 April 1843, image 140), and,
      after 1843, to FELICITAS née BANIĆ, whom the census enters at house
      No. 53. The filmed marriage register (from 1859) cannot show them.
  4f. The burial of NICOLAUS KRALJIĆ himself. The family tree I inherited has
      1845, but I find no such entry in your death register of 1843-1858 on
      film (I read it whole to its last page, «Explicit Anni 1858»), so he
      may have died later or elsewhere; the census does not say.

I am not asking blind about the books: the film shows the registers were kept
carefully, with the village and house number in the margin of each entry.

Whatever you can say, including that a book does not survive or is held
elsewhere, would help — and if the originals are held by the Državni arhiv u
Gospiću or by the diocese rather than the parish, I would be grateful to be told
where to write.

Thank you for your time, and for keeping these books.
