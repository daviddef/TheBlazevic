1. RIJEKA, 1892 — a birth in your own city that I have never been able to place
===========================================================================

LJUBOMIR "LJUBO" BLAŽEVIĆ, my great-grandfather, was born on 1 MARCH 1892 at
RIJEKA. He married Milka Papić at Senj on 8 December 1920 (Senj marriages,
p. 366, entry 41) and died at Senj in 1966.

His parents were JURAJ "JURKO" BLAŽEVIĆ, a seljak of SMOKVICA KRMPOTSKA house
114, and TEREZA née ŽUBRINIĆ, born at Otočac on 29 November 1867. Their other
children were all baptised in the parish of Sv. Jakov Krmpote — Anka (1893),
Marijan (12 April 1895) and Ruža (1897).

  1a. The BAPTISMAL ENTRY for Ljubomir Blažević, Rijeka, 1 March 1892 —
      in whichever Rijeka parish it falls.

This is the one I would most like. **A Krmpote peasant household had a child in
Rijeka in 1892 and then went home** — the next child is baptised back at Krmpote
in 1893. The entry would give the address, and the address would explain why.

I have never searched Rijeka's holdings at all; I do not know which parish, and
I would be grateful even for a direction.

2. SENJ — three deaths, all after the filmed register stops in 1907
===========================================================================

The Senj death register on film runs 1859-1907. All three of these fall after it.

  2a. MILKA LUCIJA PAPIĆ, born Senj 9 October 1886, died 1971.
      **I can give you the page and the line.** Her baptismal entry (Senj
      births, str. 122, 1886) carries a registrar's annotation added in 1971

  2b. ZVONIMIR MATEJ VUKELIĆ, born 13 January 1883, Milka's first husband.

  2c. TEREZA BLAŽEVIĆ née ŽUBRINIĆ, born Otočac 29 November 1867, died at

  3a. The BAPTISM of JURAJ / JURKO BLAŽEVIĆ, Sv. Jakov Krmpote, most likely

Everything above Juraj — five further generations, back to a Mateša Blažević

  3b. His MARRIAGE to TEREZA ŽUBRINIĆ, about 1888.
      **The Krmpote waypoint has no marriage register at all**, and I have now

      marriage is at Krmpote, at Otočac, or somewhere I have not thought of.

  3c. His DEATH, Krmpote or Senj, before December 1920 — his son's marriage

4. A class of book, if any survives
===========================================================================

A STATUS ANIMARUM for SV. JAKOV KRMPOTE, KRIVI PUT, SMOKVICA KRMPOTSKA or SENJ
would answer more than several single entries, because it lists a household
together with dates of birth.

I am not asking blind. A page of one survives in my own family papers, captioned
"Our Lady of the Snow, Krivi Put", running 1897 to 1939. And I have just read the
Karlobag status animarum (HDA 715) right through, which shows what these books
give: a date of birth to the day for every soul in the house, and a communion
column for every year.

For SENJ there is a specific question attached. I have read Senj baptisms and
marriages and found that this town's entries record NO HOUSE NUMBER at all,
where the village books give one without hesitation. A Senj status animarum
would tell me whether the households were numbered and simply not carried into
the registers.
