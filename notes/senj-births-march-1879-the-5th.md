# Senj births, 1878–1894: the register for 5 March 1879

Waypoint 9R2D-JWL (Senj births 1878–1894, 269 images), read 24 November 2026: image 30 for the first time (printed Str. 22, entries 44–49, baptised 1 to 22 June 1879). Images 25–29 (entries 15–43) are the earlier readings (`senj-births-february-1879-the-25th.md`, `senj-births-march-1879-the-3rd.md`, `senj-births-march-1879-the-4th.md`); the dates below are from those readings.

## The question

Is any child born on 5 March 1879, or baptised on 5 March 1879, in the Senj register, and is any of them a Perpić, Prpić or Tomljanović of the household at Klaričevac 82?

## What the pages are

The printed Croatian table of the earlier 1879 notes: entries numbered from 1 each year, in order of baptism. Image 30 is Str. 22 (entries 44–49), both pages read at full size. Months: svibnja = May, lipnja = June.

## What was found

- **Baptised on 5 March: none.** The baptisms run 3 March (entry 19), then 11 March (entry 20), 16 March (entry 21) and on.
- **Born on 5 March: none in entries 15–49.** Births of entries 15–43 are in the 3 March and 4 March notes (the nearest to the date are 2 March, entry 20, and 7 March, entry 30; the births of 5 May and 22 May in entries 38–43 are May, not March). Entries 44–49 are born 20, 22, 24 and 28 May and 12 and 16 June, baptised 1, 3, 8, 8, 22 and 22 June.
- **Where the late baptisms stop.** The longest delay in entries 15–49 is entry 30, born 7 March and baptised 23 April (47 days). A child born on 5 March and baptised by 22 June would be 109 days late.
- **No Perpić, Prpić or Tomljanović in entries 44–49.**
  Snapshot: `/snapshots/#senj-births-1879-06-entries-44-49` (image 30); the earlier ones are `#senj-births-1879-02-entries-15-21` to `#senj-births-1879-05-entries-38-43`.

## What this does not show

A birth on 5 March baptised after 22 June would stand at entry 50 or later; those were not read. Not a walk of the register or of 1879: entries 15–49 of images 25–30 only.

## What follows

Nothing new for the Klaričevac household, and no surname or given-name form met that the Atlas catalogue lacks, so nothing was added to the catalogues.
