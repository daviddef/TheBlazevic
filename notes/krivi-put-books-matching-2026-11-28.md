## Questions for the tree's owner

Nothing here has been merged.

- **Kate**. One woman entered twice? Kate Šojat, 19, married the widower Joso Blažević of Mrzli Dol 9 in 1883; a daughter of Joso and Kate died there in 1886; Joso was a widower again in April 1887.
- **Joso Blažević**. How many Josos at Mrzli Dol 9? A Josephus «son of Joannes» and his wife Maria née Blažević have children at Mrzli Dol 9 from 1863 to 1876; Joso of that house, a widower, married in 1883 and again in 1887, aged 40 and 44, as a son of Ivan Blažević and Ane née Prpić.
- **Martin Pavelic**. One couple entered twice? One Martha Pavelić of Martinus and Antonia née Blažević, of Mrzli Dol 23, born 24 January 1875, was read.
- **Antona Blažević**. One couple entered three times? Marriages of 1878 to 1886 and baptisms of 1863 and 1865 name Anton Blažević and Marija née Šojat of Mrzli Dol 6.
- **Ivka Prpic**. One marriage entered twice? On 7 July 1888 Mate Prpić married Joka Prpić, daughter of Jure and Ane.
- **Maria Blažević**. Probably her baptism. A Maria, daughter of Antonius Blažević and Marta née Blažević of Mrzli Dol 9, was baptised in February 1850; the day is blotted.
- **Franjica Blažević**. A candidate baptism. A Francisca, daughter of Nicolaus Blažević and Maria of Mrzli Dol 6, was born on 1 August 1862.
- **Kata Prpic**. Her mother. In 1888 Franjica married Nikola as an unmarried woman, and he was a widower; in 1907 Kata is the daughter of Nikola and the late «Francika Prpić».

Other pairs that look like one couple entered twice: Anton Blažević and Marta of Mrzli Dol 9 (two records); Nicolaus or Nikola Blažević and Maria Pavelić of Mrzli Dol 6 (two); Josip Blažević and Francika (a second record of Josephus Blažević and Francesca Pavelić); Maria Blažević and Toma Pavelić (a second record of the couple of 1884); Martin Blažević and Kata (against Martinus and Catharina Perpić); Dujo Pavelić and Ante Tomljanović (two records, and the marriages of 1879 and 1884 name the same two names).

## Read and not accepted

- A marriage of 1875 between a widowed Joannes Perpić and Lucia Krmpotić is not that of the parents of the children of 1859–61.
- A marriage entered at Krivi Put 27 was not accepted for the tree's Jure Perpić of house 82.
- A death of 27 February 1883 of a Marija Pavelić, four and a half, of Krivi Put 9, is not the tree's Marija born 1880 at Mrzli Dol 14.
- A death of 14 May 1904 of a Joso Prpić, 23, was not accepted for the child of 1880.
- A baptism of 1 January 1851 at Mrzli Dol 12 was not accepted for Ivan Pavelić, whom the tree has born on 2 November 1851 at house 7.
- A baptism of 1847 was not accepted for the Marko Pavelić who married in 1879: his marriage names other parents.

## Not settled

- The surname of Marko Blažević's parents on 29 October 1882 is written over, and the groom's age on 25 November 1883 too; neither is transcribed.
- A faint pencil note dated 1895 on the baptism of Francisca Pavelić (8 September 1877) is not transcribed.
- The baptism of Kata Prpić, born 19 July 1883, has not been read.
