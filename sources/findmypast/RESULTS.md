# What the FindMyPast trial actually produced

**13 September 2026.** Two days of subscription; this is the account of one
working session, written so the second day is not spent repeating the first.

---

## The one thing that changed the Croatian research

**The Kalanji are not a Klenovica family. They are Brinje people.**

Their own baptism said so and nobody had acted on it: at
**Ana Kalanj, Klenovica N° 22, 3 January 1864**, the parents are
*Josephus Kalanj et Rosalia nata Perković ejus uxor,* ***Brinjensis parochiae
ruricolae*** — country people of the parish of **Brinje**, inland over the
Kapela, forty kilometres from the coast. What made anyone re-read the Latin was a
**1910 American passenger manifest** giving a Kalanj's residence as *Brinje*.

Three of this archive's surnames then turned out to meet there — **Kalanj**,
**Perković** and **Borić** — and a **Borić of Brinje married a Papić of Senj**.

**Not one page of the Brinje register has ever been opened**, and Brinje is not
in this archive's gazetteer. → `requests/brinje.md`

## What the trial proved about method

**Post-1906 US manifests carry a last residence and the relative left behind.**
That is what makes them sortable. Seven Kalanj manifests gave **Brinje,
Plaščica, Kurjak and Velgovica** — four places, all Lika, none near each other.
**Kalanj is a regional surname, not one household**, and only Brinje is ours.

**And the spelling is the search.** `Zubrinich` returns 191 records and gives
**Australia**. `Zubrinic` returns a *different* 119 and gives **Lika** —
Šumećica, Otočac, Prozor. Anyone searching one spelling is missing the other
entirely.

**Prozor is emptying between 1907 and 1912** — Eva 1880, Miko 1893, Nikola 1886,
Anton 1891, Mate 1867, Miho — six from one village in five years, with fathers
named **Ivan**, **Sandro** and **Marco**. This archive holds Prozor 50 down to
1874 and **not one Žubrinić born at Prozor between 1875 and 1900**. The manifests
document the generation the archive is missing.

## Negatives, recorded so nobody repeats them

- **FindMyPast holds no Croatian records.** Not one parish register.
- **Nothing on Hedviga's 1948 emigration.** No *Hedviga Blazevic* anywhere; **no
  Defranceski at all in *Passenger Lists Leaving UK 1890–1960***. They went to
  **South Africa**, and this service has neither the departure nor the arrival.
  The 1948 voyage is not here and will not be.
- **The 1932 and later arrivals are FamilySearch-indexed**, giving only
  “Yugoslavia” and no village. **Only the pre-1925 Ellis manifests carry
  residences.**
- **Šestan** (519 records) was left alone deliberately. They are
  [0% connected](../../site/src/pages/buckets.astro) to Hedviga, so confirming
  they are Istrian — which this archive already says — would attach nobody.
- **Perković (2,036), Borić (2,007), Papić (1,042), Prpić (943), Boras (3,445)**
  are too common to harvest in bulk. They need a first name and a year.
