# What came back

Both letters of 13 September 2026 were answered within a day. Recorded here in
full, because a nil return is a result and this archive says so.

---

## Senj — answered 14 September 2026, and it is a nil return with a map

**From:** Denis Rukavina, Državni arhiv u Rijeci, Ispostava/Sabirni centar u Senju
**To:** the letter of 13 September (Sv. Jakov Krmpote; Zvonimir Vukelić 1915;
the Butorac godparents; any status animarum)

> Poštovani,
> **Sabirni centar u Senju nema u svom posjedu arhivsku građu kojom bi se moglo
> udovoljiti Vašem zahtjevu.**
> S istim zahtjevom obratite se **Državnom arhivu u Rijeci**, **Državnom arhivu u
> Gospiću** i **nadležnim Župnim uredima**.
>
> S poštovanjem,
> Denis Rukavina

*"The collection centre at Senj does not hold archival material with which your
request could be met. Apply with the same request to the State Archive in
Rijeka, the State Archive in Gospić, and the competent parish offices."*

**What it settles.** The Senj outpost was the wrong door, and now this archive
knows it rather than assuming it. **Three doors are named in its place**, and one
of them — **the parish offices** — is a route this archive has never tried at
all. The registers for Sv. Jakov Krmpote after 1896 and for Senj after 1907 are
in **parish custody** or at **Rijeka**, and that is now told rather than guessed.

**It does not touch the four plans the film ranges killed.** Those books exist;
this only changes which address holds them.

## Gospić — acknowledged 14 September 2026, and queued

**From:** Marija Fajdić, viši arhivist, Voditelj Odjela, Državni arhiv u Gospiću

> Poštovani,
> **potvrđujemo primitak vašeg zahtjeva.** O njegovu rješavanju bit ćete
> pravodobno obaviješteni kada vaš zahtjev dođe na red za obradu.

*"We confirm receipt of your request. You will be informed in due course when it
comes up for processing."*

A queue acknowledgement, with a named archivist. **Still open**, and the three
asks in it — the Kalanj × Perković marriage at Brinje, Otočac baptisms for
Prozor 1875–1895, and the one-man-or-two question — are unchanged.

## What follows

**Three further letters were written on that instruction and sent on 15
September 2026** — to **Državni arhiv u Rijeci**, and to the **Župni ured** of
Senj and of Sv. Jakov Krmpote (administered from Ledenice, at Klenovica). The
Senj reply named all three doors. The asks are the same ones, unchanged; they
simply have a new address. Copies in `sent/`.

**Nothing has come back from any of the three yet.** When it does — including a
nil return — it goes here, on `/changed` and on `/searched`.
