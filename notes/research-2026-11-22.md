## Caetanus Bachi (row 82), Ana Marić (row 51), Georgius Uroda (row 9): open sources

* **Bachi.** No open source places a Bachi family at Pag, Nin or Karlobag; the Pag nobility of 1783 listed by Granić
  (*Radovi HAZU Zadar* 35, 1993) does not include Bachi, so «patrician» in the 1728 baptism may be an honorific (a hypothesis). The Pag registers are at
  the Zadar State Archive (births 1721–36, marriages 1737–74, **deaths 1740–74**): whether the Pag deaths are filmed is worth checking in the FamilySearch catalogue. The Karlobag death book starts in 1774.
* **Marić / Šumbul.** No Lika text read has Šumbul, Štrumbulko or an Otočac Marić household. The Croatian State Archives hold Otočac births and
  deaths for **1834–1858 only**, so a baptism before September 1834 is in no held book; Matricula does not cover Croatia.
* **Uroda.** A long-established Šibenik family in print: at least three Vroda/Vrodich households in the Dolac baptisms 1686–1699 (Šupuk, *Čakavska rič* IV/2, 1974;
  OCR to be checked before citing), and a second centre at Betina (Juran, *Povijesni prilozi* 22, 2003). The Dolac books are at the Zadar State Archive
  (a **third baptism volume, 1699–1743**, and **deaths 1701–1768**, which would cover the Georgius Vroda who died in 1754). No source names a Georgius baptised 1720–42.
  **Not added to the Atlas catalogue**: Vroda, Vrodich and Urodić are candidate forms that still need a record read by this archive.
