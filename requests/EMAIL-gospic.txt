I am researching the family of my grandmother, HEDVIGA "SEKA" BLAŽEVIĆ, born at
Senj on 3 August 1926 and died at Johannesburg in 2001. The work is published,
free and without advertising, at:

    https://daviddef.github.io/TheBlazevic/

Everything you send would be published there in full, with your shelfmark, and

Three requests follow. Any one of them would be a real help; I do not expect all
three.


===========================================================================
1. BRINJE — the parish I have been ignoring for years
===========================================================================

This is the request I would choose if only one can be answered.

I have treated the KALANJ family as a Klenovica family throughout — I hold 216
Kalanj records, 119 of them at Klenovica. Their own baptismal entry says
otherwise, and I had read the line without acting on it. At the baptism of ANA
KALANJ, Klenovica house no. 22, 3 January 1864, the parents are entered as:

    Josephus Kalanj et Rosalia nata Perković ejus uxor,
    BRINJENSIS PAROCHIAE RURICOLAE

— country people of the parish of BRINJE. My own records independently place
Rozalija Perković at Brinje, and hold a JURE BORIĆ of Brinje whose daughter Ana
(b. 12 February 1885, Brinje) married a PAPIĆ of Senj.

So three of my surnames — Kalanj, Perković and Borić — meet at Brinje, and I
have never opened a single page of that register.

  1a. The MARRIAGE of JOSIP (Josephus) KALANJ, born 27 August 1826 at
      Klenovica, to ROZALIJA PERKOVIĆ of Brinje — roughly 1840-1865. She died
      25 September 1917. This entry would name her household, and it is the one
      I want most.

  1b. The BAPTISM of ROZALIJA PERKOVIĆ herself, roughly 1820-1835.

  1c. Any decade of the Brinje registers, 1850-1890, in which KALANJ, PERKOVIĆ
      and BORIĆ appear together — with HOUSE NUMBERS if the book gives them.
      House numbers have done more for this research than names have.


===========================================================================
2. OTOČAC — the baptisms for PROZOR, 1875-1895
===========================================================================

I hold the household at PROZOR house no. 50 in detail: Michael Žubrinić, born
1826, and his seven children, Nikola 1858, Kata 1860, Georgius 1863, Magdalena
1865, Mate 1867, Ivan 1870, Franjo 1874.

And then my record stops. I have NOT ONE Žubrinić born at Prozor between 1875
and 1900.

American passenger manifests document exactly that missing generation leaving
Prozor, and name their fathers:

    Eva Žubrinić    b. 1880   sailed 1907 from Fiume
    Nikola          b. 1886   sailed 1911   father given as SANDRO Žubrinić
    Anton           b. 1891   sailed 1911   father given as MARCO Žubrinić
    Miko            b. 1893   sailed 1910   father given as IVAN Žubrinić
    Mate            b. 1867   sailed 1911   (this is the Mate born 1867 above)

Only one of those three fathers — Ivan — appears anywhere in my records. So
Prozor held at least two more Žubrinić households than I know of.

  2a. The OTOČAC BAPTISMS covering PROZOR, 1875-1895. One volume, one village,
      twenty years — and it would restore an entire generation at once.

  2b. One entry I can give you an exact date for. A naturalisation petition
      sworn at Brooklyn, New York, in 1939 gives IVAN ŽUBRINIĆ, BORN 30 JUNE
      1888 AT OTOČAC. That is a sworn date, not a ship's estimate, so his
      baptism should be in your register on that day. It would name his
      parents. He is NOT the Ivan born 16 August 1887 at Otočac whom I already
      hold.


===========================================================================
3. OTOČAC / ŠUMEĆICA — one man or two?
===========================================================================

My record gives one couple, MICHAËL ŽUBRINIĆ and MARIA née OREŠKOVIĆ, two sons
forty-six years apart:

    Nicolaus, baptised 24 January 1834 — filius legitimus, Confiniarius ex Pago
    Shumechicza No. 24, son of Michaël Xubrinich et Maria uxor ejus nata
    Oreskovich

    Nikola, born 23 August 1880 — named at his own marriage (Senj, 3 July 1910)
    as son of the late Mate Žubrinić and Marija rodj. Orešković

Either one man married two women both called Maria, or — far likelier — there
were two men of the same name a generation apart and I have merged them.

  3a. A MARRIAGE of a Michaël/Mate Žubrinić to a Maria Orešković at Otočac
      BEFORE 1834, and a second such marriage roughly 1855-1870. If only one
      exists, the merge is real but differently shaped.

  3b. Any decade of the OTOČAC DEATH REGISTER between 1830 and 1860. I have
      six people carrying the bare year "1845" with no day, month, place or
      source, and I need to know when they actually died.

  3c. If any STATUS ANIMARUM volume survives for Otočac or Šumećica, a
      household page would answer more than any single entry — it lists a man,
      his wife and his living children in one line of sight. I know these books
      were kept in the region: a page of one from Our Lady of the Snow, Krivi
      Put, running 1897-1939, survives in my own family papers.


Villages and house numbers already known to me, so any search can be narrow:
