# Three Blažević households at Senj, 1858–1888: nine entries read against the tree (26 November 2026)

After the walk of the births of 1859–1877 (`senj-births-1859-1877-walk.md`), the Blažević entries were checked one by one against the tree, with the baptisms of 1858, the marriages and the deaths read from the archive's private copies of the leaves.
Three statements made in that walk's first write-up did not survive the check and are corrected here.

## What was wrong in the first write-up

1. **«Emericus (1868) is not in the tree».** Wrong. Emericus Joannes Andreas, born 22 November 1868, is the child the tree holds as [Mirka Blažević](/people/mirka-blazevic/), entered as a daughter born in 1869. The death register calls the same child **Mirko**, a man, and its age agrees with the baptism.
2. **«Christophorus is not in the tree».** Wrong. The tree holds [Christophorus](/people/christophorus/), with no surname, under a «Theresia Blažević» listed among the Blažević brothers and sisters.
3. **One «Antonius Blažević, merchant» with two wives in turn.** Not supported. In October 1867 the death register names Anna as wife of an Antonius Blažević, merchant, three months after an Antonius Blažević, merchant, with Francisca née Gosler had a daughter baptised. These read as two men.

The first two came from matching on surname and forename only: one child was in the tree under the other sex, the other with no surname. The death register, searched by the parents, found both.

| 21 January 1866 | marriages 1859–1920, image 61, entry 2 | Joannes Tintor, porter, 25, and «Theresia Blazević», widow, 37, **from Prizna**, living in Senj; her parents «Joannes Caćić confiniarius et Mandalina» · [snapshot](/snapshots/#senj-marriage-1866-01-tintor-theresia-blazevic) |

- The 1866 bride's parents are the parents the tree gives [Theresia Cacic](/people/theresia-cacic/), and the age (37) fits the tree's birth year of 1829. **Prizna, as her place of origin, was not in the tree.**
- The 1858 and 1861 baptisms name the mother as a widow Blažević **born** Čačić. So [Maria](/people/maria-mariam-mariam/) and Christophorus read as Theresia Cacic's children, born after the tree's last child of hers with Gregorius Blažević (1855).
- The tree puts those two under [«Theresia Blažević»](/people/theresia-blazevic/), as if a daughter of the Blažević family, with a partner called «Illegitimate». The entries do not support that. The three entries that name the mother in full agree on forename, married name, maiden name, widowhood and town; the two death entries give no maiden name. **Nothing is merged:** that is for the owner of the tree.
- The baptism of 1858 calls Maria illegitimate; her death entry of 1861 calls her legitimate. Both are copied as written.
- When Gregorius Blažević died has not been read.

## Antonius Blažević, merchant, and Anna née Trbuhović

| Date | Book, image, entry | What the page says |
|---|---|---|
| 22 April 1861 | births 1859–1877, image 55, entry 55 | Georgius Albertus, «Antonius Blažević mercator & Anna nata Tr-/buhović», the mother «graeci rit. non uniti» (Orthodox); baptised 28 April · [snapshot](/snapshots/#senj-births-1859-77-img0055) |
| 14 October 1867 | deaths, image 128, entry 116 | «Georgius Blažević filius legitimus», «Antonius Blažević negotiator et ejus uxor Anna», house 316, aged 7, whooping cough; buried 16 October · [snapshot](/snapshots/#senj-death-1867-10-georgius-blazevic) |

- The tree's «Anna Buhovic» is the end of a surname broken over two lines. The page reads **Trbuhović**, as this archive had already read it on 14 September (`senj-1888-marriages.md`).
- The tree dates [Georgius Jure Albertus](/people/georgius-jure-albertus-blazevic/) 12 April 1861; the page reads **22 April**.
- The tree also holds [a second Georgius](/people/georgius-blazevic/), born 1860, of the same parents, with the same day of death. The births of 1859–1877 hold one baptism for this couple. This reads as one child entered twice; the age of 7 in 1867 would be a year high.

- **Mirko is Emericus.** The parents match, and a birth on 22 November 1868 gives an age of 19 years and 4½ months on 4 April 1888, against «19 5/12» on the page.
- The death of 1872 fits Francisca Helena (born 30 July 1870). The tree holds it under [a separate Francisca Blažević](/people/francisca-blazevic/) with a father «Are Tonius». This reads as one child entered twice.
- The death of 1883 fits Maria Franciska Amalia (she would have been 15 years and 9 months). The tree holds this daughter, [Marija](/people/marija-blazevic-10/), under [Anton Blažević, born 1835](/people/anton-blazevic-2/), and Franica Antonia, and holds the other children under [Antonius «Ante»](/people/antonius-blazevic-3/) and Francisca Gosler. Trade, forenames and children suggest one couple held twice. **Not settled:** no entry read here gives Franjica Antonia's maiden name.

- The death of Gregorius Blažević and of Anna née Trbuhović.
