# Where did Nicolaus Gerkacs (ahnentafel 220, d. 1783 at Karlobag aged 66) come from? An index search, and one coincidence at Mrežnica

17 October 2026. Index only (FamilySearch *Croatia, Church Books*, collection 2040054, persona search); no page opened. Extends [the note on the three direct-line baptisms looked for by name](karlobag-marriage-1774-smojver-bacchi.md) (11 October 2026) and work-list row 54.

**The question.** Nicolaus died 30 October 1783 «in domo filij sui Antonij» aged 66, so he was born about 1717; his son Michael married at Karlobag in 1785. No Gergacs baptism exists at Karlobag before 1779, so he was an incomer.

**What was searched.** The surname in eleven spellings (Gergacs, Gerkacs, Gerkacz, Gergacz, Jerkacs, Gerkach, Gergach, Gergics, Gergich, Gergocz, Geraecs), baptisms 1735–1770, with the father's given name Nicolaus or Nicolai, and with the child's name Antonius, Antonio or Michael, anywhere in the collection. **No baptism of an Antonius or a Michael Gergacs to a father Nicolaus turns up in 1735–1770** (the index is not complete, so this does not mean there is none).

**The one coincidence.** At **Mrežnica** (in the then Modruš-Rijeka district, inland of Ogulin) a **Nicolaus Gergacz with a wife Margarita** has two children indexed:

| baptised | child | parents | index record |
|---|---|---|---|
| 29 November 1740 | Petrus | Nicolaus Gergacz × Margarita *Veiknich* | QKMJ-G4SG |
| 7 July 1745 | Anna | Nicolaus Gergacz × Margarita *Verbanich* | QKMJ-LGTK |

A Nicolaus who is a father in 1740 could well be born about 1717 (he would be 23). **That is all there is.** The wife's surname is written two ways, neither child is Antonius or Michael, and the same register shows another Nicolaus Gergach (father of Joannes, whose son Nicolaus was baptised there on 21 August 1760). **It is a lead, not a link**, and nothing here says the Mrežnica Nicolaus ever went to Karlobag.

**Where it could be tested.** The Mrežnica births, marriages and deaths are on film under waypoint **9R2M-C6D** (the atlas lists Births 1718–1725 and 1728–1761, Marriages 1726–1761, Deaths 1726–59, Births 1762–1774, 588 images; the births of 1766 are around image 510). Latin prose, entries grouped by village. What to look for: further children of Nicolaus and Margarita (Antonius, Michael, born 1746–62), a death of a Nicolaus Gergacz at Mrežnica before about 1760, or a marriage.

**Not the same family.** The index shows many **Gergich** (Kostajnica, Kobaš, Osijek, Našice, Nova Gradiška): a different surname, in Slavonia, and no evidence connects it to the Karlobag Gergacs.

## A look at the Mrežnica film, 17 October 2026 (not a read)

Images 480 (right) and 481 (left) of waypoint 9R2M-C6D were opened (the pages of January 1763; 1763 begins on 481). **The register is hand-written Latin grouped under village headings (Podvenac, Novaki, Vrbovac, Mikalich and others), about a dozen baptisms to a page, in a cramped cursive in which surnames are not reliably legible at page scale.** Nothing was searched for or recorded from them. A read for a Gergacz household would have to start from a village (the index gives none for the 1740 and 1745 entries) and would be a page-by-page walk of 1746–1762 in a hand this hard; **the index, not the pages, is the efficient route, and it has no Antonius or Michael to a Nicolaus**. Left open, not attempted.

Not read: the misbound pages at the end of the 1762–1789 book (the note does not say where), and the book's 1763–1774 pages.

**What this does and does not show.**

* **Nothing links the Mrežnica Nicolaus to Karlobag's Nicolaus Gerkacs.** No Antonius or Michael to a Nicolaus Gergacz turns up in the pages read, and the pages read are about a year and a half of births.
* **Read now, every page: August 1745 to October 1761 (images 216–344) and May to December 1762 (474–479).** Still not read: the winter 1761–62 baptisms that are in neither book as filmed, the misbound pages at the end of the 1762–1789 book, and the births 1763 onward. An Antonius or Michael born to this Nicolaus in 1746–1759 should be in the pages read, and **neither is: no child of any Nicolaus Gergacz after Anna (1745)**. So the Mrežnica Nicolaus either had no more children there, moved away after 1745 (the village changes from Petrakovo Berdo in 1740 to Belaj in 1745), or the Karlobag Antonius and Michael are not his.
* **A Michael Gergach in 1756** (the godfather above) is an adult at Mrežnica eleven years after Anna's baptism: a brother of Nicolaus, a son of another Gergacz, or a relative of the Milchich mother Dorothea. He is a lead for a Gergacz line there, **not for the Karlobag Michael, born about 1762**.
* **A relative, not a child:** the index and these pages show a **Dorothea Gergacz, wife of Joannes Milchich of Vellemerich**, with children baptised 1752, 1754 and 1756, and a **Margarita Gergacz, wife of Joannes Thomasich**, with a child of 1766. Nothing says how they relate to Nicolaus; they show that the surname existed at Mrežnica beyond his household.
* **A different line to test:** the death of a Nicolaus Gergacz at Mrežnica (Deaths 1726–1759 are images about 400–470: image 466 is a death page of 1756) would say whether Nicolaus was still there after 1745.


## The Mrežnica marriages, January 1726 to November 1744, 18 October 2026

**What was read.** The marriages 1726–1761 of waypoint 9R2M-C6D begin at **image 346** (a first page headed «1726»); **images 346–374, every page, both pages of each image, January 1726 to the end of 1744**, were read by eye from sheets at about 0.6 of source size (the marriage hand is much clearer than the births of the 1740s). The aim was Nicolaus Gergacz's marriage to **Margarita** (their children of 1740 and 1745), and any marriage of an Antonius, Michael or other Gergacz.

**Result.**

* **No marriage of a Nicolaus Gergacz (any spelling) to a Margarita, in 1726–1744.** So the couple's marriage, which must be before November 1740 (Petrus's baptism), is **not in the marriage book as filmed**: it was before 1726, or elsewhere, or under a spelling not recognised. If before 1726, **Nicolaus was born in the 1690s–1700s, not about 1717**, which is the age the Karlobag death record gives the Karlobag Nicolaus (d. 1783, aged 66).
* **One near miss, not a match:** **2 February 1733, Vellemerich (image 358, left page)**: «Copulati sunt **Nicolaus Gergich** cum Susana [surname not read], praesent. Joanne Geherich [?] et Margarita Milchich» ([snapshot](/snapshots/#mreznica-marriage-1733-02-nicolaus-gergich-susana)). The surname is written «Gergich», a form that could be a fast **Gergacz**; the bride is **Susana**, not Margarita. It is either another Nicolaus or the same man's **first marriage**, with Margarita a second wife. **Not established.**
* **A different surname to keep apart:** **Gerginchich** / **Gergurich** (Mrežnica, 1730s–1740s: e.g. «Nicolaus Gerginchich et Helena Prakovich», 19 November 1743, image 373; «Michael Gerginchich cum Helena», 1732) is not Gergacz.
* **No Gergacz groom or bride of any other name** in the pages read.

**Continued: the marriages 1745 to the end of the book, October 1761 (images 375–416), every page of every image, read by eye from sheets at about 0.6 of source size.** The book ends at **image 416** (last entries October 1761, then a blank leaf); image 417 is blank and 418 the film target for the deaths. **No Gergacz marriage, in any spelling, in 1745–1761**, so **no marriage of Antonius, Michael or any Gergacz at Mrežnica in 1726–1761**, and none of the Michael Gergach of 1756. The nearest forms are other surnames: **Gerginchich / Gergurich** (many, e.g. «Anna Gerginchich», 1753; «Catharina nata Gerginchick», 1759), **Gergich / Gerchich** («Georgius Gerchich cum Margaritha Tuskan», 2 February 1754, Vellemerich; «Margaritha Gerchich», February 1755), and **Gerkétich/Jerkétich** (1755) — a different family or surnames of the same stem; none is Gergacz. **Limits:** images 413–415 (1760–61) are in a hurried hand and were read less surely; the reading is at reduced scale, not entry by entry at full size; the births of Oct 1761–Apr 1762 and the deaths are separate questions.

**What it adds to the question.** The Mrežnica Nicolaus **married before 1726, or not at Mrežnica**: if before 1726 he is **too old** to be the Karlobag Nicolaus (b. about 1717), which is a reason to doubt the link. A **Nicolaus Gergich who married a Susana in 1733** is the nearest thing in the book to a first marriage of a man of the right age. Nothing ties either to Karlobag.


## The Mrežnica deaths 1726–1759: begun, 18 October 2026

**The book.** Images **419 onward** of waypoint 9R2M-C6D (417 blank, 418 the film target «Matična knjiga umrlih (1726–1759). **Napomena: Nedostaju podaci za god. 1753–1755, 1757!**» — *the film's own note says the data for 1753–1755 and 1757 are missing*), then «Liber Mortuorum ab anno 1726» (image 419, whose first entries are of 1728–29). **Every death is entered with the village, name, age and the cemetery**, in a clean hand, and children and infants are listed; about 30 entries to an image, a year to about 2 images in the 1730s.

**Read: images 419–469, the whole book as filmed, January 1728 to April 1759, every page, by eye** (a year is about 2 images in the 1730s and 1740s, about one image a year in the 1750s). **No Gergacz (any spelling) died in it.** Entries are «obiit [name] Annor circiter [age] SS. Sacramentis munita, sepulta ad S. Petrum / S. Martinum / S. Joannem», under the village heading (Bellai/Belaj, Vellemerich, Pallovichi, Novaki, Zvechai, Petrakovo Berdo…). The nearest forms are **Gerginchich/Gergichich/Gerginich** (several, e.g. «Helena Gerginchichka», 14 October 1748; «Nicolaus Gerginich», February 1756), **Gergovich**, and the village heading **Gerschaki**: other surnames. A run of **war deaths «in bello obijt»** fills 1747–48 (no Gergacz among them). The film has **gaps**: the target says the data for **1753–1755 and 1757** are missing, and the images confirm it (image 465 is early 1752, 466 is early 1756; torn leaves at 466–467, 1756).

**So no death of a Nicolaus Gergacz, of a Margarita Gergacz or of the Michael Gergach of 1756 is on the film from 1728 to April 1759**, apart from the missing years (1753–55, 1757) and the loss at the torn leaves. A Nicolaus alive at Mrežnica in 1745 who died there in 1746–52 or 1758–59 would be in the pages read, and **is not**.

**What this adds.** With the births 1745–1761 and the marriages 1726–1761 also read, **the Mrežnica Nicolaus Gergacz leaves no trace at Mrežnica after the baptism of Anna in July 1745**: no further child, no marriage, no death. He may have died in the missing years, or left the parish after 1745 (the 1740 and 1745 baptisms are from different villages). Leaving is the only reading that would be compatible with a Nicolaus Gerkacs at Karlobag, and **that is a possibility, not a link**: Karlobag's Nicolaus (b. about 1717, d. 1783) has no known birth, and nothing places a Mrežnica Gergacz at Karlobag. The one other new Gergacz is the **Michael Gergach, godfather in 1756**. Limits: read at reduced scale, not entry by entry at full size; the hand of the 1740s is hard.


## The Karlobag side: Antonius Gergacs's marriage, 26 November 1769 (18 October 2026)

**New document.** Karlobag marriages 1736–1804 (DGS 5481649, waypoint 9RK1-HZK), **image 303, left page** (book page 50, «Anno Dni 1769»): «*habito mutuo consensu* **Antonij filij Nicolai Gergacs** *et* **Franciscae filiae Michaelis Berkliacich** *eos solemniter … per verba de praesenti matrimonio conjunxi*», on 26 November 1769, the second entry of the day ([snapshot](/snapshots/#karlobag-marriage-1769-11-antonius-gergacs-francisca-berkliacich)). Found by reading images 301–303 of the marriage book (1767–70) at full size after the index showed no Gergacs record at Karlobag before December 1777.

**What it gives.**

* **Nicolaus Gerkacs was alive in November 1769** (the entry says «filij Nicolai», not «defuncti»; in 1785 his son Michael is «filii defuncti Nicolai», and he died on 30 October 1783). So the **Gergacs family is at Karlobag by 1769**, eight years before the first indexed Gergacs baptism there (1777).
* **Antonius's wife Francisca is the daughter of Michael Berkliacich** (a Karlobag surname), the Francisca of every Gergacs baptism of 1777–88.
* **Antonius was born in or before about 1748** (a groom of 1769, at 21 or more). **Nicolaus, born about 1717, was then about 31 or younger at Antonius's birth**, which is possible, and **Nicolaus, if he had Antonius at Mrežnica, would have him before the baptism of Anna (July 1745) or just after**: the Mrežnica births of August 1745 to 1761 were read in full and have **no Antonius to a Nicolaus Gergacz**. So **if Antonius was born at Mrežnica it was before July 1745 (the births of December 1735 to July 1745, images 140–215, **have since been read: see the section below**)**; otherwise he, and Nicolaus, came from somewhere else.
* **No place of origin is given for the groom or his father.** The marriage entries of these years name a village for some grooms and brides and none for others, so the absence of a place for Antonius is no evidence either way.

**Caution.** The witness «Jacobo Mathievich et …» was not read to the end. The Antonius here is identified with the Antonius of 1783 and 1785 by the name, the surname spelling and the wife's name Francisca, which every later baptism carries; a different Antonius Gergacs is possible but there is no other Gergacs at Karlobag before 1777.

**What it leaves open.** Where Nicolaus and Antonius came from. The remaining filmed tests: the **Mrežnica births 1735–July 1745** for an Antonius to a Nicolaus, and, on the Karlobag side, the **baptisms of Antonius's own children in 1777–88 (godparents)** and any **Gergacs death entry that names an origin**.

The births before December 1735, the misbound leaves of October 1761 – April 1762 and the births of 1763 onward are still unread.


## The surname at Mrežnica before 1740 is **Jargat** (18 October 2026)

**How it turned up.** Reading the earliest Mrežnica births (images 7–12, October 1718 – April 1719) the hand wrote a surname «Jargat» / «Jergat» in the village Varos, twice: **18 November 1718** (image 7) and **30 December 1718** (image 8). The index had been searched in eleven Gergacs-type spellings and never in this one. Searched now, exactly, in the Croatia Church Books collection: **«Jargat» has 48 records, 19 of them baptisms at Mrežnica between 1704 and 1723**, and later ones. The first letter is drawn like a J in this hand (the godparents' «Gergath» of 17 February 1745 is written the same way), which makes **Jargat/Jargath** and **Gergach/Gergath** possibly one surname read two ways by the scribe and the indexers; **that is a hypothesis, not shown** (see the correction below).

**Read on the page** (images 7 and 8, [snapshot](/snapshots/#mreznica-baptism-1718-11-nikola-jargat), [snapshot](/snapshots/#mreznica-baptism-1718-12-mijo-jargat)): the **18 November 1718** entry is the baptism of a son of **Laurentius (Lovro) Jargat** and Catharina née Magličić (under *Varos*; the date line is bled through and unreadable, so **date, the child's name Nikola and the mother's surname are the index's**, matched by father, village and position); the **30 December 1718** entry reads «Baptisatus est Michael filius leg. **Petri Jargat** et Catharina nata Car; patrini Joannes Juanich et Margaretha nata Benich».

**What it changes.**

* **If the two spellings are one surname**, the Mrežnica Nicolaus Gergacz (children 1740, 1745), the Joannes and Anna Gergath of 1745 and the Michael Gergach of 1756 belong to a family **already at Mrežnica for a generation before them**, not an incomer. If they are two surnames, the Jargat are a separate long-standing family and the Gergacz remain unexplained.
* **Nikola Jargat, baptised 18 November 1718**, is the one baptism of a Nicolaus in the right years. He would be **21 at Petrus's baptism (1740)** and **64 years 11 months at the Karlobag Nicolaus's death on 30 October 1783**, where the death record says «aged 66» (born about 1717; ages in these registers are rough). **That is a candidate, not an identification**: no document says that the Nikola of 1718, the Nicolaus of 1740 and 1745, and the Nicolaus of Karlobag are one man; a Nicolaus with this surname could also have been baptised in the years 1724–1739 not yet read, or before 1718 in the book that is missing (the film's births start in October 1718).
* **The marriage and death searches above looked for «Gergacz»-type spellings only.** They must be repeated for **Jargat / Jergat / Gergat**: the Mrežnica marriage book 1726–1761 and the deaths 1728–1759 may carry the family under the older spelling.
* **The Karlobag Antonius** (groom of 1769, born 1748 or earlier) is not an indexed baptism at Karlobag (no Karlobag record under any of these spellings before 1779), and was not baptised at Mrežnica after July 1745 (read). If Nicolaus left Mrežnica after Anna's baptism (July 1745), **Antonius may have been baptised at Karlobag in 1746–48**, in a book that has been read for Uroda and Bacchi but never for this name: the next test.

**Limits.** The 1718–19 pages are badly bled through (writing from the other side shows through) and **image 10 is a faint, show-through leaf not read at this scale**; images 7, 8, 9, 11 and 12 were read for the surname at about 0.6 of source size only. Nothing here connects the Mrežnica Jargat to Karlobag.

### Correction and extension, same day: what the pages and the index say about Jargat

**Read on the page, images 1–29 (October 1718 to 10 March 1721), every page, sheets at about 0.6 of source size.** Images 1–6 are film targets; the register begins on image 7 (a 1718 hand, badly bled through; image 10 a faint show-through leaf, not read). ## A Gergach at Karlobag in 1753: Mikula and Marda, son Lovro (25 October 2026)

**New document.** Karlobag births 1691–1770, waypoint **9RK1-3Y9**, **image 118, right page (book page 213), headed «Letta 1753»**, the entry of **9 August 1753**: «Admstr Pop Jos: Suvich kerstih **Lovru sina Mikule y Marde Gergach**. Kumi Marko Milletich y Gele Kriskovich» ([snapshot](/snapshots/#karlobag-baptism-1753-08-lovro-nikola-gergach)). Read at full size from the page: **a son Lovro, born to Mikula and Marda Gergach**, godparents Marko Milletich and Gele Kriskovich. **Mikula is the Croatian form of Nikola, Nicolaus**; the surname is written once after the wife's name, as the entries do for both parents.

**What it says, and what it does not.**

* **This is the earliest Karlobag record of the family known to the archive**: **1753**, sixteen years before the marriage of Antonius (1769) and twenty-four years before the first indexed Gergacs baptism (1777). It is **not in the FamilySearch index**, which is why the index searches of 17 October found nothing earlier.
* **A Mikula/Nicolaus Gergach is a father at Karlobag by August 1753**, which makes him a plausible **Nicolaus Gerkacs (b. about 1717, d. 30 October 1783)**: same surname, same town, same given name, and a man of about 36 with children. **It is not stated by the entry**, and the mother's name «Marda» (Marta or Magdalena, not certain) is **not Margarita**, the wife of the Mrežnica Nicolaus Gergacz (1740, 1745). So **if he is the Mrežnica man, Margarita had died or he had married again by 1753**; if not, the Mrežnica Nicolaus is a different man.
* **The Karlobag Gerkacs therefore arrived before August 1753**, and after February 1751 (images 70–113 had no Gerkacs). **They are in the window February 1751 – August 1753**, or they came with a child already born and Lovro was the first baptised at Karlobag.
* **Lovro (Laurentius) is a new child** (not Antonius, not Michael); he may be the Lovro of later records.

**The Karlobag births 1751–1770 read so far for this surname:** images **114–123** (February 1751 – February 1756). Besides the 1753 entry, **no other Gergach/Gerkacs** in those images; near forms are other surnames («Anne Lerk/Jerka», «Gerseth/Jerzetich», «Berklacich»). **Images 124–177 (February 1756 – December 1770) are now read** (see below): Antonius, Michael and any other children of Mikula/Nicolaus, godparent entries, and a second wife.

## Did Mikula marry Marda at Karlobag? The marriage book, January 1741 – August 1766, and «Nicolaus Baretich alias Gergach» (25 October 2026)

**Why.** The 1753 entry gives a wife Marda, not Margarita. If Mikula (Nicolaus) took her as a second wife, or married her on arrival, the marriage would be in the Karlobag marriage book (DGS 5481649, waypoint **9RK1-HZK**, 435 images; positions are the waypoint's list order, which matches the viewer's numbering, e.g. 303 = 1769).

**Read.** Positions **280–298, both pages of each, full width (the first attempt with a cropped width cut the line ends off and was thrown away), 8 January 1741 to 22 August 1766**, by eye at about 0.67 of source size. The entries are in Latin («matrimonialiter copulavi ad faciem Ecclesiae … filiam defuncti …, praesentibus testibus …»), the priest Josip Šuvić, groom first.

**Result.** **No Gergach, Gerkacs or Jargat as groom, bride, bride's father or witness in January 1741 – August 1766, with one exception (below).** The near forms are other surnames («Gersetich/Gerseth» 1753, «Gerko» as a witness 1754 and as a groom, Georgius Gorko/Sorko, 31 July 1758, «Jerka»). So **Mikula and Marda did not marry at Karlobag between January 1741 and October 1758**, i.e. not in the window in which Lovro (August 1753) and any older child must have been conceived, **if they married at all there**. Together with the births this makes the likeliest reading **a family that arrived at Karlobag already married**, with the wedding in the place they came from.

**Limits.** By eye; the 1736–40 marriages (positions 278–279, seen only with the line ends cropped off) are not counted; marriages after August 1766 are read for this name only for 1767–70 (Antonius's, 1769) and 1789–1801.

**The exception: 29 June 1766, «Nicolai Baretich alias Gergach» (position 298, right page, book page 21).** «Idem eadem die ut supra matrimonialiter copulavi ad faciem Ecclesiae **Antonium filium defuncti Georgii Carnkovich et Matti[am] filiam Nicolai Baretich alias Gergach**, praesentibus Joanne Starcevich ac Martino Jellinich» ([snapshot](/snapshots/#karlobag-marriage-1766-06-carnkovich-baretich-alias-gergach)). Read at full size from the page.

* **A Nicolaus, called «Baretich alias Gergach», is at Karlobag in June 1766 and has a daughter marrying.** The **Gergach** is a **by-name (alias)** of a family written **Baretich** (Barić/Baričević are the forms of this surname elsewhere in the book). No other entry read has shown this alias.
* **It is likely, not shown, that he is Nicolaus Gerkacs** (b. about 1717, d. 30 October 1783): same given name, same by-name, same small town, and a man of 49 with a married daughter. If he is, **the family's own surname may be Baretich/Barić, with Gergach/Gerkacs the by-name that became the written surname**; and **Mattia is a daughter of his, a sister of Antonius (b. before 1748) and of Michael**, so a Karlobag marriage of Nicolaus's daughter is on record from 1766.
* **What it opens.** The Mrežnica search was for Gergacz/Jargat; **the Karlobag Nicolaus may have been baptised under the family name «Baretich/Barić»**, in a parish where the by-name was not used at all. The next tests are (a) the Karlobag **deaths 1774–1783 and the death of 30 October 1783** for an alias, (b) the Karlobag baptisms of 1753–70 for a **Baretich/Baričević with Nicolaus/Mikula as father** (a Lovro of 1753 was entered as «Gergach»), and (c) the index for **Baretich/Barić/Baričević** with a father Nicolaus at Mrežnica and elsewhere.
* **Limits:** the bride's first name is split at the line end («Matti-/am»); the entry's page number (book page 21) and the list position (298) are from the viewer's list, which earlier notes number one lower for this stretch (the arks are exact).

**Tests of the alias, 25 October 2026.**

* **The death entry of 30 October 1783 has no alias.** Re-read at half size from the page (list position 376, right page, folio 39): «30 8bris. **Nicolaus Gergacs annorum 66**, munitus omnibus sacramentis obiit in domo filii sui Antonii, et die 31 ejusdem sepultus est in Coemeterio laterali ex parte dextra Ecclesiae Parochialis». The same page writes «**vulgo**» for another family's by-name («Joannes … filius Petri Mandekich vulgo Kerpatich», 21 September), so the priest did record by-names when he used them; **he gave none for Nicolaus Gergacs**. That does not separate the two entries (1766 and 1783): a man known as «Gergach» in life could be entered by that name at his death, with «Baretich» dropped.
* **The FamilySearch index has no Baretich/Baretić, Barić or Baričević with a Nicolaus/Nikola/Mikula-type name at Karlobag or Mrežnica** (about 20 spellings tried, exact search). The alias is therefore only on the page: the index would not have found it.
* **Still to test:** the Karlobag births 1753–70 (images 114–176 of the births book) for a **Baretich/Barichevich** couple Nicolaus/Mikula × Marda with children, and Mattia's own baptism (about 1745) in a parish not yet found.

**The Baretich at Karlobag, a first look (25 October 2026).** Re-reading the Karlobag births 1753–1757 (images 118–129) for the name: a **Georgius Baretich with a wife Barbara** is a father at Karlobag in **January 1754** (a son Antonius, «Gerge y Barbare Baretich», 13 January 1754), **May 1755** (a daughter, 29 May 1755) and **March 1757** (a son Josephus, «Gergura y Barbare Baretich», 22 March 1757), and a «Baretich» appears as a mother's name in 1754–55. **No Nicolaus/Mikula Baretich is a father in these images**, and **the 9 August 1753 entry of Lovro is written «Mikule y Marde Gergach» without Baretich**. So **Baretich is a real Karlobag family surname** (with a Georgius, not Nicolaus) and **the Gergach by-name was already the written surname of Nicolaus's household in 1753**. Half-size readings, not cropped. **Not yet read for the Baretich name: images 130–176 (1757–70).** What the 1766 «Baretich alias Gergach» means is **not settled by these pages**: it may record a second name of the Gergach household, or a tie to the Baretich family (a Georgius Baretich is the only Baretich father seen so far), or a different Nicolaus; **no relationship is established**. The FamilySearch index has no Baretich with a Nicolaus-type name at Karlobag or Mrežnica.
