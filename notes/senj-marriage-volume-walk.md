# The Senj marriage and death volume (9RK1-W3P): a full read, begun 13–14 October 2026

The volume is FamilySearch waypoint **9RK1-W3P**, 592 images: marriages 1734–1819, marriages 1820–1858 and deaths 1820–1858. It is **not one book but a bundle of interleaved sequences**, so the only complete method is every image, both pages. The control file is `sources/senj-walk-ledger.json` (one entry per image, every hit, what is still to re-read), driven by `tools/senjwalk.py`.

**How it was read.** One page per screenshot at about a quarter to three-quarter scale, then a zoomed crop of every hit kept as a snapshot. **Images 10–79 were read through a narrow window** that missed the right-hand edge of every page (brides' fathers, witnesses); they are listed in the ledger as `strip_todo` and are NOT complete. **The Latin numbered entries of 1790–1795 (images 139–151 and on) are in a small hand** and were read coarsely; they are listed as `reread_todo`. Nothing below is a finding of absence.
