# The index gives Marijan Blažević the wrong father, and the entry says how

*20 September 2026. Question 1b's evidence, checked against the index for the
first time — and the index disagrees. It is wrong, and the reason is visible in
the entry itself.*

---

## The two readings

**What this archive read off the image**, 9 September 2026 —
`notes/reading-2026-09-09.md`, Krmpote/Sv. Jakov register **p.168 no.26**:

> **1895, travnja 12 / 16** — born 12 April, baptised 16 April
> **Marijan**, *zakoniti*
> Parents: **Jurko Blažević i Tereza rođ. Žubrinić**, ***selj.***
> Residence: **Smokvica kbr. 114**
> Godparents: **Ive Blažević, *sin Tomin*** and **Marija Blažević, *ćer Tomina***

**What the index holds** — `1:1:XQMS-HRQW`, *Croatia, Church Books* /
*Hungary, Croatia-Slavonia, Births*:

> **Marijan Blažević** · baptism **1895**, Krmpote-Vodice · birth **Dec 1895**
> · **Ivko Blažević × Tereza Zubrinić**

Same parish, same year, same child's name, same mother. **Two things differ: the
month, and the father's given name.**

## Why it is one entry and not two

Two entries would mean **two women called Tereza Žubrinić**, married to **two
Blažević men** in one small parish, each naming a son **Marijan** in **1895**.
That is not a coincidence anyone should buy.

## And the entry shows how the father's name went wrong

**The godfather is «Ive Blažević».** The father is «Jurko Blažević». **Both
names are on the line**, and the index has taken **Ivko** — which is *Ive* — and
put it in the father's slot.

**That is a mechanism, not a guess.** It explains the one difference that
matters without requiring the image read to be wrong, and the image read carries
what the index does not: the **house number, Smokvica 114**, on which the whole
*zadruga* argument of [question 1b](/open-questions/) rests, and the
**occupation**, *seljak*, which is the first ever attached to Juraj.

**The month is a second error on the same entry** — *travnja* is **April**, and
the index says December. This archive has already caught this indexing naming
the wrong mother for ahnentafel 106, where the register plainly reads *Antonie*
and the index reads *Marie*.

## What it changes

**Nothing in question 1b, and that is the point of checking.** The 1895 baptism
still names **Jurko Blažević** with Tereza Žubrinić at Smokvica 114. **But
anybody who searches the index rather than the film will be told the father was
an Ivan**, and will not find Juraj at all.

## And two negatives from the same search

**Krmpote's baptisms are indexed for 1880–1894.** Ljubomir was born **1892** and
Anka **1893** — both inside that window — and **neither baptism is in the
index.** The only Ljubomir entry anywhere is his **1920 marriage**; the only
Anka entry is her **1919 marriage**.

So the couple's two eldest are **not in the Krmpote baptism index even where it
covers their years**. That is consistent with what
[question 13](/open-questions/) already argues from the other direction — that
**Ljubomir was baptised at Rijeka**, whose 1892–1899 birth register is missing
from the film entirely.
