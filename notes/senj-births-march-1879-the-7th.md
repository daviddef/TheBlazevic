# Senj births, 1878–1894: the register for 7 March 1879

Waypoint 9R2D-JWL (Senj births 1878–1894, 269 images), read 24 November 2026: image 27 again (printed Str. 19, entries 27–31) with entry 30 zoomed, and images 32 and 33 for the first time. Image 32 is a second exposure of Str. 23 (entries 50–56, already read from image 31 in `senj-births-march-1879-the-6th.md`); image 33 is Str. 24 (entries 57–62, baptised 23 July to 3 August 1879). Images 25–31 (entries 15–56) are the earlier readings (`senj-births-february-1879-the-25th.md` and the notes for 3, 4, 5 and 6 March).

## The question

Who is born or baptised on 7 March 1879 in the Senj register, and is any of them a Perpić, Prpić or Tomljanović of the household at Klaričevac 82?

## What the pages are

The printed Croatian table of the earlier 1879 notes: entries numbered from 1 each year, in order of baptism. Both pages of each image were read at full size; the name, parents and godparent cells of entry 30 were zoomed. Months: ožujka = March, travnja = April, srpnja = July, kolovoza = August.

- **Image 32 is not new pages.** It is a second photograph of Str. 23 (entries 50–56), with the same entries and the same printed page number as image 31; in image 32 the pasted slip of the right-hand page is not over entry 52, so a later correction note of 1890 on entry 52 can be read (it concerns that entry's child only). Nothing in it concerns the question.
- **Image 33** is photographed with the edge of the previous leaf (Str. 23) lying over the left-hand margin; the entries and names of Str. 24 (57–62) are whole, and the religion column and the edge of the parents column are partly cut.

## What was found

- **One child is born on 7 March 1879: entry 30**, baptised 23 April («7 ožujka / 23 travnja 1879»). The names are «Bruno, Jure, Toma Acquin»; zakonit; father «Ivo Franceskini trgovac» (merchant), mother «Katarina rodj. Stamšić»; house Senj; baptiser Tomo Matić, a canon and the royal gymnasium's professor and director; Opazke empty. It is a different household from the Klaričevac one, and none of the surnames is Perpić, Prpić or Tomljanović. This is the entry with the 47-day delay mentioned in the earlier notes.
- **Baptised on 7 March: none.** The baptisms run 3 March (entry 19), then 11 March (entry 20), 16 March (entry 21) and on. The only entry born on 7 April (entry 28, Julio Amadeo) is April.
- **No other entry in 15–62 is born on 7 March.** Entries 57–62 are born 16, 20, 26, 20, 26 and 27 July and baptised 23, 27 and 30 July and 2, 3 and 3 August.
- **Where the late baptisms stop.** The longest delay in entries 15–62 is entry 30 (47 days). A child born on 7 March and baptised by 3 August would be 149 days late.
- **No Perpić, Prpić or Tomljanović in entries 57–62.**

## What this does not show

A birth on 7 March baptised after 3 August would stand at entry 63 or later; those were not read. Not a walk of the register or of 1879: entries 15–62 of images 25–33 only.

## What follows

Nothing new for the Klaričevac household. Franceskini and Stamšić are names of another household; nothing ties them to this archive's lines, so nothing was added to the Atlas catalogues. The name Franceskini is not read here as any other spelling of a surname in this archive.
