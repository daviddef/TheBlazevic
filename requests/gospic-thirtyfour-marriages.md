# Državni arhiv u Gospiću — the thirty-four Žubrinić marriages

**To:** Državni arhiv u Gospiću, Kaniška 17, 53000 Gospić
**zahtjevi@arhiv-gospic.hr**

**Status: drafted, not sent.** A companion to `gospic-otocac-zubrinic.md`, which
asks a narrow question about one man. This asks a broad one about thirty-four,
and it is the request that would change this archive most.

## Why this, and why now

Our Žubrinić material is **422 records**. Strip out the research notes somebody
left in the tree where an ancestor should be, and **only 21 of 414 published
Žubrinići are connected to our subject by documented links at all** — five per
cent. The rest hang together by filing rather than by evidence.

So confirming a date we already hold adds nothing. **Joining one household to the
line adds a branch**, and a *marriage entry* does that better than anything else,
because it names both parents of both parties in a single act.

Below are **thirty-four Žubrinići** who carry enough detail to be looked for. For
each we give a birth year and, where we have it, a village and house number. The
marriage would fall roughly **eighteen to thirty years after the birth**, and that
window is given so the search can be bounded.

**We are not asking for all thirty-four.** If any one decade of the Otočac
marriage register can be looked at, the first four rows are where we would start —
they are the ones with the most children hanging off them, so joining any of them
moves the most people.

## What we already know about the parish

- **Šumećica**, houses **2** and **24**, is a Frontier village of the Otočac
  regiment — our 1834 baptism there gives the household as *Confiniarius*.
- **Prozor**, houses **46**, **50** and **245**, five km south-east of Otočac.
- **Dubrava**, house **29** — *Confiniarius ex Pago Dubrava N° 29*, 1835.
- Žubrinić offices we have read at Otočac: *Vigiliarum Magister pensionatus*,
  *Silvarum Custos*, *Custos Carcerum*, and a Forest Ranger born 1837.

Publish it, in full, with the shelfmark, on a free public archive at
**daviddef.github.io/TheBlazevic**, and say plainly which of our existing claims
it corrected. We have published our own errors throughout — including, this week,
the collapse of our theory that an epidemic struck this family in 1845.
