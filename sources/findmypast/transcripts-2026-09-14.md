# FindMyPast, day three — the ZUBRINIC manifests read out

14 September 2026, the last day of the trial. The 13 September sessions took the
**ZUBRINICH** spelling. This one took **ZUBRINIC**, and read the transcripts
rather than only the index.

**Nothing here has been added to the tree.** These are index transcriptions on a
commercial site, not documents read.

## The method finding, first, because it decides how to spend a subscription

**Only the `ELLIS2` dataset carries a residence and a relative.** Three
passenger datasets came back for this surname and they are not equal:

    US/PASS/NY/ELLIS2/…     residence, relatives, ship, age   ← the useful one
    US/PASSENGERLISTS/…     name, age, ship. No residence.
    R_1028…  R_1029…        name, age, ship. No residence.

A transcript whose id begins `R_` or `US/PASSENGERLISTS` **will not tell you
where in Croatia they came from**. Open the `ELLIS2` ones.

And the id suffix matters: `…/1` is the passenger; **`…/2` and `…/3` are the
*relations named on that record***, not travellers. Roza, Sandro, Ture, Franci,
Miho and "Miss" all surface in the index as if they were passengers and are not.

## ŠUMEĆICA is an emigration village — this archive did not know

> **Franjo Zubrinic**, b. **1883**, age 27 · ship **Noordam**, Rotterdam →
> New York, **1910** · Nationality *Hungary, Croatian* ·
> **Residence: "Sumenica"** · departure relation **(WIFE): Franci Zubrinic** ·
> arrival relation **(BROTHER): Ture Zubrinic**

**Šumećica** is the hamlet of ahnentafel 10's baptism — *Confiniarius, ex pago
Shumechicza N° 24* — and of Toma Žubrinić at Šumećica 2. The archive's
emigration page had **Prozor and nothing else**. Here is a Šumećica household of
three: a man born 1883, a wife left behind, a brother already in New York.

**It is exactly the generation the archive is missing.** The note of 13 September
recorded that this archive holds *not one Žubrinić born at Prozor between 1875
and 1900*. The same hole runs at Šumećica, and this fills a corner of it.

## And the Žubrinići appear in other families' records

Two of the "Zubrinic" index hits are not Žubrinić records at all — they are
**Žubrinići named as the relative** on somebody else's manifest:

* **"Miss" Zubrinic**, 1911, *Saint Louis* — named on the record of
  **Dragutin Orešković**. Orešković is the surname of **ahnentafel 21**.
* **Miho Zubrinic**, 1912, *Saint Paul* — named on the record of
  **Nicola Ladisić**.

That is a village network travelling together, and it is the kind of thing a
surname-only search hides.
