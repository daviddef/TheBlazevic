# Where did Nicolaus Gerkacs (ahnentafel 220, d. 1783 at Karlobag aged 66) come from? An index search, and one coincidence at Mrežnica

17 October 2026. Index only (FamilySearch *Croatia, Church Books*, collection 2040054, persona search); no page opened. Extends [the note on the three direct-line baptisms looked for by name](karlobag-marriage-1774-smojver-bacchi.md) (11 October 2026) and work-list row 54.

**The question.** Nicolaus died 30 October 1783 «in domo filij sui Antonij» aged 66, so he was born about 1717; his son Michael married at Karlobag in 1785. No Gergacs baptism exists at Karlobag before 1779, so he was an incomer.

**What was searched.** The surname in eleven spellings (Gergacs, Gerkacs, Gerkacz, Gergacz, Jerkacs, Gerkach, Gergach, Gergics, Gergich, Gergocz, Geraecs), baptisms 1735–1770, with the father's given name Nicolaus or Nicolai, and with the child's name Antonius, Antonio or Michael, anywhere in the collection. **No baptism of an Antonius or a Michael Gergacs to a father Nicolaus turns up in 1735–1770** (the index is not complete, so this does not mean there is none).

**The one coincidence.** At **Mrežnica** (in the then Modruš-Rijeka district, inland of Ogulin) a **Nicolaus Gergacz with a wife Margarita** has two children indexed:

| baptised | child | parents | index record |
|---|---|---|---|
| 29 November 1740 | Petrus | Nicolaus Gergacz × Margarita *Veiknich* | QKMJ-G4SG |
| 7 July 1745 | Anna | Nicolaus Gergacz × Margarita *Verbanich* | QKMJ-LGTK |

A Nicolaus who is a father in 1740 could well be born about 1717 (he would be 23). **That is all there is.** The wife's surname is written two ways, neither child is Antonius or Michael, and the same register shows another Nicolaus Gergach (father of Joannes, whose son Nicolaus was baptised there on 21 August 1760). **It is a lead, not a link**, and nothing here says the Mrežnica Nicolaus ever went to Karlobag.

**Where it could be tested.** The Mrežnica births, marriages and deaths are on film under waypoint **9R2M-C6D** (the atlas lists Births 1718–1725 and 1728–1761, Marriages 1726–1761, Deaths 1726–59, Births 1762–1774, 588 images; the births of 1766 are around image 510). Latin prose, entries grouped by village. What to look for: further children of Nicolaus and Margarita (Antonius, Michael, born 1746–62), a death of a Nicolaus Gergacz at Mrežnica before about 1760, or a marriage.

**Not the same family.** The index shows many **Gergich** (Kostajnica, Kobaš, Osijek, Našice, Nova Gradiška): a different surname, in Slavonia, and no evidence connects it to the Karlobag Gergacs.

## A look at the Mrežnica film, 17 October 2026 (not a read)

Images 480 (right) and 481 (left) of waypoint 9R2M-C6D were opened (the pages of January 1763; 1763 begins on 481). **The register is hand-written Latin grouped under village headings (Podvenac, Novaki, Vrbovac, Mikalich and others), about a dozen baptisms to a page, in a cramped cursive in which surnames are not reliably legible at page scale.** Nothing was searched for or recorded from them. A read for a Gergacz household would have to start from a village (the index gives none for the 1740 and 1745 entries) and would be a page-by-page walk of 1746–1762 in a hand this hard; **the index, not the pages, is the efficient route, and it has no Antonius or Michael to a Nicolaus**. Left open, not attempted.


## Read on the pages, 18 October 2026 (Mrežnica births, waypoint 9R2M-C6D)

**Method.** Pages downloaded at full size and read from sheets ([tools/familysearch.md](https://github.com/daviddef/TheBlazevic/blob/main/tools/familysearch.md)). Image numbers are positions in the waypoint's list of 588.

**1. The two indexed entries, read at the page.**

* **Image 182, left page (foot), «Pechurkovs Berdo» (Petrakovo Berdo)**, a day 29 of late 1740: «*Baptizavi Petrum filium Nicolai Pergáer* [the index reads *Gergacz*] *et Margarita nata Veik/nich coniugum. PP. Joannes Sitschak et Barbara Silischka*». The month is not on the line itself; the index and the page order (it stands on the page before the December heading) give 29 November. [Snapshot](/snapshots/#mreznica-baptism-1740-11-petrus-gergacz). **The surname is written in a form that could be read as *Gergacz*, *Pergaér* or *Gerkacz*; the mother's name as *Veiknich* or *Veik[ʻ]nich*.**
* **Image 215, right page (top, page 144), village «Bellai» (Belaj)**, 7 July 1745: «*Die 7 Julij Baptizata est filia Anna legitima Nicolai Gergach* [*Pergach*] *et Margaritha nata Verbanich coniugum. PP. Mathias Polya et Barbara Stischak*». [Snapshot](/snapshots/#mreznica-baptism-1745-07-anna-gergacz). **So the two children were baptised from two different villages, Petrakovo Berdo (1740) and Belaj (1745)** — either the family moved or they are two Nicolai Gergacz, and the second wife-name, *Verbanich* for *Veiknich*, is a second reason to be careful.

**2. Pages read for another Gergacz child of a Nicolaus (none found).**

| **330–344** | the births book of 1728–1761 from **December 1759 to its last entry, 18 October 1761** (image 344; the marriages 1726–1761 begin at 346), every entry of 15 pages read | **No Gergacz** (any spelling) |
| **474–479** | the births book 1762–1789 from its first page (**May 1762**, image 474) to **December 1762** | **No Gergacz** |

**3. The gap that matters for Michael.** The tree has Michael Gerkacs (the groom of 1785) born **about 1762**. **The births book 1728–1761 ends on 18 October 1761, and the next book, 1762–1789, begins in May 1762, right after a film target «Torn page(s)»; the film's own note says a few births stand near the end of that book, «probably because of a wrong binding».** So **the baptisms of 19 October 1761 to about April 1762 are in neither book as filmed**, which is exactly where a Michael born «about 1762» may be. Not read: the misbound pages at the end of the 1762–1789 book (the note does not say where), and the book's 1763–1774 pages.

**What this does and does not show.**

* **Nothing links the Mrežnica Nicolaus to Karlobag's Nicolaus Gerkacs.** No Antonius or Michael to a Nicolaus Gergacz turns up in the pages read, and the pages read are about a year and a half of births.
* **Read now: 1745–1752 (images 216–272) and December 1759–1761 (330–344). Still not read: January 1753 to November 1759 (images 273–329), about 57 images.** Antonius, if the Karlobag Antonius is this Nicolaus's son, would have been born between about 1740 and 1760 (his own children were baptised at Karlobag from 1777), so 1745–1752 was the likeliest stretch and shows no child of a Nicolaus Gergacz.
* **A relative, not a child:** the index and these pages show a **Dorothea Gergacz, wife of Joannes Milchich of Vellemerich**, with children baptised 1752, 1754 and 1756, and a **Margarita Gergacz, wife of Joannes Thomasich**, with a child of 1766. Nothing says how they relate to Nicolaus; they show that the surname existed at Mrežnica beyond his household.
* **A different line to test:** the death of a Nicolaus Gergacz at Mrežnica (Deaths 1726–1759 are images about 400–470: image 466 is a death page of 1756) would say whether Nicolaus was still there after 1745.
