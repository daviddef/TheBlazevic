# Marija Findrić, udovica, 29 July 1849 — and the false null that hid her

15 September 2026. Work list row 28. **Ahnentafel 41 is read, on exactly the day
the tree gives her** — and she was missed this morning by a misread month.

## The entry

Otočac, *Umrli 1834–1858*, **page 153, entry 53**, image **167**, ARK
`3:1:3QS7-899C-PVHR`. The book is in Croatian by now, not Latin.

| Column | What it says |
|---|---|
| *Annus, Mensis, Dies mortis* | **Leta 1849 dne 29 Srpnja preminula je** |
| *Nomen, Cognomen Defuncti* | **Maria Žubrinić, Udovica** |
| *Aetas Annorum* | **69** |
| *Morbus* | *Slaboća starosti po dokazu Lečnika* — weakness of old age, on the physician's evidence |
| *Sacramenta* | *Sa Svetimi Otajstvi umirućih providjena* |
| *Locus Sepulturæ* | *u župničkoj grobnici* |
| *Sepeliens* | *Vatroslav Vinsb…* |

**The age corroborates the birth year.** 69 on 29 July 1849 puts her birth in
**1779 or 1780**; the tree says 1780. And *Udovica* is right: her husband
[Toma Žubrinić](otocac-toma-zubrinic-1838.md) died on 4 January 1838, eleven
years and seven months earlier.

**What the entry does not give is a house.** «*Maria Žubrinić, Udovica*» is the
whole of the name cell — no village, no number, no husband named. The
identification rests on **the date to the day, the age against the tree's birth
year, and the widowhood** — three independent agreements, and no fourth. It is
not anchored to Šumećica N° 2 the way her husband's entry is, and this note does
not pretend otherwise.

## The false null, and how it happened

This morning's pass over the same book concluded:

> page 151 ends at *28 Srpnja* and page 152 opens at *9 Kolovoza*, and the entry
> numbers run continuously through, so **nothing is missing from the book and
> there is no death recorded on 29 July 1849 at all**.

**Every month in that sentence was wrong.** Read again at full resolution:

| | What was recorded | What the page says |
|---|---|---|
| page 151 | ends *28 **Srpnja*** (July) | runs **4 *Svibnja* (May) to 8 *Lipnja* (June)** |
| page 152 | opens *9 **Kolovoza*** (August) | runs **9 *Lipnja* (June) to 14 *Srpnja* (July)** |
| a Žubrinić entry | *Kate, žena Mate Žubrinića*, **July** 1849 | **7 *Svibnja* — 7 May 1849** |

**Svibnja, Srpnja, Lipnja.** Three Croatian months that differ by two letters in
a cursive hand, and mistaking one for another turned *"July has not been read
yet"* into *"there is no such death"*. The entry numbers really do run
continuously — that part was true, and it is exactly what made the wrong
conclusion feel safe. **A continuous sequence proves no page is missing. It
proves nothing at all about which months you are looking at.**

She was three pages further on.

## The rule this leaves behind

**Never conclude a negative from a date range until the month names at both ends
have been read at full resolution.** The archive already has a rule about
reading scans at full size before recording a date; this extends it. A null is a
claim, and in a book that changes language partway through it is the most
fragile claim available.
