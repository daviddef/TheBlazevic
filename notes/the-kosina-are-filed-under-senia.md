# The Kosina are filed under «Senia», and two infants died in 1896

**21 September 2026.** Asked to find more on **Martin Kosina** and his mother **Gertruda**,
the two names at the top of the Kosina line where the tree simply stops.

**Martin and Gertruda were not found.** What was found is the generation below them, and a
reason the family had been invisible.

## Why every Senj search had missed them

A surname sweep of Senj under **Kosina · Kossina · Kozina · Cosina · Kosinac**, as surname
and as father's surname, returns **nothing at all.**

**The records are there. The place string is Latin.**

> `Antonius Joannes` · born **13 May 1891**, baptised **15 June 1891**
> father **Martinus Kosina**, mother **Angelica Boras**
> ***Senia*, Lika-Korbava megye, Magyarország** · `1:1:X9DP-3YJQ`

**«Senia», not «Senj».** The birth date **agrees with the tree to the day**.

### And that corrects something this archive wrote yesterday

`indexed.psv` records, under the fourth pass: *«Senj also under the Latin place string
SENIA … `q.anyPlace=Senia` returns NOTHING. Either the place string was never right or it
has been normalised away.»*

**The place string is alive and in use.** What failed was the **query**, not the records —
`q.anyPlace=Senia` does not resolve to a place the search will filter on, and the rows
carrying «Senia» come back only when something else brings them. That is exactly the
distinction this archive drew the same day and then fell straight into: **a probe that
fails to reproduce a result is not a probe that disproves it.** Corrected in the table.

## Two children of Martin and Angelika that the tree does not have

> **Marija Kosina** — born **1896**, **buried 1896**, Senj · `1:1:6B47-LG4G`
> **Josip Kosina** — born **1896**, **buried 1896**, Senj · `1:1:6B47-LGH2`

**Both born and both dead in the same year**, both entered to *«Martin Kosina × Angjelina
Kosina»* — the mother under her married name. **They are probably twins**, which a register
would say and an index does not.

So this household had **at least four children**, not two: Antun 1891, Pavao 1894, and two
infants in 1896. **Angelika died in 1902**, six years after burying them.

## And a woman who was a name and nothing else now has parents

The tree gives the elder Antun a wife, **Anka Kasunić**, and nothing else. Her marriage
entry gives her **birth year and both her parents**:

> **Anka Kasuniž**, b. **1893** — of **Petar Kasuniž** and **Marija Turina** ·
> `1:1:X9D5-MWF2`

**And the same collection confirms the join this whole page rests on**, independently of
the family tree: *Pavao Kosina b.1894, of Martin Kosina and Andjelika Boras*, married
*Anka Blažević b.1893, of **Juraj Blažević** and **Tereza Žubrinić*** — ahnentafel 4 and 5
— `1:1:X9D5-MWXX`.

## Where the name is from, and why this archive is not saying yet

**Kosina is not a Lika surname.** Swept across Croatia it clusters hard in the north:

    Lipovljani 65 · Petrovsko 39 · Jalžabet 37 · Donja Pušća 29 · Začretje 19
    Krapina 10 · Marija Gorica 7 · Suhopolje 11 · Prelog (Međimurje)

**Zagorje, Varaždin, Međimurje and Slavonia** — not the Velebit coast. The Senj Kosina look
like **incomers**, which would fit a man who appears there in the 1890s with a wife from a
Senj family.

**One candidate, and it is offered as nothing more.** A ***Martinus Kosina*, baptised 5
October 1832 at Donja Pušća**, of *Georgius Kosina* and *Catharina Bzik* — `1:1:QKMN-NH7L`.
**Nine years earlier than our Martin's 1841**, in the right surname cluster, and **the
mother is Catharina and not Gertruda.** That last is enough to leave it alone.

**And the village called Kosinj is a trap this archive has already fallen into once.**
*Donji Kosinj* and *Gornji Kosinj* are real Lika villages and they are **not** this
surname. [Gologorica](/worklist/) was exactly this mistake — a place-name read as a family
— and it cost a sitting.

## What would settle it

**Martin's own baptism**, about 1841, which names his father and usually a birthplace; and
**his marriage to Angelika Boras**, before 1891, which names both sets of parents and would
give **Gertruda a surname**. Neither is in the index under any spelling tried.

**Senj baptisms are indexed by three collections back to 1692** and Martin is not among
them — which, with the surname map above, is itself an argument that **he was not born at
Senj.**
