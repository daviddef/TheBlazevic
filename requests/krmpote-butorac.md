# Župa sv. Jakova, Krmpote — the Butorac household

**Status: drafted, not sent.** Small, and cheap to answer.

## The question in one line

Who were **Jandre Butorac** and his wife **Marija**, of the Krmpote parish in the
1880s — and what house number was their household entered under?

## Why we are asking

They appear twice in the baptismal register of **Sv. Jakov Krmpote**, as
godparents to two children of **Toma Blažević and Tereza rođ. Pećanić** at
**Smokvica kbr. 114**:

| | |
|---|---|
| **25 July 1882** | Ivan Blažević — godparents *Pere ?ećanić* and **Marija Butorac, supruga Jandrina** |
| **25 October 1884** | Antonija “Tonka” Blažević — godparents **Jandre i Marija Butorac, supruzi, rimo-kat. seljaci** |

Standing twice, two years apart, is not a courtesy. It is a **standing
relationship between two households** — and Tonka is a direct ancestor of the
person this archive is about.

**Neither Jandre nor Marija exists in our family tree.** The only Butorac we hold
is a Margareta born at Ledenice in 1870, far too young and in the wrong village.
So this couple is known to us from **one column of one register** and from
nowhere else.

## What would answer it

1. The **marriage of a Jandre (Andrija) Butorac** at Sv. Jakov Krmpote, roughly
   **1870–1882**, with the house number of his household.
2. Failing that, **any Butorac baptism at Smokvica Krmpotska** in the 1880s, for
   the house number alone.

## Why the house number is the thing

House 114 at Smokvica is a **zadruga** — several married couples of one kin-group
under one number. If the Butorac household sits close to 114, the tie is
neighbourhood. If it sits far away, it is much more likely to be **kin** — and
the mother of these children is a **Pećanić**, a family we have almost nothing on.

## One more, if the same volumes are open

The godfather at the 1882 baptism is written **“Pere ?ećanić”**. The initial is
illegible in our copy, and it matters: if it reads **Pećanić** he is the child's
mother's own kin, and we would have a second household to trace. We would rather
be told what the register says than guess.

*Hvala vam unaprijed.*
