# Krivi Put births, images 135–173 (December 1870 to the end of the 1847–1873 book): the parents' column and the house column

Waypoint 9R28-DP8 (DGS 5494757), read 23 November 2026 from the list's images 135–173, in the parents' column and the house column
of each opening (for 145–173 a window across the left page's parents and house columns and the left strip of the right page; for 135–144
the house-column strips of the earlier walk). Continues `krivi-put-houses-135-136-walk-1856-1870.md`.

## The question

After the earlier walk (images 10–134, 1848–1870): did Joannes Perpić and Lucia have children at house 135/136 after Anna (1870), and who
else stood at 135 and 136 once the numbering changed?

## What the pages are

- **The book ends at image 173.** Image 173 (left page) closes the last entry and carries the words «… hujus Libri»; image 174 is a blank left
  page and a right page of handwritten Croatian (a list of silver offerings, with years in the 1850s), not a register; **image 175 is the filming slate «ŽUPA
  KRIVI PUT … MATIČNA KNJIGA RODJENIH 1847–1873», END OF ITEM.** The waypoint's list therefore holds two books: images 1–174 (births 1847–1873)
  and 176–382 (the next book, from 1873: Joanna 1874 is on image 181, Josephus 1876 on image 226). Images 176–382 are not part of this walk.
- **The house numbers were renumbered in 1871.** From about image 141 (1871) an entry gives the old number and the new one: «Nro 69 novo 142»,
  «143 N. novus 20», at image 165 «N.D. nov. 146 / antiquus 93» (a Krmpotić household) and at image 171 «novus 1. antiquus 47» (a Tomljanović
  household at Alan). **So from 1871 a number may be the new one: where an entry does not say «novus» or «antiquus» the number cannot be
  assumed to be the old house, and the number alone no longer finds the couple.**
- The table is the printed Latin one (Annus, Mensis, Dies; Baptisati; Parentum; Locus domicilii et numerus domalis; Patrinorum; Baptisantis;
  Observationes). On images 145–152 the house column is on both pages of an opening; from image 159 it is on the left page only. The priest is
  Vincentius Segulja (to 1871), then Simon Serdoc.
- Duplicate exposures and slips: images 145, 146 and 147 are three exposures of one opening (printed page 233, January–February 1872); 149 and
  150 carry a pasted baptismal certificate («Krstni list», 1872) over the right-hand rows; 151–152 and 157–158 are two exposures each with a
  loose sheet over the middle rows; 160 right carries a slip («N° 60 … po meni Ivan Kovačić kapelan»); 154 is a handwritten sheet headed as a pastoral visitation (September), not an entry.

## What was found

- **No entry with parents Joannes (Ivan) Perpić and Lucia stands in any parents' or house window read on images 145–173.** The window shows
  every entry's parents' names in the rows not under a slip. Entries with a Joannes Perpić in these years have other wives (Joannes Perpić ×
  Francisca Perpić, Krivi put 149, January 1872; Joannes Perpić × Maria née Sojat, Krivi put 55; Joannes Perpić × Catharina née Perpić, Krivi
  put 80). Lucias met: Marcus Perpić × Lucia née Perpić (Krivi put 146, image 170), Marcus Perpić × Lucia née Pavelić (117), Petrus Perpić ×
  Lucia née Blažević (48), Gregorius Perpić × Lucia née Bionić (1872), Georgius Perpić × Lucia née Perpić (Merzlidol 2, 1872), Franciscus
  Perpić × Lucia née Tomljanović (137, image 159). None is Joannes × Lucia.
- **Petrus Perpić × Anna is at 136 in 1871 and in 1873.** Augustinus, born 30 July 1871, Petrus Perpić et Anna nata Tomljanović, «Krivipůt
  N° 136» (image 140 right, entry 109; snapshot `/snapshots/#krivi-put-baptism-1871-augustinus-perpic`; a later note says he died on 29
  December 1871); and an entry on image 163 left, «Petrus et Anna Perpić», «Krivi N. 136» (1873). That is the household of Lucia 1856, Maria
  1859, Josephus 1862, Joanna 1866 and Gregorius 1869 at 136, found in the earlier walk. **136 is still their number after the renumbering.**
- **Anna, the daughter of Joannes and Lucia (20 November 1870, «N° 135», image 135 left) is re-found** in the opening the new walk started on
  (godparents Andreas Perpić and Martha Tomljanović): the control for the start of this walk, which passed.
- The entry on image 140 right directly below (entry 111, 4 August 1871, Josephus, Mathias Perpić and a wife née Perpić) is at «N° 36» (the
  digits are written like 136; read as 36), not at 136; it is mentioned so the look-alike is not counted at 136.

## What this does not show

- **Not a walk of the pages and not a nil.** Read: the parents' and house columns of the windows named above. Not read: the names, dates and
  godparents' columns of the left pages of 145–158 (the window is the parents' and house columns), the rows under slips on 141 right, 142
  left, 143 left, 144 right, 149–152, 157–158 and 160 right (and no second exposure of them was found), the right-hand strip's lower rows on
  some images, and every entry's godparents (so a Lucia, wife of Joannes, who is a godmother in these years is not looked for).
- Images 135–144 were read in the house-column strips of the earlier walk; the look-alikes (13x written with flourishes) at 137 left, 138 and
  139 were not zoomed individually, only the one at 140 right.
- The last births of Joannes and Lucia in the index are Joanna (6 January 1874, image 181, in the **next** book) and Josephus (7 July 1876,
  image 226, at Klaričevac, not shown to be a brother). Images 176–382 are not read here except those two.

## What follows

Row 31 is left for the second book, images 176–382 (1873–1884): the number there is again a house number with its own renumbering and a
«Locus domicilii et numerus domalis» on the right-hand page (image 230, 1876), so the walk needs its own calibration; the parents' names are the
safer key. Surname forms met: Perpić (the clerks write Perpić and Perpii), Krmpotić (Krmpotić, Kermpotić), Tomljanović (Tomjanović,
Tomjanovich), Sojat, Solić, Pavelić, Vukelić, Butković: all in the Atlas catalogue or ordinary typography; none new.
