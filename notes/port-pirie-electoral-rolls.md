# Fifty electoral rolls, nineteen people, two trades

*Work list row 57, 16 September 2026. The Australia Electoral Rolls entries for
**Zubrinich**, read in full on the month's subscription. The trial of 13–14
September read eight of the fifty and expired; `searched.json` has carried the
rest as **pending** ever since.*

---

## The finding the archive already believed, now with nineteen people behind it

**Two trades and no third.** Across nineteen adults in five roll years:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Foundry** | Arthur Leslie *ironmoulder*, Thomas Leo *ironmoulder*, Keith Leo *moulder* |
| **Labourer** | Edward Malcolm, John Stephen, John William, Malcolm Theodore, Maurice Joseph, Michael Valentine |
| **Shop** | Gwenyth Florence *shop assistant*, Leonard Francis *assistant* |

**Not one office. Not one rank. Not one trade requiring a licence.**

Set that against what the same families are in the Lika registers — *Silvarum
Custos*, forest warden; *Vigiliarum Magister*, master of the watch; *Custos
Carcerum*, keeper of the prison; *vicedecurio*; and at Senj a **municipal excise
controller**. **The offices did not travel.** What crossed was the capacity for
heavy skilled work, and Port Pirie — a lead smelter — had a use for it.

The women's column is not a trade and is kept as the roll gives it: *Domestic
duties*, *Home duties*, *Married*. That is what the Commonwealth form asked for.

## What a roll gives that an index does not

An index row gives a name, a year and a state. **The transcript gives an
occupation, a street address, and the other people at that address.** Read three
years together and a household moves:

> **Keith Leo and Rita Laurel Zubrinich** — **9 Queen Street** (1939) →
> **46 Cottell Street** (1941) → **58 King Street** (1943). Three addresses in
> four years, and his trade *moulder* in all three.

**And two marriages fall between roll years.**

* **Asta Louise** is alone at **21 St Ann's Terrace, St Leonards, Glenelg** in
  1939 — an Adelaide seaside suburb, in the Barker division, **two hundred
  kilometres from the rest of the family**. By 1943 she is at **Ward Street,
  Whyalla**, with **Leonard Francis Zubrinich**. (The 1939 roll spells her
  *Austa*.)
* **Una Blanche** is at **11 Queen Street** in 1939 and at **30 Beach Road**
  with **Thomas Leo Zubrinich** in 1941 and 1943.

## Read the address, not the household field

**The «household members» list is not a complete household** and must not be
used as one. The proof is in this set: **Maurice Joseph and Violet Mary are at
56 Simpson Road in 1939, 1941 and 1943**, and only the **1941** transcripts list
them together. The field records who the index grouped on that page, not who
lived there.

Grouped by address instead, the nineteen fall into **eleven households**:

| Address | Who |
|---|---|
| 65 Queen Street, Solomontown | Edward Malcolm · Emma Benedicter · **Malcolm Theodore** |
| 2 Daisy St, Pt Pirie E | Arthur Leslie · Florence Johanna · **Gwenyth Florence** |
| 11 Queen Street | Phyllis Blanche · Una [Blanche] |
| 9 → 46 Cottell → 58 King | Keith Leo · Rita Laurel |
| 56 Simpson Road | Maurice Joseph · Violet Mary |
| 30 Beach Road | Thomas Leo · Una Blanche |
| 63 Fifth Street | Michael Valentine |
| 143 Main Road | John William |
| 13 Three Chain Rd, Pt Pirie E | Rose |
| Ward Street, Whyalla | Asta Louise · Leonard Francis |
| Whyalla → 19 Forsyth Street | John Stephen · Irene Dorothy |

**Three separate Zubrinich households stand in Queen Street** — numbers 9, 11
and 65. A third generation is visible in two of them: **Malcolm Theodore** at his
parents' 65 Queen Street, **Gwenyth Florence** at 2 Daisy Street.

## Four people are indexed twice under one surname

*Austa* / **Asta Louise**. *Emma Benedicter* / **Benedieter**. *Michael
Valentino* / **Valentine**. *Una* / **Una Blanche**. **Four of nineteen**, and
the variation is **inside a single record set**. This is the Zubrinich/Zubrinic
lesson one level down: the spelling is the search, and it fails at the given
name as readily as at the surname.

**The transcription is Findmypast's and it slips.** Emma's 1941 occupation reads
**«Blamed»** where 1939 and 1943 both read *Married*. Eveline's 1935 address
reads **«178'M11rt0i1 Street»**. Una's 1939 address reads
**«Blanche, 11 Queen Street»** — her own second given name has run into the
address column, which is also why she is indexed as plain *Una*. All three are
recorded exactly as given, with the reading noted separately: **an OCR slip is
evidence about the index, not about the family.**

## The two New South Wales rolls, and an open question

The Australian records are not all Port Pirie. **John William and Catherine
Zubrinich are at Wolfram Street, Broken Hill, in 1913** — twenty-six years before
the South Australian rolls — and **Eveline Frances is at Broken Hill North in
1935**.

Broken Hill is a silver-lead **mining** town. Port Pirie is where Broken Hill's
ore was **smelted**, at the other end of the ore railway. So the family's two
Australian addresses are the two ends of one industry.

**Whether the John William of Broken Hill 1913 is the John William of 143 Main
Road, Port Pirie in 1939 is not established here.** He would have to be an adult
in 1913 and in his fifties by 1941, which is possible; two men of one name is
equally possible. **It is written down as a question, not a move.**
