WHAT I HAVE ALREADY READ. Tereza's parents were NIKOLA (Nicolaus) ŽUBRINIĆ,
born at Otočac on 24 January 1834, and ANA, his wife. In your baptismal
register, read from the filmed copy, they are at ŠUMEĆICA No. 2:

    3 July 1856      Stjepan — mother entered "Anna nata Štrumbulko"
    15 September 1858 Marija — mother entered "Anna Marić"

The same woman is written with two different family names two years apart. I
think the first is a house name (a nadimak) and the second her surname, but that
is an inference from two entries, not something any document says.
