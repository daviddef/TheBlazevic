# Martha 1853 and Toma 1856 are not at Krivi Put 136

9 October 2026. Work list row 31. The index lists a Martha (baptised 29 July 1853) and a Toma
(14 December 1856) as children of an «Ivan Perpić» and a «Lucia Perpić» at Krivi Put, and
`found.psv` carried them as **candidate** elder siblings of ahnentafel 7 (Antonija «Tonka»
Perpić, b. 12 May 1859), because at least two Joannes Perpić households sit in the parish.
Ahnentafel 7's own baptism is at **Krivi Put N° 136**, so the house number is the test.

## What the images say (Krivi Put births 1847–1884, DGS 5494757, waypoint 9R28-DP8)

* **Martha, 1853:** image **34, right-hand page, entry 63.** «1853 Dne 29 … Martha», house
  **«Krivi put № 177»** (as read; the last two digits are in a faded cell), father **Ivan
  Perpić**, mother **«Lucia rodjenja Perpić»**, godparents Ivan Perpić and Martha.
* **Toma, 1856:** image **46, right-hand page, entry 88.** «Dana 14. Prosinca 1856 … sina Ivana
  Perpić i Luce Perpić imenom Toma, kumovase Gerga Perpić i Ruža istog žena», house **«Krivi put
  111»**, clearly written, with a later marginal note «Vjenčan 23.I.1930 sa Matijom Perpić u
  Krivom Putu».

**Neither is house 136.** (Ahnentafel 7 at 136 is image 49, 1859, entry 51, read in September.)

## What that does and does not show

* The house number is how these Frontier registers identify a household, so **a different number
  is a different household** unless the family moved. These two look like children of **other**
  Ivan × Lucia Perpić couples.
* **It is not a proof.** The parents' names are the same pair as ahnentafel 14 × 15 (Ivan and
  Lucia, both Perpić), a house number can change, and the 1853 digits are in a faded cell. The
  honest result is «probably not her siblings», and both `found.psv` rows now say exactly that.
* It also means **three or more Ivan × Lucia Perpić households at Krivi Put in the 1850s**, which
  is the warning the first row had given and a good reason not to attach a child to ahnentafel 14
  × 15 by name alone.

## What was not done

* Only the two named entries were read for house. **No other 1853–1858 entry at house 136 was
  looked for**, so a true elder sibling of ahnentafel 7 was neither found nor excluded.
* Georgius 1868, Joanna 1874, Josephus 1876 and Stephanus 1861 were not opened.
* House digits were read at about three times the page's resolution; 177 is less certain than 111.
