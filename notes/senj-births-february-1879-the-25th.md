# Senj births, 1878–1894: the register for 25 February 1879 (the day Matija was born)

Waypoint 9R2D-JWL (Senj births 1878–1894, 269 images), read 23 November 2026 at images 25–27 (printed Str. 17–19: entries 15–31, baptised 12 February to 2 May 1879).
Follows `senj-births-march-1879-matija.md`, which read entry 17 (Matija, «25 veljače / 2 ožujka 1879»); this note walks the day itself.

## The question

Who else is born on 25 February 1879 in the Senj register, and is anything there a sibling or twin of Matija, or a Perpić or Tomljanović?

## What the pages are

- The printed Croatian table, as in the 1879 note. Entries are numbered from 1 each year and follow the baptism date, with some babies baptised weeks late.
  Image 25 is Str. 17 (entries 15–21, baptised 12 February to 16 March), image 26 is Str. 18 (entries 22–26, baptised 16 March to 6 April), image 27 is Str. 19 (entries 27–31, baptised 6 April to 2 May). Both pages of each opening were read at full size; the name cells of entries 18 and 19 were zoomed.
- Months: veljače = February, ožujka = March, travnja = April. Baptisers: Mirko Babić (kapelan), Juraj Dobrović (kapelan), Ante Bosnić.

## What was found

- **Two births are dated 25 February 1879 in these fifteen entries:**
  - **entry 17: Matija**, zakonita, baptised 2 March: «Ivan Tomljanović poljodjelac i Lucia rodj. Prpić», Krivi put, Klaričevac kb. 82 (the 1879 note).
  - **entry 19: Ferdinand Vjekoslav**, zakonit (a boy), baptised 3 March: «Jandro Perpić» (the occupation word is not read with certainty) and «Ana rodj. Kovačević»; house «Senj, Karlobag»; the godparent cell names a Smojver (written «Vjek. Smojver», struck through, then «t.j. Nikola Smojver»); baptiser Juraj Dobrović kapelan; Opazke empty.
    A different Perpić: the house column says Karlobag, not Krivi Put, Klaričevac or Krmpote.
- **No twin or sibling entry for Matija.** Entry 17 stands alone («dvojki», twins, is written against entries 15 and 16, born 9 February, Frane Grzin and Matija Prpić); entry 18 is a different child (Mate, born 28 February, baptised 2 March, Ivan Bačić and Anna née Vijan).
- **No other entry in the fifteen is born on 25 February.** The births of entries 15–31 are 9 Feb (15, 16), 25 Feb (17), 28 Feb (18), 25 Feb (19), 2 Mar (20), 15 Mar (21), 8 Mar (22), 27 Feb (23), 19 Feb (24), 20 Mar (25), 24 Mar (26), 2 Apr (27), 7 Apr (28), 15 Apr (29), 7 Mar (30), 16 Apr (31).
  Snapshots: `/snapshots/#senj-births-1879-02-entries-15-21` (image 25), `#senj-births-1879-02-entries-22-26` (image 26), `#senj-births-1879-04-entries-27-31` (image 27).
- **The archive's tree already holds Matija and her parents**, from this same baptism: `/people/matija-tomljanovic/` (born 1879, Senj; her birth cites the image 25 ark), whose parents are the tree's Ivan Tomljanović and Lucia Prpić (Krivi Put), two persons with no parents and no other children.
  The tree also holds `/people/joannis-perpic/` (Joannis «Ivan» Perpić of Krivi Put) with `/people/lucia/` and the children Antonija (1859), Stephanus (1861) and Marcus (1872, Krmpote 82), with no Michael, Josephus, Matija or Pave.
  Whether the tree's Ivan Tomljanović and Ivan Perpić are one man is the open question of the 1879 note; nothing here settles it, and nothing is merged on a resemblance.

## What this does not show

- A birth on 25 February baptised after 2 May (more than ten weeks late) would stand further on; entries after 31 were not read. Not a walk of the register or of 1879: entries 15–31 of images 25–27 only (image 24, before entry 15, holds babies baptised before 12 February).
- Whether Ferdinand Vjekoslav's father is of any Perpić line of this archive: the tree has no Ferdinand and no Jandro or Andro Perpić with an Ana Kovačević.

## What follows

Michael (1874) and Matija (1879) are the couple's children found at Senj; Josephus (1876) and Pave (1881) are not (`senj-births-july-1876-josephus.md`, `senj-births-september-1881-pave.md`).
Surname and given-name forms met: Perpić, Prpić, Tomljanović, Kovačević, Smojver, Bačić; Jandro (not in the given-name catalogue; which name it is a form of is not established here, so nothing was added); Ferdinand, Vjekoslav. Kovačević and Smojver are not in the surname catalogue as families of this line and nothing ties them to it; nothing was added.
