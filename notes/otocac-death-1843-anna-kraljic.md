# Otočac, 27 April 1843: Anna, wife of Nicolaus Kraljić, aged 33 — and no death of Nicolaus himself in 1843–1858 (17 October 2026)

Follows [otocac-census-kraljich-1807.md](otocac-census-kraljich-1807.md) and [otocac-death-1821-jacobus-kralich.md](otocac-death-1821-jacobus-kralich.md). The question was **when and where Nicolaus Kraljić died**: the tree has «d. 1845», which the census page does not state (it carries only a «845» beside the struck rows of the brothers at house No. 40). The register: **Otočac Deaths 1834–1858**, the second item of waypoint 9RK1-Y4C (Otočac births 1846–1858 and deaths 1834–1858, 218 images; image 218 is the target card of the next book). Latin tabular to 1849, Croatian 1849–51, Latin again from 1852; **the village and house are entered with each death only from 1852**.

## What was found: his wife

**Image 140, right-hand page (printed page 100), protocol no. 40** (read at full size): [snapshot](/snapshots/#otocac-death-1843-04-anna-uxor-nicolai-kraljic)

> «**1843 Aprilis 27** · **Anna consors Nicolai Kraljić Confiniarii** · **33 annorum** · Inflammatio Pulmonum»

(Anna, wife of Nicolaus Kraljić, border-guardsman; aged 33; inflammation of the lungs.) The tree's **Anna Žubrinić Xubrinich, born 5 September 1809**, would be 33 years 7 months. She is the mother of Georgius (19 April 1836, «Anna … nata Xubrinich», Prozor No. 40), Petronilla (1838) and Vincentia (12 March 1840), so she was alive in March 1840, and she is struck in the census («Anna uxor Nicolai · 5 7bris 1809»). **So Anna died on 27 April 1843, and Felicitas née Banich, entered at house No. 53, is a later wife of Nicolaus** (not yet proved to be his: the census names her «uxor Nicolai»).

**Caution:** the 1843 table has no village or house, so the identification is by the husband's name, the wife's age and the family's dates, not a house number. A **Nicolaus Kralić of Dubrava No. 24** (died August 1855, aged 33, wife Lucia née Orešković, children baptised 1845–1855) is another man and cannot be Anna's husband, since Lucia was alive after 1843.

## What was not found: Nicolaus's own death

**Full-resolution pass, completed 17 October 2026.** **Images 140–217, the whole of the book from 27 April 1843 to its last page («Explicit Anni 1858», image 217), were read by eye, entry by entry, at about half source size** (both pages of every image, full height; 78 pages downloaded in the background and read from sheets, see [tools/familysearch.md](https://github.com/daviddef/TheBlazevic/blob/main/tools/familysearch.md); the strip joins checked on a separate seam montage). **No Nicolaus Kraljić (Kraljich, Kralić, Kraljić) died in it**, so **the tree's «d. 1845» is tested across all of 1843–1858 and not found.** Kraljićs seen, none Nicolaus: Anna (140R); Ursula, daughter of Carolus Kraljićević (144R); Martinus Kralić of Dubrava 24, 56 (1853) and his widow Maria; **Nicolaus Kralić of Dubrava 24, 33, August 1855**, the other Nicolaus (wife Lucia née Orešković); **Georgius Kraljić's four children of Prozor No. 40** (two in January 1855, Maria on 18 and Catharina on 21 August 1855, cholera); and a **Franciscus Kralić «adoptatus Dujmović», drummer, of Prozor No. 34** (wife Maria, 41, and son Joannes, 14, in February 1856; a daughter Anna in June 1856; wife Catharina, 40, on 6 January 1857; himself, 44, «son of Martinus and Anna», in August 1858), another Kraljić taken into a Dujmović house, as Nicolaus was at house No. 53 (nothing here says they were related). Because Nicolaus was «adopt. Kraljić» in the Dujmović household, the Dujmović entries were also looked at: the OCR flagger marked eleven Dujmović lines in 1843–1851 and none is a Nicolaus; **OCR catches only about half the lines, so that check is a pointer, and my own reading was for the Kraljić name, not for every Dujmović.**

**Other readings from the pass.** «Martha Banich uxor Nicolai Confiniarii», aged 45, early 1846 (image 149), a Banić wife of some Nicolaus, not Felicitas (who is alive in the census); and a «Martha, daughter of the late Nicolaus Žubrinić, unmarried» (150).

**Continued, 17 October 2026 (later): the next book, Otočac Deaths 1859–1872** (item 4 of the census film, waypoint 9R2D-16X; **images 336–379**; 335 and 381–384 are covers and target cards, 380 a blank ruled leaf). It is a Latin ruled table with village and house for every death, about 80–110 deaths a year. **I read the sheets of images 336–379 by eye, quarter-page strips at about 0.6 of source size, for a Kraljić (Kraljich, Kralić, Kraljić) of Prozor or any Nicolaus, and found no Nicolaus Kraljić.** The book **ends in mid-June 1872** (the last entry, no. 52, is 13 June 1872, on image 379). Kraljićs seen, none Nicolaus: «Anna, daughter of Michael and Francisca Kraljić, Prozor No. 40», aged 22 days, 14 February 1866 (image 357, the surname partly smudged), so Prozor 40 was still a Kraljić house, and the people of the same house in 1855 (above); and a **Kranjčević** family of Prozor (a different surname, which the hand and the OCR make easy to confuse with Kraljić, so a «Kraljić» death could hide among them; I did not treat them as his). Limits: read at reduced scale, not entry by entry at full size as images 140–217 were; the 1859 pages were read from the sheet only.

**So, as far as the filmed Otočac deaths go (27 April 1843 to 13 June 1872), no Nicolaus Kraljić is found.** He was alive in April 1843. The tree's «1845» has nothing behind it. What remains: **a death after June 1872** (not on this film; Otočac deaths from mid-1872 are in books not on FamilySearch's Croatian church-book collection as far as this archive has found), **a death away from Otočac**, or **an entry under a form this reading missed**. This is now a question for the parish (letter 4f) and, if one exists, for the *status animarum* after 1846.

**What is left.** The book stops at the end of 1858, so **he died after 1858 or away from Otočac, or is entered under a name this reading did not recognise.** 1835–April 1843 is not needed (he was alive in April 1843). The next book (deaths 1859 on, the third item's later part and the Croatian registers) is the next place; the parish letter (4f) also asks.

The earlier, reduced-scale pass, kept for the record:

I scanned the **name column of every spread of images 140–217 (April 1843 to the end of 1858)** at reduced scale, looking for a Kraljić (any spelling) of Prozor No. 40 or No. 53, and also images 143–145 (the winter of 1843–44). **No Nicolaus Kraljić died there.** Limits: the scan was at about 0.7 of a page's width per column, not entry by entry at full size; the bottom row or two of some pages (images 141–144, 148, 149) were cut off; and the register before April 1843 (images about 100–139, 1835–1843) was **not scanned**, although he was alive in 1843 (his wife is «consors Nicolai», and Felicitas is entered later). So:

* **The tree's «d. 1845» is not supported.** The census «845» is not a death date, and no 1845 death of a Nicolaus Kraljić of Prozor was found.
* He may have died after 1858 (the film ends there), or away from Otočac (a border-guardsman could be posted or move), or under a spelling or description not recognised at that scale. This is **not** proof that he did not die here.
* Kraljićs seen in the same register, none Nicolaus: **Georgius Kraljić of Prozor No. 40** (the census's «Georgius b. 1814», very probably) lost four children in the cholera of August 1855 (Georgius, Anna, Maria, Catharina, «e Prozor Nº 40»), so house No. 40 stayed a Kraljić house; a **Franciscus Kralić** of the Lešće century, aged 38, in 1852; and a **Carolus Kraljićević** whose daughter Ursula died in 1844.

## What it changes

* **Anna Žubrinić** has a death date and age, and the tree's wife is found on three documents: the census (birth date), her son's baptism (maiden name, house) and this entry.
* **Felicitas née Banich** (house No. 53) is, on this reading, his second wife, married after April 1843 and before the census was closed (1846): the marriage is in no filmed register (the marriage books on film begin in 1859). The parish letter asks for it.
* **Nicolaus's death** remains the open question, with the tree's 1845 withdrawn as a source. See the [corrections](/corrections/).
