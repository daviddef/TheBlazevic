# The Michaels of Otočac, by house number

15 September 2026. Work list row 1. The 1846–58 Otočac baptism book writes the
household into its *Conditio* column, so the column can be harvested; the record
index gives the image ARK of every indexed entry, so each one opens at the page.
**Fifteen** indexed Žubrinić baptisms in that window have a father whose name is
a Michael variant — *Michael · Michaël · Miho · Mio · Miha · Miko*. The work list
said seven; the index says fifteen.

Nine of the fifteen are still unread. FamilySearch closed again before they could
be reached.

## Four things this establishes

**The Michaels are not one man, and now they are not one village either.** In
four years the register puts a Michael Žubrinić at **Obilje**, at **Poljica**, at
**Šumećica** and at **Prozor** — four villages, four households, four different
wives. Question 4d asked whether the twenty-three Michael baptisms are one man or
many. They are many, and the *Conditio* column is what separates them.

**Šumećica N° 5 is new.** The houses known before today were N° 2, N° 10 and
N° 24. **Miho Žubrinić and Mara Marković are at Šumećica Br. 5 on 4 November
1849**, and that is a household this archive did not have.

**Two Žubrinići married Žubrinići.** Michael × *Catharina nata Žubrinich* (1847)
and Miho × *Kata rodjena Žubrinić* (1850). The index carries neither surname —
both mothers appear there with a given name only. The register gives them.

**One of them was the village headman.** The 1847 Conditio is not a trade but a
standing: ***Senior populi Obilje*** — elder of the people of Obilje.

## Two cautions for whoever reads the other nine

**The book changes language.** The 1848 openings are Latin — *Rusticus e Poljica
N° 13*. The 1849 and 1850 openings are **Croatian** — *Leta 1850 dne 6e Travnja
u Župničkoj Cerkvi prisvetoga Trojstva u Otočcu bi karstjen*, and the Conditio
becomes ***Graničar iz …*** with ***Br.*** for *N°*. A reader looking only for
Latin forms will walk past whole stretches of this book.

**The numbering practice starts inside the book.** July and August 1846 carry no
village and no number at all — *Agricola Confiniarius*, *Sutor*, *Miles
gregarius*, bare. By December 1846 the villages and numbers are there, and from
1847 every entry on a page carries one. So the harvest is real, but it does not
begin at the first page.

## And a caution about method

The ARK opens the **spread**, not the row, and the entry is as often on the
right-hand page as the left — the 29 April 1847 entry is the seventh row of the
right page, and the 6 April 1850 entry is the **last row of the left page**,
below where a first crop of that page stopped. Worse, **the opening does not sit
in the same place in every frame**: column fractions measured on one image land
off the paper on the next. Survey each frame before cropping it.

The Luce entry of 30 June 1850 is marked *provisional* for the reason this
archive marks anything provisional: the number was read from a two-page composite
displayed at about 27 per cent, and the session was cut off before it could be
cropped at the page. It is very likely Šumećica Br. 10 — the same household as
Thomas 1846, *Mio* being *Michael* and *Jele* being *Helena Orešković* — but
likely is not read.
