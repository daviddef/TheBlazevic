# Ured Riječke nadbiskupije — a holdings question, not a research request

**Status: DRAFTED 21 September 2026, NOT SENT.** The email is
`requests/EMAIL-nadbiskupija.txt`. **Sending is David's call**, and the postal or
email address must be confirmed from the archdiocese's own directory first —

## Why this letter exists

[Row 52](/worklist/) named a **third kind of holder** that nobody had tried: not
a state archive, not a parish. **One question is now asked from two directions
and answered from neither.**

* **The parish says the books went to the Archdiocese.** vlč. Jakov Filipović,
  who administers Sv. Jakov Krmpote from Ledenice, replied on 15 September 2026:
  *«Matice i arhiva svih župa kojim upravljam je predata u Riječku Nadbiskupiju.
  Koliko mi je poznato oni su istu predali na skrb Državnoga Arhiva.»*
* **The State Archive at Rijeka says it does not do research of this
  complexity** — and **never said what it holds**. That is fair: it was asked to
  search, not to list.

**So nobody has said whether these books survive or where they are.** The
Archdiocese is the one party that *handed them over*, and a transfer leaves a
record.

## What makes the ask answerable

**It is deliberately not a request to look anything up.** The three asks are
*does this volume survive, and who has it* — plus a fourth that would settle all
three at once:

> **the primopredajni zapisnik**, the inventory of what the Archdiocese passed to
> the State Archive, for the Crikvenica deanery or for Sv. Jakov Krmpote.

**That is the point of writing here rather than back to Rijeka.** An archive that
will not search on a description will often produce a volume that is *named*.
This archive does not yet know the names.

## The three asks, all of them film-gaps already proved

| | what is asked | why the film cannot answer |
|---|---|---|
| **1a** | Sv. Jakov Krmpote baptisms **before 1879** | catalogued *1815–1896*; **the film begins 1879** — image 10 is page 2, «Godine 1879», checked leaf by leaf |
| **1b** | **Any** Sv. Jakov Krmpote marriage register | **none is filmed at all** — not a gap in a range, the event type is absent |
| **2a** | Rijeka baptisms covering **1 March 1892** | the filmed volumes run …1890–1891 · **1900–1901**…, and the Kazalo skips **1889–1895**. A gap by image *and* by index |
| **3a** | the **transfer inventory** | answers 1a, 1b and 2a together, and converts a description into a shelfmark |

**What hangs on 1a**: Juraj «Jurko» Blažević, named in the Krmpote register in
1895 at his son Marijan's baptism, page 168 entry 26. His own baptism names his
father outright, and **five further generations rest on a link no document yet
states**.

## What was reused and what is new

The asks were already refined in `requests/sent/EMAIL-rijeka.txt` and
`EMAIL-zupa-krmpote.txt`. **What is new is the framing and the covering
paragraph** — who sent us, what the State Archive did and did not say, and the
reframing from *please find this entry* to *please tell me what you handed over*.
That is the thing Rijeka's refusal did not cover and an archdiocesan chancery can
answer from its own records.

**The Senj and Milka death asks are deliberately left out.** Those are civil and
state-archive business and do not belong in a letter about parish transfers; a
letter that asks for everything gets answered about nothing.
