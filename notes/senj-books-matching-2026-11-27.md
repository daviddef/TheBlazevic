The books were first copied privately and their FamilySearch name index set beside the tree. The index only says where to look: every entry below was then read on the page image at full size, and what is written here is the page. The books were not walked for this, and a person not listed has not been shown to be absent.
Snapshots: `/snapshots/`, one per entry. Disagreements with the tree are on `/corrections/`. The tree is unchanged.

## Probable, not proved

- The godparents of Lucija Belobarbić in November 1880, «Joso Papić» and «Tonka Prpić», are probably the Josip Papić and Antonija Perpić who married in May 1885. The entry gives no more than the names, a trade and the date.
- The Maria Belobarbić who died aged seven in November 1882 is probably the Maria Matthia born in November 1875.
- Andreas Belobarić with Joanna, and Andre Belobarbić with Ivka, are probably one couple.

## Not settled

- The bride of 19 January 1884. The age fits Mathia, born 1857; pencil notes in later hands name Christina, born 1862, whom a death entry of 1863 appears to bury.
- Whether Ivan Prpić and Matia, parents of Olga Oktavia in 1886, are the couple married in 1884.
- The parents the tree gives Nikola Žubrinić, born 1880.
- The death year on the slip pasted beside Marijan Krmpotić is read as 1952 for the reasons on `/corrections/`; the Zagreb death register would settle it.
