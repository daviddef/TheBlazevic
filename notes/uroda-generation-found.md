# Ahnentafel 54's baptism, his parents, and six siblings

15 September 2026. **A generation has been added to the direct line, and the
family it adds is not the family this archive thought it had.**

## The route, which is the reusable part

The **Karlobag births are in the FamilySearch record index**, and the index
payload carries **the image ARK of every entry**. So the search service

    /service/search/hr/v2/personas?count=100&f.collectionId=2040054
      &offset=N&q.surname=Uroda

returns 257 Urode across the collection; filtering in hand on the place string
**Karlobag** leaves **41**, and sorting them by date produces a family at once.

**No film had to be hunted.** The *Kazalo* on DGS 5481648 was never found — the
END target for *Rođeni 1771–1804* sits at image 593 with no index behind it —
and it did not matter.

## Read at the page

Image ark **`3:1:3QSQ-G99C-L991-3`**, folio 36/37, **Anno Dñi 1777**, right-hand
page, under ***Aprilis***:

> *Ego **Joës Antonius Ivankovich, Parochus Loci**, baptizavi Infantem **heri
> natum** ex **Georgio, et Maria Uroda, Conjugibus, hujus Loci**, cui impositum
> est Nomen **Joannis Georgij**. Patrini fuerunt **Ill[ustrissi]mus D[omi]nus
> **Pro-Colonellus Joannes** [de Z…]** et ejus D[omi]na **Maria** Conjux,
> D[omi]ni **Centurionis Danielis** …*

**Three things in one sentence.**

**1. The tree's date is the baptism, not the birth.** Ahnentafel 54 is carried
as *born 22 April 1777*. The register says ***infantem heri natum*** — an infant
**born the day before**. **He was born 21 April 1777 and baptised on the 22nd.**

**2. His parents are named.** ***Georgius and Maria Uroda, married, of this
place.*** That is **ahnentafel 108 and 109**, and neither existed here yesterday.

**3. The godfather is a lieutenant-colonel.** ***Illustrissimus Dominus
Pro-Colonellus Joannes***, with his lady **Maria**, and a **Centurion Daniel**
named beside them. **Nobody asks a *Pro-Colonellus* to stand for a fisherman's
son.**

## And it corroborates the crypt

Read the day before: **Dominus Georgius Uroda died 10 September 1791 and was
buried in his own crypt before the doors of the parish church.** The dates fit
without strain — he fathers children from 1764 to 1784 and is dead by 1791 — and
the standing fits too. ***Dominus*** in the death register, ***Illustrissimus
Dominus Pro-Colonellus*** standing godfather at his son's baptism, **the village
judge** witnessing his other son's wedding in 1806.

**This archive has carried the Urode as fishermen.** They were that by 1834 and
1846, when ahnentafel 55 is buried as *«uxor **piscatoris** Georgii Uroda»* and
ahnentafel 54 as *«annorum 72, **piscator**»*. **In the 1770s they were not.**
That is a family coming down in the world across two generations, and it is now
visible in the documents rather than inferred.

## What this does to the 1806 marriage — and the answer was already on disk

The marriage read the day before — **10 February 1806**, *«Joannis filii
defuncti Dñi Georgij Uroda ac Mariæ filiæ Nicolai Barletich»* — now has two
candidates, both sons of a dead Georgius: **Joannis Georgÿ, baptised 1777**, who
would be twenty-eight, and **Joannis, baptised 1784**, who would be twenty-one.

**It is the younger one, and this archive had already read the proof without
knowing what it proved.**

Ahnentafel 27's baptism, **Karlobag, 23 April 1810, entry 23**, read earlier this
month:

> *Mathiam, Georgiam hodie natam a **Georgio et Maria Uroda** [conjugibus hujus
> parochiæ] baptizavi. Patrini fuerunt Jacobus Blažević et **Maria conjux
> Joannis Uroda**.*

**The godmother is «Maria, wife of Joannes Uroda».** Four years after a Joannes
Uroda married a Maria, a Maria who is the wife of a Joannes Uroda stands
godmother to the child of a **Georgius** Uroda. **They are two different
couples, and the obvious relation between them is brother and brother.**

So:

* **ahnentafel 54 is the 1777 child**, *Joannis **Georgÿ***, who is called
  **Georgius** in every later record — the 1810 baptism of his daughter, his
  wife's death in 1834, his own in 1846.
* **the 1806 groom is the 1784 child**, plain *Joannis*, who marries **Maria
  Barletich** — and whose wife then stands for her brother-in-law's daughter.

**The tree's «Maria Gerkacs» for ahnentafel 55 therefore survives.** The doubt
raised yesterday is answered, and answered from a page this archive had already
read. **A brother's wife in a godparent column did it.**

## What is now safe to say

**Ahnentafel 54 — Joannis Georgius Uroda — born 21 April 1777, baptised 22
April 1777 at Karlobag, son of Georgius Uroda and Maria, with six siblings.**
Read at the page, from a register, with the priest's own hand.
