# Daniel Špalj: three children at Senj (1852, 1854, 1857), no baptism of his own, no marriage — an index search, 17 October 2026

The tree's **Daniel Špalj** has no dates and no place, a wife **Francesca Prpić**, and children **Margaritte (1871, born at Krmpote 152), Stanislava (1873) and Mathea (1875)**. This archive's earlier reading ([senj-baptism-index.md](senj-baptism-index.md), item 6) found him at Senj in 1854 and 1857. This note records what the full search for **his own baptism and his marriage** found. Index only for the baptisms; the marriage volume is the archive's own Senj walk.

## What the index holds for a father Daniel (Danielis) Špalj

| baptised | child | parents | index record |
|---|---|---|---|
| **26 February 1852** | **Matthia** (the first of the name) | Danielis *Spaly* × Francisca *Penpic* [Prpić] | QKMJ-S8Y7 / S8YQ |
| 1 December 1854 | Lucia | Danielis Spalj × Franciscaina Perpić | QKMJ-SSNK |
| 22 February 1857 | **Mathia** (the second) | Danielis Spalj × Francisca Prpić | QKMJ-S31G |

**This adds a third child and moves the marriage earlier:** the Matthia of **1852** is under a spelling («Spaly», «Penpic») the earlier sweep did not capture, and the **Mathia of 1857 is a second child of the same name**, so the first died or the name was reused. **Daniel and Francesca were married before February 1852** and were at **Senj** in 1852, 1854 and 1857.

## What the index does not have

* **No baptism of a Daniel Špalj** in the collection, searched under **ten spellings** (Spalj, Špalj, Spal, Spaly, Spaljich, Špaljić, Spalich, Szpalj, Spalyj) with the given name Daniel/Danielis/Danijel, births **1815–1845**, anywhere: nothing. The Senj baptisms of 1820–1858 are indexed, so **he was not baptised at Senj in those years**. The only Špalj baptisms of 1825–1840 are at Bribir, Smiljan and Gospić, none a Daniel.
* **No marriage of Daniel and Francesca** in the Senj marriage volume (1820–1858, read entirely in the walk of 13–15 October; the walk's negatives are weak). The Senj marriage volume holds Špalj entries of 1747, 1814 and 1820 and deaths of Špalj in 1820, 1828, 1831 and 1836, none of them his.
* The **Krmpote births begin in 1879** and the film for Krmpote earlier does not exist, so a baptism at Krmpote or Ledenice before the 1850s is on no film.

## Result

**Daniel Špalj's baptism is not reachable on any film or index this archive can open**, and the better reading is that he was born about 1820–1830 at **Krmpote, Vodice or Ledenice** (where his children later lived: Krmpote 152) and came to Senj before 1852. Two routes remain: the Senj marriage entry of about 1850–51 (a second read of the 1850–51 pages of the Senj volume, which the walk read once) and an entry in his children's later registers that names his parents (the Krmpote births from 1879 name grandparents only by chance).

## The Vodice marriage index read for Daniel's marriage, 17 October 2026: not found, and a correction

Vodice marriages 1826–1857 (waypoint 9R24-W3N, 395 images). Images 88–90 are a name index by year, one line per marriage («Giacomo Antoyin con Sinca Sladojev», in Italian forms of the given names, surnames Italianised). **Read: 1844 to 1848 (the left page of image 89, at about 4x) and 1849 to 1851 (the right page, seen earlier): no line has a Spalj, Špalj or Spagl groom and a Perpić/Prpić bride, and no Daniel.** 1852–1857 (the rest of images 89–90) were not read for him.

**A correction to the first version of this note**, which suggested that the «Sperljan/Spertjan/Sperglian» forms in those lines might be the local spelling of Špalj. **They are not read as Špalj and there is no ground for it:** Sperljan (Šperljan) recurs as a separate family surname in the lines of 1844–1851, with its own brides and grooms. The suggestion is withdrawn.

* **Result:** Daniel Špalj did not marry at Vodice in 1844–1851, so far as the index lines go (an index line with a misspelt surname would be missed). Together with the Senj marriage volume and the index, **his marriage and baptism are on none of the books read**, and the births (Ledenice 1820–1861, 9R2F-4WP, 510 images, mixed layout) were not opened for him.
* **The index for 1852–1856 was then read too** (image 89 right, image 90 left and the first part of its right page, at about 4x): again no Špalj groom with a Prpić bride and no Daniel. The surnames there are the Vodice families (Juričić, Martinčić, Škocić, Udovičić, Šperljan, Spagna and others) in Italianised spellings, and a Špalj written in some Italian form I did not recognise could have been missed. **The end of 1856 and 1857 were then read as well (image 90, right page, to the last line of 1857, entries 1–14 of that year and the blank leaf below): none.** So the whole index of images 88–90, **1844 to 1857, was read for him.**
* **What this closes:** because the Senj baptisms of 1852 show Daniel and Francesca already married, the window that matters is **before 1852**, and the Vodice index covers it (1844–1851). **He did not marry at Vodice in that window as far as the index lines go**, and not in Senj (the walked marriage volume). The marriage is not on the books read; the parish could be Krmpote–Vodice's neighbour **Ledenice** (marriages 1826–1865, waypoint 9R2F-4WP, 9R29-W3D) or Otočac, neither indexed.
* **Left for the next sitting:** the Ledenice marriages 1826–1865, and Krmpote 152 as the house of his daughter's birth in 1871 (Krmpote births begin in 1879).

## Ledenice marriages opened, not read (17 October 2026)

Images 160, 200 and 240 of the Ledenice marriages/births/deaths film (waypoint 9R29-W3D, 504 images) were opened to see the hand. It is **dense multi-column Latin cursive, with no name index** and several registers interleaved; a read for one surname across marriages 1826–1865 would be a page-by-page walk of a few hundred images at high zoom, with a poor chance at the first pass. **Not attempted.** If wanted, start with the marriage section for 1848–1851 (the years just before the 1852 baptism), where a Špalj × Prpić marriage would fall.
