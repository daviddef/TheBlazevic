# Arcanum Digitheca — the Hungarian press, opened for the first time

14 September 2026. Lika and the Kvarner were administered from Budapest, and
this archive's own place strings still say so: it carries *Ledenice, Modruš-Fiume,
Magyarország* and *Otočac, Komitat Lika-Krbava*. Arcanum holds the press and the
official publications of exactly that administration, and **no archive in this
estate had ever run a search there.**

**What is readable and what is not.** Arcanum returns a full-text *snippet* for
every hit without a subscription; the page image behind it is paywalled
(«You have no active subscription»). Everything below is a snippet, quoted as
it was returned, with the OCR's own errors left in. **None of it has been read
at the page.** Nothing here has been added to the tree.

---

## The tobacco factory, counted

This archive's most-quoted finding is read out of the Senj marriage register:
the brides' occupation column goes from *tvorničarka* on three of five in 1909
to seven of eight in 1919. It had no figure for the factory itself.

> **A Pallas nagy lexikona, vol. 16 (1897)** — «Zengg (Senj, Segnia) szab. kir.
> város … kir. révhivatala, tengeri halászati bizottsága, **dohánygyára 120
> munkás**»

> **Révai Nagy Lexikona, vol. 19 (1926)** — «… tengeri halászati bizottsága,
> **dohánygyára 500 munkás**»

**One hundred and twenty workers at the end of the 1890s; five hundred by the
1920s.** In a town of a few thousand people that is not a factory growing, it
is a town changing employer — and it is the arithmetic behind the marriage
register's occupation column.

## The Žubrinići of Otočac, in print

> **Mihók-féle Magyar Compass 36/1 (1908)**, *I. Horvát-Szlavonország*, p. 1298 —
> «**Srpska štedionica dioničarsko društvo u Otočcu**, 1904 … Vorstand Oskar
> Gara, Otocac, Komitat Lika-Krbava … Buchhalter Stjepan Meniga, **Kassier
> Venzeslav Žubrinić**, Hilfsbuchhalter Juraj Milošević»

A Žubrinić as **cashier of a joint-stock savings bank** at Otočac, founded 1904.

> **Wiener Zeitung, May 1862** — «**Daniel Žubrinić, Handelsmann in Otočac**»,
> in a column of notices that also names the «Regimentsgericht in Otočać, Ungarn
> Militärgrenze»

A Žubrinić **merchant** at Otočac in 1862, beside the Frontier regimental court.

> **Neuigkeits-Welt-Blatt, October 1885** — «Diözese Zengg … Ernannt wurden zu
> Kooperatoren … **Daniel Zubrinic in Otocac**»
> **Militär-Zeitung, 1885** — «**Daniel Zubrinić**, Weltpriester der römisch-
> katholischen Diöcese Zengg-Modrus»

A Žubrinić **priest of the Senj-Modruš diocese**, appointed to Otočac in 1885 —
the same diocese whose registers this archive reads.

> **Salzburger Wacht, Arbeiter-Zeitung and Vorarlberger Wacht, April 1914** —
> «**Mijo Zubrinic**, der im Jahre **1886 in Otocac** … geboren ist … Polizist:
> Haben Sie einen Reisepaß? *Zubrinic:* Nein, aber einen Militärpaß … **Mijo
> Zubrinic, 1886 geboren, aus Otocac, Kom. Lika-Krbava**»

The same story carried by three Austrian papers: a man of the archive's own
surname, **born 1886 at Otočac**, stopped without a passport. This is precisely
the generation and the place where the archive holds nobody.

> **Službeni list, 29 June 1966** — «**Zubrinić Franje iz Prozora sina Jure**.
> Rok 8 dana. Općinski sud Otočac»
> **Službeni list, August 1975** — «**Zubrinić Josip iz Otočca**»

A court notice naming **Franjo Žubrinić of Prozor, son of Jure** — a patronymic,
in print, for a Prozor household.

## Senj's own men of business

> **Mihók-féle Magyar Compass 35/2 (1908)**, p. 860 and 39/2 (1912), p. 1144 —
> «Zengg. **Hrvatsko parobrodarsko družtvo na dionice** … Rukavina, Ivan
> Lončarić, **Papić Ivan** … » and later « … Vukelić Ivan, Polić Ivan, Lončarić,
> **Papić Karlo**, Budak Frane, Budak Ante»

A **Papić on the board of the Croatian steamship company of Senj**, 1907–1912.
The archive's Papići are *težaci* and tobacco workers. Same surname is not
kinship — but it is the same small town.

> **Magyarország tiszti cím- és névtára, 1898, 1901, 1903** — «Főszámvevő
> **Blažević Milán**», chief accountant of the coastal line serving
> «Zengg–Fiume–Obrovazzo–Cirkvenica–Novi»

## The Prpići of Krivi Put, in a criminal court

> **Morgenblatt (Zagreb), 1926–1928** — «der **Prpić-Bande**» · «Raubüberfall im
> Walde **Guvno zwischen Krivi Put und Brinje**, 11. Juli 1926» · «Raubüberfall
> im Walde **Lom zwischen Krivi Put und Otočac**, zum Schaden … Viehhändler
> **Ilija Butković aus Krivi Put**» · «der Hut der Prpić» · names **Smojver**
> and **Uremović**

Twenty-one hits. A robbery trial that ran for two years and names the woods,
the fairs and the cattle-dealers of the archive's own villages — and two of its
surnames, **Prpić** and **Smojver**, in the dock.

## The First World War, which this archive has never once opened

> **Verlustliste, June 1915** — «**Kalanj Ivan**, Inft., k.u. LIR Nr. 26,
> 3. Ers.Komp., **Kroatien, Brinje, 1891**, kriegsgef. Prokuplje, Serbien»
> **Verlustliste, March 1916** — «**Kalanj Franjo**, Inft., IR Nr. 79, 13. Komp.,
> **Kroatien, Brinje, 1893**, tot 6.3.1915» · «**Kalanj Mile**, LstInft., IR Nr. 79»

Austro-Hungarian casualty lists print **regiment, company, province, district,
commune and year of birth** to tell men apart — which makes them a genealogical
record by accident. **Brinje** is the parish this archive discovered a day ago
that the Kalanji actually belong to, and here are three Kalanj men of Brinje
with their birth years and what happened to them.

## And a migration nobody suspected

> **Feldkircher Anzeiger, 1902** (Vorarlberg) — «**And. Blazevic, geb. 1867 in
> Staroselo, Krmpote**, und dahin zust., verehelicht, **Maurer**» ·
> «**Sof. Blazevic, geb. 1869 in Staroselo**»

Blaževići **of Krmpote, working as masons in western Austria in 1902**, listed
with their birth years and their home commune. This archive knows an emigration
to America and one to Australia. It did not know there was one to the Alps.
