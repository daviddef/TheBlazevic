# Martin Kozina came from Carniola, and the scan was on the shelf

**21 September 2026.** A morning was spent establishing that Martin Kosina's origin could
not be found: his baptism is in no index anywhere in the world, his mother Gertruda appears
in exactly one record, and the surname clusters in Zagorje rather than Lika. The note ended
by saying the marriage entry was **the thing to read at full resolution first**, because a
Croatian marriage register gives the groom's birthplace even where an index drops it.

**The scan was already in this archive's media library, and had been for weeks.**

    sources/media/manifest.psv
      4505845 | Martin Kosina and Angjelika Boras Marriage - 1888
      4505826 | Pavao Kosina Birth - 1894
      4503210 | Kosina-Blazevic and Kosina-Anka Marriage

**All three on disk. No page used them and no reading cited them.** The reader who asked
«you haven't taken them to our site?» was looking at the same files.

## The answer, in one cell

> **Senj marriages, page 209, entry 1** — **«1888 dne 12 siečnja»**, 12 January 1888
>
> **Martin KOZINA**, *trgovac* — a merchant — age **47**, bachelor
> ***Locus Originis*: «Kranjska župa sv. Martin kod KRAINBURG»**
> *Domicilii*: Senj
>
> **Angjelika Boras**, of Senj, age **30**

**Kranjska is CARNIOLA. Krainburg is the German name of KRANJ.** So Martin Kozina was born
in the **parish of St Martin near Kranj, in what is now SLOVENIA**, about 1841, and came
down to Senj as a trader.

**It explains every negative of the morning at once.** No Croatian baptism, because he was
not baptised in Croatia. The surname spelled **Kozina** with a *z*, because that is the
Slovene spelling. And his mother called **Gertruda** — a German name that looked out of
place on the Velebit coast and is entirely ordinary in a Habsburg crownland whose towns
carried German names.

## And the father is absent on the page itself

The parents' column reads:

> **Gertruda Kozina, *posjednica*** — a property-owner
> **—**
> *Josip Boras, gradski stražar, i Maria rodjena Kabalin, rimo-katolici*

**A dash stands where the groom's father should be.** The tree's missing father is not a
gap in the tree: **the register records the absence.** Gertruda is entered with a standing
of her own — *posjednica*, a woman who owned property — and no husband.

**What it does not say is why.** Illegitimacy and a long-dead father look the same in that
column.

## The rest of the entry

**Witnesses**: *Mile Crnković, trgovac* — and **Ivan Boras, «kr. pomorske straže
nadstojnik»**, superintendent of the royal maritime guard. That is the bride's brother,
whom this archive already had in the customs service. **He signed at his sister's wedding.**

**Celebrant**: *Mirko Babić, kapelan*. **Married by dispensation** of the Senj Ordinariate,
7 January 1888, number 38, with **the banns read once instead of three** — the same
irregularity as the Boras marriage of 1857, which took place in a private house.

## And then two brothers married four days apart

The 1919 scan holds **both Kosina marriages on one opening**, page 359:

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| **44** | **20 November 1919** | **Pavao Kosina**, *trgovac*, b. 15/III 1894 × **Anka Blažević**, *radnica u tvornici duhana*, b. **7/X 1893** |
| **46** | **24 November 1919** | **Antun Kosina**, *trgovac*, b. 13/V 1891 × **Anka Kasunić**, *švelja*, of **Dol**, b. **11/VII 1893** |

**Four days.** Two brothers, two brides both called Anka, both weddings in the same
fortnight in the same church.

**Every date agrees with the tree to the day** — Anka Blažević 7 October 1893, Pavao
15 March 1894, Antun 13 May 1891.

**Martin is daggered in both entries.** **† Martin Kosina** — so he was dead before November
1919, which is the first thing this archive has ever known about his death.

**And all three men are *trgovci*.** Martin came from Carniola as a merchant and both his
sons were merchants after him. Angelika was a *šilica*, Anka Blažević a tobacco-factory
worker, Anka Kasunić a *švelja* — a seamstress.

## One thing that does not fit, recorded rather than resolved

**Anka Blažević's *locus originis* is entered as «OTOČAC».** This archive has had her
baptised in the parish of **Sv. Jakov Krmpote** with her siblings Marijan and Ruža. Her
mother Tereza Žubrinić was born at Otočac, so a clerk may have written the mother's parish;
or the household may genuinely have been at Otočac in 1893, between Ljubomir's birth at
**Rijeka** in March 1892 and Marijan's at Krmpote in 1895.

**That household moved three times in three years if all three are right**, and this
archive does not yet know which of them is wrong.
