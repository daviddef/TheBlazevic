# Otočac, 27 April 1843: Anna, wife of Nicolaus Kraljić, aged 33 — and no death of Nicolaus himself in 1843–1858 (17 October 2026)

Follows [otocac-census-kraljich-1807.md](otocac-census-kraljich-1807.md) and [otocac-death-1821-jacobus-kralich.md](otocac-death-1821-jacobus-kralich.md). The question was **when and where Nicolaus Kraljić died**: the tree has «d. 1845», which the census page does not state (it carries only a «845» beside the struck rows of the brothers at house No. 40). The register: **Otočac Deaths 1834–1858**, the second item of waypoint 9RK1-Y4C (Otočac births 1846–1858 and deaths 1834–1858, 218 images; image 218 is the target card of the next book). Latin tabular to 1849, Croatian 1849–51, Latin again from 1852; **the village and house are entered with each death only from 1852**.

## What was found: his wife

**Image 140, right-hand page (printed page 100), protocol no. 40** (read at full size): [snapshot](/snapshots/#otocac-death-1843-04-anna-uxor-nicolai-kraljic)

> «**1843 Aprilis 27** · **Anna consors Nicolai Kraljić Confiniarii** · **33 annorum** · Inflammatio Pulmonum»

(Anna, wife of Nicolaus Kraljić, border-guardsman; aged 33; inflammation of the lungs.) The tree's **Anna Žubrinić Xubrinich, born 5 September 1809**, would be 33 years 7 months. She is the mother of Georgius (19 April 1836, «Anna … nata Xubrinich», Prozor No. 40), Petronilla (1838) and Vincentia (12 March 1840), so she was alive in March 1840, and she is struck in the census («Anna uxor Nicolai · 5 7bris 1809»). **So Anna died on 27 April 1843, and Felicitas née Banich, entered at house No. 53, is a later wife of Nicolaus** (not yet proved to be his: the census names her «uxor Nicolai»).

**Caution:** the 1843 table has no village or house, so the identification is by the husband's name, the wife's age and the family's dates, not a house number. A **Nicolaus Kralić of Dubrava No. 24** (died August 1855, aged 33, wife Lucia née Orešković, children baptised 1845–1855) is another man and cannot be Anna's husband, since Lucia was alive after 1843.

## What was not found: Nicolaus's own death

I scanned the **name column of every spread of images 140–217 (April 1843 to the end of 1858)**, looking for a Kraljić (any spelling) of Prozor No. 40 or No. 53, and also images 143–145 (the winter of 1843–44). **No Nicolaus Kraljić died there.** Limits: the scan was at about 0.7 of a page's width per column, not entry by entry at full size; the bottom row or two of some pages (images 141–144, 148, 149) were cut off; and the register before April 1843 (images about 100–139, 1835–1843) was **not scanned**, although he was alive in 1843 (his wife is «consors Nicolai», and Felicitas is entered later). So:

* **The tree's «d. 1845» is not supported.** The census «845» is not a death date, and no 1845 death of a Nicolaus Kraljić of Prozor was found.
* He may have died after 1858 (the film ends there), or away from Otočac (a border-guardsman could be posted or move), or under a spelling or description not recognised at that scale. This is **not** proof that he did not die here.
* Kraljićs seen in the same register, none Nicolaus: **Georgius Kraljić of Prozor No. 40** (the census's «Georgius b. 1814», very probably) lost four children in the cholera of August 1855 (Georgius, Anna, Maria, Catharina, «e Prozor Nº 40»), so house No. 40 stayed a Kraljić house; a **Franciscus Kralić** of the Lešće century, aged 38, in 1852; and a **Carolus Kraljićević** whose daughter Ursula died in 1844.

## What it changes

* **Anna Žubrinić** has a death date and age, and the tree's wife is found on three documents: the census (birth date), her son's baptism (maiden name, house) and this entry.
* **Felicitas née Banich** (house No. 53) is, on this reading, his second wife, married after April 1843 and before the census was closed (1846): the marriage is in no filmed register (the marriage books on film begin in 1859). The parish letter asks for it.
* **Nicolaus's death** remains the open question, with the tree's 1845 withdrawn as a source. See the [corrections](/corrections/).
