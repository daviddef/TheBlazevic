JURAJ "JURKO" BLAŽEVIĆ, a *seljak* of Smokvica house 114, is entered in your

**Everything above Juraj — five further generations, back to a Mateša Blažević
born about 1750 — rests on a link nothing yet states.**

  1a. His BAPTISM, most likely **1855-1870** — before the film begins.
      This names his father outright, and it is the single entry I most want.

  1b. His MARRIAGE to TEREZA ŽUBRINIĆ (born Otočac, 29 November 1867),

  1c. His DEATH, before December 1920 — his son's marriage entry of that

2. A household that stood godparent twice and is in no tree
===========================================================================

Reading the godparent column of your baptisms — a column I had never read — I
found JANDRE (Andrija) BUTORAC and his wife MARIJA standing twice for children
of Toma Blažević at Smokvica 114:

    25 July 1882    Ivan Blažević — "Pere ?ećanić i Marija Butorac,
                    supruga Jandrina"
    25 October 1884 Antonija "Tonka" Blažević — "Jandre i Marija Butorac,
                    supruzi, rimo-kat. seljaci"

Standing twice, two years apart, is a standing relationship between two
households — and Tonka is my direct ancestor. Neither Jandre nor Marija exists
anywhere in my records.

  2a. The MARRIAGE of a JANDRE (Andrija) BUTORAC, roughly 1870-1882, with the
