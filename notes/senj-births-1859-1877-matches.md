# Senj births 1859–1877: the families' own children, matched on the page (26 November 2026)

Waypoint 9R2D-JW5, after the walk of the whole book (`senj-births-1859-1877-walk.md`). People the tree holds were looked for in the captured table by parents and date, and **each match was then read again on the page image**, with a zoom wherever a date or a name differed.
It is not a full match of the tree against the book: people not listed here have not been shown to be absent.

## Six children the tree does not give to their parents

Each couple is in the tree; the child is not among their children there, and no tree person of that name and date was found. Each is on `/found/`.

- **Thomas Boras, porter, and Paulina née Belobarbić** (written Belobalbić, Belobarbić): Paulus Antonius, born 13 January 1861 (image 48, entry 5); Thomas, born 13 January 1862 (image 72, entry 4; a later note, died in the Banovina hospital at Sušak on 10 January 1937); Josephus, born 14 February 1869 (image 227, entry 18). The tree gives this couple one child, Franciscus, born 1872.
- **Benedictus Rivosechi and Maria née Fabiani**: Marianus, born 6 September 1864 (image 139, entry 95); Ignatius, born 4 September 1868 (image 218, entry 72; a later note of death in March 1933 whose day is not wholly read).
- **Georgius Grandić, gardener, and Joanna née Bašić**: Nicolaus Georgius Joannes, born 8 May 1869 (image 231, entry 40). A pencil note: «† 3.XI.1890 kao bogoslov od sušice», he died as a seminarian, of consumption.

## Left open

- **Stjepan «Stipe» Papić**, born 1864 in the tree: none of the 133 entries for children born in 1864 is a child of Georgius Papić and Mathia. Whether he is the Stephanus of 1860 entered twice in the tree, or was born elsewhere, is not settled.
- The tree also holds a Marija Prpić born 1878 to Fabijan and Ruža, beside Maria de monte Carmelo of July 1877. The births of 1878 have not been read for her; nothing is merged.

Both were checked later the same day: see `senj-papic-prpic-open-items.md`. Marija of 1878 has her own baptism; Stjepan reads as the Stephanus of 1860, which is an inference, and nothing is merged.

## How the names are written

Perpić and Prpić are both written for one couple's name (Franciscus and Anna; Fabianus and Rosalia). Anna's maiden name reads Brozinčević. Paulina's reads Belobalbić in 1861–62 and Belobarbić later. Mathia Papić's reads Pilipić, Pillipić and, in 1873, Pilipović. Spellings are for the Record Atlas catalogue; this note copies what the page has.
