# Georgi Jelličić × Margarita Drozović, Selce, 14 November 1800: ahnentafel 50 and 51 married, and ahnentafel 51's surname is DROZOVICH, not Brozović

11 October 2026. Work list rows 34 and 25. Selce marriage book 1790-1815 (DGS 5482267), image 251, left page, entry
numbered 96 in the margin (as read), one of about a dozen entries of the same mass day.

## The entry

«Die 14. 9bris A° 1800 praemissis tribus inter Missarum solemnia habitis denuntiationibus, nullo impedimento
detecto, ego Michaël Kombol C.L. junxi Matrimonio **Georgium filium Mathiae et Margaritae Jellichich**, cum
**Margarita filia Joannis et Catharinae Drozovich**, praesentibus testibus [Laurentio Alloy] et Joanne Drozovich.»

## What it gives

* **Ahnentafel 50, Georgi Jelličić, is the groom, and his parents are Mathias and Margarita Jelličić** — ahnentafel
  100 and 101, who had names in the tree and no document.
* **Ahnentafel 51, Margarita, is the bride, and her parents are Joannes and Catharina — ahnentafel 102 and 103 —
  and the family name is written DROZOVICH** (Drožović), in the bride's line, in the witness «Joanne Drozovich»
  and throughout the book (a Joannes Jelličić marries a Helena Drozovich in 1807). **The tree's «Brozović» looks
  like a misreading of the first letter**, and so does my own «Brozović?» of the Petrus baptised on 18 October 1784
  to «Joannes et Catharina Dr/Brozovich» — it is a Drozovich baptism, and Petrus is probably Margarita's brother.
* **Margarita was not born in 1784** — she was married at about sixteen, which this book and her husband's age (33)
  make unlikely. The tree's 1784 should be treated as wrong; her baptism is more likely about 1776-1781, which is
  why the 1783, 1784 and 1785 pages were clean. **Selce births 1778-1783 were then read for «Margarita … Joannis et Catharinae Drozovich» and she is not there** (search register): she was probably born before the film opens in 1778, or elsewhere.
* The marriage is 14 November 1800, so the first child of this couple dated 22 March 1809 is not their first;
  1801-08 children (and the bride's age at her death in 1842) are open.

## Caution

* «Drozovich» against «Brozovich» is a reading of a hand in which D and B differ by a small loop; the same word is
  read as Drozovich by me in every entry of this book that names it, as a witness and as a bride's surname, and
  never as Brozovich.
* The witnesses' names (Laurentio Alloy?) are read at one zoom.
* The house number 96 in the margin is as read.
