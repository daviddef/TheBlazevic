# «Od kolere» — Selce, October 1850, and the death of ahnentafel 24

15 September 2026. Work list rows 3 and 25. **Josip Antić is read, on the day
the tree gives him** — and the register says what killed him.

## The entry

Selce, LIBER DEFUNCTORUM, **page 99**, image **340**, ARK `3:1:3QSQ-G99X-13PY`.

| Column | What it says |
|---|---|
| *Tempus mortis* | **Letta 850. na 21. 8bre** — 21 October 1850 |
| *Nrus Domus* | **74** |
| *Nomen Defuncti* | **Preminu Osip Antić težak i Mare kćer** |
| *Sexus* | one masculine, one feminine |
| *Aetas* | **44 / 6** |
| remarks | ***Od kolere***, buried as above in the local common grave |

**It is one entry for two people.** Osip — *Josip*, the Latin *Josephus* — and
his six-year-old daughter Mare died the same day and were written into one line.

**The age fixes him.** 44 on 21 October 1850 puts his birth between October 1805
and October 1806; the tree gives **14 March 1806**. His widow's own entry,
already read for row 3, calls her «*Udova Maria Antić, **supruga pok. Josipa***»
— widow of the late Josip — and that is this man.

**The house is 74, not 29.** Ahnentafel 25 died in 1882 at **Selce 29**. Thirty-two
years separate the two entries and she was a widow for all of them; a move to a
married son's house is the ordinary explanation, and nothing here proves it.
Recorded as two house numbers, not one.

## What the page is

**A cholera epidemic, and the register is a casualty list.** The remarks column
repeats ***Od kolere*** down the page. Pages 98–99 carry deaths dated **30
September to 21 October 1850**, several to a day and in one stretch **six on 10
October alone**. The surnames repeat: Lončarić, Brozović, Jelličić, Antić,
Karšul, Kombol — the same names this archive reads as ancestors.

Among them, in the eleven days before Josip:

* **Pavao Antić**, house 121, 11 October
* **Maria and [a second child] of Andria Antić, težak**, house 42, 11 October
* **Andre Antić**, house 42, 11 October
* **Kate, wife, and the daughter of Ivan Antić**, house 122, 11 October
* **Jele Antić**, house 87, 11 October
* **Gerga, son of … Antić, težak**, house 74, 11 October
* **Maria, daughter of Frane Antić**, house 76, 14 October

**None of these is attached to anyone here**, and this note does not attach them.
They are listed because they show what the family walked through, and because
**house 74 appears twice** — Gerga on 11 October and Josip with Mare on the 21st.

## The page numbers, checked before any negative was written

The last three entries on page 99 are all of **21 October**, and the register
then opens at ***Mens. Februario 1851***. That looked like a missing page, so the
printed page numbers were read: image 340's right leaf is **99**, image 341's
right leaf is **101**, so the leaf between them is **100** and **the sequence is
unbroken**. There are no Selce deaths recorded between 22 October 1850 and 20
February 1851. The epidemic stopped, and so did the dying.

This check was made because [the month names in the Otočac book](otocac-marija-findric-1849.md)
had already produced one false null today. **A gap in the dates is not evidence
of a missing page until the page numbers say so.**
