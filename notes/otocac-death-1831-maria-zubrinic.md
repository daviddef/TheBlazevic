# Otočac, 24 April 1831: Maria, wife of Michael Xubrinich, aged 38, of Prozor — the tree's Maria (b. 1793) died where and as the tree says, and her husband then has a second wife, Catharina née Žubrinić

17 October 2026. Otočac deaths 1780–1836 (the third item of DGS 5498269, waypoint 9R2D-16X, 384 images; the deaths run from about image 150 to the end and are Latin prose entries with the village and house in the left margin). **Image 300, left-hand page, headed «In Aprili 1831».** Read at 3–4x. This follows [otocac-zubrinic-michael-catharina-1836-1841.md](otocac-zubrinic-michael-catharina-1836-1841.md): the marriage of Michael and Catharina is not on film (the Otočac marriage books on film begin in 1859), so the question was put to the other end: *did the tree's first wife die in 1831, and where?*

## The entry

Margin: «**Prozor** · [house number] · **24**». Text: «Obiit in Dño **Maria uxor Michaelis Xubrinich**, provisa omnibus SS. Sacramentis morientium, aetatis suae **38 annorum**, ex febri nervosa, et sepulta in Coemeterio hujus Parochialis Ecclesiae SS. Trinitatis.»

[Snapshot](https://blazevic.defranceski.com/snapshots/#otocac-death-1831-maria-zubrinic): the entry and the page, with the film, the image number and the viewer link.

## What it gives

* **The tree's first wife, Maria (b. 14 March 1793, d. 1831), is found dead on 24 April 1831, aged 38, of nervous fever** — the age agrees with the tree's birth year to the year. The tree's death year is confirmed and the day and the cause are new.
* **She lived at Prozor**, the same village as the household of Michael and Catharina née Žubrinić in the baptisms of 1836 and 1841 (and Prozor 1 of the baptism of 1850), so the tree's Michael is very probably that Michael: a widower of April 1831 who married Catharina, and bore Josephus (1836), Budimir (1839), Theresia (1841), Andreas (1844) and Joannes (1847) with her. **The tree's second wife is missing.**
* **The tree's Stephanus (born 1833) has no mother in this book:** he was born two years after Maria died, so he too is Catharina's, not Maria's.

## Cautions

* **The house number in the margin is a single stroke that reads as 1 or 7 and is not firm.** The 1836 baptism has «Prazor N° 7» (a crossed 7); a Miho × Kata née Žubrinić is at Prozor 1 in 1850; and a Michael senior × Catharina née Žubrinich is at Obilje 7 in 1847. All three are very probably the same couple moving between houses, but the number alone cannot prove it.
* The entry names the husband as Michael Xubrinich and gives no father; **the identification with the tree's Michael b. 1793 rests on the wife's age and the village.** Otočac had several Michaels (see [otocac-michael-house-numbers-2026-09-15.md](otocac-michael-house-numbers-2026-09-15.md)).
* The tree is not changed.
