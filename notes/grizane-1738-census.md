# Grižane, Stanje duša 1738 — a second household census, older and in Croatian

Located 14 September 2026. *Croatia, Church Books* → Roman Catholic → **Grižane**
→ the film holding *Births 1735-1811 · **Church Census (Stanje duša) 1738** ·
Births 1812-1857 · Deaths 1735-1790 · Marriages 1735-1858 · Deaths 1811-1817*.
**579 images**; the census sits at roughly **images 100–140**.

## What it looks like

Not a table. A list, household by household, with the relationship written out
in **Croatian** — a century before any other Croatian-language register this
archive holds. Image 120, read at full resolution:

    Andrij Malinarich
        žena njegova Marica          his wife
        hći svoja Mora               his daughter
        divička Katarina             maiden
        divička Ursula
    Matij Blazich
        žena njegova Jelena
        sin Matij · hći Katarina · sin Ivan · hći Agnia · sin Juraj
    Martin Blazich
        žena njegova Jelena · divička Barbara
    Juraj car Vulin
        žena njegova Uika · hći ovih Anicka · hći ovih Katarina
    Matij car Jurišin
        žena njegova Marica · hći ovih Ursula
    Mikula car Blaškovicha zet       son-in-law of Blašković
        hći njegova Ursula
    Lovro car Mikulin
        žena njegova Jelena · hći ovih Jelena
    Mihovil Blaškovich
        žena njegova Katarina · hći ovih Agnia · sin Ivan …

*žena njegova* wife · *sin* son · *hći* daughter · *divička* maiden ·
*zet* son-in-law · *hći ovih* "daughter of these". Two ruled columns at the
right carry tick marks, presumably confession and communion, as at Otočac.

## The by-names again — in 1738, in another parish

**Vulin · Jurišin · Mikulin** are patronymic by-names: *Juraj [son] of Vule*,
*Matij of Juraj*, *Lovro of Mikula*. One household is identified purely by
relationship — *Mikula … Blaškovicha zet*, **Blašković's son-in-law**.

That is the same naming system question 5 is about, **seventy years earlier and
thirty kilometres up the coast**. Compare Selce: *Jellichich dictis **Jurešina***
in 1809. Whether *Jurišin* and *Jurešina* are the same by-name travelling, or
simply the same obvious formation from the same common given name, is **not
claimed here** — the second is much likelier.

*A word not resolved:* the repeated element read as ***car*** in *Juraj car
Vulin*, *Matij car Jurišin*, *Lovro car Mikulin*. It may be an abbreviation
rather than a word. Left unread rather than guessed.

## The Blaževići on this page are NOT claimed

**Matij Blazich** and **Martin Blazich** head two households here with their
wives and children named. **No connection to this family is claimed or implied.**
Blažević is among the commonest surnames in Croatia; this archive's Blaževići
are a **Smokvica Krmpotska** family thirty kilometres south, and nothing
whatever links them to Grižane. It is recorded because the name is one of this
archive's own and a reader will otherwise find it and wonder.

## Why it still matters

Grižane is the parish next to **Novi Vinodolski**, which holds Klenovica and
Povile and **120 of the reachable events** on `/harvest`. A 1738 household
census of the adjoining parish is a control document: it shows what Vinodol
households looked like, in Croatian, a generation before the Kalanj records
begin — and it proves the vernacular register tradition on this coast is not a
one-off at Novi Vinodolski.
