**What it says.** The wife of Michael Gergacs is named Lucia in every baptism from March 1787 to February 1801 — five children, and no other name. Michael (born 1762) and Lucia Tomljanović married on 2 November 1785 (see [karlobag-marriage-1785-gerkacs-tomljanovic.md](karlobag-marriage-1785-gerkacs-tomljanovic.md)), and the first child follows sixteen months later. The baptisms are two years apart (1787, 1789), then **five years** (1794), then three and four (1797, 1801).

**What it does to the age — less than it first looks.** [Her death entry of 19 September 1809](karlobag-deaths-1809-1828.md) gives *annorum 34*, which a bride of 1785 cannot be. Two readings remain, and **the baptisms do not choose between them**:

* *One Lucia, age wrong.* She bore all five children and was born in the 1760s, about 40–45 in 1809; the 34 is a misreading or a misentry (44 would do). Nothing in the baptisms contradicts this, and the five-year gap of 1789–94 has natural explanations.
* *Two Lucias.* The first (Tomljanović, married 1785) bore Magdalena and Maria and died between 1789 and 1794; Michael married a second Lucia, who would have been 19 in 1794 and 34 in 1809, and bore Catharina, Bartholomæus and Mathia. A second wife with the same name is **not excluded by the index**, because the baptism entries name only «Lucia». The five-year gap sits exactly where a change would be.

So the index adds **one fact that matters and one that does not settle anything**: the children, and the fact that the question is still open. The way to settle it is a page: the 1809 age at full resolution, or a death or marriage of Michael's between 1789 and 1794 (none has been looked for).

**New children.** The tree gives Michael one child, Maria (1789). The index adds **Magdalena (1787), Catharina (1794), Bartholomæus (1797) and Mathia (1801)** — brothers and sisters of ahnentafel 55, and none is in the tree.

**What it does not say.** Where Lucia was born. The index has no baptism of a Lucia to a Paulus Tomljanović, and the Karlobag births are thin in the 1760s; see [the Tomljanović page](https://blazevic.defranceski.com/tomljanovic/).
