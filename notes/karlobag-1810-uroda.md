# Karlobag, 23 April 1810 — ahnentafel 27's baptism

Read 14 September 2026 from `photos/line/4502632-matia-pillipic-birth.jpg`,
**4,500 × 3,526**, on disk since the media sweep and marked *scanned* — an image
attached, never read out.

A prose baptismal register, *Annus Domini 1809* on the left page and *1810* on
the right, with the entry boxed in red by whoever uploaded it.

> **23. d°** — *Mathiam, Georgiam hodie natam a **Georgio et Maria Uroda**
> conjugibus hujus Parochiæ baptizavi. Patrini fuerunt **Jacobus Blaxevich** et
> **Maria conjux Joannis Uroda**.*

"Mathia Georgia, born today of Georgius and Maria Uroda, spouses of this parish,
I baptised. The godparents were Jacobus Blažević and Maria, wife of Joannes
Uroda."

**It confirms the tree exactly.** Ahnentafel 27 born **23 April 1810** at
Karlobag, daughter of ahnentafel 54 and 55 — named here by their first names,
*Georgius* and *Maria*, the surname carried by the couple.

## And a reading I nearly published wrong

The month column looked like **7ma** — which in these registers would be
*7bris*, **September**. That would have contradicted the tree by five months and
gone up as a correction.

Enlarged **3.4×** on the column alone it is a **ditto mark**, `d°`, and the
three rows read:

    26  Marty
    15  Aprily
    23  d°        <- this entry

April, not September. The archive's rule — never record a date from a
whole-page view — earned its keep for the second time in two days.

## One thing worth noticing and not claiming

The godfather is **Jacobus Blažević**, standing at a Uroda baptism in Karlobag
in 1810. The Blaževići of this archive are a Krmpote family two hundred
kilometres north, and Blažević is among the commonest surnames in Croatia.
**No connection is claimed**; it is recorded because the name is one of this
archive's own and someone will otherwise notice it and wonder.
