# Senj death register, 1915 — Zvonimir Vukelić

**Status: drafted, not sent.**

## What is wanted

The **death entry of Zvonimir (Matej) Vukelić** in the **Senj** death register
for **1915** — date, cause and place.

## What is known

| | |
|---|---|
| **Born** | **13 January 1883, Senj**; baptised 25 February (Senj baptisms p.77, entry 15) |
| **Parents** | **Petar *pl.* Vukelić**, *gostioničar*, and **Lojza** |
| **Married** | **Milka Papić**, 4 November 1907, Senj (marriages p.294, entry 30), by episcopal dispensation with the banns read once |
| **At marriage** | *gostioničarski pomoćnik* — innkeeper's assistant, aged 24 |
| **Later** | recorded as ***gostioničar, bankar*** — innkeeper and banker |
| **Children** | Miroslava (1907) and Josip (1913) |
| **Died** | **1915**, place given in the family record as **Senj**, aged 32 |

## Why not the casualty lists

This archive first proposed the Austro-Hungarian *Verlustlisten*, reasoning only
from his age in the second year of the war. **That reasoning was probably wrong.**
The family record places the death at **Senj**, in his own town, and in a settled
trade. A man who dies at home is not a casualty-list entry. The Senj civil or
parish death register is the right book, and this request goes there.

## What it would settle

How Milka Papić came to be a **widow at twenty-eight**, with a daughter of eight
and a son of two, five years before she married Ljubomir Blažević — a period
about which this archive currently knows nothing at all.
