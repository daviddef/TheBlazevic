# Ante Babić's baptism, Senj, 17 May 1837

Read from the image on 17 October 2026, after the FamilySearch index failed to return it.

**The entry.** Senj births 1756–1858, FamilySearch waypoint **9R2M-L27** (589 images), the Latin *Matricula Baptisatorum* of the cathedral church, **page 25, entry 71**, on the right-hand page of the spread at position 254 of 589 (ark `3:1:3QS7-899C-51B9`). The date column reads «1837 die 17 Maij in Ecclesia Cathedrali … baptisatus est»; the child is **Joannes Antonius**, «filius legitimus, Romano Catholicus»; the *conditio* column says **Bajulus** — a porter; the parents are «**Augustinus Babich**, conjux ejus **Maria nata Jurcich**, Romano Catholici». The godparents and the baptising priest are written in a hand and a place on the page that this reading does not give with confidence, and are not transcribed.

**What it settles.** The tree's *Anton Ivan «Ante» Babić*, born 1837, son of Augustin Babić and Maria Jurčić, is the child baptised here: «Antonius Joannes» in the marriage index, «Joannes Antonius» in the register. **The register gives a baptism date, not a birth date**, so the tree's 1837 stands and the day is 17 May. His father is a porter (*bajulus*), which is what the 1850s Babić fathers of the Senj death register mostly are.

**What it does not settle.** Why the index never returned this entry — the index of this volume is one of three overlapping collections and the surname paging is unreliable — and whether the volume has other Babić entries of 1837 in the page and a half either side; only this one spread was read.
