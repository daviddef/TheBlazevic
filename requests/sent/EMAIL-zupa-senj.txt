I am researching the family of my grandmother, HEDVIGA "SEKA" BLAŽEVIĆ, born at
Senj on 3 August 1926 and died at Johannesburg in 2001. Her family were of this
parish for as far back as I can see: Papić, Blažević, Vukelić, Tomljanović,
Bezjak and Rogić — the surnames on the stones around their own.

The work is published, free and without advertising, at:

    https://daviddef.github.io/TheBlazevic/

Anything you send would be published there in full and credited.

The filmed copies of the Senj registers (Hrvatski državni arhiv, through
FamilySearch) stop at **1894 for baptisms** and **1907 for deaths**. I have read
those films — fifteen pages of the marriage book at full resolution, and the
whole of the year 1888 end to end. **Everything below falls after the films
stop**, which is why I am writing to the parish.

===========================================================================
1. Three deaths, and for the first one I can give you the page and the line
===========================================================================

  1a. MILKA LUCIJA PAPIĆ (married Blažević), born Senj 9 October 1886,
      died 1971.
      Her baptismal entry — Senj births, str. 122, 1886 — carries an
      annotation added by a registrar in **1971** citing her death entry at
      **page 280, line 37**. Someone in this office closed that loop
      eighty-five years later and left the reference behind. I would like the
      entry it points to.

  1b. ZVONIMIR MATEJ VUKELIĆ, born 13 January 1883, her first husband.
      Married to her in this church on **4 November 1907** (Senj marriages,
      p. 294, no. 30). He died in **1915**, leaving her a widow at twenty-eight
      with a daughter of eight and a son of two. I know nothing else about him
      — not the day, not the cause, not his trade.

  1c. TEREZA BLAŽEVIĆ née ŽUBRINIĆ, born Otočac 29 November 1867, died at
      Senj in **1931**. She was my great-great-grandmother.

===========================================================================
2. Two sisters recorded eighteen days apart, in January and February 1895
===========================================================================

The tree I inherited gives two daughters of the same Papić household born
**eighteen days apart** — which cannot be. Both fall in **January and February
1895**, and **the filmed baptismal register stops at 1894**, one year short.

  2a. The BAPTISMS in the Senj register for JANUARY and FEBRUARY 1895 —
      or simply a note of which of the two dates the register actually
      carries.

===========================================================================
3. A question about this parish's own practice
===========================================================================

Reading the Senj baptisms and marriages I noticed something I cannot explain:
**Senj entries record no house number at all**, where the village books around
it — Krmpote, Krivi Put, Selce, Otočac — give one without hesitation.

  3a. Does a STATUS ANIMARUM (stanje duša) survive for Senj? And were Senj
      households numbered at all, the number simply not carried into the
      registers?

I am not asking blind: I have just read the Karlobag status animarum right
through, and a page of a Krivi Put one, captioned "Our Lady of the Snow", runs
1897 to 1939 in my own family papers. So the books were kept in these parishes.

Thank you for your time, and for keeping these books.
