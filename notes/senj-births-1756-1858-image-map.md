# Senj births 1756–1858 (waypoint 9R2M-L27, 589 images): which images hold which years — a working map, 17 October 2026

**Why.** The Kraljić search (the tree's Nicolaus Kraljich b. 29 September 1807 and his siblings of 1812–1820, [boras-kraljich-source-search-2026-10-17.md](boras-kraljich-source-search-2026-10-17.md)) and any other Senj 1800–1820 search needs to know where those years are. The volume is **a bundle of several books and sequences**: Latin prose entries in cramped hands (1700s and the 1820s), a tabular Latin *Matricula Baptisatorum* (1800s–1840s, the parish priest's and a cooperator's entries), and the printed Croatian-form books of the 1850s. **The list order is not year order.**

## What this means for 1807–1820

* **There is a tabular baptism sequence of the cathedral parish that starts in 1806 on image 336, left page** (entries numbered, protocol numbers 81, 82 on the same spread). The years **1807–1820 are most probably the images just before and after 336** (about 330–339), but **the same spreads carry 1840–1848 on their other pages**, so a spread is not one year. The first thing to do is to read the left-page year of images **336, 337, 339, 341, 342–345** at 4x and fix each.
* The prose entries of images 338–340 and 394 are **1820s**, not 1810s.
* **Nothing was searched for a Kraljich on these pages.** This note is a map and a pointer, not a reading.

## Method, so that the map can be extended

Index points come from persona searches by common surname and five-year window with the place «Senj», keeping only baptisms whose `sourceDescriptions` ark is in this waypoint's ark list (the list from `/platform/records/waypoints/9R2M-L27:391644801,391920301,391920401`); eyeballed years come from a 4x strip of the top of a spread. A surname sweep for 1780–1821 found none: the index starts at 1822.
