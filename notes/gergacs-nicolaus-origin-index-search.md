# Where did Nicolaus Gerkacs (ahnentafel 220, d. 1783 at Karlobag aged 66) come from? An index search, and one coincidence at Mrežnica

17 October 2026. Index only (FamilySearch *Croatia, Church Books*, collection 2040054, persona search); no page opened. Extends [the note on the three direct-line baptisms looked for by name](karlobag-marriage-1774-smojver-bacchi.md) (11 October 2026) and work-list row 54.

**The question.** Nicolaus died 30 October 1783 «in domo filij sui Antonij» aged 66, so he was born about 1717; his son Michael married at Karlobag in 1785. No Gergacs baptism exists at Karlobag before 1779, so he was an incomer.

**What was searched.** The surname in eleven spellings (Gergacs, Gerkacs, Gerkacz, Gergacz, Jerkacs, Gerkach, Gergach, Gergics, Gergich, Gergocz, Geraecs), baptisms 1735–1770, with the father's given name Nicolaus or Nicolai, and with the child's name Antonius, Antonio or Michael, anywhere in the collection. **No baptism of an Antonius or a Michael Gergacs to a father Nicolaus turns up in 1735–1770** (the index is not complete, so this does not mean there is none).

**The one coincidence.** At **Mrežnica** (in the then Modruš-Rijeka district, inland of Ogulin) a **Nicolaus Gergacz with a wife Margarita** has two children indexed:

| baptised | child | parents | index record |
|---|---|---|---|
| 29 November 1740 | Petrus | Nicolaus Gergacz × Margarita *Veiknich* | QKMJ-G4SG |
| 7 July 1745 | Anna | Nicolaus Gergacz × Margarita *Verbanich* | QKMJ-LGTK |

A Nicolaus who is a father in 1740 could well be born about 1717 (he would be 23). **That is all there is.** The wife's surname is written two ways, neither child is Antonius or Michael, and the same register shows another Nicolaus Gergach (father of Joannes, whose son Nicolaus was baptised there on 21 August 1760). **It is a lead, not a link**, and nothing here says the Mrežnica Nicolaus ever went to Karlobag.

**Where it could be tested.** The Mrežnica births, marriages and deaths are on film under waypoint **9R2M-C6D** (the atlas lists Births 1718–1725 and 1728–1761, Marriages 1726–1761, Deaths 1726–59, Births 1762–1774, 588 images; the births of 1766 are around image 510). Latin prose, entries grouped by village. What to look for: further children of Nicolaus and Margarita (Antonius, Michael, born 1746–62), a death of a Nicolaus Gergacz at Mrežnica before about 1760, or a marriage.

**Not the same family.** The index shows many **Gergich** (Kostajnica, Kobaš, Osijek, Našice, Nova Gradiška): a different surname, in Slavonia, and no evidence connects it to the Karlobag Gergacs.
