# Selce deaths 1790–1815: the three ancestors worklist row 25 had left (1793, 1807, 1811)

8 October 2026. Work list row 25, the three direct ancestors it said were left in the Selce
death book: **ahnentafel 96** (Joannis Antić, d. 27 February 1793), **99** (Anna Papić,
d. 24 August 1807) and **98** (Pauli Lončarić, d. 29 September 1811). All three are on the
page, on the day the tree gives. One of them carries the wrong first name.

**Where.** Selce *Umrli (deaths) 1790–1815*, DGS 5482267, FamilySearch waypoint **9R2M-BZF**
(the Atlas lists it as «Deaths (Umrli) 1790-1815», years 1778–1861, which contain all three
frames). The waypoint enumerates **294 images**: the deaths item runs from about image 77
(1790) to 127, an END card at 128, and image 70 is still the baptisms. The register is Latin
prose, one entry to a paragraph, a house number («Nrus Domus») in the left margin and the
parish priest *Michael Kombol, Capellanus Loci* signing most of it.

## Ahnentafel 99 — «Anna uxor Pauli Lončarich Papich», 24 August 1807

Image **110, left page**, ark `3:1:3QS7-899C-5962-V`, house **134** (as read):

> «Die 24ᵃ Mensis Augusti anno ut supra 1807. **Anna uxor Pauli Lončarich Papich**
> annorum circiter **65**, in communione S. M. Ecclesiæ et per me Mich: Kombol C.L.
> requisitis sacramentis provisa animam Deo creatori suo reddidit, cujus corpus idem per me
> in loco S. Catharinæ cœmeterio sepultum est.»

**The day and month are the tree's. The age fits the tree's birth year**: about 65 in 1807 is
1742, the tree's year. **She is entered with the surname *Papich*** after her husband's, which
is her own maiden name written in the register: the tree gives *Anna Lončarić (rođen Papić)*,
and this is the first document in this archive that has her as a Papić at all.

## Ahnentafel 98 — «Paulus Lončarich viduus», 29 September 1811

Image **120, left page** (printed page number 111), ark `3:1:3QS7-899C-596R-H`, house **134**:

> «Die 29 7bis Aᵒ 1811. **Paulus Lončarich viduus** annorum circiter **70**, in communione
> S. M. Ecclesiæ et per me Michaëlem Kombol Parochum requisitis sacramentis provisæ vitæ
> provisus animam Deo creatori suo reddidit, cujus corpus idem per me in loco S. Catharinæ
> cœmeterio sepultum est.»

**The day, month and year are the tree's, the age fits the tree's 1741, and *viduus* (widower)
is right for a man whose wife died in 1807.** **And the house is 134 for both**, the wife's
entry four years earlier and his own: one household, read from two registers that did not
know about each other.

## Ahnentafel 96 — «Antonius Antich Martini», 27 February 1793, and the name is wrong

Image **81, left page**, ark `3:1:3QS7-L99C-59DD-H`, under *Anno 1793*, house **34** (as read):

> «Die 27ᵃ Februarii 793 **Antonius Antich Martini** annorum circiter **54**, in communione
> S. Matris Ecclesiæ, omnibus per me Mich: Kombol Capell: Loci sacramentis, excepto SS.
> Sacramento Eucharistiæ [the rest of that clause is not read] … animam Deo
> creatori suo reddidit; cujus corpus in loco locali S. Catharinæ cœmeterio ibidem per me
> sepultum est.»

* **The day and month are the tree's, to the day, and the age is the tree's**: 54 in 1793 is
  a birth year of 1739.
* **The given name is not.** The tree has «Joannis Antić, PAPA», from the Karić genealogy.
  The register's first word is **Antonius**, underlined, and read the same way at two zooms.
  The surname is *Antich* and what follows it, *Martini*, is a by-name or a patronymic and is
  **not** *Papa*.
* **Three readings, and this note takes none of them:** the Karić tree has the right man and
  the wrong first name; the Karić tree has the right date and age from somewhere and attached
  them to a man it called Joannes; or this is a **different Antić** who died on the same day
  at the same age, which in a village of Antići is not impossible and is not likely.
  **What would settle it** is a document that names his son: the 1829 entry for ahnentafel 48
  is «Joannes Antich Papa», house 51 (the household is not 34), and the Selce births before
  1778 are not filmed, so his marriage or a *Status Animarum* would be the way.
* **So the entry is recorded as evidence that an Antonius Antich, about 54, died at Selce on
  27 February 1793, and not as a reading of ahnentafel 96.** The reading row says that.

## What was not done

* Only the three entries were read. The pages around them were not walked, and the deaths
  1790–1815 were not read entry by entry. The Selce *Umrli 1816–1886* and the births and
  marriages items were not opened.
* **No wife was named in any of the three**, apart from the first two naming each other, so
  ahnentafel 97 (Ursula, the wife of ahnentafel 96) is still a name and nothing else.
* Whether *Papich* in Anna's entry is a maiden name or a household by-name was not tested
  against another entry.
