# Senj births, 1878–1894: the register for 6 March 1879

Waypoint 9R2D-JWL (Senj births 1878–1894, 269 images), read 24 November 2026: image 31 for the first time (printed Str. 23, entries 50–56, baptised 26 June to 21 July 1879). Images 25–30 (entries 15–49) are the earlier readings (`senj-births-february-1879-the-25th.md` and the notes for 3, 4 and 5 March); the dates below are from those readings.

## The question

Is any child born on 6 March 1879, or baptised on 6 March 1879, in the Senj register, and is any of them a Perpić, Prpić or Tomljanović of the household at Klaričevac 82?

## What the pages are

The printed Croatian table of the earlier 1879 notes: entries numbered from 1 each year, in order of baptism. Image 31 is Str. 23 (entries 50–56): both pages read at full size, and the name and house cells of entry 53 zoomed. Months: lipnja = June, srpnja = July.

## What was found

- **Baptised on 6 March: none.** The baptisms run 3 March (entry 19), then 11 March (entry 20), 16 March (entry 21) and on.
- **Born on 6 March: none in entries 15–56.** The nearest births are 2 March (entry 20) and 7 March (entry 30); entries 50–56 are born 25 June, 12 June, 29 June, 29 June, 6 July, 6 July and 21 July, baptised 26 June, 29 June, 1 July, 2 July, 13 July, 13 July and 21 July.
- **Where the late baptisms stop.** The longest delay in entries 15–56 is entry 30, born 7 March and baptised 23 April (47 days). A child born on 6 March and baptised by 21 July would be 137 days late.
- **Entry 53 is a Tomljanović, and not the Klaričevac household.** Entry 53: **Petar Pave**, zakonit, born 29 June, baptised 2 July 1879; father «Eduard Tomljanović težak», mother «Joka rodj. Bevandić»; house Senj; godparent «Ivan Thian težake»; baptiser Mirko Babić kapelan. The surname was zoomed.
  What it shows: a farmer named Eduard Tomljanović, with a wife of the Bevandić name, living in Senj in 1879. The Klaričevac father is Ivan Tomljanović, married to Lucia née Prpić. What it does not show: whether Eduard is related to Ivan or to any Tomljanović of this archive. The tree has many Tomljanovićs and none called Eduard, no Petar Pave of 1879 and no Joka Bevandić, so nothing is added to it. The child's second name Pave is a coincidence of name only: the Pave of 1881 is the Klaričevac couple's (`senj-births-september-1881-pave.md`).
- Entries 50–56 hold no other Perpić, Prpić or Tomljanović.
  Snapshots: `/snapshots/#senj-births-1879-07-entries-50-56` (image 31, both pages), `#senj-births-1879-07-entry-53-tomljanovic` (entry 53 zoomed).

## What this does not show

A birth on 6 March baptised after 21 July would stand at entry 57 or later; those were not read. Not a walk of the register or of 1879: entries 15–56 of images 25–31 only.

## What follows

Tomljanović and Prpić are in the Atlas catalogue and were covered. Bevandić is not; nothing ties it to this line (it is the mother's maiden name in an entry for another household), so nothing was added to the catalogues.
