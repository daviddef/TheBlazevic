# Karlobag baptisms 1729–1732: three Smojver children, read on images 52 and 54

Read on 22 November 2026 from the FamilySearch images of the Karlobag births volume (DGS 5481648, waypoint 9RK1-3Y9), at about 4x,
three or four strips to a page, then each entry at full zoom. The clerk is the parish priest Andrija Kolikar (Kolikar, Kolekar), who
enters some months out of order. Image numbers are the waypoint's own list (the ark's place in it): image 52 is book pages 92–93,
image 53 pages 94–95 (read earlier the same day, no Smojver), image 54 pages 96–97.

## What was found

| date | page | entry as read | child | father | godparents |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 13 Oct 1729 | image 52, left (p. 92) | «Na 13 Octobra 1729 Ja Pop Andriy Kolikar karstih cher Marka Smoyvera i njegova … imenom Franicu, bi kum Simun …» | Franica | Marko Smojver | Simun …, and his wife |
| 21 Apr 1732 | image 54, right (p. 97) | «Na 21 Agrilya 1732 Ja Plovan pop Andre Kolakar karstih cher Antona Smovara i nyegove xene Šaga(?) imenom Marta, bi kum Anton Roxarich svojon xenom Gelom» | Marta | Antun Smojver | Anton Rozarić and his wife Jela |
| 12 May 1732 | image 54, right (p. 97) | «Na 12 maia 1732 ia plovan pop Gandre Kolekar karstih cher Juana mladoga Smovera i negove xene imenom Orsula, bi kum Sigor Karlo Stoner a kuma Maria udova Dokoin…oga male udovicha» | Orsula | Ivan «the younger» Smojver | Karlo Stoner; Maria, a widow |

- «Imenom X» introduces the CHILD in this clerk's formula (as in «… imenom Maricu» in the entries around them), so Franica, Marta and
  Orsula are read as the children and the Smojver men as the fathers. The mother of the 12 May child is not named; the mother of the
  21 April child is named with one word that is not settled (Šaga, or Stoga).
- **The spelling.** «Smoyvera» (1729, the y is plain), «Smovara» and «Smovera» (1732, no j written). The clerk declines the
  name (genitive -a), so the stem is Smoyver / Smover. The Latin entries of 1774–1781 and the index write Smojver, Smoyver, Smoiver. Read
  as the same name; the spelling without a j is the clerk's own.
- **«Mladoga» («the younger»)** says there was an elder Ivan Smojver, so at least two generations of the family are at Karlobag by 1732.

## What it does and does not say

- It puts three Smojver households (Marko, Antun, the younger Ivan) at Karlobag in 1729–32, the years of the baptism of ahnentafel 212,
  Josephus Smojver «dictus Vukcich» (died 1776, aged 45, so born about 1730–31).
- It does **not** show whose son ahnentafel 212 was. None of the three entries is his baptism, and no entry on images 52, 53 or 54
  gives a Vukić by-name. Any of the three men may be his father, uncle or no kin at all. Nothing is added to the tree.
- **Still to read for 212:** the baptism itself (not on images 52–54, which cover October 1729 to about June 1732 out of order), and the
  following images, where Josephus Smojver is a father in 1733 (an earlier row).
