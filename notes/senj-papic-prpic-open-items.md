# Two possible duplicates in the Senj families, checked on the page (26 November 2026)

Both were left open by `senj-births-1859-1877-matches.md`. Read from the archive's private copies of the leaves; each entry was found through FamilySearch's index of its book (terms read in the index: Papić, Fajdetić, Prpić) and then read at full size. Neither book was walked.

## Stjepan Papić of the tree, born 1864: probably Stephanus of 1860 — not merged

| Date | Book, image, entry | What the page says |
|---|---|---|
| 16 August 1860 | births 1859–1877, image 39, entry 83 | Stephanus, of Georgius Papić, porter, and Mathia née Pilip[…]; a faint pencil note «† 8.XII.1917» · [snapshot](/snapshots/#senj-births-1859-77-img0039) |
| 13 May 1888 | marriages 1859–1920, image 219, entry 25 | «Stjepan Papić bremenar», of Senj, **24**, unmarried, son of «Jure Papić i Matija rodjena Pilipović»; «Ana Fajdetić služkinja», from Brinje, living at Senj, 21, daughter of «Šime Fajdetić i Katica» of Brinje · [snapshot](/snapshots/#senj-marriage-1888-05-papic-fajdetic) |

- The groom is a son of this couple: both parents are named.
- An age of 24 in May 1888 gives a birth about 1864, which is the year the tree has for [Stjepan](/people/stjepan-papic/).
- The births of 1859–1877 were read whole. They hold one Stephen born to this couple, [Stephanus](/people/stephannes-papic/) of 1860, and no child of theirs born in 1864. He would have been 27 at the marriage.
- In the tree the two halves fit together: Stephanus has a birth and a death and no wife; Stjepan has a wife and children and neither a birth day nor a death date.
- **So they read as one man whose age was entered three years short. That is an inference.** Nothing is merged: a merge needs the tree owner's word.
- Katica's maiden name is not settled from this page (C[…]ković); the tree has Castkovic.
