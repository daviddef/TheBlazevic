# Otočac census (Status Animarum) pages that name a Žubrinić household: what was read (10 November 2026)

Film 5498269, volume 9R2D-16X, viewer images 9-122; a household list: house number, village in the margin, every member with a baptism date, a cross or strike for a death (often with a year), «nupsit ad N° …» for a woman who married into another house. The census carries no date of its own on the pages read; the entries run from the 1760s births to deaths and marks of the 1840s (the title card says 1710-1846).

How it was read: the census was NOT walked. The alphabetical index (images 123-125) lists 15 households headed by a Xubrinich; the household pages the index points to were fetched and read by page-crop readers (one reader per three images, reports kept outside the repo). Page numbers are those written on the pages as the readers read them; several differ from the index's (the index's «112» and «115» are read as pp. 142 and 145 on the pages), and the film is not in page order: image 101 is pp. 184-185 and image 102 is pp. 206-207, so pp. 186-205 (the index's Poljice 19 on p. 188, Šumećica 2 on p. 197, Šumećica 5 on p. 201, Šumećica 6 on p. 202) were not found and are NOT read.
