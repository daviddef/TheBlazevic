# Senj baptisms 1834 and 1840: two more children of Augustinus Babić and Maria nata Jurcic(z) — and the index's «Turcich» is a misreading

Read from the images on 23 November 2026: Senj births 1756–1858 (FamilySearch waypoint 9R2M-L27, 589 images), the Latin *Matricula Baptisatorum*
of the cathedral church. Work list row 29 (the page half). The two entries are the baptisms of the brother and the sister of Ante Babić
(ahnentafel line, baptised 17 May 1837, [that note](senj-baptism-1837-ante-babic.md)); the FamilySearch index named them (father-name query,
[the family list](../sources/babic-augustinus-family-index.txt)) and gave each its image; the arks were then opened and the entries read.
**Control:** the 1837 baptism of Ante is at image 254 in this list (ark 3QS7-899C-51B9, as in his note); these two are at images 251 and 259.

**The date of 1834.** The month is written «9bris» in the entry before (entry 37, «die 3ª 9bris», clear) and in a looped form in this one; the index
has 6 November and the sequence of the page (21 October, 3 November, 6 November) agrees. The 1840 entry reads «Die 24 Aprilis natâ et 26 ejusdem mensis baptizata est»: **born on the 24th, baptised on the 26th**. The index has «Baptism 24 April 1840»,
so it files the birth date as the baptism date for this entry.

**What the reading settles.**
- **The mother's maiden name.** At **1834 the entry writes «Jur-cicz»**, divided across two lines, with a clear *J* and *u* *r*: **Jurcicz**, i.e. the
  tree's **Jurčić**. The index has «Turcicz» for this entry (and «Turcich» for the 1840 one): **a J read as a T**; the marriage index of the same child (Nicolaus Ambrosius) has a third reading, «Furcić». For **1840** the first letter of the surname is a hooked stroke that starts below the line, closer to a J than to the barred T that the other hands on the page write; I read **Jurcich**, but this is the less firm of the two readings (the 1834 one is firm; the same hand's J in «Jacobina» was not put beside it). Ante's own 1837 entry reads
  «Jurcich» ([note](senj-baptism-1837-ante-babic.md)).
- **Both children are of Augustinus Babić and Maria Jurčić**, so the sibling list of Ante (1826, 1828, 1832 twins, 1834, 1837, 1840, and a Theresia b. 1842) has two more
  entries read from the page and two more confirmed in the register as one family. The index's other four (Helena Francisca 1826, Joannes 1828, the twins Gasparus and
  Paulus 1832) are not read here: **images 244, 245 and 249 are the places to open**.
- **The father's trade.** The 1834 entry and Ante's 1837 entry give Augustinus as a porter (*bajulus*, the godfather of 1834 is also a *bajulus*), and the 1840 entry has *Bajulus*
  in the same column.

## What was not read

Two entries on two images; nothing else on those pages was read for anything (the neighbouring rows were looked at only for their dates). Images 244, 245, 249 (the older
Babić children), 450–461 (the Babić families of the 1840s), 481, 503, 529, 537, 562–563 (the Špalj × Perpić households) and the Tomljanović and Krmpotić entries
of 1820–1850 are **not read**. The index's results (about 70 Babić and Špalj rows with an image in this waypoint, 1820–1858) are the list to open.

**Atlas catalogue:** Jurčić is not in the Record Atlas surname catalogue; the forms read here (Jurcicz, Jurcich) should go in with this evidence when the catalogue's own check is clean
(it fails today on another session's uncommitted edits, so nothing was added, and nothing was forced). Terms tried for these pages: Babić, Babic, Babich, Babicz (the Atlas
catalogue's Babić family; Babicz is a form read on the page and is not yet in the catalogue).
