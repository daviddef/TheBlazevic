# Otilija died in November, and Neda was the one the raid killed

**21 September 2026.** Two photographs arrived from the family. Between them they answer
**the single most-wanted fact** on the Kosina page and correct the way this archive had
been telling the story.

## The stone: OTILIJA i NEDA KOSINA, Senj

> Ovdje počivaju u miru Božjem
> **OTILIJA i NEDA KOSINA**
>
> **OTILIJA** · *čin. Velike župe* · ✻ **1920** † **14.11.1943.**
> **NEDA** · *maturantica,* ***žrtva zračnog napada*** · ✻ **1924** † **7.IX.1943.**
>
> **VLČ. ANTE** ✻ 1921 † 2003 — IN MEMORIAM — **OSKAR** ✻ 1922 † 1954
>
> *Prerano ste rekle zbogom svijetu. Snivajte slatko anđeli moji.*
> **Ražalošćena Majka i Braća.**

*«Too soon you said goodbye to the world. Sleep sweetly, my angels. Your grieving Mother
and Brothers.»*

## What it settles, and it is not what this archive expected

**OTILIJA DIED ON 14 NOVEMBER 1943 — five weeks after the raid.** The Kosina page said her
death was *«1943, no day recorded»* and called whether she died with her father **the single
most wanted fact on this page**. The answer is **no**: Pavao was killed on 8 October and
Otilija died on 14 November, and **the stone does not call her a victim of anything**.

**NEDA IS THE ONE IT CALLS A VICTIM.** *«maturantica, **žrtva zračnog napada**»* — a
final-year schoolgirl, **a victim of an air attack** — died **7 September 1943**.

**And that is a month before the October raid.** This archive has written about *«the
bombing of 7–8 October 1943»* as though it were the event. **Senj was bombed more than
once**, and the raid that killed the nineteen-year-old daughter was not the raid that
killed her father.

**Otilija had a job, and it dates her.** *«čin. Velike župe»* — **činovnica Velike župe**, a
clerk of the *Velika župa*, which was the administrative region of the wartime Independent
State of Croatia. A twenty-three-year-old woman in a government office in 1943.

**And Ante's cross is confirmed in stone**: *«VLČ. ANTE»*, **velečasni**, Reverend. The same
man who had his grandmother Tereza Žubrinić's grave restored in 1991 and signed it
**«OBNOVIO POP ANTE KOSINA»**.

**The stone was raised by «Majka i Braća»** — the mother and the brothers. **Anka, Ante and
Oskar**, burying two sisters in one autumn, having buried the father between them.

## The card: OSKAR KOSINA

> **OSKAR KOSINA**
> ✻ **4. IV. 1922. SENJ — HRVATSKA**
> † **15. I. 1954. BS. AIRES — ARGENTINA**

**A death day at last**: **15 January 1954, Buenos Aires.** The archive had only the year.
He was **thirty-one**.

**And a conflict on his birth that is worth stating rather than resolving.** The card reads
**4. IV.** — **the fourth of April**. The family tree says **4 September 1922**. The day
agrees; the month does not — and the two candidates are **IV and IX**, Roman four and Roman
nine.

**This archive has a note about exactly this shape.** `the-month-is-wrong-and-the-day-is-right.md`
tested a Roman-numeral theory, killed it against seventeen image reads, and closed with the
tree holding a perfect record. **This is the first case where the DOCUMENT carries the Roman
numeral and the TREE carries the Arabic month** — the reverse of every earlier one. The
stone gives only *1922* and cannot break the tie.

**A printed memorial card is not a register.** It was set by a grieving family in Argentina
and could be wrong; so could a tree. **The Senj baptism of 1922 would settle it**, and Senj
births are filmed only to 1894 — so this one goes on the list for the parish office with
Otilija's and Neda's.
