# What the readings of 13–14 September changed about the two sent letters

Two letters went out on 13 September 2026 — `EMAIL-gospic.txt` and
`EMAIL-senj.txt`. **Nothing here has been sent.** This is the brief for whoever
follows them up, written the day after two days of register reading changed what
we know and, in two places, what we should be asking for.

---

## 1. The Senj letter's main ask is now much stronger

It asks Rijeka/Senj for **Juraj "Jurko" Blažević's baptism or his marriage to
Tereza Žubrinić** at Sv. Jakov Krmpote — the document that ends open question 1b.

**We can now say that neither is obtainable any other way.** Checked against the
films on 13 September:

* **Krmpote-Vodice has no marriage register on FamilySearch at all.** Not a gap
  in a range — the event type is simply absent.
* Its birth film is **catalogued 1815–1896 and actually begins in 1879**: image
  10 is page two and reads *Godine 1879*. Juraj's children start in 1892 and he
  has no birth date at all in the tree, so he was born around the 1860s —
  **inside the catalogue range and outside the film.**

That is worth saying in a follow-up, because it converts a polite request into a
specific one: *the microfilm does not carry these years; does the archive hold
the volume itself?*

## 2. The Senj letter's second ask is confirmed as unobtainable elsewhere

**Zvonimir Vukelić's death, Senj 1915.** FamilySearch's Senj deaths stop at
**1907**. Same point: the film cannot answer it, so the archive or the parish is
the only route.

The same is true of two questions the letter does *not* raise, and which could be
added: **Miroslava Vukelić's baptism, Senj 1907** (question 1e) and the
**Senj baptisms of January–February 1895** for the two Papić sisters born
eighteen days apart (question 5b). **Senj births stop at 1894**, so all three
sit just outside the filmed range.

## 3. A third letter is now wanted, and it is not to either of these archives

Two questions opened on 14 September point at **Krk and the Vinodol coast**,
which is **Državni arhiv u Rijeci**, not Gospić and not the Senj branch.

**Question 5d — Vrbnik or Selce?** The 1806 baptism of ahnentafel 24 carries
*Antich Papa* underlined, and a modern pencil annotation reading **VRBNIK**. The
officiant of the entry below is a **Canon of Dobrinj**, which is on Krk. But the
tree gives the birthplace as **Selce**, on the mainland, and the same by-names
appear in the Selce registers of 1835. Either the family crossed from the island
between 1809 and 1835, or one of the two labels is wrong.

*Wanted:* whether the **Vrbnik** baptismal register for 1806 contains the entry
of **Josephus, son of Joannes and Martha Antich Papa, 14 March 1806** — and, if
not, which parish does.

**Question 13b — one Margarita Jelličić or two?** The Selce marriage of 1835 and
the baptism of 1838 both call Maria Jelličić *filia **Denatorum** Georgii et
Margarittæ* — both parents dead — yet the Selce death register buries
**Margareta Jellicich, uxor Georgii** on **8 May 1842**, at house 204, while her
husband died in 1832 at house **39**.

*Wanted:* the **marriage of Georgius Jelličić and Margarita, Selce, before
1809** — which would give her maiden name (the tree says *Brozović*) and whether
the entry carries the by-name *Jurešina*.

## 4. What to do with a nil return

Both sent letters may come back empty, and that is a result. The rule stands:
**a reply, including a negative one, goes on `/changed` and `/searched`.** The
holdings audit on `/searched` now exists precisely so that a nil return can be
recorded against a specific book rather than a vague absence.
