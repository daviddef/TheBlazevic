# The Kraljich household in the Otočac status animarum: Nicolaus, «son of the late Jacobus» (17 October 2026)

**The question.** The tree's Nicolaus Kraljich (b. «29 September 1807», d. 1845) has a father Jacobus, an «Anna, b. 1809» as his mother (which is his wife's birth year, see [integrity-audit-triage-2026-10-17.md](integrity-audit-triage-2026-10-17.md)), brothers Mattheus (1812) and Stephanus (1817), and a sister Francisca (1820). [boras-kraljich-source-search-2026-10-17.md](boras-kraljich-source-search-2026-10-17.md) looked for their baptisms in the Senj births of 1807–1820 and found no way in. **That search was aimed at the wrong parish.** The tree's own source for Nicolaus's birth is the Otočac church census, image 76 of 384 (waypoint 9R2D-16X), which the earlier sessions had never opened for him.

## What it shows

1. **Nicolaus and Stephanus are entered as «filius defti Jacobi», sons of the late Jacobus** (*defti* = *defuncti*). So the tree's father **Jacobus is a real entry on a document, and was dead by the time the line was written**. He is not otherwise on the page, and **no mother is named**. Matthaeus is in the same run of struck rows with the father's name left to the dittos; that he is a third son of Jacobus is a reading of the layout, not a statement on the page.
2. **They lived in the household of a Georgius Kraljich (b. about 1778), whose relationship to them the page does not say** (an uncle, or an older cousin, is the natural guess; nothing here proves either). The three brothers' rows are struck and carried to **house No. 53** in the margin, with «845» (1845?) in the next column. That is probably the year of the move; the tree's «d. 1845» for Nicolaus has the same figure behind it, but the page does not say that anyone died. **The household at house No. 53 is the next page to find**, and is where the wife Anna and the mother question will show.
3. **The month of Nicolaus's date is «9bris», not September.** In this old Latin shorthand the months from September run 7bris, 8bris, 9bris, Xbris (September, October, **November**, December), and the next rows of the same page use «8bris» for October. The tree's «29 September 1807» is therefore probably **29 November 1807**, a misexpansion of the same page. The glyph was read at full size beside Stephanus's «7 9bris 1817». The correction is on the [corrections page](/corrections/); **only a baptism entry would settle it, and the filmed Otočac baptisms begin in September 1834.**
4. **The family is Otočac's, so the Senj books of 1807–1820 were never the place to look.** This also explains the map note's finding that the Senj film (9R2M-L27) has no 1790–1819 baptisms to offer: the Kraljić baptisms are in no filmed book. Only the parish itself, or the Gospić archive if the parish gave its old books up, can have them.

## What is still unproved

* **Who Jacobus's wife was.** The tree's «Anna, b. 1809» is the wife's entry attached to the wrong person; a second wife «Vincentia, b. 1811» of Jacobus is also in the tree and is not on this page.
* **Nicolaus's wife, Anna Žubrinić:** not on this page; she should be in the house No. 53 household.
* **Francisca, b. «1820»** (the tree) is not among the rows read.
* **Year digits** of Georgius (b. 1778), Franciscus and the second Georgius were read once at about 0.55x and are marked not firm in the snapshot.

## The letter

The questions the page cannot answer (the baptisms of 1807, 1812 and 1817, the parents' marriage, Jacobus's burial, and Nicolaus's marriage to Anna Žubrinić of about 1830–35) go to the **Otočac parish**, not to Senj, as **section 4 of the Otočac draft** on the [errands page](/errands/): *EMAIL-zupa-otocac.txt*, not sent.
