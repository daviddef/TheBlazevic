# Karlobag 1727: no Luckinić baptism on 30 March, and Vicko Luckinić's wife is Morica

9 October 2026. Work list row 26, the last ancestor it still lists: **ahnentafel 105, Catherina «Catta»
Luckinić**, whom the tree gives as born **30 March 1727** at Karlobag, daughter (per the tree) of Vicentius
Luckinić. Her death (29 May 1790, «annorum 70») is read; her baptism is not. This sitting went to the baptism
book directly, by year, instead of through the Kazalo.

## What image 49 holds for March 1727

The entries run 14 March, **20 March** (a son, the father's name read as Petar Bulanich), **30 March** («Lovri Bulanichia … imenom Jella»,
godparent Ivan Amoich?), then 1 April («Lesi Lukatalichin … imenom Madalenu») and 6 April. **None is a Luckinić.**
The surnames read on the January–April page (Lukatalich, Kintelich, Bartula Jurse, Bulanich, Zubrich, Dobričich) do
not include one.

## The one Luckinić there is in 1727

On image 49's right-hand page, **24 August 1727**: «…a kuma [gospoja] xena **Vicka Luckinicha** imenom **Morica**» —
the godmother is *the wife of Vicko Luckinich, named Morica* (a form of Marija, as read). So **Vicko Luckinić is alive
at Karlobag in 1727 and his wife is Morica.** That agrees with the tree and with the 1725 baptism of *Mare
Luckinicha* (index, father «Vicka Luckinicha», no mother named). It does **not** make Morica Catherina's mother.

## What this changes, and what it does not

* **The tree's 30 March 1727 is not confirmed.** There is no Luckinić baptism on that date in this book. The tree's
  date, or its year, is either wrong or taken from a source that is not this register.
* **Her baptism has not been found**, so row 26 stays open. The Kazalo route (the L column, a folio for her) was not
  used and is still the way to settle it: it names the folio whatever the year is.
* **A candidate mother:** a *Morica* wife of Vicko Luckinich in 1727. Recorded in `found.psv` as a godmother, not
  as a mother.

## What was not done

* Only the January–April left page of image 49 was read entry by entry for the surname; May–December 1727 and 1726
  and 1728 were not searched, so a Luckinić baptism on another date in 1727 is not excluded.
* Surnames are spelt variously in this hand (Lukatalich, Lukinić, Luckinich, Lukanić); a Luckinić under an unfamiliar
  spelling could have been missed.
* The Kazalo was not used.
