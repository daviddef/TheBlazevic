# The whole of 1888, read — and Juraj Blažević is not in it

14 September 2026. Written in answer to one question: **can this archive get a
birth or a death date for Juraj "Jurko" Blažević (ahnentafel 4)?** He has
neither. His wife has 1867–1931. Every one of his four children has a date.

Three things were done, in order of cost.

---

## 1. The lead that was already in his own record — opened, and it is not him

The tree carries a note on ahn 4 that nobody here had ever acted on:

> *"Potentially him? 1861?"* — Croatia, Church Books, 1516-1994 → Senj →
> Births (Rođeni) 1859-1877 → **image 55 of 399**,
> `ark:/61903/3:1:3QS7-899X-1W4Q`

Image 55 is **page 50** of the Senj baptismal register, April 1861, entries
49–55. Entry 55 is the one meant:

> **55.** born **22 Aprilis 1861**, baptised **28 Aprilis 1861** ·
> **Georgius** · *legitimus* ·
> father **Antonius Blažević, *mercator*** ·
> mother **Anna nata Trbuhović**

**It is a different family.** Ahn 4's father is **Toma Blažević**, a
*nadničar* — a day labourer — of Smokvica Krmpotska, and in 1861 Toma was
twenty-three. The Georgius on this page is the son of a **merchant** of Senj.
The given name and the year fit; the father does not, and the father decides.

Read at level 13 from the deep-zoom tiles — about six times the width of the
viewer's own image. The word *mercator* is unambiguous at that scale.

## 2. What FamilySearch's index knows about him — three records, all already read

A record search for Blažević × Žubrinić in *Croatia, Church Books* returns
exactly three entries that touch him, and **every one is a document this
archive has already read**:

| Indexed as | Record | Already read |
|---|---|---|
| **Juraj Blažević**, father | Ljubomir's marriage, Senj, 8 Dec 1920 | yes — p.366 |
| **Juraj Blažević**, father-in-law | Anka's marriage, Senj, 1919 | yes — p.359 |
| **Ivko Blažević**, father | Marijan's baptism, Krmpote, 1895 | yes — p.168 |

None gives his age. He is a name in the parents' column each time.

**One thing is new and small:** at his son Marijan's baptism the priest wrote
him **Ivko**, not Juraj or Jurko. A fourth form of the name.

## 3. The whole of 1888 at Senj, read end to end — the marriage is not there

The tree says Juraj and Tereza married in **1888**. The Senj marriage register
1859–1920 is filmed and this archive has read fifteen of its pages, so this was
the cheapest remaining test.

**1888 runs from image 214 to image 221 and holds 39 marriages**, entry 1 on
12 January to entry 39 on 15 November. Every entry has now been read.

**No Blažević married a Žubrinić at Senj in 1888.** No Blažević married anyone.

Three entries are worth carrying away even so:

· **28. 21 June 1888 — Vinko Antić, *kremenar*, × Julka Vukelić, *sluškinja*.**
  Two of this archive's surnames marrying each other at Senj, neither of them
  ours as far as anything shows.
· **1. 12 January 1888 — Martin Kozina × Anjelika Boras**, Senj. The Boras
  surname again, twenty-one years before Božica Papić married Franjo Boras.
· **34. 7 September 1888 — Josip Draženović × Marija Jovanović of Otočac.**

## What that leaves

If they married in 1888 at all, they married **somewhere else** — and the
obvious somewhere else is the bride's parish. Tereza Žubrinić was born at
Otočac in 1867, and **the Otočac marriage register on film stops in 1872**.
Krmpote has no marriage register filmed at all. Novi Vinodolski's marriages
stop in 1859, Ledenice's in 1882.

So the marriage falls into the same hole as open question 1b, and for the same
reason: the books that would hold it exist in Croatian custody and are not in
this collection. **That is now three separate routes to Juraj's dates that end
at the same wall**, which is worth more than another afternoon of guessing.

Still untried, in order of promise:

1. **Senj deaths 1859–1907** — filmed, and only two of its pages have been
   opened. If Juraj died at Senj before 1907 he is in it. (The tree puts his
   death at Smokvica Krmpotska and gives no year.)
2. **Rijeka** — his son Ljubomir was born there in 1892 and this archive has
   never checked what Rijeka holdings exist. Question 13.
3. **Krmpote baptisms 1879–1896** — his other children. The register gives the
   house number and would place the household, though rarely the father's age.
