# Ursula Sedej's marriage to Johann Kozina is not in the Nova Oselica book for 1851–1853

8 October 2026. Work list row 78. Ursula Sedej, born about 1826–27 at Nova Oselica (so her
second marriage of 28 May 1873 says), married Johann Kozina of Zgornje Bitnje 9 after his
first wife Lucia Labornik died on **30 November 1850** and before their first daughter,
Franziska, was baptised on **19 September 1853**. The Kranj–Šmartin marriage book has no entry
for them between April 1850 and February 1853. A marriage celebrated in the bride's own
parish would be in **Nova Oselica sig. 01590** and would give her age, house number and
parents, so this sitting read it.

## What was read

Matricula Online, Nova Oselica, *Poročna knjiga / Trauungsbuch* sig. **01590** (87 images,
1826–1908), images **19, 20 and 21**: the groom and bride columns of the left-hand page,
which carry the parish of residence, house number, name, age and marital state of each. The
book is a printed German-headed form, one marriage to a row, five or six to a page.

* **1850:** entries of January and February only (image 19).
* **1851:** 17 January and 10 November.
* **1852:** 4 February, two on 11 February, and 23 February.
* **1853:** 31 January, then the rest of 1853 on image 21.

**No groom is a Kozina, no bride is a Sedej, and no one is from Zgornje Bitnje.**
(Names were read from a clerical hand at about twice the page's resolution; none is a Kozina
or a Sedej on any reading, and the individual names are not transcribed here because several
were not read with confidence.)

## What it settles and what it leaves

It rules out one place for the marriage: **not Kranj–Šmartin, and not Nova Oselica**, for
the two years in which it can fall. Where it is, if it is recorded at all, is open: Poljane,
Škofja Loka, Stražišče and Kranj itself are the neighbouring registers on Matricula, and
nothing here says which. Franziska's baptism calls her *ehelich*, so a marriage did happen.

## What was not done

* **The parents' column on the right-hand page was not read**, so a bride entered under a
  surname other than Sedej would be missed.
* Nova Oselica marriages before 1850 and after early 1853 were not read, and no other parish
  was opened.
* **Ursula's own baptism was found the same day** in the volume before the one first walked: [ursula-sedej-was-born-on-2-october-1826.md](ursula-sedej-was-born-on-2-october-1826.md).
