# Otočac, 1821: Maria, wife of Jacobus Kralich (6 February), and Jacobus Kralich (1 August) — the Kraljich orphans (17 October 2026)

Follows [otocac-census-kraljich-1807.md](otocac-census-kraljich-1807.md): the Otočac census calls Nicolaus (b. 1807) and Stephanus (b. 1817) «filius defti Jacobi», sons of the late Jacobus. The question was when and where Jacobus was buried. **Otočac Deaths 1780–1836** (the third item on the census film, waypoint 9R2D-16X, images 156–332: Latin prose paragraphs with the day in the left margin and the age in the right, no village or house until the table begins in 1828).

## The window

Stephanus was born in September 1817, so Jacobus was alive in December 1816, and the census calls him dead when the line was written. The death register runs about 2.5 images to a year (255 = 1817, 259 = 1818, 261 = 1819). A first look at the spreads from **image 264** (the year 1821) found both.

## The two entries

**Image 264, right-hand page (archive frame 507)**, under «**Anno 1821 · In Januario**» (read at full size) and then «**In Februario**», the last entry on the page: [snapshot](/snapshots/#otocac-death-1821-02-maria-uxor-jacobi-kralich)

> **6ª** «Obiit in Dño: **Maria uxor Jacobi Kralich**: provisa omnibus SS. Sacramentis morientium, et sepulta est in Coemeterio Ssmae Trinitatis» · age **36**

**Image 266, left-hand page (archive frame 509)**, under the heading «**In Aug.**», the first entry (left margin «1ª», right margin age «41»): [snapshot](/snapshots/#otocac-death-1821-08-jacobus-kralich)

> **1ª** «Obiit sine Sacramentis ob negligentiam Domesticorum **Jacobus Kralich** et sepultus est in Coemeterio Ssmae Trinitatis» · age **41**

So **Maria, aged 36, died on 6 February 1821 with the sacraments, and Jacobus, aged 41, on 1 August 1821 without them, «because of the negligence of his household»** (no priest was fetched in time). The year's total, at the foot of image 266, is 22 adults and 39 children, 61 in all.

## What it gives

* **A date and an age for the tree's Jacobus**, who had none: died **1 August 1821, aged 41**, so born about 1780.
* **A probable mother** for the four orphans: **Maria**, aged 36 (born about 1785), who died six months earlier. The tree has two wives for Jacobus, «Anna (b. 1809)» and «Vincentia (b. 1811)»; neither fits a mother of children born 1807–1820, and **Anna b. 1809 is Nicolaus's wife** (below), so the tree's mother is wrong. Maria is a better candidate; her maiden name is not given.
* **The orphans were 13, 9, 4 and 1** in August 1821 (Nicolaus 1807, Matthaeus 1812, Stephanus 1817, Francisca about 1820). The adoption of the boys into the Dujmović house (No. 53) follows.

## And the wife of Nicolaus

The FamilySearch index names the parents of **Georgius Kraljich, baptised 19 April 1836** (Otočac births 1834–1846, waypoint 9RK1-N3B), and the page agrees: [snapshot](/snapshots/#otocac-baptism-1836-04-georgius-kraljic-prozor-40)

> «Georgius, filius legitimus Catholicus · Confiniarius ex Pago **Prozor N° 40** · **Nicolaus Kraljich et Anna uxor ejus nata Xubrinich**, Catholici»

So **the tree's Anna Žubrinić has her maiden name on a document** (the baptism), and her birth date on another (the census, «5 7bris 1809»); and **house No. 40 of the census is Prozor No. 40**. An earlier version of the Kraljić note said the Žubrinić was unsupported; that was written before this baptism was read and is withdrawn (see [corrections](/corrections/)). Anna died on 27 April 1843, aged 33 ([otocac-death-1843-anna-kraljic.md](otocac-death-1843-anna-kraljic.md)). A **Nicolaus Kraljich of Dubrava** with a wife **Lucia née Orešković** had a Josephus baptised on 9 May 1848; he is by village almost certainly a different man, and the tree's Nicolaus is not shown to have died in 1845 or after by it.

## Cautions

* **Identification is by name, age and date, not by a house number.** The prose register of 1821 has no village or house. A Jacobus Kralich of 41 and a Maria uxor Jacobi Kralich of 36 dying the same year in the same parish could be another family; the census naming Nicolaus and Stephanus as sons of a dead Jacobus, and the ages fitting the children's births, make this one very probably the tree's. The parish's own register, or a death entry with a house number, would settle it.
* **The age «41»** was read once in the right margin; the entry above it also carries «41» (a Stanislava, «circiter»).
* **The year** is the page headed «Anno 1821» on image 264, with the 1821 total on image 266; images 265 and 266 were looked at as spreads, not read entry by entry.
* **No maiden name** for Maria.
