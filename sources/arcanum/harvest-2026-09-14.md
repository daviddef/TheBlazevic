# Arcanum Digitheca — the Hungarian press, opened for the first time

14 September 2026. Lika and the Kvarner were administered from Budapest, and
this archive's own place strings still say so: it carries *Ledenice, Modruš-Fiume,
Magyarország* and *Otočac, Komitat Lika-Krbava*. Arcanum holds the press and the
official publications of exactly that administration, and **no archive in this
estate had ever run a search there.**

**What is readable and what is not.** Arcanum returns a full-text *snippet* for
every hit without a subscription; the page image behind it is paywalled
(«You have no active subscription»). Everything below is a snippet, quoted as
it was returned, with the OCR's own errors left in. **None of it has been read
at the page.** Nothing here has been added to the tree.

## The tobacco factory, counted

This archive's most-quoted finding is read out of the Senj marriage register:
the brides' occupation column goes from *tvorničarka* on three of five in 1909
to seven of eight in 1919. It had no figure for the factory itself.

> **A Pallas nagy lexikona, vol. 16 (1897)** — «Zengg (Senj, Segnia) szab. kir.
> város … kir. révhivatala, tengeri halászati bizottsága, **dohánygyára 120
> munkás**»

> **Révai Nagy Lexikona, vol. 19 (1926)** — «… tengeri halászati bizottsága,
> **dohánygyára 500 munkás**»

**One hundred and twenty workers at the end of the 1890s; five hundred by the
1920s.** In a town of a few thousand people that is not a factory growing, it
is a town changing employer — and it is the arithmetic behind the marriage
register's occupation column.

## The Žubrinići of Otočac, in print

> **Mihók-féle Magyar Compass 36/1 (1908)**, *I. Horvát-Szlavonország*, p. 1298 —
> «**Srpska štedionica dioničarsko društvo u Otočcu**, 1904 … Vorstand Oskar
> Gara, Otocac, Komitat Lika-Krbava … Buchhalter Stjepan Meniga, **Kassier
> Venzeslav Žubrinić**, Hilfsbuchhalter Juraj Milošević»

A Žubrinić as **cashier of a joint-stock savings bank** at Otočac, founded 1904.

> **Wiener Zeitung, May 1862** — «**Daniel Žubrinić, Handelsmann in Otočac**»,
> in a column of notices that also names the «Regimentsgericht in Otočać, Ungarn
> Militärgrenze»

A Žubrinić **merchant** at Otočac in 1862, beside the Frontier regimental court.

> **Neuigkeits-Welt-Blatt, October 1885** — «Diözese Zengg … Ernannt wurden zu
> Kooperatoren … **Daniel Zubrinic in Otocac**»
> **Militär-Zeitung, 1885** — «**Daniel Zubrinić**, Weltpriester der römisch-
> katholischen Diöcese Zengg-Modrus»

A Žubrinić **priest of the Senj-Modruš diocese**, appointed to Otočac in 1885 —
the same diocese whose registers this archive reads.

> **Salzburger Wacht, Arbeiter-Zeitung and Vorarlberger Wacht, April 1914** —
> «**Mijo Zubrinic**, der im Jahre **1886 in Otocac** … geboren ist … Polizist:
> Haben Sie einen Reisepaß? *Zubrinic:* Nein, aber einen Militärpaß … **Mijo
> Zubrinic, 1886 geboren, aus Otocac, Kom. Lika-Krbava**»

The same story carried by three Austrian papers: a man of the archive's own
surname, **born 1886 at Otočac**, stopped without a passport. This is precisely
the generation and the place where the archive holds nobody.

> **Službeni list, 29 June 1966** — «**Zubrinić Franje iz Prozora sina Jure**.
> Rok 8 dana. Općinski sud Otočac»
> **Službeni list, August 1975** — «**Zubrinić Josip iz Otočca**»

A court notice naming **Franjo Žubrinić of Prozor, son of Jure** — a patronymic,
in print, for a Prozor household.

## The Prpići of Krivi Put, in a criminal court

> **Morgenblatt (Zagreb), 1926–1928** — «der **Prpić-Bande**» · «Raubüberfall im
> Walde **Guvno zwischen Krivi Put und Brinje**, 11. Juli 1926» · «Raubüberfall

surnames, **Prpić** and **Smojver**, in the dock.

## The First World War, which this archive has never once opened

> **Verlustliste, June 1915** — «**Kalanj Ivan**, Inft., k.u. LIR Nr. 26,
> 3. Ers.Komp., **Kroatien, Brinje, 1891**, kriegsgef. Prokuplje, Serbien»
> **Verlustliste, March 1916** — «**Kalanj Franjo**, Inft., IR Nr. 79, 13. Komp.,
> **Kroatien, Brinje, 1893**, tot 6.3.1915» · «**Kalanj Mile**, LstInft., IR Nr. 79»

Austro-Hungarian casualty lists print **regiment, company, province, district,
commune and year of birth** to tell men apart — which makes them a genealogical
record by accident. **Brinje** is the parish this archive discovered a day ago
that the Kalanji actually belong to, and here are three Kalanj men of Brinje
with their birth years and what happened to them.

# Second harvest, same day — the queries the first pass did not run

The first pass searched surnames against place names. This one searched **the
places themselves**, and the gazetteers answered.

## Šumećica, counted and described — 67 hits

The first pass recorded `"Žubrinić" Šumećica` as **0** and stopped. **Šumećica
on its own returns sixty-seven.** The village this archive has been calling a
hamlet for a fortnight is in the Hungarian gazetteers, and they say what it was.

> **A Magyar Korona országainak helységnévtára, 1913** — *I. Általános rész ·
> Horvát-Szlavonországok · 2. Lika-Korbava vármegye · VII. Perušići járás*,
> p. 240 — «… Otocko Staroselo 423 · **Šumećica 200** · Bobinci · Grno Jezero 67 …»

**Two hundred people in 1913.**

> **Degen Árpád, *Flora Velebitica*, vol. 1 (1936)**, *Ortsregister*, p. 446 —
> «… Veliki Stolac über Lukovo Šugarje · **Šumećica Häusergruppe SW von
> Otočac** · Šumećicki …»

***Häusergruppe*** — **a group of houses**, south-west of Otočac. Not a village:
a cluster. Degen also puts a **ponor** at its foot that runs turbid when the
Lika is in flood (vol. 1 pp. 189, 196; vol. 3–4 p. 45).

> **Magyarország tiszti cím- és névtára, 4. évf. (1884)**, *A magyar korona
> országainak közig. beosztása*, p. 2_64 — «Otočac · Poljica · Ponore · Prozor ·
> Staroselo · **Šumećica** · Sinac · pk Čovići · Lešće · Ravljani»

**Šumećica listed as an administrative unit of Otočac in its own right**, beside
Prozor and Sinac.

**Why this matters for the census.** Two hundred people is **roughly
twenty-five households**. The Otočac *status animarum* lists **seven or eight**
at Šumećica. Even allowing eighty years and a different definition of the place,
**that gap is the same gap the missing Žubrinići sit in** — and it is
independent evidence that the census's Šumećica block is not the whole village.

# 15 September 2026 — the Smojvers of Karlobag, in the Hungarian state record

The second harvest found **650 hits for *Smojver*** and stopped at a line in the
1884 officials' directory. Following it produced a family's public life across
**thirty-three years**, in a town this archive has been reading parish registers
for. *Smojver* is **ahnentafel 53 and 106**.

## A registered company, and a woman who signed for it

***Központi Értesítő*** is the **official gazette of the Hungarian company
register**. It carries the firm three times.

> **30 July 1905** — «*Odpremnički posao **Dragutina Smojvera**. Fordításban:
> **Smojver Károly szállító üzlete**. Főtelep **Karlobag**. A czég tulajdonosa
> **Smojver Károly**, ki feleségét **Smojver Ljubicát** …*»

**"Dragutin Smojver's forwarding business"**, in Hungarian translation
**"Károly Smojver's haulage firm"**, **head office Karlobag** — and the owner
appoints **his wife Ljubica**.

> **8 September 1907** — «*… Főtelep Karlopago. **Ljubica Smojver czégvezető
> czégjegyzési jogosultságának megszűnése bejegyeztetett**.*»
>
> **14 May 1908** — «*… **Smojver Ljubica czégjegyzői meghatalmazása
> töröltetett**.*»

***Czégvezető*** is a **registered company manager**, and ***czégjegyzési
jogosultság*** is **the legal right to sign for the firm**. So **Ljubica Smojver
held signing authority over a registered Hungarian company at Karlobag**, and
its granting and its cancellation were both gazetted in Budapest. **A woman with
power of attorney over a Croatian coastal haulage firm in 1905** is not
something this archive would have predicted from the parish books.

## The same man, twenty-two years earlier, at school in Fiume

> ***Horvát gimnázium, Fiume, 1883***, p. 134 — «*… Prpić Vjekoslav **Karlobag**
> · 27 Perušić Milan Čavle … 35 **Smojver Dragutin — Selce** · 36 Sušanj Josip …*»

**Dragutin Smojver, of Selce, on the roll of the Croatian gymnasium at Fiume in
1883.** Selce is this archive's own place — Antić-Papa and Jelličić are from it.
A *Prpić* of **Karlobag** is two lines above him on the same page.

From the ***Magyarország tiszti cím- és névtára***, the officials' directory,
and the *Athenaeum* almanac:

| **1906–07**, p. 46 | **Smojver János**, ***pénztárnok*** — treasurer, tax office at **Belovár** |

**A Smojver was the customs controller at Karlobag itself.**

## Two Smojvers wounded, March 1915

> ***Veszteség lajstrom* / *Verlustliste*, 27 March 1915** — «*… Kroatien
> **Gospić Karlobag 1882 verw[undet] Smojver Mijo**, ResZugsf[ührer],
> IR Nr 79 … Kroatien **Korenica 1892 verw Smojver Blaž**, ResKorp[oral],
> IR Nr 79 …*»

**Mijo Smojver of Karlobag, born 1882, reserve sergeant of Infantry Regiment
79, wounded**, and **Blaž Smojver of Korenica, born 1892, reserve corporal**, in
the same regiment and the same casualty list. This archive already holds six
Žubrinići in these lists; **the Smojvers are in them too.**

## What is claimed and what is not

**Nothing here is joined to the tree.** Not one of these people has been matched
to a baptism. What the seam gives is **the shape of a surname's public life** —
school at Fiume, a registered firm at Karlobag with a wife holding the signature,
three men in the revenue service, two wounded in Galicia — in exactly the town
and the decades where the parish registers have the family.
