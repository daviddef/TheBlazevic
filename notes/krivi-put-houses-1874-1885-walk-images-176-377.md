# Krivi Put births, the second book (images 176–377, 1874 to about 1885): the parents' column and the house column

Waypoint 9R28-DP8 (DGS 5494757), read 23 November 2026 from the list's images 176–377, in the parents' column and the house column of
each opening (the left page's parents column and the right page's «Locus domicilii et numerus domalis» column, with a look at the godparents
beside it). Continues `krivi-put-houses-1871-1873-walk-images-135-173.md`.

## The question

Where do Joannes (Ivan) Perpić and Lucia (née Perpić) stand after Anna (1870): at house 135/136, or elsewhere, and who else do they have?

## What the pages are

- **Images 176–179 are not entries**: slates and a target (176–177), the book's cover (178: «Liber baptisatorum parochiae Krivipůtensis B.V.M. ad
  Nives ab anno 1874–188…») and a flyleaf (179). The first opening is 180 («Octingentesimus Septuagesimus Quartus», 1874).
- **Images 180–373 are the entries.** The left page holds the child and the parents, the right page the house, the godparents, the baptiser and
  the Observationes. The table is Latin to about image 247; **from image 248 (1878) it is written in Croatian** («Krivi put kbr. 136»,
  «Jvana Perpića i Klare rodjene Tomljanović»), and the forms to look for change from Joannes × Lucia to Ivan/Jvan × Luca/Luce.
  Years begin at 180 (1874), about 199 right (1875), about 215 left (1876), 232 (1877), 248 (1878), 266 (1879), 283 (1880), 306 left (1881),
  323 left (1882), 338 right (1883), 357 left (1884).
- **The house number is double**: «Klaričevac N.D. 82 / N. 163», «Veljun N.D. 71 [struck] 136». A number alone does not find a couple.
- Duplicate exposures and slips met: 292 and 293, and 294 and 295, are two exposures each of one opening, with a printed form slip
  («PODACI O RODITELJIMA») or a pasted letter over some house cells; 304 and 305 are two exposures with a later-hand baptism note pasted over three rows' house
  cells (their parents are on the left page and were read).
- **Images 374–376 are loose slips**, not entries: a Senj baptism extract of 1875 (Maria, daughter of Josephus Prpić and Lucia née Špalj, Krivi put;
  received 26 June 1875), a Vratnik parish note, and a Senj extract of 1875 (Mirko Babić, received 13 December 1877). None is our couple, but they show
  that the parish **received Senj baptism extracts**: a Krivi Put child baptised in Senj need not be in this register's rows.
- **Image 377 right is a pasted sheet** copied from Senj baptism extracts «u svrhu novačenja» (for conscription), 1893, signed by a parish priest:
  four 1874 baptisms, Br. 162–165. **Images 378–381 are a filming slate («NASTAVAK … SVITKA BR: 58»), a resolution chart and END OF ROLL.** The book
  therefore continues on roll 58, which is not on this film: **after about 1884–85 the register is not here.**

- **Pave (1881) is new**: a further child of the couple at Klaričevac 82, who died aged two (10 October 1883, in a later hand).
- **Matija (1879), a girl («zakonita»), is re-found** (already in the Senj baptism index), with the house, the godparents and the baptiser added; her Senj entry is read (`notes/senj-births-march-1879-matija.md`).
- **The house moves**: Joanna 1874 stands at 135; from 1876 (Josephus) the couple's children are at Klaričevac 82, number 163 in the old numbering.
  Klaričevac 82 holds other Perpić households at the same time (Mile × Mande, 82/165 in 1878 and 1880; Nikola × Franjica, 1883).
- **Michael (1874) is a real baptism, found in the Senj register the same day (entry 5, image 318 of waypoint 9R2D-JW5):** «6a die 11a Januarii 1874, Michael, legitimus,
  Joannes Perpić conf. et Lucia nata Perpić, Rom. Cath.», house «Krivipút N° 22», godparents Thomas Thian and Maria Perpić, baptiser Joannes Kocmut coop. loci.
  So the 1893 sheet copied a real entry, and Michael was baptised at Senj, not at Krivi Put (he is not in this register's rows on images 180–181, entries 1–15 read).
  The same day of birth and the same parents' names as Joanna (born 6 January, baptised 12 January at Krivi Put, house 135) stand against **two different house numbers
  in the registers (22 at Senj, 135 at Krivi Put)**; the sheet's «82 (prov. No 163)» is a third form of the number, the one the couple's later children carry. **Twins, or two couples of the
  same names, is still not settled**: the Senj register does not say, and no entry names a sibling. It has been narrowed to a question about house numbers.
- **Matija's father is written two ways, and the Senj register is now read.** The Krivi Put row writes «Jvana Perpića i Luce rodjene Perpić»; the Senj register (image 25 of 9R2D-JWL,
  printed page 17, entry 17) writes «Ivan Tomljanović poljodjelac i Lucia rodj. Prpić», as the index extract said. The house («Klaričevac kb. 82»), the godparents and the baptiser are the same, so it is
  one baptism recorded twice. Which surname the father bore is not settled here.
- **Not our couple, noticed**: a Matija born and baptised 24 February 1884 at image 360 (entry 22) with «Ivan Prpić» written small above
  «Luce Prpić kćeri Petra i Ane» (Krivi put 45, «sin nezakoniti po naknadnoj ženitbi zakonit», married 11 October 1908): this is a different Luce, a
  daughter of Petar and Ana. On 369 right, row 3 (Krivi put kbr. 114, about 1885), the godparents are «Ivan Prpić i Luce Prpić rimo kat. seljaci»: a sighting
  that is not an identification.
- **135 and 136 afterwards**: 136 reappears in 1876 for Nicolaus Perpić × Antonia Krmpotić (image 209 right; «Veljun N.D. 71 [struck] 136»), and 135 for
  Lucas Perpić × Theresia née Perpić (image 217; «133 [struck] 135»). At image 249 (1878) Franica Perpić, daughter of Pero Perpić, is at Krivi put kbr. 136 (Petrus's number),
  with a godmother «Luce Perpić rimo kat. poljodjelkinja»: a godmother, not a parent, and not identified.

## What this does not show

- **Not a walk and not a nil.** Read: the parents' and house columns of images 180–373 (and the slips 374–376, the sheet 377 and the ends 378–381).
  Not read: images 1–9 and 175 of the waypoint's list; the foot rows of 190–192 (a white sheet over them), the rows covered on 180 left, 292–295 and 304–305 (house cells; parents read);
  the godparents' column, except where it was seen beside a house (so a Lucia, wife of Joannes, as godmother elsewhere is not looked for); and everything after about 1884–85, which is on roll 58.
- The Senj register for Matija (2 March 1879) and for Michael (11 January 1874) is read elsewhere: `notes/senj-births-march-1879-matija.md`, `notes/senj-births-january-1874-michael-perpic.md`.
- The 1893 sheet is a copy made from extracts, not the register; read it as a later witness.

## What follows

- The Senj baptism register for 11 January 1874 is now read (Michael, entry 5; see the note named above) and does not settle twin or second couple; the register at 2 March 1879 (Matija) is read in `notes/senj-births-march-1879-matija.md` and gives the father as Tomljanović.
- Roll 58 (the continuation of this book, from about 1885) is not on this film: Josephus, who has a later note on image 226 («umro 3.1…»), and the later children
  of the couple would be there.
- Surname and given-name forms met: Perpić/Perpii, Prpić, Tomljanović/Tomjanović, Krmpotić/Kermpotić, Šojat/Sojat, Durbešić, Glavičić, Serdoc; Ivan/Jvan/Joannes,
  Luca/Luce/Lucia, Pave/Pavao. All are in the Atlas catalogues or are ordinary typography; none is new.
