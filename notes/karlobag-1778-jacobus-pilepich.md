# «D. Jacobus Pilepich, Capitaneus portûs» — Karlobag, 10 April 1778

15 September 2026. Work list row 23. **Ahnentafel 104 is read**, and he is not
who this archive had him down as. He is the **Captain of the Port of Karlobag**,
styled *Dominus*, and he is buried **inside the parish church**.

## The entry

Karlobag, *Umrli 1774–1804*, **folio 17**, image **364**, ARK
`3:1:3QS7-L99C-GSBG`. The entry gives no age.

> «**10: Aprilis** · **D. Jacobus Pilepich Capitaneus portûs**, munitus omnibus
> Sacramentis per M.R. D. Andream Pintor Capellanum Loci, **spiritum Deo
> reddidit**, … **in propria Crypta in Parochiali Ecclesia existente**, per me
> Joannem [Antonium Frankovich Parochum] tumulatus est.»

*"D. Jacobus Pilepich, captain of the port, fortified with all the sacraments by
the Very Reverend Andreas Pintor, chaplain of this place, gave back his spirit to
God … and was buried in his own crypt existing in the parish church, by me
Joannes [Antonius Frankovich, parish priest]."*

## What is new in it

**A rank.** *Capitaneus portûs* — **captain of the port**. Karlobag is a harbour
on the Adriatic under the Military Frontier, and the port captain is its
harbourmaster: a crown office, not a trade. Nothing in this archive had him as
anything but a name and a date.

**A title.** The ***D.*** before his name is *Dominus*, and the register does not
give it away — the other men on this opening are *Decurio*, *infans*, or nothing
at all.

**And a grave that says the same thing again.** He is not in the *Coemeterium
Parochiale* with his neighbours on the same page. He is ***in propria Crypta in
Parochiali Ecclesia*** — **in his own crypt inside the church**. In an
eighteenth-century Adriatic parish that is reserved for the few.

**Three independent marks of standing in one entry**, and this archive had none
of them an hour ago. It also gives the Pilipići a **place in the town** that the
later generations — ahnentafel 52 and 26, both read elsewhere — can now be
measured against.

## How he was found, and the method that found him

**Through the book's own KAZALO, not through FamilySearch's index.** Row 43
established that the record index holds **baptisms only** for Karlobag; the
deaths must be walked. But the volume carries its own A–Z index, and it works:

1. **Image 427, right page — the letter P**, three dense columns, entries ordered
   **by folio, not alphabetically within the letter**.
2. «**Pillepich Jacobus · 10 Aprilis · folio 17**», read at full resolution.
3. **Folio 17 → image 364**, and the entry is the ninth on the right-hand page.

**The folio-to-image map, measured directly.** Image **364** carries folios
**16** (left) and **17** (right); image **365** carries **18** and **19**. So

    image = 356 + folio ÷ 2      (even folios, left-hand pages)

**This disagrees by one with `holdings.psv`**, which records *folio 62 = image
388* where this formula gives 387. One of the two is off, or a folio is skipped
somewhere between 17 and 62. **Check the folio number printed at the top of the
page before reading**, every time — it is written there, and it costs one crop.

## And the surname this index put on the table

The P columns of image 427 hold **five Pekas entries** — *Pekas Anton*, *Pekas
Blasius*, *Pekas Maria*, *Pekas Joanna*, and on image 428 *Pekkas Magdalena, 5
Augusti, folio 106*.

**Pekass is the surname of ahnentafel 213, 426 and 427** — three direct ancestors
with no document of any kind. **These are index lines, not register entries**,
and none of them has been opened. They are recorded here as a list of places to
look, and nothing more.

## And a second ancestor, found because the index knows about by-names

**Ahnentafel 212 is read.** Karlobag *Umrli 1774–1804*, **folio 11**, image
**361**, ARK `3:1:3QS7-L99C-G3WG`:

> «**16 ejusdem** [*Maij* 1776] · **Josephus Smojver dictus Vukcich, annorum
> 45**, munitus omnibus [Sacramentis] … in Domino obijt, **atque die 17** … in
> **Coemeterio Parochiali** … tumulatus.»

**16 May 1776 is the tree's date to the day**, and *annorum 45* puts his birth
between May 1730 and May 1731 where the tree says **1731**. He was buried the
next day, in the parish cemetery — not, like the port captain, inside the church.

**What found him is the thing worth keeping.** He is not in the **S** columns
where a Smojver belongs — or rather, the S columns were read down to about folio
9 and the sitting turned away before folio 11. He was found under **V**:

> «**Vukich — vide Smojver — 16: Maij — folio 11**»

**The KAZALO indexes by-names as headwords and cross-references them.** The
scribe wrote *Vukić* in the index, then *vide Smojver* to send the reader to the
surname. The same page does it again for another family: «*Verbanovich Margarita
… **vide Urbanovich** … 15 9bris*».

**That changes how this index should be used.** A surname missing from its own
letter is not absent from the book — **it may be filed under the household's
by-name, in another letter entirely, with a *vide* pointing back**. For a family
this archive already knows by two names — [*Smoyver vulgò Vukić*](karlobag-1781-oliva-smojver.md),
read on the 1781 baptism of ahnentafel 53 — that is not a curiosity. It is the
difference between finding an ancestor and writing a null.

**And the register agrees with the baptism, with a different connector.** 1776
says ***dictus* Vukcich**; 1781 says ***vulgò* Vukić**. Same pair, two
connectors, five years apart, in the same parish.
