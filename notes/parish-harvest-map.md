## The two numbers that matter

**Novi Vinodolski baptisms begin in 1650. Grižane in 1692.**

The oldest document this archive currently holds is a **Karlobag baptism of
1754**. These two parishes go back a further sixty to a hundred years, and one
of them — Novi Vinodolski — is the parish for **Klenovica**, where 119 Kalanj
records sit and where this archive established that *Klenovica 22 and Povile 22
are one house holding 67 people*.

**Grižane also has a status animarum**, dated 1738 — a second household census,
smaller and far older than the Otočac one, in a parish next door to the Kalanj
villages.
