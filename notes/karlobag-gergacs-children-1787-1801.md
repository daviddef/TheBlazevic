# Michael Gergacs and his wife Lucia: five baptisms, 1787–1801, and what they do to «annorum 34»

17 October 2026. A FamilySearch index query by the father's surname (Gergacs, Gerkacs, Gerkacz, Gergacz, Jerkacs, Gerkach), Karlobag, 1780–1815. The baptisms are **index only; no baptism page was opened.** The 1809 death entry was re-read from its page (below).

| baptised | child | father | mother | index record |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 11 March 1787 | Magdalena | Michael Gergacs | Lucia | QKMX-BCZC |
| August 1789 | **Maria** (ahnentafel 55) | Michael Gergacs | Lucia | QKMX-YJP7 |
| 25 November 1794 | Catharina | Michael Gergacz | Lucia | QKMX-YVGM |
| 17 August 1797 | Bartholomæus | Michael Gergacs | Lucia | QKMX-YBDF |
| 23 February 1801 | Mathia | Michael Gergacs | Lucia | QKMX-YK1J |

**What it says.** The wife of Michael Gergacs is named Lucia in every baptism from March 1787 to February 1801 — five children, and no other name. Michael (born 1762) and Lucia Tomljanović married on 2 November 1785 (see [karlobag-marriage-1785-gerkacs-tomljanovic.md](karlobag-marriage-1785-gerkacs-tomljanovic.md)), and the first child follows sixteen months later. The baptisms are two years apart (1787, 1789), then **five years** (1794), then three and four (1797, 1801).

**The 1809 age, re-read.** The death entry was opened again at full resolution (image 125, left page, level 13) and the age is **34**: a 3 with two humps and a 4 with a loop, unlike the 7 of «74» two entries above. **It is not 44, so a misreading by the first session is ruled out** — [snapshot](/snapshots/#karlobag-death-1809-lucia-maja-age). The priest wrote 34; whether the figure is right is a separate question.

**What that leaves.** A bride of 1785 cannot be 34 in 1809, so either the priest's figure is wrong or there are two Lucias, and **the baptisms do not choose**:

* *One Lucia, the figure wrong.* She bore all five children and was born in the 1760s, about 40–45 in 1809. Ages in these registers are often rounded, but twenty years is a large error.
* *Two Lucias.* The first (Tomljanović, married 1785) bore Magdalena and Maria and died between 1789 and 1794; Michael married a second Lucia, who would have been 19 in 1794 and 34 in 1809, and bore Catharina, Bartholomæus and Mathia. The change could sit anywhere between the baptism of 1789 and that of 1801 (the five-year gap of 1789–94 is the most obvious slot), and the written age fits this reading without any correction. A second wife with the same name is **not excluded by the index**, because the baptism entries name only «Lucia».

**Search for the change, 17 October 2026.** No marriage of a Michael Gergacs (Karlobag marriages 1789–March 1801) and no death of a wife of Michael Gergacs (Karlobag deaths 1788–January 1801) turns up: see [karlobag-gergacs-wife-search-1789-1801.md](karlobag-gergacs-wife-search-1789-1801.md). That **favours one Lucia and a wrong figure in 1809**, but only weakly: a second wife could have married in her own parish, and the first could have died away from Karlobag. The two-Lucias reading is not ruled out.

**One more baptism, 1815.** The same query turns up *Antoniam Vincentiam* Gergach, baptised at Karlobag on 19 January 1815, daughter of **Michael Gergach and Martha** (QKMX-YG2W). **Found 17 October 2026: Michael Gergacs, widower, married Martha, widow of Laurentius Kovačević, at Karlobag on 8 October 1809** ([note](karlobag-marriage-1809-gergacs-martha.md)), nineteen days after Lucia's burial, so the 1815 baptism is that couple's child and he **married again after Lucia's death in 1809** (a third marriage on the two-Lucias reading). The entry gives no age. The same surname set shows Michael's relatives Antonius Gergacs and Francisca (sons Joannes Antonius 1786 and Simon Lucas 1788), who may be the witness Antonius of the 1785 marriage. **No Gergacs baptism with Michael as father is indexed between 1801 and 1815**, and none between 1789 and 1794 other than Catharina's.

**New children.** The tree gives Michael one child, Maria (1789). The index adds **Magdalena (1787), Catharina (1794), Bartholomæus (1797) and Mathia (1801)** — brothers and sisters of ahnentafel 55, and none is in the tree.

**What it does not say.** Where Lucia was born. The index has no baptism of a Lucia to a Paulus Tomljanović, and the Karlobag births are thin in the 1760s. Two other brides of 1789 and 1795, daughters of a Paulus Tomljanović of Stanište, are probable sisters ([note](karlobag-tomljanovic-daughters-1789-1795.md)); see [the Tomljanović page](https://blazevic.defranceski.com/tomljanovic/).
