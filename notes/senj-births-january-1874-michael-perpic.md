# Senj births, 1859–1877: the register for 11 January 1874 (Michael, son of Joannes Perpić and Lucia née Perpić)

Waypoint 9R2D-JW5 (Senj births 1859–1877, 399 images), read 23 November 2026 at images 317–319 (the openings holding the baptisms of 1 to 28 January 1874).
Follows `krivi-put-houses-1874-1885-walk-images-176-377.md`, whose pasted sheet (image 377 right) copied four 1874 Senj baptisms for conscription.

## The question

The sheet's Br. 163 says a Michael, born 6 January 1874 and baptised 11 January to Joannes Prpić and Lucia née Prpić of Krivi put, was baptised at Senj. Joanna, born the same day
to the same names, is in the Krivi Put register (baptised 12 January, house 135). Is Michael in the Senj register, and does it say whose child he is?

## What the pages are

- The book is the Latin printed table (Annus, Mensis, Dies; Baptisati; Parentum; Locus domicilii et numerus domalis; Patrinorum; Baptisantis; Observationes) of the Senj
  cathedral parish, entries numbered from 1 each year. Image 317 is the opening for printed page 309 (right page) and carries entries 2–7 (6 to 17 January 1874); image 318,
  printed page 310, carries entries 8–13 (17 to 20 January); image 319, printed page 311, entries 14–19 (read at page scale only). Entry 1 of the year is on the opening before.
- The entries run in the order of baptism. The baptisers on these pages are Antonius Bosnić cooperator loci, Joannes Kocmut cooperator loci and Casparus Matković (canonicus).

## What was found

- **Entry 5, image 318 of the waypoint's list (ark 3QSQ-G99X-1Q1Y): «6a die 11a Januarii 1874 — Michael — legitimus — Joannes Perpić conf. et Lucia nata Perpić — Rom. Cath. —
  Krivipút N° 22 — Thomas Thian, Maria Perpić — Joannes Kocmut coop. loci»; the Observationes column is empty.** Snapshot `/snapshots/#senj-baptism-1874-01-11-michael-perpic`.
  The house number was zoomed (two 2s).
- **The 1893 sheet copied real entries.** Br. 162 (Joannes Tomljanović, born 7, baptised 10 January 1874, «Krivi put 34») is entry 3 on the same page (Matheus Tomljanović and Lucia, «Krivipút N° 34»);
  Br. 163 is entry 5 above; Br. 164 (Mathias, son of Paulus Perpić and Rosaria née Perpić, Krivi put) is entry 13 on image 318 (born and baptised 20 January 1874, godparents Thomas Thian and Antonia (surname as read: Mladineo), baptised by Ignatius Vinski). Br. 165 was not looked for.
- **The sheet and the register give the house differently:** the register «N° 22», the sheet «82 (prov. No 163)». The Krivi Put register's Joanna (1874) is at house 135, and the couple's later children
  (Josephus 1876, Matija 1879, Pave 1881) are at Klaričevac 82 (old number 163). The sheet's number is the one of 1893 or the later children, the register's is the one of 1874.
- **Not found: any entry for Joanna at Senj, and any later note on Michael's entry.** Entries 2–13 on images 317–318 were read; images 316 (entry 1) and 319 (entries 14–19, 20 to 28 January)
  were not read entry by entry. Joanna is not among entries 2–13.

## What this does not show

- **Twin or second couple is not settled.** Two children of the same names, born the same day, baptised on two days in two churches, with house numbers 22 (Senj) and 135 (Krivi Put),
  fit twins split between two churches as well as two couples. No entry on either page says "gemellus" or names a sibling. Nothing here is a finding about who the children's parents were beyond what the entries say.
- This is the Senj register for 1 to 20 January 1874 only: not a walk of the volume.
- The Matija entry of 2 March 1879 (father written Tomljanović in the Senj index) is in the next volume (9R2D-JWL) and not read.
