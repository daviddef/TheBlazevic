# Joannes Antić × Martha Lončarić, Selce, 18 November 1798: ahnentafel 48 and 49 married, and the register names all four parents

11 October 2026. Work list rows 34 and 25. The Selce marriage book 1790-1815 (DGS 5482267, images about
240-294) is Latin prose, one paragraph an entry, with the house number in the left margin, and — unlike the
baptisms of the 1780s — it names the parents of both parties. Image 248, left page, is headed «Anno 1798».

## The entry

House number in the margin: **27** (as read). The entry begins «Die 18° 9bris» — 18 **November** 1798 (9bris is
November) — «praemissis tribus inter Missarum solemnia habitis denuntiationibus et detecto impedimento 4ti et 4ti
duplicis consanguinitatis, obtentaque desuper ab Illmo et Rmo Ordinario dispensatione … sub No 18 existente. Ego
Michaël Kombol Capellanus Localis junxi Matrimonio **Joannem filium Defunctorum Anto[nii] et Ursulae Antich**, cum
**Martha filia Pauli et Annae Lončarich**, praesentibus testibus Mathia Jellichich et Georgio Antich.»

## What it gives

* **Ahnentafel 48, Joannis Antić (the tree: b. 1765, d. 13 March 1829), is the groom, and his parents are named:
  ANTONIUS and URSULA Antić, both dead by 1798.** The tree has his father as «Joannis» (ahnentafel 96); the death
  register of 27 February 1793 reads «Antonius Antich Martini, aged about 54» (notes/selce-deaths-1793-1807-1811.md),
  and this entry agrees with the register and not with the tree. **Ahnentafel 97, Ursula, now has a source.**
* **Ahnentafel 49, Margarita (Martha) Lončarić, is the bride, and her parents are Paulus and Anna Lončarić** —
  ahnentafel 98 and 99, whose deaths of 1811 and 1807 are already read. The tree's two names for her
  (Margarita, Martha) are the book's «Martha».
* **A dispensation for double consanguinity in the fourth degree** («4ti et 4ti duplicis»): the couple were
  related twice, in the fourth degree each time, which says the Antić and Lončarić (Papić) houses of Selce were
  already kin. Nothing here says how.
* The date, 18 November 1798, is a mass day for the parish (several entries of the same day).

## Caution

* The groom's father's first word is cut by the page's right edge and the line-end; it reads «Anto…», and
  Antonius is the only given name that fits it and the 1793 death.
* The house number 27 is as read; Joannes Antić's house at his death in 1829 is 51 (and 56 is his wife's), so a
  move is possible and nothing here shows it.
* The page was read at about 6x for the entry and only the entry; the others of the day were not.
