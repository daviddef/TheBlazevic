# Karlobag deaths 1804–1861: Lucia «Maja» (19 September 1809) and Josephus Pilepich (June 1828)

7 October 2026. Work list row 24, the two ancestors it still had open: **ahnentafel 111**
(Lucia «Maja» Tomljanović) and **ahnentafel 52** (Josephus Pilipić). Both are read from the
images. One agrees with the tree to the day and the other is two days out; neither is settled
by an age that fits.

**Where.** Karlobag *Umrli 1804–1861*, on DGS 5481649, FamilySearch waypoint **9RK1-HZK**
(the Atlas lists it as «Deaths (Umrli) 1804-1861 + Kazalo», years 1736–1861, which contain
both frames). The waypoint enumerates **435 images**; the deaths register is images ~120–165
and its alphabetical KAZALO ~168–192. The register's left-hand page number runs two to an
image (image 125 L = page 12, 126 L = 14, 138 L = 34, 139 L = 36).

## Ahnentafel 111 — «Lucia, vulgo Maja, uxor Michaelis Gergacs», 19 September 1809

Image **125, left page (page 12)**, ark `3:1:3QS7-L99C-G99J`. A prose entry, September 1809, in
the lower third of the page, margin «19.» with the month written as a ditto from the
September entry above it:

> «**Lucia, vulgo Maja** [read: *Maša/Maja*, the middle letter is the hand's long form],
> **uxor Michaelis Gergacs** [first letter G or J], annorum **34**, provisa omnibus
> sacramentis obiit in Domino, atque in propria crypta in cœmeterio existente humata est.»

Three things agree with the tree (`data/ancestors.tsv`, ahnentafel 111: «Lucia (Maja)
Tomljanović», d. **19 SEP 1809** Karlobag, wife of Michael Gerkacs, m. 2 Nov 1785):

* **the day and month**, 19 September 1809;
* **the by-name «Maja»**, which the tree carries in brackets and which is not a given name
  anyone would invent;
* **the husband**, Michael Gergacs, who the tree has living to 9 July 1836, so *uxor* and not
  *vidua* is right.

**One thing does not agree, and it is not explained: the age.** The register says **34**
(read at the highest zoom this session reached; the first digit is a 3, not a 5). A woman
of 34 in 1809 was born about 1775 and would have married Michael at **ten** in November 1785
and borne Maria, ahnentafel 55, at **fourteen** in August 1789. Either the age is wrong in
the register (this register's ages are good to a year or two elsewhere — her daughter's was
exact — so a wrong age by twenty years would be unusual), or this is a **different Lucia,
also Michael Gergacs's wife**: a second marriage after 1789 would fit an age of 34, and the
by-name «Maja» might then be a coincidence of a common nickname. **This archive does not
choose.** The entry is recorded as evidence that a Lucia «Maja», wife of Michael Gergacs,
died on the day the tree says; it is not recorded as evidence of her age or birth.

**Why the index never found her.** She is not under Tomljanović in the KAZALO (the 1809
column of that surname — 3 March, 9 April, 2 July, then rows dated the 17th, 9th, 14th
and 16th of the months after — has no Lucia and no entry dated 19 September) — a wife is indexed under her husband's surname, and the T
and G sections are different instruments. Her entry was found by turning to the right
September page, not by the index. **An index proves what it contains and never what it
omits** — the lesson of [the-kozina-of-zgornje-bitnje.md](the-kozina-of-zgornje-bitnje.md),
again.

## Ahnentafel 52 — «Josephus Pilepich Carlobagensis, annorum 61», June 1828

Image **138, right-hand page (page 35)**, ark `3:1:3QSQ-G99C-G9HP`, *Anno Domini 1828*.
Found through the KAZALO: under **P**, image 185 right page, the 1828 row «**Pilepich
Josephus · 28 Junii · 828 · folio 35**», ark `3:1:3QS7-L99C-G93H`. The entry, fourth on
the page:

> «**Josephus Pilepich Carlobagensis** annorum **61** in communione S. Matris Ecclesiæ
> animam Deo reddidit, cujus corpus die **29** h. sepultum est in cœmeterio S. Caroli
> Boromei [sic as read], M.R.D. [Cooperatori Paulo Naglich] confessus, sanctissimi viatico
> refectus, et Sacri Olei Unctione roboratus per eumdem.»

* **The day: the register reads 28 June** in the margin («28ᵗʰ Junii», the second digit a
  looped 8 that could be a 6 at the resolution reached), **the KAZALO reads 28**, and the
  **burial is on the 29th**. The tree has **26 June 1828**. The burial the next day fits a
  death on the 28th and fits a death on the 26th badly. **The tree is two days out against
  two independent readings**; the tree is not changed here, because this archive does not
  edit the tree.
* **The age: 61.** The tree's birth, 11 March 1768 (baptism read as index only, *Josephum
  Piberich*, the index's misreading of Pillopich), makes him 60. A year's difference is
  ordinary in this register.
* **Is he ahnentafel 52?** He is the only Josephus Pilepich the 1828 column holds, he is of
  Karlobag, and the age is within a year of the tree's. **That is a probable match, not a
  proven one**: the entry names no wife and no parent, and the register does not say
  «hujus loci» or give a house. What would prove it is his marriage entry of 14 February 1798
  (Oliva Maria Smojver) read against a household, or a *Stanje duša* line.

## What was not done

* The 1809 deaths were read for **19 September only**, on one page; the rest of 1809 and the
  page that follows were not read entry by entry.
* **Lucia's baptism and the 1785 marriage** were not looked for in this sitting; her
  birthplace in the tree, Montani Stanište, rests on a MyHeritage tree and not on a register.
* No Gergacs second marriage was searched for.
* The Karlobag deaths **1804–1861 were not walked**; two entries were fetched through the
  KAZALO and one by page.
