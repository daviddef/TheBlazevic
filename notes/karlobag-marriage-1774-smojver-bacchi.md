# Karlobag, the Smojver × Bacchi marriage of 2 May 1774, and the long Uroda reading it started — which breaks the tree's Caetano Bacchi of 1779

> **SUPERSEDED IN PART, 11 October 2026 (night).** This note began as one marriage and grew into the
> whole Uroda reading, written in the order it was done; the sections near the foot are later and
> win where they disagree with the ones above. **Withdrawn claims:** (1) *«the index lists none of
> Dume's children»* — it lists them under the spelling «Urode»; (2) *the May 1744 son's name
> «Gratiova?»* — the index reads him **Giakova (Jakov)**, so he is not a Georgius; (3) *Dume's
> by-name «Horaca [Kovaca?]»* — re-read at 3x it is **«Kovaca» (Kovač)**; (4) *the 1764 godparents
> «ordinary Karlobag households»* — the godfather is **Antonio Barach «of Šibenik»**; (5) *the tree's
> Angelo Bacchi as evidence for the Pag candidate* — he comes only from the 1779 baptism of a
> different Caetanus Antonius. The current picture is drawn on the /lines/ page,
> and its open ends are work-list rows 9, 81 and 82.


11 October 2026. Work list rows 24, 26 and 34. Karlobag marriages 1736-1804 (DGS 5481649, waypoint 9RK1-HZK), image 306,
right-hand page; the page's head carries «Anno Dni 1773» and a second heading «Anno Dni 1774» near the top, and this
entry stands below it (day column «2 Maij»). The following entries are of 26 June and 10 July 1774.

## The entry

«2. Maij. Praemissis praemittendis et habito mutuo consensu **Vincentii filii Josephi Smojver, vulgo nuncupati
Vukich** [Vukić], ac **Olivae filiae defuncti Caetani Bacchi**, eos per verba de praesenti solemniter conjunxi,
praesentibus Dno Nicolao Jurich et Francisco [Lukulich?].»

## What it gives

* **The marriage of ahnentafel 106 (Vincentius Fabiano Smojver Vukić, b. 1754) and 107 (Oliva Andreana Bacchi)**: 2 May
  1774, he about twenty, she «daughter of the late Caetanus Bacchi».
* **ahnentafel 106's father is named: JOSEPHUS Smojver (the tree's ahnentafel 212, Josiph Smojver Vukić, b. 1731, d.
  16 May 1776)**, and the surname is written «Smojver, vulgo nuncupati Vukich» — the book itself says Vukić is the
  by-name, the same pattern as the Antić «Papa».
* **ahnentafel 107's father is named, and was dead by May 1774: CAETANUS BACCHI (ahnentafel 214).**
* **Ahnentafel 53 (Oliva Maria Smojver, b. March 1781) is therefore not their first child** — they had been married
  seven years.

## A conflict this opens

The tree gives **Caetano Bacchi (ahnentafel 214) as born 12 January 1779 (marked «maybe»)**, the son of Angelo Bacchi and
Catharina (ahnentafel 428 and 429). A man born in 1779 cannot have been the dead father of a bride in 1774. **The 1779
baptism in the Karlobag index is a different, later Caetano, and the tree has attached the wrong one** — and with him
ahnentafel 428 and 429, whose only support was that baptism. The marriage entry gives no father for the elder
Caetanus, so ahnentafel 428 and 429 revert to «not known». On /corrections.

## Caution

* The bride's surname «Bacchi» is as read (the final letters are cut by a line end); the witnesses are read at one
  zoom; «Vukich» is the word after «nuncupati» (read at about 6x).
* The year 1774 is read from the page heading above the entry; the entries below it carry 26 June and 10 July.

## Added later the same day: the elder Caetanus Bacchi was dead by October 1760

Walking the marriage book backward for another search (image 293, left-hand page, year-heading «Anno Domini 1760»),
the entry of **20 October 1760** reads, after «Idem eadem die»: «matrimonialiter copulavi **Paulum Perpich et Annam
viduam Caetani Bachi**. Praesentibus testibus Joanne Mat[hosich] ac Petro Bovandich.» So a Caetanus Bacchi had **died
before 20 October 1760**, leaving a widow ANNA who then married Paulus Perpich — a better fix on his death than the
«before 2 May 1774» of the 1774 entry, and nothing like a man born in 1779.

* It is **probably** the same Caetanus: Bacchi is a rare surname in this book and the 1774 bride's father is a dead
  Caetanus Bacchi. NOT PROVED, because neither entry names the other, and Oliva is not called Anna's daughter anywhere.
* If Oliva was Anna's daughter she was born before the father's death, so by about 1760 at the latest; she was married in 1774.
* Perpich is spelt «Cerpich»/«Perpich» in the hand and was read at one zoom (the line is clear).

## Added later the same day: the elder Caetanus Bacchi alive in 1753–55, with a wife Anna and a son Silvestro

Reading the Karlobag baptism book for 1750–1759 for Oliva's own baptism (Karlobag births 1691-1804, DGS 5481648, waypoint
9RK1-3Y9, book pages 200–241; the viewer helper's image numbers 112–135) turned up the family instead:

* **18 March 1753** (book page 211, helper image 117 right): at the baptism of a daughter of Petar Tomljenovich, «bihu
  kumi **Caietan Bachi, svojom xenom Annom**» — Caetanus Bacchi as sponsor with his wife Anna.
* **27 May 1753** (page 212, image 118 left) and **late October 1755** (page 221, image 123 left, the line partly cut by the
  page edge): «Caietan Bachi y Anne» again among the sponsors.
* **2–3 January 1754** (page 215, image 119 right, the entry after 1 January): «krstih **Antola [Antonu?] Sylvestra,
  sina Gajetana y Anne Bachi**», sponsors «Jose y Venera Dur[…]ch» — a **son, Sylvester, of Caetanus and Anna Bacchi**.
  (The 18th-century hand writes the surname «Bachi»; the index has none of these.)

So **Caetanus Bacchi was alive from March 1753 to at least October 1755 and dead by 20 October 1760, with a wife Anna** —
the same Anna who is his widow in 1760. Together with the 1774 marriage this makes a consistent family: Caetanus and
Anna, a son Sylvester (b. January 1754), a daughter Oliva (married 1774, so born before about 1759). **Oliva's own baptism
is not on the pages read** — 1750 to about the end of August 1759 (flaps of cardboard and a loose slip hide parts of book
pages 226–227 and 235 in some frames; the same pages show in the neighbouring frames; helper images 124 and 125 are the
same pages twice). She may be older than 1750 (baptised 1745–49, unread) or younger than the pages read, or
baptised elsewhere. Read at one zoom, names in the Croatian hand of 1750–57 and the Latin of the later pages.

## Added later the same day: the marriage of Caetanus Bacchi and Anna Lukatelli, 4 February 1753 — and a correction to the paragraph above

Karlobag marriages 1736-1804 (DGS 5481649, waypoint 9RK1-HZK), image 287 right-hand page (the heading «Anno Domini 1753»
opens at 15 January; the entries run 15 January, 23 January, 4 February): «**Die 4ta Febr:** … *Idem eadem die
matrimonialiter copulavi ad faciem Ecclesiae* **Cajetanum Bachi et Annam filiam defuncti Alexii [Lukatelli]**.
Praesentibus Wolffgango Suvich ac Joanne Radkovich.» The father's surname is read from the full-page view
(«Lukatelli», a Karlobag surname that fills the book) and the day as the 4th at about 6x.

* **Caetanus Bacchi's marriage: 4 February 1753, to Anna, daughter of the late Alexius Lukatelli.**
* It makes the 1753 sponsor «Caietan Bachi, svojom xenom Annom» (March 1753) a newly married couple, and the son Silvester of
  January 1754 a child born eleven months after the wedding (no earlier child of theirs was seen).
* He appears in the same book as a **witness** at marriages of **10 February 1754** (Joannes Basletich × Lucia Muhvach) and
  **30 January 1755** (Michael Dieich × Matthia, widow of Jacobus Vorban) — an adult man living at Karlobag.
* **Correction to the baptism paragraph above:** I wrote that Oliva could be older than 1750. If she is Anna's daughter
  she was born **after the wedding of February 1753 and, because Silvester was born in January 1754, from about
  October 1754 and before about 1760** — inside the pages I read for her (1754 to August 1759), where she was not seen at one
  zoom, or in September 1759 – 1760, unread. She was then **14–19 at her wedding in 1774**. A child of an earlier marriage
  of Caetanus is not excluded and would be older than 1753; none is known.
* One entry looked like a second marriage and is not: on 12 August 1753 (image 288) the clerk wrote «Caietanum Bachi» and
  struck it through before «Nicolaum Braikovich et Dorotheam filiam Marci Stoiavich».

## Uroda leads from the same pages

«Mathiam Kriskovich et Magdalenam filiam **Dominici Uroda**» married on 5 February 1755 (image 289 right), and «Jacobus Uroda»
is a witness on 28 July 1754 (image 289 left); the November 1767 bride of the entry noted on the work list was also
«filiam defuncti Dominici Uroda». **A Dominicus Uroda, dead by 1767, with at least two daughters, is a Karlobag man of the
right age to be a relative of Georgius Uroda (ahnentafel 108)** — a lead, not a link: nothing read says Georgius is his son.

## Dominicus «Dume» Uroda was a regular marriage witness, 1744-1749

Reading the marriage book back to January 1742 (images 280-286) for the Bacchi and Uroda names found no Bacchi (the 1753 wedding is the
first) and no Uroda groom or bride, but **«Dume Uroda» / «Dominico Uroda» witnesses marriages again and again between 1744 and 1749**:
in the Croatian-language pages «Dume Uroda» (January and February 1744, January, February and June 1745, 1746, January 1747) and, when the
book turns to Latin in April 1748, «Dominico Uroda» (8 May, 5 and 7 October and 17 November 1748), roughly ten entries I counted by eye.
So **Dominicus Uroda was an adult, settled Karlobag man from at least 1744 and dead by November 1767** (his daughter Maria's
marriage then calls him «defuncti»), and his daughter Magdalena married in 1755. **He is the right age to be the father of Georgius
Uroda (ahnentafel 108, b. about 1745-55) or of an older Georgius** — but not a word read says so, and Georgius's own baptism
and marriage are still unfound. A baptism of a «Georgius» to Dominicus Uroda and a wife in 1745-55 would settle it; it was not looked for in
the 1750-59 baptism pages read earlier except by eye for Bacchi, so **a targeted re-read of those pages for Uroda is the next test.**

**Narrowing the Uroda marriage (index, 11 October 2026).** The FamilySearch index already lists a **Dominicus**, son of Georgius Uroda and Maria,
baptised **6 May 1764** — the first known child. A boy named Dominicus at the first-born position fits the habit of naming a son
after the father's father, which supports Dominicus «Dume» Uroda as Georgius's father (still not proved). It also means **the marriage of Georgius and Maria
falls before about August 1763**, not «about 1771-76» as the work list first assumed — and the marriage book read for 1742-1776 shows no such entry, so it is
either on one of the crossed-through or unread pages (1736-41; 302 left; 305 right), hiding behind a spelling of «Uroda» not recognised at one zoom, or
outside Karlobag (the bride's parish). The 1756-1763 pages (images 290 right to 298) are the ones to re-read slowly.

**Re-read of 1756-1763 (same day).** Images 290 right to 295 right (January 1756 to October 1763) were read again slowly, both pages of each opening: **no Uroda groom
or bride, and no «Georgius» marrying a Maria**. Together with 1742-1755 earlier the same day, **the Karlobag marriage book holds no Georgius
Uroda wedding from January 1742 to October 1763**; the unread parts are 1736-41 (images 278-279), the last weeks of 1763, and the two crossed-through pages.
**CORRECTION (same day, after the page itself was captured).** An earlier version of this paragraph said Georgius Uroda's death entry of 1791 gives his age as 72, so he
was born about 1719 and Maria was probably a second wife. **That was wrong.** The «annorum 72, piscator» is the death entry of **ahnentafel 54** (Georgius, d. 1846); the 1791 entry
for the *Dominus* Georgius Uroda (ahnentafel 108) **carries no age at all** — see the snapshot `karlobag-death-1791-uroda`. So **nothing is known of ahnentafel 108's age, and the
«second marriage» guess has no support.** What stands is the narrowing from the baptisms: the first known child, Dominicus, was baptised 6 May 1764, so the marriage was before about August 1763, and the
marriage book read for 1742-1776 shows no such entry — so it is behind an unrecognised spelling, on a page not read, or **outside Karlobag** (the bride's parish).

## Georgius Uroda × Maria: two baptism pages, 11 October 2026 (later)

* **2 November 1763 (image 149 left, book page 266):** Georgius and his wife **Maria Uroda** are the godparents of Carolus, son of **Paulus and Ursula Smojver** — [snapshot](https://daviddef.github.io/TheBlazevic/snapshots/#karlobag-baptism-1763-smojver-uroda-godparents). So the couple were married and in Karlobag by November 1763, consistent with the first known child six months later; and the godchild's family is written **«Smoivar»**, which I read as Smojver — eleven years before Vincentius Smojver married Oliva Bacchi (ahnentafel 106 × 107). That is a **possible** link between the two families, not a proved one: Smojver is a common Karlobag name and nothing says these are the same Smojvers.
* **6 May 1764 (image 151 left, book page 270):** the first child is **Joannes Dominicus** (the index has «Dominicum»), godparents **Antonio Barach of Šibenik and Vincentia, wife of Joannes Margvart** — [snapshot](https://daviddef.github.io/TheBlazevic/snapshots/#karlobag-baptism-1764-dominicus-uroda).
* **What still stands unanswered:** the marriage itself. Karlobag's marriage book has none for them from 1742 to 1776, the baptisms I looked at on images 146-150 (July 1762 – May 1764) name no Uroda as a parent, and the indexed Uroda baptisms start in 1733 (Antona, daughter of Sime Uroda and Eva) and then jump to 1764. Of the Dalmatian Urode in the index (Drniš 1771, 1780) nothing connects them. **The unindexed baptisms of 1740–1763 (images ~100-148) are the only place left to look, and a skim of the pages at about 1x is not a reliable search for a surname in cursive; it needs the same entry-by-entry reading as the marriage book.**

## Karlobag births read entry by entry, 1750 – June 1752 (images 112–116), 11 October 2026

Both pages of images 112, 113, 114, 115 and 116 were read in half-page strips at about 2x (January 1750 to 9 June 1752). The baptisms are in Croatian until about 1753.
**No Uroda appears as a PARENT on any of them.** Georgius Uroda is not baptised there and no baptism of a child of a Dominicus or a Georgius Uroda falls in these two and a half years. What does appear is a **widow, Jerolima ("Gjerolima udova … Dume Uroda"), godmother again and again** — 23 April 1750, 25 April 1751, 21 March and 3 April 1752 — and on **12 March 1752 a «Jure Uroda» (Georgius) is godfather beside her**. Snapshots: [karlobag-baptism-1750-04-jerolima-uroda](https://daviddef.github.io/TheBlazevic/snapshots/#karlobag-baptism-1750-04-jerolima-uroda); [karlobag-baptism-1751-04-jerolima-uroda](https://daviddef.github.io/TheBlazevic/snapshots/#karlobag-baptism-1751-04-jerolima-uroda); [karlobag-baptism-1752-03-12-jure-uroda](https://daviddef.github.io/TheBlazevic/snapshots/#karlobag-baptism-1752-03-12-jure-uroda); [karlobag-baptism-1752-03-21-dume-uroda](https://daviddef.github.io/TheBlazevic/snapshots/#karlobag-baptism-1752-03-21-dume-uroda); [karlobag-baptism-1752-04-03-jure-uroda](https://daviddef.github.io/TheBlazevic/snapshots/#karlobag-baptism-1752-04-03-jure-uroda).

* **Dume (Dominicus) Uroda was dead by April 1750.** This archive had him as a regular marriage witness 1744–1749 and as «defuncti» only in 1755 and 1767. The widow's title at the first baptism of 1750 shortens that to a death between about the last witness entry (1749) and 23 April 1750. (His death entry was not looked for.)
* **A Jure Uroda is an adult Karlobag man in 1752 and stands with Dume's widow.** That fits Georgius (ahnentafel 108) being Dume's son, and it fits nothing else seen. It is **not proof**: Jure is the commonest given name in the book, and the only link is that two godparents of one child share a surname.
* **It also changes the chronology.** If this Jure is ahnentafel 108 he was an adult by 1752 (a godparent is at least a teenager, probably older), so he was **born no later than about 1735**, which makes Maria (his wife by 1763) a **second** or late marriage, or him already about 30 at the first known child in 1764. The 1791 death entry gives no age; ahnentafel 54's father could be much older than the 1745–55 birth I had assumed.
* **Unread:** images 117–143 (June 1752 – 1762) were read only for Bacchi; 100–111 (before 1750) not at all.

## Karlobag births read back from October 1749 to November 1744 (images 98–111), 11 October 2026 (night)

Both pages of images 98–111 were read in half-page strips at about 2x (the hand is large and legible, Croatian). **The session ended in the middle of the next stretch (images 97 and 96 were being opened and were NOT read).**
* **No baptism of a Georgius/Jure Uroda, and no Uroda parent other than Dume, in these fifty-one months.**
* **Dume (Dominicus) Uroda and his wife Jerolina are the parish's commonest godparents** — «bihu kumi Dume Uroda i Jerolima xena Dume Uroda» turns up on almost every page from 1745 to 1749, and «Dominik Uroda» as kum in 1746 and 1748. So a Uroda in a godparent line is NOT evidence of kinship; it is the usual sound of this parish. Dume is still «xena Dume» (Jerolina his wife, not widow) in December 1748; **«udova» first in April 1750**, so he died between those dates.
* **One baptism of their own child: 11 August 1745, image 100 left, «Dume urode i xene Jerolime» — a daughter, name read as Marta Franceskina.** Snapshot [karlobag-baptism-1745-08-marta-uroda](https://daviddef.github.io/TheBlazevic/snapshots/#karlobag-baptism-1745-08-marta-uroda).
* **What this does to the «Jure Uroda» of 1752:** he is not Dume and Jerolina's baby baptised in 1744–49, and nothing here says he is their son either; if he was born before November 1744 his baptism is earlier still (images before 98).

### Continued: images 94–97 (June 1743 – November 1744), read the same night
* **Dume Uroda and Jerolina again serve as godparents** all through 1743–44 («Dume urode … Jerolina xena Dume urode»).
* **A SON of theirs is baptised on 10 May 1744** (image 96 left, book page 170): «Korstih sina Dume urode i njegove žene Jerolime imenom [Gratiova?]» *(the index reads the child Giakova — Jakov)*. The name is not read. Snapshot [karlobag-baptism-1744-05-son-of-dume-uroda](https://daviddef.github.io/TheBlazevic/snapshots/#karlobag-baptism-1744-05-son-of-dume-uroda). With the daughter of August 1745 that makes **two children of Dume and Jerolina in 15 months**: a young couple in the mid-1740s, which makes it unlikely that Dume was the father of a man already old enough to stand godfather in 1752.
* **So the «Jure Uroda» of 1752, if he is a Georgius at all, is more likely Dume's brother or nephew, or unrelated.** The name Jure is the commonest in the book.
* **Not yet read: images before 94 (before June 1743).** The next stretch is where a Jure Uroda baptism of about 1725–40 could be.

### Images 90–93 (January 1742 – June 1743), read the same night — a possible baptism of a Georgius, son of Dume Uroda
* Dume Uroda is again a godparent through 1742–43 («Dume urode» as kum 14 February 1742, 14 October 1742; «Jerolima xena Dume urode» in January 1743).
* **Image 91, right page, last entry (a 4th of a month in mid-1742): «Korstih sina Dume urode i njegove xene … imenom [Cvidora?] Juria.»** A son of Dume Uroda, and the name ends in what I read as **Juria** — Jurja, Georgius. [Snapshot](https://daviddef.github.io/TheBlazevic/snapshots/#karlobag-baptism-1742-son-of-dume-uroda). The first word of the name is unclear (it could be an Isidor-type name, or a second name), and the month is a doubtful reading, so **this is a lead, not an identification.**
* **If it is Georgius, it fits everything known:** born mid-1742; father Dume (Dominicus) — whence his own first son **Dominicus** in 1764; a young man of 21 in 1763 and 22 at his first child; dead in 1791 at about 49 (the 1791 entry gives no age). Dume and Jerolina would then have a son in 1742, another in May 1744 and a daughter in August 1745. (Magdalena, who married in 1755, and Maria, who married in 1767, are daughters of a Dominicus Uroda too, but whether of this Dume is not established.)
* **What it does NOT fit is the «Jure Uroda» godfather of 12 March 1752**, who would then be a ten-year-old; that entry must be a different, older Jure (or misread).
* **To close it:** read the line again at higher zoom (the tiles go no further than the image's own resolution, so the month may stay uncertain), and look for other mentions of this son (a godparent entry, a marriage, the 1764 godparents Antonio Barach and Vincentia Margvart).

### Re-read of the 1742 line at higher magnification (11 October 2026, late)
At about 1.3x the entry reads: «Na 4: [month: Ajmila/Apmila — NOT read] 1742 · [K]orstih **sina Dume urode** i njegove xene … imenom **[Gvidora / Evidora] Juria [Furia]**, bi kum Milo Bulinc … a kuma Marija xena Martina …». **«Sina Dume urode» — a son of Dume Uroda — is clear**; the child's name is **two words, the second read as Juria/Furia, the first as Gvidora/Evidora**, neither certain. The month cannot be April (the entry follows 3 and 4 June 1742 on the same page) and is not read. **So the lead stands at «a son of Dume Uroda, baptised in the second half of 1742, whose name may end in Jurja»; it is NOT shown to be Georgius.** A name like «Guido Jure» would be unusual in this parish, which is itself a reason for caution.
* *(Withdrawn 11 October 2026: the index does list them, under «Urode» — see the banner.)* The FamilySearch index lists none of Dume's children (it knows Uroda baptisms in Karlobag only for 1733 — Antona, daughter of Sime Uroda and Eva — and from 1764); so the 1742, 1744 and 1745 baptisms are unindexed, and no index will confirm them. A **Pompeo/Pompio Uroda, husband of Maria Anna Bachi,** also has children in the index (1760s–70s): a Bacchi marriage into an Uroda family, noted, not followed.

### Šibenik cathedral marriages, images 360–382 (December 1763 – January 1770): a living «Dno Dominico Uroda» (12 October 2026)
Read forward from image 360 in half-page strips at 0.85 (about one image to two months in these years; the right-hand page of 381 ends in smeared ink and the top of 382 left is scribbled out). **No Uroda groom or bride from December 1763 to January 1770; but on image 373, in the first entry of the left-hand page, a [«Dno Dominico Uroda»](https://daviddef.github.io/TheBlazevic/snapshots/#sibenik-marriage-1767-06-grubisich-witness-dominicus-uroda) is the second witness** (with Matthaeus Xeravizza) at the marriage, on **3 June 1767**, of Joannes Grubisich of the parish of S. ### Šibenik, Dolac parish marriages, images 440–471 (mid-1752 – July 1764): five sightings of a Dominicus Uroda, and a son Joannes (12 October 2026)
The other Šibenik parish whose marriages are filmed for these years is **Dolac** (S. * **27 September 1757, image 450** — «testibus Joseph Fevetti, et **[Joanne filio Dominici Uroda](https://daviddef.github.io/TheBlazevic/snapshots/#sibenik-dolac-marriage-1757-09-kvancich-witness-joannes-son-of-dominicus-uroda)**» — **a son Joannes of a Dominicus Uroda, of an age to witness**.
* **11 September 1759, image 458** — «[Magistro Domico Uroda](https://daviddef.github.io/TheBlazevic/snapshots/#sibenik-dolac-marriage-1759-09-meich-witness-magister-dominicus-uroda), et Antonio Lucenti» (**Magister**: a master of a trade).
* **10 August 1762, image 469** — «Illmo Domino Angelo Semonich alias Perova, et **[Dno Dominico Uroda](https://daviddef.github.io/TheBlazevic/snapshots/#sibenik-dolac-marriage-1762-08-loccasovich-witness-dominicus-uroda)**» (**Dno**: a gentleman).
* **3 June 1767, cathedral parish, image 373** — «Dno Dominico Uroda» again (see the section above).

**What it means, and what it does not.** There was a Dominicus Uroda in Šibenik from at least 1756 to 1767 who had a son Joannes — a living Uroda family in the town, a generation or two after the dead Dume of 1739. **A lead, not a link:** Georgius Uroda (ahnentafel 108) named his *first son Dominicus* in 1764, and the custom of the region is to name the first son for the father's father; Georgius's later son is Joannis (1784), as this Dominicus's is Joannes. But nothing read says Georgius is a son of this Dominicus, or from Šibenik at all, and Dominicus/Dume are one name, so the Karlobag Dominicus Uroda (father of Magdalena, 1755), Dume Kovač of Karlobag and this Šibenik man are **not** merged. What would settle it: a baptism of a **Georgius «filius Dominici Uroda»** (c. 1730–1742) in the Dolac, cathedral or Varoš baptism books (Dolac births 1701–1784 are waypoint 9R2C-L2Q; the cathedral births 1648–1737 and 1737–1812; Varoš 1711–1738 and 1738–1767), or Dominicus's own marriage.

**Where this leaves Georgius × Maria's marriage:** not in the Karlobag book, not in the Šibenik cathedral book (1752–1770), not in the Dolac book (1752–1764). The Varoš marriage book begins only in 1764 and Grad's in 1858, so a marriage at Varoš before 1764 is in no filmed book.

### Šibenik, Dolac parish baptisms, images 108–123 (August 1731 – April 1741): the Šibenik Uroda as godparents, 1733–1741 (12 October 2026)
The Dolac baptism book opens at image 108 (image 107 is its cover: «ŠIBENIK (DOLAC) KNJ. KRŠTENIH (22.VIII.1731 – 14.III.1762)»; the volume before it, 1701–1731, is not read). Read in half-page strips at 0.7, **the child's surname in the margin** being what was looked for. **No child of any Uroda in these ten years.** But three Uroda men and a wife stand as godparents, **earlier than any marriage sighting**:
* **Franciscus Uroda**, godfather to children of a Joannes and Anastasia/Antonia [Maraglia?] on [25 April 1733](https://daviddef.github.io/TheBlazevic/snapshots/#sibenik-dolac-baptism-1733-04-maria-maraglia-godfather-magister-franciscus-uroda) (image 109: «Mr Franciscus Vroda») and [30 January 1735](https://daviddef.github.io/TheBlazevic/snapshots/#sibenik-dolac-baptism-1735-01-domica-godfather-magister-franciscus-uroda) (image 111), and, as **«Magister»**, to a child of Antonius and Margareta Vidovich on [12 October 1738](https://daviddef.github.io/TheBlazevic/snapshots/#sibenik-dolac-baptism-1738-10-maria-vidovich-godfather-magister-franciscus-uroda) (image 118).
* **A Magister Georgius Uroda, married**: on [19 May 1738](https://daviddef.github.io/TheBlazevic/snapshots/#sibenik-dolac-baptism-1738-05-lucia-dragovich-godmother-wife-of-georgius-uroda) (image 117) the godmother is «Dna [Su]guetia [the name is unclear], uxor Mri Georgij Vroda». A master with a wife in 1738 was born by about 1715.
* **Magister Dominicus Uroda**, godfather on [1 January 1741](https://daviddef.github.io/TheBlazevic/snapshots/#sibenik-dolac-baptism-1741-01-joana-peresich-godfather-magister-dominicus-uroda) (image 123) — the same man (or the same name) as the Dominicus of the 1756–1767 marriages.

**What the picture is now.** Šibenik had a family of at least **three Uroda masters, Franciscus, Georgius and Dominicus**, all adults by 1733–1741, on top of the dead Dume of 1739 whose daughter Simona married at Karlobag. The obvious reading, not proved by any entry, is that they are **Dume's sons** (and Simona his daughter). **None of them can be Georgius of Karlobag's own self if «Georgius» of 1738 is married by 1738 and Georgius of Karlobag first fathers a child in 1764** (that would make him in his fifties or older, and Maria his late wife); more likely Georgius of Karlobag is a **son or nephew** of one of them, born about 1730–1742, and named for the master Georgius or for the first-born custom. That birth was **not** in the Dolac baptisms of 1731–1741 (read), so it is either before August 1731 (the earlier volume), in another parish (cathedral, Varoš), or at Karlobag.

Recorded as found persons `franciscus-uroda-of-sibenik` and `georgius-uroda-of-sibenik`, and the Šibenik lane on /lines/#uroda now carries all four men.

The Dolac baptism book opens with this volume's own first entries on image 6 (20 March 1701); the later book from August 1731 (images 108 on) was read first, and this reads it from the front. **Three Uroda households in the first five years:**

The masters of the 1730s–60s (Franciscus, Georgius, Dominicus, and Joannes) are most likely **their sons or grandsons**, and the dead Dume of 1739 probably belongs here too, but no entry in 1701–1708 says so (no Franciscus, Georgius, Dominicus or Dume child was baptised in these eight years). Read to image 41 (February 1712); 42–106 (to August 1731) remain.

**Addendum, images 42–59 (to January 1717):** **Franciscus Vrodich's family grows:** after Paulus (1710) a daughter **[Stanislava](https://daviddef.github.io/TheBlazevic/snapshots/#sibenik-dolac-baptism-1712-11-stanislava-daughter-of-franciscus-vrodich)** (21 November 1712, image 44), and on 28 October 1715 (image 53) a **[SIMONA](https://daviddef.github.io/TheBlazevic/snapshots/#sibenik-dolac-baptism-1715-10-simona-daughter-of-franciscus-vrodich)**, born 21 October, with the mother written **«Lucia»** (not Anna) and a godfather «D. Georgius Vrodi[ch?]» — **a Georgius of the Uroda already in 1715, and another second wife for Franciscus (or a second Franciscus)**. The Simona of 1715 is **not** the Simona who married at Karlobag in 1739 as «daughter of the late Dume Uroda od Šibenika» (her father is Franciscus here), though she would be 24 in 1739 and the coincidence is worth keeping in view: the 1739 bride's father may have been mis-named, or there were two Simonas. Images 42–52 and 54–59 otherwise show no Uroda (a «Vidrayovich» of 1714 is a different surname). **Images 60–106 (January 1717 – August 1731) remain.**

### Šibenik, Dolac deaths, images 223–254 (November 1740 – August 1754): the death of Georgius, and a third master, Antonius (13 October 2026)
The Dolac deaths book 1701–1783 (waypoint 9R2Z-C62; no margin surnames after 1740, so each entry was read by eye) was read for the Uroda masters. **It gives the death of the Georgius whose son Dominicus is «filius Magistri Georgii» in 1746 and «filius qd. Georgij» in 1757:**
* **[«Dominus Georgius Vroda aetatis annorum 60», 17 August 1754](https://daviddef.github.io/TheBlazevic/snapshots/#sibenik-dolac-death-1754-08-dominus-georgius-vroda-aged-60)**, confessed and anointed but, for continual frenzy, without Communion, buried in the Dominicans' church of S. Dominic (image 254). This is the only Georgius Vroda found dying between 1746 and 1754, and it falls inside the window the marriage and baptism books fixed (alive 4 August 1746, dead by 13 February 1757). **Born about 1694** (ages in death registers are loose). **His daughter [Magdalena](https://daviddef.github.io/TheBlazevic/snapshots/#sibenik-dolac-death-1740-10-magdalena-daughter-of-magister-georgius-vrode) was buried on 29 October 1740** — and she is not in the 1731–1744 Dolac baptism book either.
* **[Paulus, an infant son of Magister Dominicus and Floria Vrodda](https://daviddef.github.io/TheBlazevic/snapshots/#sibenik-dolac-death-1744-01-paulus-infant-son-of-magister-dominicus-and-floria-vrodda)**, buried 7 January 1744 — a first child of the pair whose Lucia (born December 1744) and Vittoria (May 1745) follow.
* **[Lucia, «ux. q. Mag: Dominici Vrodda», aged about 77, 21 June 1746](https://daviddef.github.io/TheBlazevic/snapshots/#sibenik-dolac-death-1746-06-lucia-of-the-late-magister-dominicus-vrodda-aged-77)** — the widow of a **Magister Dominicus Vrodda who was dead by June 1746** and who must be of the elder generation (his wife was born about 1669). So there are two masters called Dominicus: an **elder** (the Dominicus Vroda with a wife Ursula in 1692, godfather and witness in 1726 and 1739, dead before June 1746?) and the **younger**, son of Georgius, married to Floria (1744–45).
* **A third master, Antonius:** [Marietta](https://daviddef.github.io/TheBlazevic/snapshots/#sibenik-dolac-death-1742-01-marietta-daughter-of-magister-antonius-vroda) (18 January 1742) and [Dominica, about 13](https://daviddef.github.io/TheBlazevic/snapshots/#sibenik-dolac-death-1742-07-dominica-daughter-of-magister-antonius-and-antonia-vrodda) (19 July 1742), children of **Magister Antonius and Antonia Vrodda** — the Antonius son of Lucas of the 1692 baptism and the 1718 marriage.

**What it means for Georgius of Karlobag (ahnentafel 108).** The Šibenik Georgius (b. c. 1694, d. 1754) **cannot be the Karlobag Georgius, who is alive from 1763 to 1791**; his only recorded children are a Magdalena (1740) and a Dominicus. **No Georgius Uroda was baptised in any Dolac baptism book (1685–1762)** — so the Karlobag Georgius, if he is from Šibenik, was baptised in the cathedral or Varoš registers or is a grandson or nephew named for the Šibenik Georgius. The name pattern is **Georgius → Dominicus (his son) → Georgius?**, which would make the Karlobag Georgius a son of the younger Dominicus; Dominicus's Dolac children are Paulus, Lucia, Vittoria (1744–45) and a Joannes (b. before 1757), **none named Georgius**. **This is no longer simply a pattern in the names: the Dolac books contradict the easy version of it.**
Not found: the death of the **Franciscus** Uroda (dead by 6 May 1742; probably in the unread images 216–221), of a **Dume** (dead by 7 June 1739; unread, images before 215) and of the wife of Georgius. Not read: images 216–221 and 255–262, and 1–214 (1701–1738).

### Šibenik cathedral baptisms 1693–1702 (9R28-SPJ, images 261–329) and 1737–1740 (9R2D-MNY, images 10–26): Georgius is baptised on 6 October 1694, son of Dominicus and Lucietta Vroda

**The finding.** The cathedral book «Liber Baptizatorum 1693–» (cover at image 260) carries, in 1693–1701, a **surname in the margin** beside every entry, so it can be scanned without reading each line; from 1702 the margin gives only forenames. A margin scan of images 264–329 (1693 to about 1702) turned up three Vroda entries, all **baptisms done in the cathedral baptistery for the Dolac parish** — which is why the three Dolac baptism books never held them:

* **[«Georgius Vroda», baptised 10 October 1694, born 6 October, «ex Coniugibus Dominico et Lucietta Vroda»](https://daviddef.github.io/TheBlazevic/snapshots/#sibenik-cathedral-birth-1694-10-georgius-son-of-dominicus-and-lucietta-vroda)** (image 272). The godparents are the Illustrissimus Dominus Hieronymus Sopra[?] and Donna Franceschina, wife of Vincenzo de Dominis — a noble sponsorship. **The Georgius Vroda who died at Dolac on 17 August 1754 «aetatis annorum 60» would have been 59 years 10 months old that day:** the age fits to the month, and nothing else in the book contradicts it. His parents' names — **Dominicus (a Magister) and Lucia** — are those of the **elder Magister Dominicus Vrodda and his widow Lucia, who died aged about 77 on 21 June 1746** (born about 1669): so the elder Dominicus is Georgius's father and Lucia his mother, and the «two Magister Dominicus» of the deaths section become **grandfather and grandson**.
* **[Simeon, son of «Magister Domco» and «Lucia Vrodda», of the parish of S. Marci Suburbii, 14 [October] 1696](https://daviddef.github.io/TheBlazevic/snapshots/#sibenik-cathedral-birth-1696-10-simeon-son-of-magister-dominicus-and-lucia-vrodda)** (image 286) — the same couple two years later, now called of the Dolac parish, with Georgius's brother Simeon; the godparents are an officer of the Lombard militia and the Governor's wife.
* **[Michaela, daughter of «Magister Lucas and Maria Vrodal», January 1694](https://daviddef.github.io/TheBlazevic/snapshots/#sibenik-cathedral-birth-1694-01-michaela-daughter-of-magister-lucas-and-maria-vroda)** (image 268) — the Lucas Vrodi × Mariota household of the Dolac books (Antonius 1692, Michael 1698), whose Michaela is in no Dolac book.

**What it changes.** (1) The Dolac baptism books are **not complete for the Uroda**: a family that sponsors at the cathedral baptises there. The earlier statement «no Georgius, Dominicus or Dume baptism in any Dolac book» stands for the Dolac books and is **now answered for Georgius** — he is in the cathedral book. (2) The Šibenik line gets a **third generation above Dominicus**: Dominicus (b. c. 1665?) × Lucietta (b. c. 1669) → **Georgius (1694–1754)** and Simeon (1696) → **Dominicus (c. 1715–20 ×  Floria)** → Paulus (1744), Lucia (1744), Vittoria (1745), Joannes. (3) Whether the elder Dominicus is the Dominicus × Ursula of 1687–93 is **not shown** — a first wife Ursula and a second Lucietta is possible, as is a second Dominicus. (4) **The Karlobag link stays unproved and is no stronger**: the Šibenik Georgius died in 1754; a Georgius alive at Karlobag 1763–91 must be another man, and no baptism of one has been found.

**How far the scan went (and did not).** Read as a margin scan at about a quarter scale, **not entry by entry**: images 264–329 (January 1693 – about mid-1702), seen as spreads, and 10–26 of the 1737–1768 book (December 1737 – about 1740). A margin scan finds an entry only if the surname is written in the margin and is legible at that scale; it cannot prove an absence, and from about 1702 the margin has forenames only. **Not read at all:** cathedral births images 330–672 (1702–1737) — forename-only margins, so these need entry-by-entry reading; images 27–140+ of the second book; the later book 1768–1812; the earlier books 1648–1692 (images 1–254, among them the births of Dominicus, of Lucietta, and of any children before 1694).

### Where the Uroda question stands (13 October 2026) — for row 9, and Šibenik left open

**Georgius Uroda of Karlobag (ahnentafel 108) and Maria (109), what is established.** A «Dnus Georgius Uroda» at Karlobag, first met in 1763, his first child (Dominicus) baptised 6 May 1764 with a godfather Antonio Barach «of Šibenik», children to 1777, buried 12 September 1791 in his own crypt before the parish church. Maria's maiden name is not found, their marriage is in no filmed book or index, and the godparents of six children are officials, not kin. His age is never given, so he was born between about 1720 and 1742.

**What Šibenik now says.** A Magister Georgius Vroda, **baptised at the cathedral on 10 October 1694** as the son of Magister Dominicus and Lucietta, died at Dolac on **17 August 1754**. His son Dominicus (× Flora) had Joseph Joannes (1739), Anna (1741), Paulus, Lucia (1744) and Vittoria (1745). **None of that is Georgius of Karlobag:** the Šibenik Georgius is dead nine years before Karlobag's first sighting, and no child of either Georgius or Dominicus is a Georgius. What remains is a **possibility, not a link**: that the Karlobag Georgius was a younger son of the Šibenik Georgius, or of another Šibenik Uroda household, baptised at a place not read, between about 1720 and 1742. The only things in common are the surname, a Šibenik godfather in 1764, and a first son named Dominicus.

**What would settle it** (none is found, none is likely to be found by sweeping): a baptism «Georgius, son of …» at Šibenik 1720–1742; Georgius × Maria's marriage, 1760–63, anywhere; Maria's maiden name; an age at death; a Karlobag or Šibenik record that calls him «of Šibenik». **Recommendation for row 9: hold ahnentafel 108 and 109 at Karlobag, origin unproved; do not attach him to Šibenik.**

**Šibenik, what was read and what was not — plainly.**

* **Read entry by entry:** the three Dolac baptism books (1685–1762), the Dolac marriage book (1701–1764), the cathedral marriage book from May 1752 to January 1770, and the Dolac deaths of September 1738 – March 1756 (images 215–259; a few half-read).
* **Read only as a margin scan (names visible in the margin, not every entry):** cathedral births 1693 – mid-1702 (9R28-SPJ images 264–329) and December 1737 – January 1744 (9R2D-MNY images 11–59). These found the baptisms of Georgius, Simeon and Michaela, and the Dominicus × Flora children.
* **Tried page by page and not reliable:** cathedral births July 1702 – October 1703 (SPJ 330–341). Nothing seen; not a finding of absence.
* **Not read:** cathedral births 1648–1692 (images 1–254), 1703–1737 (342–672) and after 1744 (MNY 60–691); the Varoš births (9R2C-2JW and 9R28-BZ4, 280 images); the Dolac deaths 1701–1738 and 1756–1768; the cathedral marriages 1770–1787. FamilySearch's full-text search has no Šibenik coverage.
* **Why it stops here:** after 1701 the cathedral books carry forenames only in the margin, the hand is cramped, and a trustworthy sweep would take about 1,400 screenshots for one block alone. A Šibenik read from now on should be **targeted** — a year taken from another register — not a sweep.

### Caetanus Bachi — what the index and three pages add (13 October 2026), and a Bachi at Karlobag in 1715

* **Angelus Bacchi, apothecary of Pag, died 19 January 1764 aged about 68** ([page](https://daviddef.github.io/TheBlazevic/snapshots/#pag-death-1764-01-angelus-bacchi-aromatarius)) — born about 1696, the age of the «Dno Angelo Bachi, patrician» who had Caietanus Antonius Gregorius baptised at Pag in 1728; the trade, «Aromatarius», is new. The book is Italian; Nin's burials are in waypoint 9R2H-PYM. Not shown to be the Pag family.
* **A possible Bachi at Karlobag 38 years before the marriage:** a son Luca baptised in October 1715 to a «Mikule Bach[…]» ([page](https://daviddef.github.io/TheBlazevic/snapshots/#karlobag-baptism-1715-10-luca-son-of-mikule-bach)). If it is Bachi, **Caetanus (1753) need not have come from Pag**: a Karlobag Bachi family from at least 1715 would make him local. Not proved either way.

* **Recommendation for row 82:** the Pag candidate stays a candidate — right name, right age (24–25 in 1753), a Pag mother and a Karlobag daughter both called Oliva — and gains nothing from these three pages, while a Karlobag Bachi of 1715 is a second candidate home. **The decisive record would be a Karlobag or Pag entry that calls him «of Pag» or names his father**; none has turned up in the index or in the 1753 entries read.
