# Two more daughters of «Paulus Tomljanovich of Stanište», Karlobag 1789 and 1795

17 October 2026. Found while reading the Karlobag marriage book (DGS 5481649, waypoint 9RK1-HZK, which the atlas lists as Marriages 1736–1804) for a marriage of Michael Gergacs after 1789 (see [karlobag-gergacs-wife-search-1789-1801.md](karlobag-gergacs-wife-search-1789-1801.md)). Both entries are read from the image at about 4x.

| date | image | groom | bride |
|---|---|---|---|
| 27 July 1789 | 330, right page, folio 53 | Joannes Medvidich, widower, «vicariani Brušanensis» | **Barbara**, daughter of **Pauli Tomljanovich, loci Stanište** |
| 25 May 1795 | 340, left page, folio 122 | Philippus Gerpich, widower, of Ledenice | **Thoma**, daughter of **Pauli Tomljenovich, loci Stanizte** |

Snapshots: [1789](https://blazevic.defranceski.com/snapshots/#karlobag-marriage-1789-medvidich-tomljanovic) and [1795](https://blazevic.defranceski.com/snapshots/#karlobag-marriage-1795-gerpich-thoma-tomljanovic).

**Why it matters.** Lucia, wife of Michael Gergacs (ahnentafel 111), married in 1785 as «Luciae Pauli Tomljenovich, loci Montani Stanize» ([note](karlobag-marriage-1785-gerkacs-tomljanovic.md)). The same father's name and the same hamlet turn up in 1789 and 1795 for two other brides. **They are probable sisters of Lucia and probable further children of ahnentafel 222.** That is an inference, not a reading: Paulus was a common name and Stanište had several Tomljanović households (an entry of 9 February 1789 names a Joannes Tomljenovich of the same hamlet as groom, and others appear as brides' fathers in 1797–1800). Neither entry names a mother.

**Cautions.**

* «Thoma» as a woman's name is unusual; the letters are plain at 4x but the name may be an abbreviation or a slip. The father and the hamlet are not in doubt.
* The 1789 entry has a struck-through word before «Joannis» (a groom's first name begun and cancelled).
* Neither bride is in the tree.
