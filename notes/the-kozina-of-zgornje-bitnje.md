# The Kozina of Zgornje Bitnje: a Carniolan household, for the other seven archives

**Written from the Blažević archive's own data on 28 September 2026.** Everything below was read
out of the parish registers of **Kranj – Šmartin** in Slovenia between 21 and 28 September 2026.
Those registers are on **[Matricula](https://data.matricula-online.eu/sl/slovenia/ljubljana/kranj-smartin/)**,
they are **free, and they need no login** — which is the first reason this note exists. Every other
archive in this estate is waiting on FamilySearch for something. This parish is open right now.

**The second reason is that this is a whole family nobody was looking for.** It reaches this estate
through one line on a Senj marriage certificate of January 1888, and it turned out to run four
generations back into Carniola.

---

## How it attaches

**Hedviga Blažević's** line does not pass through these people. They attach twice, sideways:

* **Anka Blažević married Pavao Kosina** at Senj — which is how the surname enters this estate at all.
* **Angelika Boras married Martin Kozina** — so the **Boras** archive touches them too, and did not
  know it until this week.

**Martin Kozina** is the hinge. He is born at **Oberfeichting — Zgornje Bitnje — house 9**, near
Kranj, on **16 November 1841**, *unehelich*, the father's column blank. He turns up in Senj forty-odd
years later. Everything above him was in Slovenia the whole time.

## The line, as read

| | Who | What the register says |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Franz Kosinar** × **Maria «Mina» Dortschen** | m. 27 October 1800, he 24, she 20. He d. 22 January 1849 aged 73, *Ausnehmer*, at Oberfeichting 9. She d. 11 April 1841 aged 60, *Auszüglerin*, in the same house — **seven months before her grandson Martin was born in it**. |
| **2** | **Gertraud «Jera» Kozina**, b. 18 March 1814 | Martin's mother. Never married. d. **5 March 1880**, *neomožena gostja* — an unmarried lodger — at **Zgornje Bitno 9**, aged 65, thirteen days short of sixty-six. |
| **3** | **Martin Kozina**, b. 16 November 1841 | Oberfeichting 9, *unehelich*. Marries at Senj in January 1888. |

**And beside them, the house.** Franz and Maria had **six known children**, three of whom died
small: Elisabeth (5 months), Maria (9 months), Valentin (14 days). Of the three who grew up,
**two sisters never married and never left**: Maria d. 1868 a *ledige Inwohnerin* and Gertraud d.
1880 a *neomožena gostja*, both lodgers in the house they were born in, twelve years apart. One of
them had borne Martin there.

**Their brother Johann** held the house. He married **Lucia Labornik** in January 1833 and had
**Anton 1834** and **Petrus 1836**; Lucia died in that house on 30 November 1850; by 1853 he had
**Ursula Sedej**, and three daughters — **Franziska 1853, Anna 1856, Maria 1859**. He died there on
**10 October 1868**, aged 64, a *Häubler* to the end.

## Three things this cost, and they are the transferable part

**1. AN INDEX PROVES WHAT IT CONTAINS AND NEVER WHAT IT OMITS.** The letter-tabbed death index for
Kranj–Šmartin 1836–1869 lists **two** Kozina. The register itself, walked page by page — all 174 —
holds **nine**, and **seven of them died at house 9**. Two separate published conclusions had been
built on that index's silence and both were wrong.

**2. THE MAGNIFICATION THAT SHOWS A COLUMN HAS NAMES IN IT IS NOT THE MAGNIFICATION THAT READS
THEM.** Page 16 of the 1812–31 baptisms was read at **2.4×** and published as holding no Kozina; at
**9×** it gives *«Vater: LUKAS KOSINER, Kaischler»*. An hour later page 8 was read at **3.2×**, two
Dorotheas were found, and the same sentence was published again — **there are three**, and the third
is *«ANTON KOSINER recte KATZIN»*. Before writing *not on this page*, re-crop the one column at 8×
and **count the rows against the page's own sequence**.

**3. A MARRIED WOMAN IS NOT ALWAYS UNDER HER MAIDEN NAME.** The 1890–1911 deaths were walked for
**KOZINA** and gave Marija's two children and not Marija. Walked again for **ČESEN**, she is on page
45: *«MARIJA ČESEN, hišarja in sitarja žena, starost 35 let, OTROŠKA MRZLICA»*, 2 April 1895. The
same register gives *«Marija roj. Eržen»* and *«Neža roj. Mravlje»* their maiden names on the same
openings, **so this was not a rule anyone could have known in advance**. When a woman is missing,
walk for her husband's surname too.

**And one that is only a warning.** *«Kosiner recte Katzin»* — **recte** marks the *legal* surname
against the name a family is called by. So a surname in an index can be a **house-name**. When a
surname is the whole thread being followed, an index line is not yet a person of that name.

## 11 October 2026 — Franz's baptism, and what it changes

The 1603–1779 index had no Franz, and the register has him: **3 December 1775, Franciscus Xaverius Kosina, son of Lucas and Helena Kosina, S. Thomae No. 10.** Both of Franz's ages fit only 28 October 1775 – 22 January 1776, and house 10 is the number his marriage and death entries give. Probable, not proved (Kosina against Kosinar). If it holds, **Lukas Kosina the elder and Helena Perner are his parents**, the 1773 Gertrud and the 1779 Helena his sisters, and the house test of 9 October (inconclusive because Helena's entry read 17) comes out for rather than against — though the 1779 number should be re-read. **Third lesson of the same kind: the index proves what it contains.**
