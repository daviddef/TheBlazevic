# Senj baptisms 1854 and 1857: Daniel Špalj and Franciscae nata Perpić — the same names as the tree's Daniel Špalj and Francesca Prpić, and a house at Krivi Put

Read from the images on 23 November 2026: Senj births 1756–1858 (FamilySearch waypoint 9R2M-L27, 589 images). Work list row 29, done **by the parents' names**: the tree
carries a **Daniel Špalj** and a **Francesca Prpić** as the parents of Margaritte (born 1871 at Krmpote 152), Stanislava (1873, Senj) and Mathea (1875, Senj); the FamilySearch index
was asked for children of a father Daniel (Danielis) and a mother Franciscaina under the surname Spalj, 1850–1860, and returned two, at images 537 and 563. Both were opened and read.
The index note ([senj-baptism-index.md](senj-baptism-index.md), no. 6) had put the 1850s «bridge» as «very probable» on the two names alone; this is the page.
**Control:** the same pass opened images 244, 245 and 249 (the Babić children) first; their entries were found as the index described them.

**Reading limits.** Both are in a clear hand (the same priest, Antonius Lukovich, writes both). The 1854 entry's second godparent's surname is read with a query, and the priest's surname is written with a flourish over the last letter; every other word quoted is clear.
The 1854 line division reads «Franciscae na-» then «ta Perpić», so the wife's maiden name is Perpić as written there; in 1857 it is written *Prpić*.

## What the reading settles

- **The index's names are the register's.** Daniel Spalj and Franciscaina/Franciscae Perpić/Prpić, as the index had them; Lucia's baptism is 1 December 1854 and Mathia's is 22 February 1857 (**the register adds that Mathia was born the day before**,
  which the index does not carry).
- **Krivi Put.** The 1854 entry places the family in the parish of B.M.V. ad nives (Krivi Put), **house No 33**. The 1857 entry gives the father's status: a frontier soldier (*Confiniarius*) of the Ogulin regiment,
  of the Krmpote company (*Centuria Kermpotensis*), parish B.M.V. ad nives. Krmpote is where the tree places the 1871 child of **Daniel Špalj and Francesca Prpić** (Margaritte, Krmpote 152): a second thing the 1850s couple and the tree's couple share, beside the names.
- **The couple of the tree is probably, not provably, the couple of 1854 and 1857.** The names agree (Daniel, a wife Francisca née Perpić/Prpić), the Krmpote company agrees with the tree's 1871 address, and the years fit a family of five or more children
  (1854, 1857, 1871, 1873, 1875 — **the register's two and the tree's three leave 1858–1870 empty**; no child of those years is read). What would prove it is a marriage or a child's entry that names both with a date or a house; none is read here.
- **The godparents.** Andrea Špalj and **Maria Špalj, wife of Laurentius** stand in 1857. The index holds a Lovre (Laurentius) Špalj × Marie Perpich baptism of 1849 (image 481); the Maria of 1857 may be that Marie, a Perpić by birth, and then the two Špalj households would be linked by the wife's family — **a suggestion only**: nothing in the 1857 entry says her maiden name.

**Where it leaves the other Špalj × Perpić households.** Images 481 (Lovre × Marie, 1849), 503 and 529 (Illia/Eliae Špalj × Lutia Perpić, 1 June 1851 and 30 March 1854) and 562 (Eliae Špalj × Luciae Perpić, 15 February 1857: a second Mathia, a week before Daniel's, on the page before it) are **not read**: the tree names no parents for them, so they were not asked for by name. The index is the list to open if one is wanted.

**Terms tried (the Atlas catalogue's Špalj family):** Spalj, with the father Danielis/Daniel and the mother Franciscaina; the pages write Spalj with a mark over the s. The mother's maiden name: Perpić, Perpic, Prpić (the catalogue's Prpić family); read as Perpić (1854) and Prpić (1857). Near names seen on the pages, not counted: none.
