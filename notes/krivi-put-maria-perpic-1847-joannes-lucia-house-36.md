# Krivi Put, 7 February 1847: Maria, daughter of Joannes Perpić and Lucia nata Perpić, house No 36

Read from the image on 22 November 2026: Krivi Put births 1847–1884 (DGS 5494757, FamilySearch waypoint 9R28-DP8, 382 images), **image 6**
(the sixth ark of the waypoint's list), right-hand page, printed page 3, entry 21. Image 5 is page 1, headed «Anno 1847», and the entries
begin on 3 January 1847, so this entry is 7 February 1847.

**The entry.** «Die 7ma Februarii … Baptizata est **Maria** … Krivi put **N° 36** … Filia legitima **Joannis Perpich et Lucis ejus conjugis
nata Perpich**. Levantibus Patrinis: Michaele Perpich et Maria Perpich.» The baptiser is Josephus (the surname is not settled), «adm.»;
number 21. Both parents are written with the one surname, the wife as «nata Perpich». The same formula is the one the 1868 entries use for
the house-135 and house-136 couple (Joannes and Lucia, «nata Perpić»).

**What this does and does not say**
- It is a **candidate** for an elder sister of ahnentafel 7 (Antonija, b. 1859, whose parents are ahnentafel 14, Joannes Perpić, and 15,
  Lucia). It is not proved. The parents' names and the wife's maiden-name formula match, but **the house does not**: No 36 here, No 136 in
  1859 and 1861, No 135 for the 1868 and 1874 children, No 177 for the Martha of 1853 and No 111 for the Toma of 1856 (notes of 9 October
  2026). Either houses were renumbered or moved between 1847 and 1859, or there are several couples called Joannes and Lucia Perpić at
  Krivi Put. The register does not say which.
- Nothing is changed in the tree. This is a find beside the tree, not a person in it.

## What was read (and what was not)

Images 5 to 8 (printed pages 1 to 8), **both pages of each, top and bottom halves**, in three columns: the child's name, the house number and
the parents, at about 0.4x with the entry number and house kept beside each row. The name Perpić occurs in many rows there (fathers Lucas,
Vitus, Michael, Franciscus, Georgius, Martinus, Thomas, Stephanus and others); **no other row has parents named Joannes and Lucia**.
Images 9 to 11 were looked at the same way but the window drifted off the columns on several pages (the leaves are photographed at
different positions), so they are **not** counted as read. The right-hand page of image 8 has a different layout (a list of entries
with «Domus» numbers, headed 1848) and was not read. Images 12 to 63 are not read.
